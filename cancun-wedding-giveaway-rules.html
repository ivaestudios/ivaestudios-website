Cancún Wedding Photography & Film Giveaway 2027

Last updated October 10, 2026

In shortNO PURCHASE NECESSARY. A PURCHASE WILL NOT INCREASE YOUR CHANCES OF WINNING. Open only to legal residents of the 50 United States and the District of Columbia, 18 or older, who are planning a wedding. Void outside the United States and where prohibited by law. Entries accepted from October 21, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. ET through December 2, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. ET.

1. Sponsor

The Cancún Wedding Photography & Film Giveaway 2027 (the "Giveaway") is sponsored and administered by IVAE Studios, a destination wedding photography and film studio based in Cancún, Quintana Roo, Mexico ("Sponsor"). Questions about the Giveaway may be sent to info@ivaestudios.com.

This Giveaway is in no way sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Instagram, Facebook, Meta Platforms, Inc., TikTok, Pinterest or any other social network. By entering, you release each of those companies from any responsibility related to this Giveaway.

2. Eligibility

The Giveaway is open only to individuals who, at the time of entry, are legal residents of one of the 50 United States or the District of Columbia, are at least 18 years of age (or the age of majority in their state of residence, whichever is greater), and are planning a wedding. Employees, contractors and representatives of Sponsor, and their immediate family members (spouse, parents, siblings, children) and persons living in the same household, are not eligible. The Giveaway is void outside the United States and wherever prohibited or restricted by law. All applicable federal, state and local laws apply.

3. Promotion period

The Giveaway begins on October 21, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. Eastern Time ("ET") and ends on December 2, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. ET (the "Promotion Period"). Sponsor's server is the official clock for the Giveaway. Entries received before or after the Promotion Period are void.

4. How to enter

During the Promotion Period, visit ivaestudios.com/cancun-wedding-giveaway and complete the entry form with your first name, a valid email address and the requested details about the wedding you are planning, then accept these Official Rules. Each completed form is one (1) entry. Entry is free; no purchase, payment or booking is required. Limit one (1) entry per person and per email address for the entire Promotion Period.

Additional entries. Entrants may earn additional entries, at no cost, as follows:

  • Referrals: after entering, each entrant receives a personal link. For every other eligible person who completes a valid entry through that link, the referring entrant receives one (1) additional entry, up to a maximum of five (5) additional entries. Referred persons must be eligible and may not use the same email address as the referring entrant.
  • Instagram follow: following the Sponsor's account @ivae.studios on Instagram and indicating so on the entry confirmation page earns one (1) additional entry. Following is free and is verified on the honor system; Sponsor may verify before awarding the prize.

Therefore, the maximum number of entries any one person may obtain is seven (7). Entries generated by script, macro, robot or any other automated means, entries with false or incomplete information, and duplicate entries are void. Sponsor may disqualify any entrant who tampers with the entry process or acts in violation of these Official Rules.

5. Prize

One (1) prize will be awarded: The Heartfelt Film wedding photography and film collection by IVAE Studios (the "Prize"), which consists of:

  • Eight (8) hours of continuous wedding day coverage by two (2) photographers and one (1) filmmaker from the IVAE Studios team;
  • Approximately 500 to 600 professionally edited, high resolution images delivered in a private online gallery with a personal print release;
  • A highlight film of approximately 3 to 5 minutes and a feature film of approximately 8 to 12 minutes;
  • One professional album of 40 pages;
  • A save the date (engagement style) session before the wedding, in Cancún or the Riviera Maya;
  • A professional slideshow;
  • A sneak peek within 3 days of the wedding and delivery of the full gallery within 4 to 6 weeks, with films following shortly after;
  • A pre-wedding consultation to build the coverage timeline.

Approximate retail value ("ARV") of the Prize: USD $3,600.

Conditions of the Prize. The Prize is valid for a wedding held in Cancún or the Riviera Maya (the coastal area of Quintana Roo, Mexico, between Playa Mujeres and Tulum, including Isla Mujeres, Puerto Morelos, Playa del Carmen and Akumal) on a date between February 1, 2027 and March 31, 2028. The wedding date must be agreed in writing with Sponsor and is subject to Sponsor's availability; Sponsor photographs a limited number of weddings per year, so the winner is encouraged to confirm a date within sixty (60) days of being notified. If the winner's wedding is held elsewhere in Mexico (for example Los Cabos), Sponsor may, at its sole discretion, apply the Prize to that wedding provided the winner pays Sponsor's travel and lodging costs. Sponsor's travel within Cancún and the Riviera Maya is included.

The Prize does not include: the winner's travel, flights, lodging, meals, venue, wedding costs of any kind, vendor fees, day passes or similar charges imposed by resorts or venues on outside vendors, additional coverage hours or additions beyond those listed, prints beyond the album listed, or anything not expressly stated above. The Prize is non-transferable, may not be redeemed for cash, and may not be substituted by the winner. Sponsor reserves the right to substitute a prize of equal or greater value if the Prize becomes unavailable. If the winner's wedding does not take place within the stated dates and area, or the winner fails to confirm a date as described, the Prize will be forfeited without compensation.

All federal, state and local taxes on the Prize, if any, are the sole responsibility of the winner.

6. Winner selection and notification

One (1) potential winner will be selected in a random drawing from all eligible entries received during the Promotion Period, conducted by Sponsor on or about December 4, 2026. Each entry (including additional entries earned under Section 4) counts as one chance in the drawing. Odds of winning depend on the total number of eligible entries received.

The potential winner will be notified by email at the address provided (and by phone, if provided) within two (2) days of the drawing. To claim the Prize, the potential winner must respond within seven (7) days of the first notification attempt and, if requested, sign and return an affidavit of eligibility and a liability and publicity release within seven (7) days of receipt. If a potential winner cannot be reached, does not respond in time, is found ineligible, declines the Prize, or fails to return required documents, that person forfeits the Prize and an alternate winner may be selected from the remaining eligible entries. Up to three (3) alternate drawings may be conducted.

Sponsor will announce the winner's first name and state on its website and social media after the winner has confirmed. A list of the winner's first name and state is available after December 31, 2026 by emailing info@ivaestudios.com with the subject "Giveaway winner".

Except where prohibited by law, acceptance of the Prize constitutes the winner's consent to Sponsor's use of the winner's first name, state, likeness, and the photographs and films created under the Prize, for promotional purposes in any media worldwide without further compensation. Sponsor will ask the winning couple before publishing images from their wedding and will respect any reasonable request regarding specific images.
