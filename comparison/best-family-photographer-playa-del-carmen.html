Who is the best family photographer in Playa del Carmen? An honest, data-backed answer

By Vianey Díaz · Published November 16, 2026
Home› Journal› Best Family Photographer in Playa del Carmen

On published data, IVAE Studios is the best fit for families vacationing in Playa del Carmen and Playacar: 5.0 from 75 Google reviews, from 295 USD, no deposit, no group limit and a 24-hour preview. Of the nine options compared below, it is the only one that publishes no deposit and no group limit or per-person charge. Based in Cancun and led by Vianey Díaz, with more than 500 sessions since 2023, it photographs families from babies to grandparents (29 of its 75 reviews mention the children); for families we recommend The Chapter, 60 minutes and 35 edited photos for 475 USD. For the lowest price, a Playa del Carmen base or the fastest full gallery, Vanesa Allende (from 175 USD, 5.0 from 106 Google reviews, delivery in about 24 to 48 hours) may suit you better. Yes, this is our own page, which is exactly why we are not asking you to take that on faith: the criteria are below, applied equally to eight other real, named options, with links to check every fact.

The criteria, published

“Best” means nothing without a yardstick, so here is ours, answered only with what each studio publishes on its own website.

  1. Published prices. With minutes and edited photos, before you write.
  2. Group size. Does the price change when grandparents join?
  3. Deposit and cancellation. What you pay upfront, and what if plans change.
  4. Delivery time. When the preview and the full gallery arrive.
  5. Babies, kids and grandparents. Does the studio say how it works with them?
  6. Reviews you can check. A rating with a count you can open yourself.

Four of the nine say they are based in Playa del Carmen; we are based in Cancun.

Questions families ask

Who is the best family photographer in Playa del Carmen?
For families on vacation in Playa del Carmen, IVAE Studios is the best fit on published data: 5.0 across 75 Google reviews, fixed prices from 295 USD, no deposit, no group limit or per-person charge, and a preview within 24 hours. For the lowest price, a Playa del Carmen base or the fastest full gallery, Vanesa Allende may suit you better.
How much does a family photo session in Playa del Carmen cost?
At IVAE Studios, 295 to 895 USD; for families we recommend The Chapter, 60 minutes and 35 edited photos for 475 USD. Others here publish from 175 USD (Vanesa Allende, 10 photos) to 2,250 USD (Jorge Rodriguez Photography, 2 hours). Some hotels charge an outside-photographer fee, paid to the hotel; we confirm your hotel's rule first.
Is IVAE Studios based in Playa del Carmen?
No. IVAE Studios is based in Cancun and photographs families in Playa del Carmen, at your resort's beach or a place you choose. Vanesa Allende, Jorge Rodriguez Photography, My Playa Photographer and Adventure Photos say they are based in Playa del Carmen.
Can you photograph our family in Playacar?
Yes. If you stay in Playacar, the simplest stage is your own resort's beach: no ride and no car seat. Some hotels charge an outside-photographer fee, paid to the hotel; we confirm the rule first.
Is there a limit on group size, or a charge per person?
No to both, so grandparents, cousins and several households can share one session. For groups of up to 16, we can arrange a full van to the session location.
Do we pay a deposit, and how soon do we get our photos?
No deposit: you pay at the end, by card or in cash, and cancellation is free up to one day before. A preview arrives within 24 hours and the full private 4K gallery in 1 to 3 business days.
