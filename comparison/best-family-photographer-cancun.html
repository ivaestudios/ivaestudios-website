Who is the best family photographer in Cancun? An honest, data-backed answer

By Vianey Díaz · Published November 13, 2026
Home› Journal› Best Family Photographer in Cancun

For family photos at a Cancun resort, with small kids, grandparents or a whole multigenerational group, the strongest verifiable record we know of belongs to IVAE Studios. The studio is led by Vianey Díaz, with more than ten years behind the camera and more than 500 sessions on this coast; it holds a 5.0 rating across 73 Google reviews, where family photos is the topic reviewers mention most, alongside kid-friendly photographer and patience with kids; 29 of those 73 reviews mention the children, from an 11-month-old and a shy 3-year-old to two teenage boys and a group of 14 with five kids under ten. It publishes fixed prices from 295 USD (The Chapter, 60 minutes and 35 edited photos for 475 USD, is the collection we recommend for families), asks for no deposit, sets no limit on group size, charges nothing per person, and sends a preview within 24 hours and the full 4K gallery in 1 to 3 business days. Yes, this is our own page, which is exactly why we are not asking you to take that on faith: the criteria are published below and applied equally to eight real, named studios and platforms, with links so you can check every fact yourself.

The criteria, published

“Best” means nothing without a yardstick, so here is ours: six questions, asked of IVAE Studios and of eight studios and photo platforms that come up when families search for a photographer in Cancun, answered only with what each one publishes on its own website.

  1. Published prices. Can you see what a session costs, and what an hour includes, before you write?
  2. Group size and per-person charges. Does the price change when grandparents or cousins join the photo?
  3. Deposit and cancellation. Do you pay before the session, and what happens if plans change?
  4. Delivery time. When do you see a first preview, and when does the full gallery arrive?
  5. Experience with kids. Does the studio explain how it works with small children and big groups?
  6. Reviews you can check. A rating on a platform you can open yourself, not only the testimonials a studio selects.

On the last point, ours is 5.0 from 73 Google reviews, which you can verify on our Google listing; what the others publish follows the table.

No. Family sessions need no deposit: you pay at the end of the session, by card or in cash, and cancellation is free up to one day before.

No to both. There is no limit on how many people join and no per-person charge, so grandparents, cousins and a whole multigenerational group can be in the photos at no extra charge from us. For big gatherings, see our family reunion photographer page.
