The criteria, published

"Best" means nothing without a yardstick, so here is ours. We compared IVAE Studios with seven studios that photograph couples and proposals in and around Cancun, using only what each one publishes on its own website or what the third-party pages we link show.

  1. Published prices. Can you see what a session costs before you write to anyone?

  6. Reviews you can check. A rating with a review count, on a platform you can open yourself.

Our own rating is 5.0 from 73 Google reviews, and you can verify it on our Google listing.

  • Video included. Roots bundles a Reel in its 375 and 595 USD packages, and OLA's Luxury Proposal includes video; our Reels are priced separately, from 295 USD.
  • Years and awards. Tam Rico publishes 19 years, 800+ weddings and Fearless Photographers recognition; Pixan cites the One Eyeland award. IVAE Studios was founded in 2023, though its team brings more than 20 years of photography experience and thousands of couples and families photographed, and Vianey Díaz more than ten years of her own.

  • Speed. Coral Dream's chosen photos (under 48 hours) and Tam Rico's proposal galleries (48 hours) arrive as fast as ours, or faster.

If one of these matters most to you, that studio may be your better fit.
