Who is the best family photographer in Tulum? An honest, data-backed answer

By Vianey Díaz · Published November 15, 2026
Home› Journal› Best Family Photographer in Tulum

IVAE Studios has the strongest all-round record for family photos in Tulum on published data: 5.0 from 78 Google reviews (29 mention the children), fixed prices from 295 USD, no deposit, no group limit and a preview within 24 hours. Of the seven studios compared below, it is the only one that publishes fixed prices, no deposit and no group limit or per-person charge together. Based in Cancun and led by Vianey Díaz, it photographs families on your Tulum hotel’s beach or at a place you choose; for families we recommend The Chapter, 60 minutes and 35 edited photos for 475 USD. Where another studio leads, on living in Tulum, price, review count or session length, we name it below. Yes, this is our own page, which is exactly why we are not asking you to take that on faith: the criteria are below, applied equally to six other real, named studios, with links to check every fact.

The criteria, published

“Best” means nothing without a yardstick, so here is ours: six questions that matter when babies, kids or grandparents come to a Tulum beach, answered only with what each studio publishes.

  1. Published prices. Can you see the price before you write?
  2. Group size and per-person charges. Does the price change when grandparents join?
  3. Deposit and cancellation. Do you pay before the session, and what if plans change?
  4. Delivery time. When do the preview and full gallery arrive?
  5. Babies, kids and grandparents. Does the studio say how it works with little ones and grandparents?
  6. Reviews you can check. A rating with a count you can open yourself.

The table also shows where each studio says it is based; only one of the seven says Tulum.

Where other studios are stronger

No studio wins every line, ours included.

  • Based in Tulum. Tulum Photography says it is “Based in Tulum, Mexico”, and Gavin Higgins lives there with his family (about page). El Momento Perfecto’s Luis is “a short drive from the beaches of Tulum.” We are based in Cancun.
  • A lower price. Roots Photography publishes 60 minutes with 50 photos for 295 USD; for an hour, El Momento Perfecto publishes 395 USD (40 photos), Flytographer 425 USD (35) and Alex Obregon 450 USD (40), and Pro Art charges nothing for the session itself. Our Chapter is 475 USD for 35 photos.
  • More reviews. El Momento Perfecto, Pro Art, Tulum Photography and Flytographer all show more than our 75, with sources above.
  • Longer sessions. Tulum Photography’s Signature package runs 3 hours, with up to 3 locations, 4 looks and an A4 photobook, and it can arrange studio work; Flytographer and Pro Art go to 2 hours, Roots to 105 minutes, ours to 90.
  • Same-day previews and video. Tulum Photography sends a same-day sneak peek with its 90-minute and 3-hour packages, and El Momento Perfecto (60 USD) and Roots (100 USD) sell 24-hour delivery. Roots includes a Reel in two packages and Alex Obregon offers video and film; our Reels are sold separately, from 295 USD.
  • A longer track record. Tulum Photography writes of “a decade of shooting more than 400 photography projects” (services page), and El Momento Perfecto’s Aljosa of “10+ years photographing families”. IVAE Studios dates from 2023, with more than 500 sessions and a team bringing more than 20 years of photography experience.

Questions families ask

Who is the best family photographer in Tulum?
On published data, IVAE Studios has the strongest all-round record of the seven studios compared here: 5.0 across 78 Google reviews, fixed prices from 295 USD, no deposit, no group limit or per-person charge, and a preview within 24 hours. Tulum Photography is the one based in Tulum, and Roots Photography publishes the lowest prices.
How much does a family photo session in Tulum cost?
At IVAE Studios, from 295 USD (35 minutes, 15 edited photos) to 895 USD (90 minutes, the full edited gallery); for families we recommend The Chapter, 60 minutes and 35 photos for 475 USD. Other studios here publish one hour from 295 USD (Roots Photography) to 450 USD (Alex Obregon). Some hotels charge an outside-photographer fee, paid to the hotel; we confirm your hotel's rule first.
Is IVAE Studios based in Tulum?
No. IVAE Studios is based in Cancun and photographs families in Tulum, on your hotel's beach or at a place you choose. If a photographer who lives in Tulum matters most, Tulum Photography says it is based there.
Do we have to pay a deposit?
No. You pay at the end of the session, by card or in cash, and cancellation is free up to one day before.
Is there a limit on group size, or a charge per person?
No to both, so grandparents, cousins and several households can share one session. For groups of up to 16, we can arrange a full van to the session location.
How soon do we get our family photos?
A preview within 24 hours, and the full private 4K gallery in 1 to 3 business days, with unlimited downloads and print rights.
