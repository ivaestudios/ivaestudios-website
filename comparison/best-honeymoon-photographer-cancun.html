Who is the best honeymoon photographer in Cancun? An honest, data-backed answer

By Vianey Díaz · Published November 13, 2026
Home› Journal› Best Honeymoon Photographer in Cancun

For honeymoon photography in Cancun and the Riviera Maya, the strongest verifiable record we know of belongs to IVAE Studios: a 5.0 rating across 75 Google reviews, more than 200 couples photographed a year, and early-morning or late-afternoon sessions on the beach or at your resort, from 295 USD with every price published. There is no deposit, you pay at the end of the session, the first preview arrives within 24 hours, often while you are still on your honeymoon, and the full gallery follows in 1 to 3 business days. Yes, this is our own page, which is exactly why we are not asking you to take that on faith: the criteria are published below and applied equally to seven other real, named studios, with links so you can check every fact yourself.

The criteria, published

“Best” means nothing without a yardstick, so here is ours. A honeymoon session is about an hour of a trip you take once, so we looked at what protects that hour and what you take home. We compared eight studios that photograph couples in Cancun and the Riviera Maya, using only what each one publishes on its own site or what a review platform or directory shows, with every source linked. Published data checked November 12, 2026.

  1. Prices you can see before you write. Real prices for a couple's session, published on the studio's own site.
  2. What the price includes. Minutes and edited photos, so you compare value, not just the headline number.
  3. Photos while you are still traveling. How fast a first preview arrives. On a honeymoon, seeing your photos before the flight home matters.
  4. Full gallery delivery. A public commitment to when the finished gallery arrives.
  5. Deposit and payment. How much you pay before the session, and what happens if plans change.
  6. Reviews you can check. A rating on a platform you can open yourself, such as Google or WeddingWire, not a testimonial wall.

Where other studios are stronger

A fair comparison has to say where we lose.

  • More reviews. Quetzal Wedding Photo has 213 reviews on WeddingWire, Flytographer shows 358 for a single Cancun photographer, Daniel Olmedo is listed with 105 Google reviews, and OLA Photos Cancun shows 237 on Google Maps. Our 73 are fewer.
  • Lower prices, more photos per hour. Roots publishes 60 minutes with 45 to 50 photos for 295 USD; our 60-minute collection, The Chapter, is 35 photos for 475 USD. Flytographer lists the same 60 minutes and 35 photos for 425 USD, and OLA publishes a couples session with 60 photos and drone shots for 3,000 MXN.
  • Video in the package. Roots' Cinematic Love Story includes a Reel for 375 USD in total; with us, a Reel is extra, from 295 USD for 30 seconds.
  • Awards and reach. Pixan cites a One Eyeland award, number 1 in the world and in Mexico in 2024, and Flytographer works in more than 350 cities.

If any of that matters more to you than paying nothing upfront, one of those studios may suit you better, and we would rather you know it now.
