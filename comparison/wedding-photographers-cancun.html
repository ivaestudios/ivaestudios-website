The best wedding photographers in Cancun, compared by reviews, awards and prices

By Vianey Díaz · Published November 12, 2026
Home› Journal› Best Wedding Photographers in Cancun

There is no single best wedding photographer in Cancun. The answer changes with what you weigh, so this page gives you the numbers and leaves the verdict to you. Counted by reviews on WeddingWire, Photostudioab (4.9 from 220 reviews) and Quetzal Wedding Photo (5.0 from 213) lead. Counted by international awards, the founder of Pixan Photography ranked first in One Eyeland's 2024 world list of wedding photographers. Counted by how soon the finished gallery arrives, the shortest time we found in writing is our own: 1 to 3 business days. This is our page, so we rank nobody, ourselves included. Every figure comes from a public source read on November 12, 2026, and the sources are linked so you can check them.

How this comparison was made

We compared seventeen studios that photograph weddings in Cancun and the Riviera Maya: sixteen that independent sources single out, plus our own. The sixteen come from three places: the studios that carry the award badge on WeddingWire's Cancun list, the studios on BrideBox's list of twenty Cancun wedding photographers (rebuilt in October 2026) that print their prices, and a local studio whose founder One Eyeland ranked first in the world among wedding photographers in 2024.

For each studio we wrote down only what a couple can check without sending a message:

  1. Independent reviews. The rating and number of reviews on WeddingWire, and how many times the studio has won WeddingWire's Couples' Choice Award.
  2. Awards. International awards, with the awarding body named so the result can be confirmed there.
  3. Published prices. Wedding prices printed on the studio's own website.
  4. Delivery time and volume. When the finished gallery arrives and how many weddings a year the studio takes, where either is in writing.

We did not test anyone's work, we did not ask for private quotes, and nobody paid or asked to be included. Review counts and prices move, so treat the date as part of every number. If you run one of these studios and a figure is out of date, write to info@ivaestudios.com and we will fix the row.

Delivery time and weddings per year

Few studios put either number in writing. These are the ones we found:

  • IVAE Studios. The complete edited gallery in 1 to 3 business days. At most 12 weddings a year.
  • Victor Herrera. A preview in 5 days and the full gallery in 15 days, guaranteed in the contract. Limited to 20 weddings a year.
  • Luckie Photography. Estimated delivery in 4 to 8 weeks.
  • Monica Lopez Photography. A preview of 30 or more images within 7 days and the full gallery within 10 weeks.

We did not read every page of every site, so a studio missing from this list may still publish its timing. Whoever you choose, ask for the delivery date in the contract. Our reasons for delivering fast are in wedding photo delivery: 6 weeks or 3 days.

How to read these numbers

  • A review count grows with years in business. By their own sites, Photostudioab has worked in the Riviera Maya since 2007, MTM Photography since 2009 and Monica Lopez Photography since 2012. IVAE Studios opened in 2023. Compare ratings and recent reviews, not only totals.
  • WeddingWire and Google measure different things. WeddingWire reviews come from wedding couples. Google reviews cover every kind of session a studio photographs.
  • A starting price buys the shortest coverage. Compare hours, whether a second photographer is included and what the gallery contains.
  • Ask who will hold the camera. A studio with several teams and a photographer who works alone can both be the right choice. You should know which one you are hiring.
