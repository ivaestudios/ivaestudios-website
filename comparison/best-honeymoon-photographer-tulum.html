By Vianey Díaz · Published November 15, 2026

IVAE Studios is the best fit for a Tulum honeymoon session if you want no deposit and your photos before the flight home: 5.0 from 75 Google reviews, prices published from 295 USD, a preview within 24 hours and the full gallery in 1 to 3 business days. Led by Vianey Díaz, with a team that brings more than 20 years of photography experience, the studio photographs more than 200 couples a year; it is based in Cancun and travels to Tulum for the session. If you would rather have a photographer who lives in Tulum, the lowest price or the most reviews, other studios lead on those points, and this page names them. Yes, this is our own page, which is exactly why we are not asking you to take that on faith: the criteria are published below and applied equally to seven other real, named photographers and studios, with links so you can check every fact yourself.

The criteria, published

“Best” means nothing without a yardstick, so here is ours: six questions that matter on a honeymoon, answered only with what each studio publishes on its own website or, for the one studio with no prices there, on the booking platform that lists them.

  1. A couples or honeymoon offer. Does the studio describe the session on its own site?
  2. Published prices. The cost, the minutes and the edited photos, before you write.
  3. Photos before the flight home. When the preview and the full gallery arrive.
  4. Deposit and cancellation. What you pay upfront, and what happens if plans change.
  5. A base in Tulum. Whether the photographer lives in Tulum or travels in.
  6. Reviews you can check. A rating on a platform you can open yourself.

On the last point, ours is 5.0 from 75 Google reviews, which you can verify on our Google listing.

Where other studios are stronger

No studio wins every line, ours included, and in Tulum the others win several.

  • A base in Tulum. Tulum Photography, Alejandro Severini Photography and Tulum Photo are based in Tulum, and Tulum Photo says on its home page that it has photographed couples there for six years. We are based in Cancun and travel to Tulum for the session.
  • A lower price. Your Tulum Concierge, which lists Gavin Higgins with two other photographers, starts at 165 USD for 30 minutes and 10 photos, with pickup for up to 3 people, and Tulum Photo’s couples photoshoot is 180 USD. Our Postcard is 295 USD for 35 minutes and 15 photos.
  • More photos for the hour. Alejandro Severini publishes 75 to 100 images in one hour for 350 USD, and Elvis Aceff all your edited photos, about 80 to 120 an hour, for 305 USD. Our hour, The Chapter, is 35 edited photos for 475 USD.
  • More reviews. Elvis Aceff shows a Google widget reading “EXCELLENT” from 248 reviews, Tulum Photo “180+ five-star Airbnb reviews”, Tulum Photography 139 Google reviews (5.0 from 157 for Gavin Higgins on Your Tulum Concierge) and Alejandro Severini 5.0 from 96. We have 5.0 from 75, on our Google listing; Gaston Bailo, Mai Medina and Violeta Bron publish no review count on the pages we checked.

  • Photos on the day. Tulum Photography’s 90-minute and 3-hour packages include a sneak peek on the day of the shoot and the digitals in 48 hours; our preview arrives within 24 hours and the full gallery in 1 to 3 business days.

If any of that matters more to you than paying nothing upfront, one of those studios may suit you better, and we would rather you know it now.

Questions couples ask

Who is the best honeymoon photographer in Tulum?
Among the eight compared here, IVAE Studios is the best fit if you want no deposit and your photos before the flight home: 5.0 across 75 Google reviews, prices from 295 USD, a preview within 24 hours and the full gallery in 1 to 3 business days. For a photographer based in Tulum, look at Tulum Photography, Alejandro Severini Photography and Tulum Photo; they and Elvis Aceff Photographer also have more reviews and lower prices for an hour, published on their own sites or, for Tulum Photography, on Your Tulum Concierge.
How much does a honeymoon photo session in Tulum cost?
With IVAE Studios, from 295 USD for The Postcard (35 minutes, 15 edited photos, made for couples) to 895 USD for The Heirloom (90 minutes, the complete edited gallery), and you can move up a collection by paying only the difference. Some hotels charge an outside-photographer fee, which you pay to the hotel; we confirm your hotel's rule before the session.
Do we need to pay a deposit for a honeymoon session?
Not with IVAE Studios: you pay at the end of the session, by card or in cash, and can cancel free of charge up to one day before. Tulum Photography, Elvis Aceff Photographer and Tulum Photo ask for a deposit, a retainer or full payment first.
How fast will we get our honeymoon photos?
With IVAE Studios, a preview arrives within 24 hours and the full gallery in 1 to 3 business days, in a private 4K gallery with unlimited downloads and print rights. Book the session early in your stay and you can share the preview before you leave Tulum.
Is IVAE Studios based in Tulum?
No. IVAE Studios is based in Cancun and travels to Tulum for the session. Tulum Photography, Alejandro Severini Photography and Tulum Photo are based in Tulum.
Where and when does a Tulum honeymoon session happen?
Usually on your hotel or resort beach, or at a place you choose, starting right after sunrise or about an hour before sunset. Online booking at ivaestudios.com/book offers 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am and 5:00 and 5:30 pm; for other times, message us on WhatsApp at +52 990 204 6514.

How to book a honeymoon session

Message the studio on WhatsApp at +52 990 204 6514 with your Tulum hotel and dates, or book online; you pay at the end, by card or in cash.
