Where other studios are stronger

No studio leads on everything, ours included. Where the published data favors someone else:

  • More reviews. Quetzal Wedding Photo holds 5.0 from 213 WeddingWire reviews, at least three about proposals, and Daniel Olmedo has 105 Google reviews according to mexicoweddingvendors.com. We have 73.
  • A faster full gallery. Tam Rico Photo delivers the full proposal gallery in 48 hours, and Coral Dream has photos ready in under 48 hours once you choose them. Ours takes 1 to 3 business days.
  • A lower price. Coral Dream starts at 2,950 MXN, about 180 USD, and OLA Photos Cancun includes 60 photos and drone shots for 3,500 MXN. Our collections start at 295 USD.
  • More in one package. Roots Photography puts a cinematic Reel and 60 to 70 photos into its 595 USD, 90-minute proposal package. Our Reels are priced separately, from 295 USD for 30 seconds.
  • A rain plan on the proposal page. Roots and Tam Rico reschedule for weather at no cost, and Drai Cabello's listing offers a full refund if weather cancels. Roots and Quetzal suggest proposing early in your trip, good advice whoever you hire.
  • Longer records and awards. Tam Rico cites 19 years, 800+ weddings and Fearless Photographers recognition, Coral Dream more than 10 years, and Pixan Photography a 2024 One Eyeland award as number 1 in the world and in Mexico. IVAE Studios was founded in 2023, though its team brings more than 20 years of photography experience and thousands of couples and families photographed, and Vianey Díaz more than ten years of her own.

Questions couples ask about proposal photographers

Who is the best surprise proposal photographer in Cancun?
Measured by published data, the strongest verifiable record we know of belongs to IVAE Studios: more than 100 surprise proposals photographed, the highest proposal count published by any studio compared here, 5.0 across 75 Google reviews, no deposit, a preview within 24 hours and the full gallery in 1 to 3 business days. Others lead on single points: Quetzal Wedding Photo on reviews, Tam Rico Photo on gallery speed and Coral Dream Photography on price.
How much does a proposal photographer cost in Cancun?
At IVAE Studios we suggest The Keepsake, 45 minutes and 25 edited photos for 375 USD, or The Chapter, 60 minutes and 35 photos for 475 USD; all six collections run from 295 to 895 USD, with no per-person charge. Among the other studios that publish a price, entry points run from 2,950 MXN, about 180 USD, at Coral Dream to 795 USD at Tam Rico. Some hotels charge a fee for an outside photographer, which you pay to the hotel; we confirm your hotel's rule beforehand.
Do we need to pay a deposit to book a proposal photographer?
Not with IVAE Studios: you pay at the end of the session, by card or in cash, and can cancel free of charge up to one day before. Among the other studios here, Roots Photography asks 175 USD to reserve and Tam Rico Photo 300 USD.
How does the photographer stay hidden during a surprise proposal?
We plan with you in private messages, scout the spot and agree on a quiet signal, such as a hand to the pocket or a message a minute before. The photographer works from a distance with a long lens, looking like one more guest with a camera, then steps in after the yes for the reveal, the ring and a few portraits. We are bilingual, so we arrange access and timing with the resort or restaurant in Spanish.
How soon will we get our proposal photos?
A preview arrives within 24 hours, and the full private 4K gallery follows in 1 to 3 business days, with unlimited downloads and print rights.
