  1. Published prices. What a session costs, with minutes and edited photos, before you write.
  2. A session that fits the trip. At your resort, in the cooler hours, at a pace that suits a pregnancy.
  3. Partner and children. Whether they can join, and whether the price changes.
  4. Deposit and cancellation. Plans change in a pregnancy: what you pay upfront, and what you lose if you cancel.

  6. Reviews you can check. A rating on a platform you can open yourself, not only testimonials a studio selects.

Questions expecting parents ask

Who is the best maternity photographer in Cancun?

How much does a maternity photoshoot in Cancun cost?
With IVAE Studios, 295 to 895 USD by collection: The Postcard (35 minutes, 15 edited photos) is 295 USD, The Keepsake (45 minutes, 25 photos) 375 USD and The Chapter (60 minutes, 35 photos) 475 USD. Two looks start with The Story, 545 USD. Some hotels charge an outside-photographer fee, paid to the hotel; we confirm the rule beforehand.
Can my partner and our children join?
Yes. There is no limit on people and no per-person charge, so your partner, older children and grandparents can all join.
Do we pay a deposit, and what if our plans change?
There is no deposit: you pay at the end of the session, by card or in cash, and cancellation is free up to one day before.
How soon do we get our maternity photos?
A preview within 24 hours, often while you are still on the trip, and the full private 4K gallery in 1 to 3 business days, with unlimited downloads and print rights.
What time of day is best for maternity photos in Cancun?
Early morning or late afternoon, away from the midday sun: online booking offers 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am and 5:00 and 5:30 pm, and other times are on WhatsApp at +52 990 204 6514. When to travel in your pregnancy is a question for your doctor or midwife and your airline.
