Timing With Kids

Start times, the sun and nap time

When you book at ivaestudios.com/book, you can pick 6:30, 7:00 or 7:30 am, or 5:00 or 5:30 pm; for any other hour, write to us on WhatsApp.

Early mornings

Small children often do best right after breakfast, and an early start beats the strongest sun. On Isla Mujeres the sun rises at about 7:00 am in mid-November and about 7:25 am in mid-January, so in those months the 7:00 and 7:30 am starts fall close to sunrise. Coming from Cancun? Your first boat sets your earliest start.

Evenings and the last light

Sunset comes at about 6:05 pm in mid-November and 6:25 pm in mid-January, so the 5:00 and 5:30 pm starts run into the last light. In mid-June and mid-July it comes at about 7:30 pm: ask us on WhatsApp for a later start.

The heat

Midday is the hottest, brightest time on the sand, and hard on small children and grandparents. Bring water, hats and a snack, and save the swim for after the photos.

Around the nap

Book the slot that follows your baby's or toddler's nap, not the one that interrupts it. Our tips for family photos with toddlers have more ideas.

Parents Ask

Planning a family session on Isla Mujeres

Can you photograph our family on Isla Mujeres?

Yes. We photograph families staying on the island and families who come over from Cancun for the day, on your hotel's beach or at a place you choose. IVAE Studios, led by Vianey Díaz, holds 5.0 on Google across 78 reviews; 29 of them mention the children.

What does a family session on Isla Mujeres cost?

Collections run from 295 to 895 USD. The Chapter, recommended for families, is 60 minutes with 35 edited photos for 475 USD; bigger groups do well with a 90-minute collection. Nobody is charged per person and there is no deposit. If your hotel charges a fee for outside photographers, it is paid to the hotel, and we check that rule with you beforehand.

Is there a limit on how many people can join?

No. Any group size works and nobody is charged per person. For up to 16 people, full-van transportation to the session location can be arranged.

What is the best time for family photos on Isla Mujeres with kids?

Mornings usually work best with little ones, before the strongest sun. You can book 6:30, 7:00 or 7:30 am, or 5:00 or 5:30 pm, online, and ask on WhatsApp for other times. From mid-November to mid-January the sun rises between about 7:00 and 7:25 am. With a baby or toddler, start from the nap schedule.

How soon do we get our photos?

Within 24 hours you get a preview, and within 1 to 3 business days your private 4K gallery, with print rights and unlimited downloads. Payment is at the end of the session, by card or cash.

We are staying in Cancun. Can we come over just for the photos?

Yes. Ultramar ferries cross to Isla Mujeres from Puerto Juarez, Playa Tortugas and Playa Caracol, and children under one ride free with a boarding pass (Ultramar, checked November 15, 2026). Check the timetable against your start time when you book.
