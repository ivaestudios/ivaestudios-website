What happens next

  1. We check the calendarWithin about two minutes our calendar confirms the time you chose.
  2. Your letter arrivesThe confirmation letter (PDF) with date, time, place and collection lands in your email. If the time is taken, we email you the closest free times.
  3. Your sessionWe meet you at the agreed place. You pay at the end, by card or in cash, and your edited gallery arrives in 1 to 3 business days.

Good to know

  • No deposit for sessions. Payment at the end of the session, by card or in cash.
  • Free cancellation up to one day before your session.
  • No limit on the number of people and no charge per person.
  • Session times: 6:30 to 8:00 AM, or 5:00 to 6:00 PM. 5:30 PM is the light we recommend.
  • Weddings are booked by video call. Message us on WhatsApp and we set it up.
