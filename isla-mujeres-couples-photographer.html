Where We Photograph

Where the session takes place

01

Your hotel's beach

Staying on the island? The simplest session is at your hotel: no transfer, and your room is close for a change of outfit. If your hotel charges a fee for outside photographers, you pay it to the hotel; we check the rule with you ahead of the session.

02

Playa Norte

Wide, shallow and facing roughly west, so the sun sets over the water in front of you rather than behind you, as it does on the Cancun coast. Busy from late morning, nearly empty at both ends of the day.

03

Punta Sur

The southern tip of the island: cliffs, open ocean and a very different frame from the beach. The park opens after first light, so at sunrise we use the viewpoints outside the gate and go inside only from mid-morning.

04

The streets of Centro

Painted walls, golf carts and the small-town feel of the island's downtown, ten minutes on foot from the ferry. A good second look between the beach and the boat home.

Coming over from Cancun? Ultramar lists crossings to the island from Puerto Juarez, Playa Tortugas and Playa Caracol (checked November 15, 2026). The crossing takes about 20 minutes, and we plan the session backwards from the last return boat.

Light & Timing

Sunset over the water, or an empty beach at sunrise

When you book at ivaestudios.com/book, you can pick 6:30, 7:00 or 7:30 am, or 5:00 or 5:30 pm; for any other hour, write to us on WhatsApp. We avoid eleven to four, when the light is hardest.

Sunset on Playa Norte

The reason most couples cross. Sunset comes at about 6:05 pm in mid-November and 6:25 pm in mid-January, so the 5:00 and 5:30 pm starts run into the last light. In mid-June and mid-July it comes at about 7:30 pm: ask us on WhatsApp for a later start.

Sunrise for privacy

The north end of the island is nearly empty until about 8 am. The sun rises at about 7:00 am in mid-November and 7:25 am in mid-January, so the 7:00 and 7:30 am starts fall close to it. Staying on the island makes a sunrise session easy; from Cancun, the first boat sets your earliest start.

The ferry

Confirm the return timetable the day before. With strong north winds, mostly between November and February, crossings are occasionally suspended; we agree a mainland backup in advance and rescheduling for weather is free.

What to wear

Light fabrics that move in the breeze and warm neutral tones suit the turquoise water. Two looks fit in The Story and The Editorial. Our guide to what to wear for proposal photos has more ideas for couples.
