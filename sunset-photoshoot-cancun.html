Where The Sun Sets

Three shores, three kinds of sunset

The sun sets between west-southwest in December and west-northwest in June. What you see depends on which way your shore faces.

01

The Hotel Zone beaches: the sun behind you

From Punta Cancun to Punta Nizuc the beaches face east and southeast. At sunset the sun is behind the hotels, the sea turns soft and the sky takes the color. Buildings can shade the sand before sunset, so we pick the stretch of beach for your date.

02

Nichupté Lagoon: the sun over the water

Across Kukulcan Boulevard, a few hundred meters to just over a kilometer from the beach, the lagoon faces west, with mangroves and calm water that mirrors the sky; from its eastern shore the sun usually sets across one to several kilometers of water. We meet at your lagoon-side hotel or a public spot nearby. Bring repellent. More in sunset photos at Nichupté Lagoon.

03

Isla Mujeres: the sun over the bay

Playa Norte, at the north end of the island, faces west and northwest across the Bahía de Mujeres, with roughly 6 to 7 km of open water toward the sunset all year. It takes a ferry, so we plan the hour around your boat back. See our Isla Mujeres photographer page.

October to January: book online

February to September: a later start

Questions Before You Book

Planning a sunset session in Cancun

Does the sun set over the ocean in Cancun?

Not from the Hotel Zone's Caribbean beaches: they face east, so the sun sets behind the hotels. It sets over water at Nichupté Lagoon, across the boulevard, and at Playa Norte on Isla Mujeres, which faces west and northwest across the bay.

What time is sunset in Cancun?

In 2027, between 6:04 pm, in late November, and 7:33 pm, in early July. On the 15th it is 6:08 pm in December and 7:30 pm in June. Cancun stays on UTC-5 all year, with no daylight saving time.

What time should a sunset photoshoot in Cancun start?

About an hour before sunset, so the session ends in golden hour. Online starts are 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am and 5:00 and 5:30 pm. From October to January the evening starts fit; from February to September, message us on WhatsApp for a later start.

How much does a sunset photoshoot in Cancun cost?

From 295 USD (The Postcard: 35 minutes, 15 edited photos, made for couples) to 895 USD (The Heirloom: 90 minutes, the full edited gallery). For families we recommend The Chapter: 60 minutes, 35 edited photos, 475 USD. No deposit and no per-person charge; some hotels charge an outside-photographer fee, paid to the hotel.

Is sunset a good time for family photos with young kids?

It can be. On the 15th of November to January golden hour starts between 5:34 and 5:54 pm, comfortably before bedtime; in June and July it starts around 7:00 pm, so with little ones a morning session is often easier. There is no limit on the number of people.
