What editorial means in an IVAE session

An editorial session is planned like a magazine story: what you wear, where you stand and how we direct you are decided before the day, so the photos read as one set rather than separate poses. The Method

Four things that make a session editorial

01

Directed posing

We tell you where to stand, where to look and what to do with your hands, in English or Spanish. Most of what we ask is an action rather than a pose to hold: a slow walk, a look back over the shoulder, a lean against a wall.

02

Wardrobe guidance before the day

Before your session we send guidance on what to wear, the scenes and how the session will run. In short: solid colors with texture, one palette for everyone without matching, one statement piece each, and no logos or neon.

03

A place chosen for light and lines

A white wall, a palapa on the water, a garden path or open sea and sky: we choose the setting for its light and the clean lines that frame you, often where you are staying.

04

One consistent tone

We edit your gallery as a set, in one consistent tone from the first frame to the last, with skin, sand and water kept true to the day, so any two photos sit well side by side.

How A Session Runs

From your dates to your gallery

Online booking at ivaestudios.com/book offers five start times: 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am, and 5:00 and 5:30 pm. Other times can be arranged on WhatsApp.

Before the day

Book online, or send us your dates and your hotel. We confirm your hotel's rule for outside photographers (some charge a fee, paid to the hotel), then send our guidance on what to wear and how the session will run.

The light

Early starts bring soft, low light and a quieter beach, kind to pale palettes and little ones. The 5:00 and 5:30 pm starts bring warmer light as the sun drops; how close they run to sunset depends on the month.

On the day

We begin with the frame you most want to print, while everyone is fresh, then move scene by scene and keep shooting between poses, where the real laughs happen.

Questions

Planning an editorial session

What is an editorial photo session in Cancun?

A session planned like a magazine story. At IVAE Studios that means directed posing, guidance on what to wear before the day, a setting chosen for its light and clean lines, and one consistent tone across the edited gallery.

Do you photograph families and maternity sessions in an editorial style?

Yes. The same planning works for couples, families and maternity sessions. With families we plan the scenes, from everyone together to each child up close, with no limit on the number of people and no per-person charge. Maternity sessions are guided pose by pose, with shade and breaks in the heat.

Which collection should we book for an editorial session?

Any of the six works; you do not need the collection called The Editorial. One look in one place fits from The Postcard, 35 minutes and 15 edited photos for 295 USD. Two looks come with The Story (75 minutes, 50 photos, 545 USD), The Editorial (90 minutes, 70 photos, 645 USD, our best value) and The Heirloom (90 minutes, the complete edited gallery, 895 USD).

What time do editorial sessions start, and where do they take place?

Online booking offers 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am, and 5:00 and 5:30 pm; other times can be arranged on WhatsApp. Sessions take place at your resort or villa, on the beach or at a place you choose. Some hotels charge a fee for outside photographers, which you pay to the hotel; we confirm your hotel's rule before the session.

When do we receive the photos, and is there a deposit?

A preview arrives within 24 hours and the full private 4K gallery in 1 to 3 business days, with unlimited downloads and print rights. There is no deposit: you pay at the end of the session, by card or in cash, and cancellation is free up to one day before.
