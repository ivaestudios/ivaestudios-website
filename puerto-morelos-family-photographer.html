Where We Photograph

Where the session takes place

01

The town beach, north of the pier

Fishing boats, soft sand and the calmest water on this stretch of coast. The reef sits about half a kilometre offshore and takes the swell, so small children can stand in the shallows while we work.

02

The leaning lighthouse

Tilted by Hurricane Beulah in 1967 and left that way, it is the landmark of the town square and the frame that says Puerto Morelos in one picture, a short walk from the pier.

03

Bahía Petempich

North of town, in front of the Riviera Cancun resorts. Quintana Roo's tourism board, on its Mexican Caribbean site, describes it as calm and shallow behind the reef and good for families with small children (checked November 2026).

04

Your hotel's beach

Staying at a resort? The simplest session is at your hotel: no transfer, and your room is close for a nap or a change. If your hotel charges a fee for outside photographers, you pay it to the hotel; we check the rule with you ahead of the session.

Timing With Kids

Start times, the sun and nap time

When you book at ivaestudios.com/book, you can pick 6:30, 7:00 or 7:30 am, or 5:00 or 5:30 pm; for any other hour, write to us on WhatsApp.

Sunrise, the best light here

The beach faces east, so the sun comes up out of the sea in front of you. It rises at about 7:00 am in mid-November, 7:15 am in mid-December and 7:25 am in mid-January, so the 7:00 and 7:30 am starts fall close to sunrise. Small children often do best right after breakfast, and an early start beats the strongest sun.

Evenings

Sunset comes at about 6:05 pm in mid-November, 6:10 pm in mid-December and 6:25 pm in mid-January, behind you, over the mangroves: there is no sunset over the water here. The 5:00 and 5:30 pm starts still give soft light on the sand.

The heat

Midday is the hottest, brightest time on the sand, and hard on small children and grandparents. Bring water, hats and a snack, and save the swim for after the photos.

Around the nap

Book the slot that follows your baby's or toddler's nap, not the one that interrupts it. Our tips for family photos with toddlers have more ideas.

Parents Ask

Planning a family session in Puerto Morelos

Can you photograph our family in Puerto Morelos?

Yes. We photograph families staying in Puerto Morelos and in the Riviera Cancun resorts just north of it, and families who drive down from Cancun for the morning, on the town beach, at your hotel or at a place you choose. IVAE Studios, led by Vianey Díaz, holds 5.0 on Google across 78 reviews; 29 of them mention the children.

Are you based in Puerto Morelos?

No. IVAE Studios is based in Cancun, 35 km (22 mi) north of the town and about 20 minutes from Cancun airport by the same highway. We come to Puerto Morelos for the session; tell us where you are staying when you book.

What does a family session in Puerto Morelos cost?

Collections run from 295 to 895 USD. The Chapter, recommended for families, is 60 minutes with 35 edited photos for 475 USD. Nobody is charged per person and there is no deposit: you pay after the session, by card or cash. If your hotel charges a fee for outside photographers, it is paid to the hotel, and we check that rule with you beforehand.

Is the water calm enough for toddlers?

Usually, yes. The reef sits about half a kilometre offshore and takes the swell, so the town beach and Bahía Petempich have shallow, nearly still water, which the Mexican Caribbean tourism board describes as good for small children. That is why we suggest Puerto Morelos to families with babies and toddlers.

Does the reef stop sargassum?

No. The reef calms the water but does not stop seaweed from reaching the sand. We check the beach conditions in the days before and, if the town beach is heavy with sargassum, we move to your hotel's beach or another stretch.

How soon do we get the photos?

Within 24 hours you get a preview, and within 1 to 3 business days your private 4K gallery, with print rights and unlimited downloads. Payment is at the end of the session, by card or cash.
