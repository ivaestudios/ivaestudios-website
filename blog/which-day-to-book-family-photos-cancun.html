Which Day of Your Cancun Trip to Book Family Photos

In short

Book the session on day 2 or 3. By then everyone has slept off the travel day, nobody is sunburned yet, the outfits are still clean and there are spare days if the weather turns. The preview arrives within 24 hours, while you are still in Cancun, and the full gallery in 1 to 3 business days. Skip the arrival evening, and keep the last morning as a backup.

Once the flights are booked, the next question is which day of the trip to give the photos. The arrival evening sounds efficient, the last morning sounds sentimental, and the middle of the week looks wide open. Our answer is day 2 or 3. Here is why, factor by factor, with a simple rule for trips of 4, 5 and 7 nights.

Four Options, Side by Side

Count the day you land as day 1. Here is how the four usual choices compare on what shows in a family portrait, and on what happens if the weather turns.

The one-line rule

Day 2 or 3, before the first big outing, with at least one spare day after it.

The Arrival Evening and the Last Morning

The arrival evening asks the most of everyone. A travel day often starts before dawn: take your landing time, add immigration, the bags, the transfer and check-in, then picture the kids at a 5:00 pm start. The outfits have spent the day folded in a suitcase, and a late flight leaves no slack. Still choosing flights? See flight times to Cancun from US cities.

The last morning comes after everything that can go wrong: packing, checkout, outfits already packed or worn, and a week of sun. MedlinePlus, the health site of the US National Library of Medicine, notes that the full effect of a sunburn may not show for 24 hours or longer, and that sunburned skin can peel several days later, which on a week-long trip can fall right on the last morning. And if the sky turns gray, no day is left.

It still works as a backup: with an afternoon flight, bags packed the night before and a 6:30 am start, you can be back for breakfast. Birthday and anniversary trips follow the same logic, as milestone birthday trip photos explains.

Why Day 2 or 3 Works

By day 2 everyone has slept a full night in the new room, and five things line up at once.

Rested kids. The travel day is behind you, and the children have had a night to settle into a new room and a new routine.

Even skin. Sunburn, fresh tan lines and peeling all show in a close portrait, and early in the trip there has been little time for any of them. Rub sunscreen in well before the session, and see why some sunscreens make faces glow white in photos.

Clean outfits, dry hair. The clothes you packed for the photos are still unworn; steam them once and they are ready. Nobody's hair has spent days in salt water or chlorine yet; before an evening session, keep heads dry that afternoon.

Spare days for weather. Cancellation is free up to one day before the session, and there is no deposit. If the forecast looks wrong the day before, cancel and book a later morning or evening, if one is still open. Day 2 leaves the most room to do that.

Photos while you are still there. The preview arrives within 24 hours, so you see the first images mid-trip. The full private 4K gallery, with unlimited downloads and print rights, follows in 1 to 3 business days; a weekend in between can add a day or two. More in our sneak peek in 24 hours.

Day 2 or day 3? Choose day 3 if you land late on day 1, or if grandparents and cousins arrive a day apart, so everyone gets one full night first. Choose day 2 when the trip is short.

Before the Big Outings, Not After

Snorkeling, cenotes, a park day, a boat to Isla Mujeres: the days that make a trip also show on camera the morning after. Hair stiff from salt or chlorine, the red ring a snorkel mask leaves, a pink nose, a scraped knee, and children who spent everything they had the day before. Book the session before the big outings, and keep the day before it quiet: shade by the pool, an early dinner, an early night.

If the only open date falls mid-trip, say because part of the family flies in later, put a rest day in front of it, and choose outfits with sleeves and hems that cover any new tan lines.

Morning or Evening, by Your Home Clock

Online, our sessions start at 6:30, 7:00 or 7:30 am, or at 5:00 or 5:30 pm; other times we arrange on WhatsApp. On day 2 the kids are still on home time, so the right slot depends on where you fly from. Cancun keeps UTC-5 all year: Mexico's time zone law puts Quintana Roo in its own southeast zone, with seasonal clock changes only along the northern border. A 6:30 am start in Cancun is:

  • 6:30 am in New York and 3:30 am in Los Angeles in winter;
  • 7:30 am in New York and 4:30 am in Los Angeles during US daylight saving time, from the second Sunday in March to the first Sunday in November;
  • 4:30 am in Vancouver all year, since British Columbia stopped changing its clocks in March 2026.

From the East, the early slots fit a body clock that has barely moved. From the Pacific coast, a 5:00 or 5:30 pm start, mid-afternoon on your home clock, is kinder in the first days. For other home cities, our guide to Cancun's time zone and your kids' body clock has the full table.

A Simple Rule for 4, 5 and 7 Nights

Day 1 is the day you land. On any trip, the preview reaches you within 24 hours. On a 7-night trip with a session on day 2, the full gallery usually arrives before your flight home; on shorter trips it may arrive just after.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best day of vacation to take family photos?

Day 2 or 3. By then everyone has slept off the travel day, nobody is sunburned yet, the outfits are still clean and there are spare days if the weather turns. Skip the arrival evening, and keep the last morning as a backup.

Should we do family photos at the beginning or the end of the trip?

Near the beginning, but not on the day you land. The last morning brings packing and checkout, leaves no spare day, and any sunburn or peeling from the week can show by then.

Should we schedule family photos before or after excursions?

Before. Snorkeling, cenotes and park days leave salty hair, sun on the skin and tired kids the next morning. Book the session before the big outing, and keep the day before it quiet.

Will we get our photos before we fly home?

The preview arrives within 24 hours of the session and the full gallery in 1 to 3 business days. With a session on day 2 of a 7-night trip, the gallery usually arrives before your flight; on shorter trips it may follow you home.

What if the weather looks bad on our photo day?

Cancellation is free up to one day before the session, and there is no deposit. If the forecast looks wrong the day before, cancel and book a later morning or evening of your trip, if one is still open.

Morning or evening for a session early in the trip?

It depends on your home clock. Cancun stays on UTC-5 all year, so a 6:30 am start is 6:30 or 7:30 am in New York but 3:30 or 4:30 am in Los Angeles. From the Pacific coast, a 5:00 or 5:30 pm start is easier.
