In short

March is the driest month of the year in Cancun: average highs of 31.5°C (89°F), lows of 21°C (71°F), only 39 mm of rain and a sea of about 26°C (79°F). It is also the busiest month for families: spring break runs through the month and Mexico's Holy Week school holiday starts on March 22, 2027. Sunset moves from 6:51 pm to 7:01 pm, and sargassum can begin to build as spring arrives.

March is two months in one. The weather is the most reliable of the year, and the beaches are the fullest. If you are coming for spring break or for Holy Week, a little planning decides whether your photos show an empty shoreline or a crowd. Here is what to expect, with Mexico's official climate normals, the key dates for 2027 and the light times we use to schedule sessions on this coast.

Temperatures and rain are the official 1991 to 2020 normals of Mexico's national weather service (SMN) for the Cancun station. Sea temperature is the typical March reading for this coast, and the light times are calculated for the Hotel Zone beach.

The Driest Month of the Year

In the official normals, March has the least rain of any month in Cancun: 39 mm, against 104 mm in January and 277 mm in October. Days average 31.5°C (89°F), nights 21°C (71°F), and the humidity of summer has not arrived yet. For an outdoor session, this is as predictable as the weather here gets.

Cold fronts, called nortes, are less frequent but not finished. For the 2026 to 2027 season the SMN forecasts 56 across Mexico, above the average of 50, with six expected in March. When one reaches this coast it brings a day or two of north wind and rough water, so we still keep a backup evening in every booking.

Spring Break and Holy Week Dates for 2027

Spring break, all month. Schools and universities in the United States take spring break at different times between early March and early April, with the heaviest weeks in the middle of the month. Canadian families arrive in the same window; our March break guide is written for them.

March 13 to 15. The third Monday of March is a public holiday in Mexico, so this is a long weekend for Mexican families too.

March 14. Clocks in the United States and Canada move forward one hour. Cancun does not change its clock, so from this date Cancun time matches Chicago, not New York. Check the time of your session against your phone, not your memory of the flight.

March 20. The spring equinox. It is one of the busiest days of the year at Chichén Itzá, where visitors gather to watch the light on the pyramid, so book any day trip well ahead.

March 22 to April 2. Mexico's school holiday for Holy Week and Easter week, according to the official school calendar. Easter Sunday is March 28. It is one of the busiest times of the year on public beaches, with Mexican families from all over the country on this coast.

Seaweed in March

Sargassum drifts in from the Atlantic and usually begins to build on this coast in spring, so March sits on the edge of the season. Some years the beaches stay clean all month; in 2026, a record year, the seaweed had already been arriving since January. Esteban Amaro Mauricio, who directs the Quintana Roo Sargassum Monitoring Network, has warned that it could return as early as January 2027.

Arrivals change by the day and by the beach. The beaches that face Isla Mujeres, such as Playa Mujeres and Costa Mujeres, are usually the last affected, and most resorts clean their beachfront in the morning. We check conditions the day before each session and move to the shore that is clear. Our sargassum season guide explains the full year, and the beach-by-beach forecast shows which shores stay clear.

"Golden light" is when the sun drops below six degrees and the color turns warm; "last light" is the end of civil twilight, when the sky still glows. For a one-hour session we start about an hour before sunset, so close to 6 pm. With children, that is late enough for a nap and an early snack first. Sunrise moves from 7:08 am to 6:41 am over the month. Our comparison of sunrise and sunset sessions explains the trade-offs.

How to Get an Empty Beach in a Full Month

Choose sunrise. Even in spring break weeks the beach is usually quiet early in the morning, including in the Hotel Zone. A session that starts just before sunrise finishes before the first beach towels arrive, and small children are at their best in the morning.

Or stay for the last light. Most people leave the sand before sunset to get ready for dinner. The final 40 minutes of the day are often the emptiest of the afternoon.

Use your resort's quieter end. Almost every resort beach has a side away from the pool and the beach bar. We look at the layout before the session and frame toward open water.

Book early in Holy Week. From March 22 the public beaches are at their fullest and resort events fill the evenings. Book the session when you book your flights, and ask your resort about access for outside vendors; our guide to bringing an outside photographer to a resort covers the usual rules. For the Hotel Zone in particular, see our spring break photo guide.

What to Wear in March

March afternoons are properly warm, so choose light, breathable fabrics: linen, cotton and loose dresses. Avoid heavy layers and anything that shows perspiration. The evening breeze is mild, so a second layer is optional this month.

For families, pick two or three colors and let everyone choose within them instead of matching exactly. Our guides to family outfits and coordinating outfits by age have full palettes, and our family beach photo ideas show what to do once you are on the sand.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is March a good time to visit Cancun?
For weather, it is the most reliable month: March is the driest month of the year, with warm days and mild evenings. It is also the busiest month for families because of spring break and Mexico's Holy Week school holiday, which starts on March 22, 2027.
What is the weather like in Cancun in March?
The official SMN normals for Cancun give an average high of 31.5°C (89°F), an average low of 21°C (71°F) and 39 mm of rain in March, the lowest of any month.
When is spring break in Cancun in 2027?
Schools and universities in the United States take spring break at different times between early March and early April, with the heaviest weeks in mid-March. Mexico's school holiday for Holy Week and Easter week runs from March 22 to April 2, 2027, and Easter Sunday is March 28.
Is there seaweed in Cancun in March?
It depends on the year. Sargassum usually begins to build in spring, so some years March is clean and in others patches arrive. In 2026, a record year, it had been arriving since January. Check the forecast the week you travel; beaches facing Isla Mujeres are usually the last affected.
What time is sunset in Cancun in March?
On the Hotel Zone beach, sunset is at 6:51 pm on March 1, 6:56 pm on March 15 and 7:01 pm on March 31. Quintana Roo does not use daylight saving, so after clocks change in the United States and Canada on March 14, 2027, Cancun time matches Chicago.
How do we avoid crowds in our spring break photos?
Choose a sunrise session, when the beach is nearly empty, or the last 40 minutes before sunset, when most people have left to get ready for dinner. We also frame toward the quieter end of your resort's beach.
