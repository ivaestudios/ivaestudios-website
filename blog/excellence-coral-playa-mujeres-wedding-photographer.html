In short

Excellence Coral Playa Mujeres is the newest adults-only resort from the Excellence Collection on this shore, for guests 18 and over. Wedding listings name two ceremony gazebos for about 100 guests each, the Seaside Gazebo on the beach and the garden-style Oasis Gazebo, plus a ballroom for receptions of about 80. It suits intimate weddings; plan the ceremony about 90 minutes before sunset.

Excellence Coral Playa Mujeres joined the Excellence family on this shore recently, with the brand's adults-only formula and a set of venues sized for intimate weddings rather than huge ones. Two gazebos, one by the sea and one in a garden, give couples a genuine choice of mood. This guide explains what each does to your photos and how to plan the day around the light.

The resort is new, and venue lists and capacities come from wedding planners' listings, so confirm the current details with the wedding team before you plan around them.

Seaside or Oasis Gazebo

The Seaside Gazebo sits on the beach, listed for about 100 guests. It gives you the classic Playa Mujeres ceremony: calm turquoise water and Isla Mujeres on the horizon behind you, with the gazebo's roof for a little shade. We place the couple toward the open side so that faces are not lost in shadow against the bright sea.

The Oasis Gazebo is garden-style, also about 100 guests. Greenery instead of sea gives a softer, more romantic mood, it is sheltered from the wind off the channel, and it is the better choice on a breezy winter afternoon or for couples who want something different from every other beach wedding. Garden light is gentler, which is kind to skin and to older guests.

For the reception, the Playa Mujeres Ballroom is listed for about 80, which tells you the resort's sweet spot: weddings of 30 to 80 guests where every person fits in the frame.

The beach faces east, so the sun sets behind the resort, which gives the Seaside Gazebo soft, even light at the end of the day and a deep blue sea behind you. We plan ceremonies about 90 minutes before sunset. In the garden the light fades a little earlier under the trees, so an Oasis Gazebo ceremony can start ten or fifteen minutes sooner. For a day-after session, mornings on this shore are beautiful: for much of the year the sun comes up from behind Isla Mujeres or just beside it.

Photographing an Intimate Wedding

Smaller weddings are a pleasure to photograph because every guest matters in the story. With 40 people we can photograph every family group, give each table a moment and still have half an hour of portraits in the golden light. For weddings of this size, half-day coverage is often enough, and the extra time goes into a sunrise session the next morning. Our guide to small weddings and elopements in Mexico has more ideas.

Outside Photographers

Excellence-brand resorts charge outside wedding vendors for access, as a vendor fee or a day pass, and amounts are revised by season and package. Ask for the exact figure in writing before you sign, and whether it applies per vendor or per person. Our Playa Mujeres vendor fee guide explains how these charges work, and we send our paperwork to your coordinator ourselves.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can you get married at Excellence Coral Playa Mujeres?
Wedding listings name the Seaside Gazebo on the beach and the garden-style Oasis Gazebo, each for about 100 ceremony guests, and the Playa Mujeres Ballroom for receptions of about 80. The resort is new, so confirm current venues with the wedding team.
Is Excellence Coral Playa Mujeres adults-only?
Yes. Only guests 18 and over may stay, according to the resort's own FAQ, which keeps the beach and venues calm in your photographs.
What time should a wedding start at Excellence Coral Playa Mujeres?
About 90 minutes before sunset at the Seaside Gazebo, and slightly earlier in the garden at the Oasis Gazebo. Sunset ranges from 5:35 pm in mid-December to 7:32 pm in mid-July.
Does Excellence Coral Playa Mujeres charge for outside photographers?
Excellence-brand resorts charge outside wedding vendors for access, as a vendor fee or a day pass. Ask for the amount in writing before you sign, and whether it applies per vendor or per person.
How much does IVAE Studios charge for a wedding at Excellence Coral?
Half-day coverage of up to four hours is 1,800 to 2,800 USD, a full day with a second photographer is 3,500 to 5,500 USD, and coverage with a wedding film is 5,500 to 8,000 USD.
