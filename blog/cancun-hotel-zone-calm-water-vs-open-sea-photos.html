Calm Bay or Open Sea: Choosing Your Hotel Zone Photo Beach

In short

North of Punta Cancún the water is calm, pale and shallow, ideal for small children and walking frames. South of the point it is deep blue and lively, more dramatic and better for couples.

Many people book a Cancún hotel without realizing that the Hotel Zone is shaped like a seven, with two very different coasts. It is the single biggest factor in how your photos will look.

The North Shore

From km 2 to km 8 the beach faces north onto the Bahía de Mujeres. Flat, shallow, pale turquoise. Side light all day long, and in summer a sun that sets near the water. This is where children can stand in the sea.

The Open Caribbean

From Punta Cancún south, the beach faces east. Deep blue, real waves and wide sand. The sun rises from the sea and sets behind the hotels, leaving soft shade on the sand at the end of the day. Couples love this look.

Cancún Hotel Zone, Through Our Lens

Real IVAE Studios work on this coast, so you can see how this light and water look on camera. It shows our work in the area, not necessarily at Cancún Hotel Zone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which side of the Cancún Hotel Zone has calm water?
The north shore, from about km 2 to km 8, facing the Bahía de Mujeres. South of Punta Cancún the sea is open and can have strong waves.
