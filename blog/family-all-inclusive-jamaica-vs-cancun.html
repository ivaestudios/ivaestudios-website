Jamaica or Cancun for a Family All-Inclusive?

In short

The resorts are more alike than you might think: Royalton and Palladium run family resorts in Jamaica and around Cancun. The difference is the trip around them. From Texas, Chicago, Denver and Los Angeles, Cancun is 276 to 589 miles closer on Southwest's figures; from Baltimore and Florida it is about even. Royalton's resorts near Cancun list 15 to 25 minutes from the airport, Royalton Negril about 75 from Montego Bay. Both share the June 1 to November 30 hurricane season and the Caribbean's sargassum, and get the same US and Canadian advisory levels.

Jamaica and Cancun keep landing side by side for family all-inclusives, often from the same hotel brands, with kids clubs and water parks that look alike in the brochures. What changes is everything around the resort: the flight, the van ride, what sits outside the gate, and how photos work once you are there. We are a Cancun photography studio, so we know our side well; for Jamaica we used only what resorts, airlines and governments publish, all read on November 14, 2026.

The Same Brands on Both Sides

Royalton tags two resorts in each place as family-friendly: in Jamaica, Royalton Negril and Royalton Blue Waters in Trelawny; near Cancun, Royalton Riviera Cancun and Royalton Splash Riviera Cancun. Palladium calls Grand Palladium Jamaica, in Hanover, family-friendly, and in Costa Mujeres, Cancun, it runs Grand Palladium Select Costa Mujeres, whose Family Selection adds family areas and a family butler. Moon Palace also has a resort on each side: the Jamaica Tourist Board lists Moon Palace Jamaica in Ocho Rios, and our Moon Palace family session guide covers the Cancun campus.

One big family name appears only on the Jamaica side: Beaches, whose website lists two resorts, Negril and Turks and Caicos, and none in Mexico. A shared brand is a starting point, not a twin, so compare resort pages one by one; for Mexico, start with our best Cancun resorts for family photoshoots.

From Baltimore/Washington and Florida it is about a tie. From Texas, Chicago, Denver and Los Angeles, Cancun is the shorter trip, and from Houston (Hobby) Southwest's pages list three nonstops a day to Cancun, every day, against a Saturday nonstop to Montego Bay.

Then comes the van, which with a toddler matters as much as the plane. The brands' own pages give the drive from the airport:

  • Royalton Riviera Cancun: about 15 to 20 minutes from CUN
  • Royalton Splash Riviera Cancun: about 15 to 25 minutes from CUN
  • Royalton Blue Waters: about 35 to 40 minutes from MBJ
  • Grand Palladium Jamaica: about 40 minutes (32 km) from MBJ
  • Royalton Negril: about 75 minutes from MBJ

Water Parks, Kids Clubs and the Sea

On paper the family features match. Royalton Blue Waters lists a pirate-themed water park, a kids club and teen disco nights; Royalton Splash Riviera Cancun lists 14 slides and two lazy rivers; Royalton Riviera Cancun has a supervised play space for ages 4 to 12 and a teens lounge for 13 to 17. Beaches puts its Negril resort on the widest stretch of Seven Mile Beach, with a waterpark, and Palladium lists a children's water park at Grand Palladium Jamaica.

Look harder at the sea than at the slides. Canada's travel advice for both countries says riptides are common and not every beach has lifeguards or warning flags. Cancun's Hotel Zone has two coasts, one facing the bay toward Isla Mujeres and one facing the open Caribbean, so ask which one a hotel is on; in Jamaica, ask about the water in front of your room.

Hurricanes and Seaweed, on Both Coasts

Neither gets a pass. Both sit in the Atlantic basin, where the National Hurricane Center sets the season at June 1 to November 30, peaking around September 10, with most activity from mid-August to mid-October. Our Cancun hurricane season guide covers planning around it.

Sargassum, the brown seaweed that washes up on Caribbean beaches, is shared too. In its monthly outlook of October 31, 2026, the University of South Florida's Optical Oceanography Lab reported that the amount fell by more than half in most regions in October, though it was still high for an October everywhere except the eastern Atlantic, and should keep decreasing until possibly the end of November or December. The lab says its outlook is not for any single beach, so see our Cancun sargassum season guide, and ask a Jamaica resort how its beach looked the week before you fly.

Outside the Resort Gate

Where you stay shapes the days out. The Jamaica Tourist Board calls Dunn's River Falls & Park, in Ocho Rios, one of Jamaica's national treasures, with Dolphin Cove a stone's throw away; its Negril page leads with Seven Mile Beach and the sunsets. From Cancun, families add Isla Mujeres by ferry, Mayan sites, cenotes or the Xcaret parks.

Getting around reads much the same. The US State Department says its staff in Jamaica may not use public buses or drive between cities at night and tells US citizens to take the same precautions; Canada says to use only taxis ordered from hotels and authorized by JUTA, the Jamaica Union of Travellers Association. For Mexico, the State Department asks US citizens to follow its staff rules: no travel between cities after dark, and taxis from regulated stands or apps such as Uber, not hailed on the street. Either way, book day trips through your resort or a licensed transfer.

As of November 14, 2026, the State Department rates Jamaica and Mexico at Level 2, exercise increased caution, and both advisories flag some areas with higher risk. Canada says to exercise a high degree of caution in both, and its list of Mexican regions to avoid does not include Quintana Roo, where Cancun is.

Family Photos at an All-Inclusive

At an all-inclusive there are two ways to get a portrait of everyone. Many big resorts run their own photo team; Beaches' wedding FAQ, for one, invites couples to meet “the wedding and photography teams.” Whether an outside photographer may work on the grounds, and for what fee, is set resort by resort, and we found no published rule on the Jamaica resort pages we checked. Going to Jamaica? Ask the resort in writing before you hire anyone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Jamaica or Cancun better for a family all-inclusive?

Neither wins on the resorts alone, since Royalton and Palladium run family resorts in both. Decide on the flight from your city, the airport drive and the days out you want. From Texas, Chicago, Denver or Los Angeles, Cancun is the shorter flight; from Baltimore or Florida it is about even.

Do kids need a passport for Jamaica or Cancun?

To fly in, yes. The State Department says US citizens need a passport book to fly to Mexico, and travelers under 18 who are not with both parents may need a notarized consent letter. For Jamaica it asks for a valid passport and proof of departure. Canada asks Canadians for a passport valid for the whole stay in both.

When are hurricane season and sargassum season?

The National Hurricane Center sets the Atlantic season at June 1 to November 30 for both, peaking around September 10. Sargassum varies by year; the University of South Florida's October 31, 2026 outlook expected it to keep decreasing until possibly the end of November or December.

Can we bring an outside photographer to an all-inclusive?

It depends on the resort, so ask in writing before you book anyone. In Cancun, some hotels charge a fee for an outside photographer, which you pay to the hotel, and we confirm your hotel's rule before the session.
