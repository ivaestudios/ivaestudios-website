When you married, it was the two of you. Now the anniversary falls in the middle of a family trip, and the people who filled the years since are coming along. You could leave them at the kids' club for an hour. Or you could make them part of it: frames of the family your marriage made, then a few of just the two of you. This guide is for the second choice, an anniversary photoshoot with kids in Cancun, planned to work for everyone in it, the smallest and most tired included.

Why Bring the Kids Into the Anniversary

An anniversary trip with kids asks a question every couple answers differently: is this hour for the two of you, or for all of you? If it is only for the two of you, see our anniversary couples photographer page and our anniversary trip photoshoot ideas for the 10th, the 25th and the 50th.

With the kids in the frame, the photos show what the years built: the toddler who did not exist on your wedding day, the eight-year-old who wants to be in every picture. At a 10th or 15th anniversary, your kids may be old enough to understand what you are celebrating and still young enough to want to be part of it. And you do not have to choose: one session can hold both stories, with no per-person charge and no limit on people.

Couple and Family Photos in the Same Session

The simplest way to have couple and family photos in the same session is a collection with two looks: one outfit for the family frames, a second for the two of you.

  • The Story: 75 minutes, 50 edited photos, 2 looks, 545 USD. A full family set and an unhurried couple set, and the better fit for little ones.
  • The Editorial: 90 minutes, 70 edited photos, 2 looks, 645 USD, the collection we call our best value. More room for both stories, for breaks and for grandparents on the trip.

The photos divide the way you divide the time: tell us which part matters most, and we plan around it. If one outfit is enough, The Chapter (60 minutes, 35 edited photos, 1 look, 475 USD) is the collection we recommend for families; The Heirloom (90 minutes, 2 looks, 895 USD) brings the complete edited gallery. For the looks, keep the family outfits soft and easy to move in, and make the couple look the dressier one: your anniversary dinner outfit, or a nod to your wedding colors.

The Order That Works With Tired Kids

Children give their best minutes at the start, and adults relax as a session goes on. So the kids come first and the two of you last:

  1. Warm-up. A few easy minutes at the water, so the kids can get to know us before the camera matters.
  2. The whole family, in the first look, while clothes are clean and faces are fresh.
  3. The kids with each of you, then the kids together, short and playful, before the energy dips.
  4. The change. A built-in break: water, a snack and sandcastles while you change, one at a time.
  5. The two of you, in the second look. These frames need nothing from the children, so it does not matter if they are done by now.
  6. One last walk, if anyone has energy left: all of you heading down the beach together.

Two exceptions. With a baby who naps on a schedule, tell us when: the nap is the time for the couple frames. With teenagers, keep their part short and let them choose a frame or two.

Then choose the hour your kids are at their best. Online, sessions start at 6:30, 7:00 or 7:30 am, or at 5:00 or 5:30 pm; for other times, message us on WhatsApp. Mornings catch early risers before a day of pool and sun, and 5:00 or 5:30 pm suits kids who nap after lunch. For tired children, the right hour beats the prettiest light. One parent wrote that we

Tell us who is coming

When you book, tell us the kids' ages, nap and snack times, who else is on the trip and which look matters most to you. We plan the start time and the order of the session around them.

What the Kids Do During Your Couple Photos

Every parent asks this: if the two of you are in the frame, who has the kids? A few answers:

  • A grandparent, an aunt or a nanny on the trip. They keep the kids a few steps behind us with a bucket and shells and, with no per-person charge, can join the family frames first.
  • The kids in the frame, out of focus. The two of you close in front, the kids digging at the waterline behind you: perhaps the truest anniversary photo in the gallery.
  • The kids call the shots. Hand the oldest the job of telling you what to do: “kiss!”, “spin!”, “dance!”. Kids who will not pose for a camera often love bossing their parents around, and your laughter is real.
  • The kids' club, after the family frames. If your resort's club is close by, one of you walks the kids over and we finish with the two of you. Check its ages and hours in our guide to kids clubs at Cancun all-inclusives.

Sometimes the kids stay in the story while you have your moment. One parent wrote:

“One cameraman was especially kind and continued capturing candid moments of my daughter while my partner and I were able to take a few photos together.”

Brennan W., Google review

However you arrange it, the kids are part of the anniversary instead of waiting for it to be over.

Anniversary Photoshoot Ideas With Your Family

These ideas put the years and the kids in the same picture. Choose two or three, and we guide everyone through them.

  1. Count the years. The kids write the number in the wet sand, a 10 or a 15, big enough for the family to stand behind: one of the simplest 10 year anniversary photoshoot ideas.
  2. Then and now. Bring a small print of a wedding photo; one of the kids holds it up while you recreate the pose.
  3. Your new wedding party. The kids stand beside you, where your bridesmaids and groomsmen once stood.
  4. Hands and rings. Your hands with your rings, and the kids' small hands on top.
  5. The family walk. The kids run ahead along the waterline while the two of you follow, hand in hand.
  6. Each child with each of you. One portrait per pairing, so every child has a frame with each parent from this anniversary.

For 25th wedding anniversary photoshoot ideas, the kids may be teenagers or grown, and they can do more than appear: keep a surprise, stand in the then-and-now frame or take a portrait with each of you. Frequently Asked Questions

Can we do anniversary photos with our kids in the same session?

Yes. Book a collection with two looks, one for the family and one for the two of you: The Story (75 minutes, 50 edited photos, 545 USD) or The Editorial (90 minutes, 70 edited photos, 645 USD). There is no per-person charge.

Should the family photos or the couple photos come first?

The family photos, while the kids are fresh and their clothes are clean. The couple frames come last, because they need nothing from the children. With a baby who naps on a schedule, the couple frames can happen during the nap.

What do the kids do during our couple photos?

They can stay a few steps away with a grandparent or a nanny, play at the waterline in the background of your frames, or call out the poses. If your resort has a kids' club nearby, one of you can drop them off after the family frames.

What time is best for an anniversary session with kids?

The hour your kids are at their best. Online start times are 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am and 5:00 and 5:30 pm; other times can be arranged on WhatsApp.

Do we pay a deposit?

No. You pay at the end of the session, by card or in cash, and cancellation is free up to one day before.

How soon will we see the photos?

A preview arrives within 24 hours. The full private gallery follows in 1 to 3 business days, in 4K, with unlimited downloads and print rights.
