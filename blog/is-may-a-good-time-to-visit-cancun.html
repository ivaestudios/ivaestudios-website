Is May a Good Time to Visit Cancun With Kids?

Is May a good time to visit Cancun with kids? Yes, if you plan for three things: the heat, the seaweed and the hour of the day. Late April and May come after an early Easter and before hurricane season, and in 2027 they hold two long weekends, one Canadian and one American. Here is the month in real numbers, and how we would plan your family photos in it.

Mexico's National Meteorological Service (SMN) publishes 1991 to 2020 averages for its Cancun station, number 23155. Here are April, May and June side by side:

April has the fewest days with rain of any month, which is good news for a late April trip. May doubles April's rain but still gets less than half of June's. And May is hot: at this station its average high is a touch above June's, and nights stay warm, around 24 °C (76 °F).

The calendar helps too. Easter comes early in 2027 (Good Friday is March 26 on the Government of Newfoundland and Labrador's 2027 holiday list), so late April and May fall well after the Easter weeks. And the US National Hurricane Center dates the Atlantic hurricane season from June 1 to November 30, so May comes before it starts.

Asking about the Riviera Maya? The long weekends, the hurricane season and the regional seaweed bulletin apply to Playa del Carmen and Tulum too; only the weather table is Cancun's own, and seaweed can differ from one beach to the next. If May does not fit, our month-by-month guide to the best time to visit Cancun weighs the rest of the year.

May Heat With Kids

An average high of 34.2 °C (94 °F) sets the rhythm of a May day with children: beach early, shade and the pool at midday, beach again late. Midday is also the hardest time for photos, with overhead light, squinting eyes and tired kids, so the session belongs in the same windows: the first hour after sunrise or the last stretch before sunset.

Bring hats, water and a snack, and plan a short shade break if grandparents or toddlers are in the group. Our heat plan for family sessions covers the rest, from session length to fabrics.

Seaweed in May: the Honest Picture

Sargassum is a floating seaweed that drifts in from the Atlantic and washes up on Caribbean beaches. The University of South Florida's Optical Oceanography Lab, which tracks it by satellite in its Sargassum Watch System, says that across the Atlantic seaweed belt it typically peaks in June or July, with the least in winter, and that it has reached many Caribbean shores in nearly every spring and summer. May sits on the way up.

2026 showed how heavy it can get: in its May 31, 2026 bulletin, the lab reported record-high amounts for the month of May in every region of the bulletin except the West Atlantic. The latest outlook when we checked, dated October 31, 2026, expects amounts to keep falling until possibly the end of November or December and gives no forecast for May 2027. The lab publishes a bulletin at the end of each month and is clear that it is a regional outlook, not a forecast for a particular beach.

Our advice: read the latest bulletin the month before you fly, and book with room to adjust. Our sessions take no deposit and cancellation is free until one day before, so you can keep an eye on the bulletin and your beach as the date gets closer and still change plans in time. If the seaweed is heavy that morning, the photos can move to where the waterline stays out of frame, such as a pool deck, a garden or a covered walkway; our guide to sargassum backup spots at your resort shows them, and our seaweed season guide goes month by month. Two Long Weekends, a Week Apart

May 2027 has one long weekend for Canada and one for the US. Victoria Day falls on the Monday preceding May 25, as Canadian Heritage explains, which in 2027 is Monday, May 24. Memorial Day is Monday, May 31, 2027, on the US Office of Personnel Management's federal holiday list. That makes Saturday, May 22 to Monday, May 24 for Canadian families and Saturday, May 29 to Monday, May 31 for American families.

Mother's Day comes first: it is the second Sunday of May in the US and in Canada, which is May 9, 2027, and Mexico celebrates its mothers on May 10, the next day. A family session on that trip is an easy gift for the mom of the group, or for a grandmother traveling with you.

On a three-day weekend, book the photos for your first full morning. The preview arrives within 24 hours, so you see your first photos before you fly home.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is May a good time to visit Cancun with kids?

Yes, if you plan for heat and seaweed. At the SMN's Cancun station, May averages a high of 34.2 °C (94 °F) and 7.3 days with rain, and seaweed often starts arriving in spring. Keep the beach, and any photos, for early morning or the last hours of the afternoon.

Is late April or May better for a family trip to Cancun?

Late April is drier and a little cooler: April averages 3.8 days with rain, the fewest of the year, and a high of 33.1 °C (92 °F), against 7.3 days and 34.2 °C (94 °F) in May. Both come before the Atlantic hurricane season, which starts June 1.

When are Victoria Day and Memorial Day in 2027?

Victoria Day is Monday, May 24, 2027, and Memorial Day is Monday, May 31, 2027, so the two long weekends fall a week apart: May 22 to 24 and May 29 to 31.

Is there seaweed in Cancun in May?

Often, yes. The University of South Florida's Optical Oceanography Lab says sargassum typically peaks in June or July, and it reported record-high amounts for May 2026 in every region of its bulletin except the West Atlantic. It has no outlook for May 2027 yet and publishes a new bulletin at the end of each month.

What time should a family photo session start in Cancun in May?

In May the sun rises between 6:16 and 6:05 am and the soft light lasts until about 6:47 to 6:37 am, so our 6:30 am online slot starts in golden light. For an evening session that ends at sunset, start around 6:10 to 6:25 pm; we arrange those times on WhatsApp.

Do you need a deposit to book a family session?

No. You pay at the end of the session, by card or in cash, and cancellation is free until one day before. The Chapter, our recommended collection for families, is 60 minutes and 35 edited photos for 475 USD, with no per-person fee.
