In short

Vianey Díaz is a Mexican photographer and the founder and Creative Director of IVAE Studios, established in Cancun in 2023. She brings more than ten years of editorial experience from studios and brand campaigns. The studio has delivered 500+ sessions and takes a maximum of 12 weddings a year.

A studio name and a polished gallery only tell you so much. The decisions made in the hour your photos are taken, light, composition, what your photographer chose to wait for, depend on the person making them. This page is the short, factual version of who that is.

Who Vianey Is

Vianey Díaz is a Mexican photographer, cinematographer and creative director based in Cancun, Quintana Roo. She leads IVAE Studios as Creative Director and Lead Photographer, and works with clients in English and Spanish.

Photography Philosophy

Three principles guide every IVAE session.

Editorial direction. An editorial frame is not posed and it is not unguided. It comes from intentional positioning, intentional waiting and intentional restraint: placement relative to light, a prompt that produces the next genuine moment, the negative space around the subject. The difference from pure documentary is laid out in editorial vs documentary luxury photography styles.

Golden hour discipline. Sessions are scheduled around the soft light window after sunrise or before sunset, when Caribbean light is warm, directional and forgiving. The studio's approach to timing is in our golden hour photography in Mexico guide.

Bilingual approach. Direction happens in whichever language puts the client most at ease, which helps when a couple, their family and a wedding planner do not all share one language.
