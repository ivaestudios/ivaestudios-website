Hawaii or Cancun for a Family Vacation? An Honest Look

In short

From Seattle or San Francisco, Honolulu and Cancun are about equally far; from Denver, Chicago or New York, Hawaii is two to three times farther. Hawaii needs no passport; Mexico needs one for everyone, babies included ($135 per child). US visitors to Hawaii spent $277 to $314 per person a day so far in 2026, before airfare. In late December, Cancun's sunset lands at 3:15 to 6:15 pm on mainland body clocks, Honolulu's at 7:59 to 10:59 pm.

The honest answer depends on where you fly from, how old your kids are and which documents you already hold. We are a Cancun studio, so we have a side; this page tries not to take it. Every outside fact was checked on an official source on November 14, 2026, and is listed at the end.

From Los Angeles, Cancun is about 440 miles nearer; from Denver east, Hawaii is two to five times as far. Maui's Kahului airport is about 40 to 75 miles closer than Honolulu. Your airline's schedule has exact flight times, and our guide to flight times to Cancun covers nonstops city by city.

Neither place changes its clocks: Hawaii stays ten hours behind UTC and Quintana Roo, Cancun's state, five, so Cancun is always five hours ahead of Honolulu. From March 14 to November 7, 2027, most of the mainland springs forward, so Hawaii falls one more hour behind home and Cancun's lead shrinks by an hour: level with Chicago, an hour behind New York.

With kids, direction matters. In winter, 6:00 am in Hawaii is 8:00 am on a Los Angeles body clock and 10:00 am on a Chicago one; an 8:00 pm bedtime there is 10:00 pm or midnight. In Cancun, Chicago kids are one hour off, New York kids not at all.

Passports, IDs and Kids

Hawaii is a domestic trip. State Department rules require no passport between parts of the United States, and TSA does not require ID from children under 18 on domestic flights; adults need an acceptable ID, such as a REAL ID-compliant license.

Mexico is international. Every US citizen needs a passport book to fly home from Mexico, babies included, because the passport card is valid only at land and sea crossings. Children under 16 apply in person with both parents (or one, with the other's notarized consent), and their passport lasts five years. Federal fees:

  • Child under 16: $135 every time ($100 plus the $35 execution fee).
  • Adult: $165 the first time, $130 to renew by mail.
  • Expedited service: $60 more per passport.

Two parents and two kids under 16 with no passports pay $600, before any expedited service; our Mexico entry rules guide covers arrival.

What Each Trip Really Costs

No official source prices both trips the same way. Hawaii's state economists at DBEDT measure what visitors spend in the islands, transpacific airfare excluded: in the first nine months of 2026, visitors from the western US averaged $277 per person per day, those from the eastern US $314. At the western average, four people for seven days come to about $7,760 before flights, an average across all visitors, not a quote.

Lodging is Hawaii's biggest line and food the second, says DBEDT's 2025 report. In Cancun, an all-inclusive rate puts meals and snacks in the room price; our 2027 Cancun family budget prices a real July week from Chicago, flights included. Cancun adds two costs: passports, if you lack them, and Visitax, Quintana Roo's visitor fee, which every foreign tourist pays, with no child rate.

Beaches, Water and Days Out

Hawaii's islands are volcanic, and some beaches show it, like Pailoa, the black sand beach at Waianapanapa State Park on Maui. Popular parks need reservations: at Waianapanapa and Oahu's Diamond Head, non-residents book ahead and pay $5 per person and $10 per car, children 3 and under free. Before small kids wade in, choose a beach with a lifeguard and ask about the surf and currents that day.

Cancun's Hotel Zone has white sand and two kinds of water: beaches facing north into the bay toward Isla Mujeres are usually calmest for little swimmers, while the long east-facing stretch takes the open Caribbean. Our guide to the safest Cancun beaches for kids names them. Off the resort, Hawaii means dollars and US driving rules; Cancun means pesos, Maya ruins, cenotes and the Isla Mujeres ferry.

December also comes after both hurricane seasons, which run June 1 to November 30: the Atlantic one, which includes the Caribbean, and the central Pacific one around Hawaii. For winter break and New Year's week, book flights, rooms and your session early.

The Light, and Your Family Photos

The Hawaii family photo most people picture is a sunset over the water, and western resort areas like Ko Olina, on Oahu's west side, and Kaanapali, in West Maui, face west toward it. The catch is the clock: on December 28, Honolulu's 5:59 pm sunset is 7:59 pm on a Los Angeles body clock and 9:59 pm on a Chicago one, while its 7:08 am sunrise feels like 9:08 am or later. In Hawaii, early sessions are the easy ones.

Cancun is the mirror image. Its long Caribbean beach faces east, so the sun rises out of the sea and white sand bounces soft light into faces; late in the day the sun drops behind the hotels, the sand falls into soft shade and the color moves to the sky. Cancun's 6:15 pm sunset that day is 3:15 pm on a Los Angeles body clock, 5:15 pm in Chicago and 6:15 pm in New York. West Coast families should book the late afternoon; East Coast early risers can take our 7:00 am start for the 7:23 am sunrise. See our sunset and golden hour times by month.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it cheaper to go to Cancun or Hawaii with kids?

It depends on your city and dates. Hawaii's state economists found that western US visitors spent $277 per person per day in the first nine months of 2026, airfare excluded, and eastern US visitors $314. In Cancun, all-inclusive rates fold in meals, but everyone needs a passport and pays Visitax.

Do we need passports for Cancun but not for Hawaii?

Yes. Hawaii is a domestic trip: no passport, and TSA does not require ID from children under 18. To fly to Mexico and back, every US citizen needs a passport book, babies included. A child passport costs $135; a first adult passport, $165.

How far is Hawaii compared with Cancun from the mainland?

From Seattle or San Francisco, about the same: roughly 2,400 to 2,700 miles. From Los Angeles, Cancun is about 440 miles closer, and from Denver, Chicago or New York, Hawaii is two to three times as far. In winter, Hawaii runs two to five hours behind the mainland, Cancun zero to three ahead.

Is Cancun or Hawaii better in December?

Both are warm. On 1991 to 2020 averages, December highs are about 82 °F in Honolulu and 85 °F in Cancun, lows near 70 °F in both, and Cancun gets more rain, 3.8 inches against 2.2. December also comes after both hurricane seasons, the Atlantic and the central Pacific, which end November 30.
