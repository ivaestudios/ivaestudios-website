Cancún Wedding Photographer and Videographer: One Team or Two?

In short

One team means one timeline, cameras that never fight for the same angle, matching color between stills and film, one outside vendor fee at the resort and one contact for the whole day. Two teams make sense when you love a specific filmmaker's style. Our full day runs 3,500 to 5,500 USD for photography and 5,500 to 8,000 with the cinematic highlight film.

Almost every couple planning a Cancún wedding asks it in the same breath: should the photographer and the videographer be the same people? Here is what changes on the day, and what it costs, from a studio that shoots both.

What changes on the wedding day

The ceremony is the clearest case. A photographer and a videographer from different companies both want the aisle, both want the first kiss from the front, and neither has planned with the other. With one team, the positions are agreed before the ceremony: stills take the aisle, film takes the side, both switch for the vows, and the couple's session at golden hour is one session, not two crews taking turns while the light goes. It sounds small; on the day it is the difference between a timeline that flows and one that stalls.

Color, style and the film that matches the album

Stills and film from separate studios rarely match. One grades warm, the other cool; one leans documentary, the other posed. The album and the highlight film end up looking like two different weddings. When the same studio shoots both, the color and the direction are the same by design, so the film feels like the album moving. That consistency is the reason most couples who see both side by side choose one team.

The resort vendor fee, counted once

Most Cancún and Riviera Maya resorts charge an outside vendor fee per vendor company. One studio for photography and film means one fee rather than two, and one arrangement to make with the coordinator, which we handle with the property directly. It also means one contract, one point of contact and one delivery, which matters more than it sounds when you are planning from another country.

When two separate teams make sense

Honesty requires the other side. If you have fallen for a specific filmmaker's work, hire them, and tell us early so the two teams plan the ceremony together. Two crews also make sense for very large weddings where the volume of coverage justifies separate leads. What we would not do is book two companies to save money, because the vendor fee doubles and the coordination cost lands on your day.

What it costs with one team

Our published full day, eight to ten hours with a second shooter and venue scouting, runs 3,500 to 5,500 USD for photography. The same day with a cinematic highlight film runs 5,500 to 8,000. Every collection includes the complete edited gallery in one to three business days through a private 4K gallery. Send the date and the venue and you get a real number the same business day, for photography alone or for both.

Frequently asked questions

Should the photographer and videographer be from the same company?

Usually yes: one timeline, no fight for angles, matching color between album and film, one vendor fee and one contact. Two teams make sense if you want a specific filmmaker.

Do you offer photography and video together?

Yes. A full day with a cinematic highlight film runs 5,500 to 8,000 USD, against 3,500 to 5,500 for photography alone.

Does the resort charge a fee for each vendor?

Most charge an outside vendor fee per company. One studio for both means one fee. We handle the arrangement with the property.

Will the film match the photos?

When the same studio shoots both, the color and the direction are the same by design, so the film feels like the album in motion.

What if we already booked a videographer?

Tell us early. We plan the ceremony positions together so both teams get their shots without crossing the aisle.
