In short

Devossion by Live Aqua is an adults-only all-inclusive on the beach in central Playa del Carmen, steps from Fifth Avenue. Use sunrise or the last hour of the day at the water, then walk into town for a second look. Sessions start at 295 USD.

Most resorts on the Riviera Maya make you choose between the beach and a real town. Devossion, which opened in 2025 in a former Hyatt building, sits at 1a Avenida and Calle 26, so you can have both within a few minutes on foot.

Devossion at a Glance

  • ResortAdults-only all-inclusive by Live Aqua (Grupo Posadas)
  • Opened2025, with a Grand Opening in October
  • Address1a Avenida at Calle 26, Playa del Carmen
  • SizeAbout 314 rooms, per listings
  • RoomsHorizon Suites with plunge pools, swim-up rooms
  • Beach facesEast, toward Cozumel
  • From the airportAbout 45 to 50 minutes

Where We Shoot

The water's edge. Sand and sea, with Cozumel on the horizon. We walk away from the loungers and vendors for a clean frame. Best early or late, since this central beach gets busy.

The oceanfront pools. All the pools look toward the ocean. They suit a couple at the quiet ends of the day, with the resort's permission.

Your Horizon Suite. Floor-to-ceiling windows and a sea-view patio, some with a plunge pool. A relaxed first frame.

Fifth Avenue and Parque Fundadores. Fifth Avenue is a few minutes on foot, full of street life and colour. The Portal Maya sculpture at Parque Fundadores is about 15 minutes from the hotel. Either one gives you a very different second look.

Frequently Asked Questions

What was Devossion by Live Aqua before?
It operated as a Hyatt until the end of July 2025 and reopened as Devossion on August 1, with a Grand Opening that October. Travel listings show it under the same property record as the former Grand Hyatt Playa del Carmen.
Where are the best photo spots at Devossion by Live Aqua?
The water's edge early or late, the oceanfront pools at quiet hours, your own suite, and Fifth Avenue a few minutes on foot.
Is Devossion by Live Aqua adults only?
Yes. It is an adults-only all-inclusive in central Playa del Carmen. Listings give a minimum age of 18, so confirm with the hotel.
