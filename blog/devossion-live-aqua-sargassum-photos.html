In short

Playa del Carmen faces the open sea, so seaweed can arrive from about April to August in a heavy year. A traveller reports barriers, netting and daily cleaning at Devossion. If it is heavy, we go early or move to town or a cenote.

Playa del Carmen is one of the more exposed places on this coast, so it is smart to have a plan. Here is what we know, and how we would handle it.

What to Expect

Sargassum usually builds from April and is heaviest from June to August. November to March is normally the clearest stretch. It changes week by week with the wind, and a beach can be covered one day and clean a few days later. A February 2026 travel review reports barriers, netting and daily cleaning here. Check recent reviews close to your date.

Plan B

Go early. Beaches are usually at their cleanest right after the morning clean.

Use the town. Fifth Avenue and its side streets do not care about seaweed.

Use the hotel. The pools look out at the ocean, and your suite has its own sea view.

Go to a cenote. Cenotes near Playa del Carmen are inland, so seaweed never reaches them. It takes more time, so plan it with us.
