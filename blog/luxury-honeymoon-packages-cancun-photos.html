In short

We read five honeymoon packages at four Cancun resorts on November 14, 2026. Most bundle sparkling wine, a romantic dinner, breakfast in bed and a massage or spa discount; the differences sit in the conditions: price, nights, room category, how recently you married and how you book. Only Excellence Playa Mujeres mentioned photos: 15% off the resort's photo package, plus five complimentary digital pictures in its Signature version. None included a photo session.

A honeymoon package reads beautifully: sparkling wine on arrival, dinner for two, breakfast in bed. What it rarely spells out is the photo part, the piece of the trip you keep long after the tan fades. We are a photography studio in Cancun, not a travel agency, and we photograph more than 200 couples a year. So we read four Cancun resorts' honeymoon packages on their own websites, with one question in mind: what do you actually come home with?

How to Compare Them

Side by side, the perks rhyme, and all four resorts ask for your marriage certificate. The differences sit in the conditions:

  • Free or paid. All five can be free with a qualifying stay. Haven also sells its package for $399, and Excellence sells the Signature for 950 USD per couple. A free package that needs six nights in a club category is only free if you wanted those nights anyway.
  • Nights and room. Three nights at Hard Rock, four at Le Blanc and at Haven, six in an Excellence Club category for the free Signature. None of the five lists a room upgrade; Haven and the Signature tie the free version to a room category instead.
  • How recently you married. Within three months of the wedding at Excellence and Haven, within the last calendar year at Le Blanc, within the first year of marriage at Hard Rock.
  • How you book. Excellence and Le Blanc want it requested when you book, Hard Rock will not add it on arrival, Le Blanc's terms exclude travel agent and tour operator rates, and Haven's free version must be booked directly with the hotel.
  • Private or reserved. A private beachfront dinner and a table at one of the resort's restaurants both read as a romantic dinner on paper. Ask which one is yours.

The Photo Part: Package Perk or Your Own Session

Only Excellence Playa Mujeres mentions photos. Both of its versions take 15% off “your photo package”, and the Signature adds five complimentary digital pictures from it. The PDF mentions Seasons Photo Studio, which it says offers photography and video, but not what a photo package costs before the discount, how many edited photos it holds, when they arrive or what you may do with them. None of the five packages includes a photo session itself.

Which Day of the Trip to Shoot

On a stay of five to seven nights, aim for the second or third day: rested, settled and with most of the trip still ahead. Leave the arrival day and the last evening free. Our honeymoon photoshoot planning guide explains the timing, and our 7-day Riviera Maya honeymoon itinerary shows where a session fits in the week. Here is how to fit it around your package:

  • Morning or evening. Online you can book 6:30, 7:00 or 7:30 am, or 5:00 or 5:30 pm; for other times, message us on WhatsApp. A morning session keeps the evening free for the package dinner; an evening session can end at the table, still dressed for the photos.
  • Early enough to share. Your preview arrives within 24 hours, so a session early in the trip gives you photos to send home while you are still there.

What to Ask the Resort Before You Book

Before you pay for a package, or for the room category that unlocks one, ask the resort five things:

  1. Is the honeymoon package added automatically, or must we request it when we book, and does our booking channel qualify?
  2. What proof of marriage do you need, and how recent must the wedding be?
  3. How many nights and which room category does the free version need?
  4. If there is a photo perk: what does the photo package cost before the discount, how many edited photos does it include, when are they delivered, and may we print them?
  5. What is your rule for outside photographers, is there a fee, and who pays it?

You can leave the last one to us. Before your session we confirm your hotel's rule; if there is a fee, you pay it to the hotel. Then pick your collection and time online, or send us your dates and resort on WhatsApp.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Cancun honeymoon packages include a photo session?

None of the five we read on November 14, 2026 did. Excellence Playa Mujeres takes 15% off its photo package, and its Signature version adds five complimentary digital pictures. Le Blanc, Haven Riviera Cancun and Hard Rock Hotel Cancun listed no photos.

What does a luxury honeymoon package in Cancun include?

Most of the five we read combine sparkling wine, a romantic dinner, breakfast in bed and a massage or a spa discount. Each has a free version that needs a marriage certificate and usually a minimum stay; Haven also sells its package for $399 and Excellence its Signature for 950 USD per couple.

Which day of the honeymoon should we book the photo session?

Your second or third day on a stay of five to seven nights: rested, settled and with most of the trip still ahead. Skip the arrival day and the last evening. A 6:30, 7:00 or 7:30 am session keeps the evening free for your package dinner.

Can we bring an outside photographer to our Cancun resort?

It depends on the resort. Some hotels charge a fee for outside photographers, which you pay to the hotel. We confirm your hotel's rule before the session.

How much is a honeymoon photo session with IVAE Studios, and how soon are the photos ready?

From 295 USD for The Postcard, 35 minutes and 15 edited photos, to 895 USD for The Heirloom and its complete edited gallery. The preview arrives within 24 hours and the private 4K gallery in 1 to 3 business days, with print rights. There is no deposit, and cancellation is free until one day before.
