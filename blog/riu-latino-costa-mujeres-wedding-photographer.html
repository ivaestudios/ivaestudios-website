In short

Riu Latino opened in October 2022 as an adults-only resort with 594 rooms on Costa Mujeres. A wedding listing says it hosts destination weddings with indoor and outdoor spaces; capacities in listings conflict, so confirm with the hotel. Plan a beach ceremony about 90 minutes before sunset.

Riu Latino is one of the newer resorts on Costa Mujeres and one of the few adults-only options at RIU prices, which makes it popular with couples who want a calm wedding without a five-star bill. This guide explains how we photograph a wedding there and what to ask before you sign.

Venue names and capacities come from the resort's wedding listings and change with renovations and packages, so your coordinator's current sheet is the final word.

Where the Ceremony Happens

A third-party wedding listing says Riu Latino hosts destination weddings with indoor and outdoor spaces, but the capacities it quotes conflict with each other, so ask the hotel's wedding team for the current venues and numbers. On this coast the best outdoor spot is almost always the beach, with calm water and Isla Mujeres on the horizon, and an adults-only beach stays uncluttered in wide frames.

If your guests stay in swim-out rooms with private pools, those make excellent getting-ready locations, with natural light and a private terrace for the dress and the details.

The beach faces east toward the channel and Isla Mujeres, so the sun sets behind the resort and the sand falls into soft, even light at the end of the day, with no glare in anyone's eyes. A good rule here: start the ceremony about 90 minutes before sunset, and keep the final half hour of daylight for portraits. Mornings have a bonus: from November through February, the sun comes up from behind Isla Mujeres, across the water.

Bringing Your Own Photographer

One budget line couples often miss: Riu Latino, like nearly every all-inclusive resort here, charges outside wedding vendors for access to the property. It may be a fixed vendor fee or a per-person day pass, and it changes with the season and the package tier. Ask for it in writing while your contract is still open, and ask whether a second photographer counts separately. Our comparison of resort and outside photographers helps you weigh it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can you get married at Riu Latino?
A wedding listing says Riu Latino hosts destination weddings with indoor and outdoor spaces, but its capacities conflict, so confirm the current venues and numbers directly with the hotel's wedding team.
What time is best for a wedding at Riu Latino?
Start the ceremony about 90 minutes before sunset, so that portraits fall in the warm last light. Sunset here ranges from 6:06 pm in mid-winter to 7:32 pm in mid-summer, and Quintana Roo does not use daylight saving.
Does Riu Latino charge for an outside wedding photographer?
Expect an access charge for outside wedding vendors, as a vendor fee or a per-person day pass, which the resort revises by season and package. Ask for the amount in writing before you sign, and ask whether it applies per vendor or per person.
How much does IVAE Studios charge for a wedding at Riu Latino?
Half-day coverage of up to four hours is 1,800 to 2,800 USD, a full day of eight to ten hours with a second photographer is 3,500 to 5,500 USD, and coverage with a wedding film is 5,500 to 8,000 USD. The resort's vendor charge is separate.
