With an adults-only guest list, the whole day feels different. Here is how we would plan the photographs at Riu Latino, and what to ask the wedding team.

Where the Ceremony Happens

A third-party wedding listing says Riu Latino hosts destination weddings with indoor and outdoor spaces, but the capacities it quotes conflict with each other, so ask the hotel's wedding team for the current venues and numbers. On this coast the best outdoor spot is almost always the beach, with calm water and Isla Mujeres on the horizon, and an adults-only beach stays uncluttered in wide frames.

If your guests stay in swim-out rooms with private pools, those make excellent getting-ready locations, with natural light and a private terrace for the dress and the details.

Seaweed and Weather at Riu Latino

Sargassum usually builds from April and is heaviest from June to August, and November to March is normally the clearest stretch. This shore has an advantage over the Riviera Maya, because the seaweed comes from the southeast and Isla Mujeres gives Riu Latino some shelter. It is a probability, not a promise, since the heaviest years have reached almost everywhere on this coast. Our month-by-month sargassum guide has the detail and the outlook for the season. From June to October afternoon showers are common and usually pass quickly, so we watch the forecast with you and move the hour if needed.

Bringing Your Own Photographer

One budget line couples often miss is the charge many resorts here apply to outside wedding vendors. It may be a fixed fee or a per-person pass, and it depends on the season and the package tier. Ask Riu Latino for it in writing while your contract is open, and ask whether a second photographer counts separately. Our comparison of resort and outside photographers helps you weigh it.

Close to Riu Latino

For a second look, Isla Mujeres is a short boat ride across the channel, and guests of Costa Mujeres resorts also come to us at Playa Langosta on the calm north shore of the Hotel Zone. Beaches in Mexico are public up to the high-water line, so the sand itself needs no resort pass; loungers, gardens and pools are the hotel's to manage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can you get married at Riu Latino?
A wedding listing says Riu Latino hosts destination weddings with indoor and outdoor spaces, but its capacities conflict, so confirm the current venues and numbers directly with the hotel's wedding team.
What time should a wedding start at Riu Latino?
About 90 minutes before sunset, so the portraits fall in the warm last light. Sunset here ranges from 6:06 pm in November to 7:32 pm in July, which puts a ceremony near 4:35 pm in November and 6:00 pm in June. Quintana Roo does not use daylight saving.
Can we bring an outside wedding photographer to Riu Latino?
Ask Riu Latino in writing before you sign. Many resorts on this coast charge outside wedding vendors a fee or a per-person pass that changes by season and package, so ask for the current figure and whether it applies per vendor or per person.
How much does a wedding photographer at Riu Latino cost?
