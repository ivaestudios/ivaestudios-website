What You Are Seeing

These images come from IVAE Studios work on different days and in different places. They are not one wedding, so read them as a sample of style and not as a complete story. Names and exact venues are not shown, to respect client privacy.

Every image in the portfolio is a real client on a real day, and our AI policy explains what we never edit. For more on the approach behind the images, see editorial versus documentary luxury photography.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these 60 images from one wedding?
No. They come from different IVAE Studios weddings and sessions on different days, to show style and consistency. Ask us to see a complete wedding gallery if you want to judge a whole day.
Are these real clients?
Yes. Every image in the portfolio is a real client on a real day. Names and exact venues are not shown, to respect client privacy.
How much editing goes into the photos?
Images receive color and tone work, and the studio follows its AI policy: nobody is added and nobody is removed. See the policy page for details.
How many photos will we receive?
Sessions deliver from 15 to 70 edited photos, or a complete edited gallery, depending on the collection. For weddings, the number depends on your hours of coverage, so ask for a range in writing.
How long does delivery take?
A complete edited gallery is delivered through a private 4K gallery in 1 to 3 business days, with a sneak peek in 24 to 48 hours.
How much does wedding photography cost with IVAE Studios?
From 1,800 to 2,800 USD for a half day, 3,500 to 5,500 USD for a full day, and 5,500 to 8,000 USD for a full day with a cinematic film.
