In short

At Paradisus Cancun the big decision is which water faces your ceremony: the sea, for turquoise behind you and soft light, or the Nichupté Lagoon, for a real sunset over the water. Start the ceremony about 90 minutes before sunset; the table below gives the time for your month.

Paradisus Cancun is one of the few resorts in the Hotel Zone where you can marry facing the sunset. Its five venues split between sea and lagoon, its glass pyramids give you an architectural portrait you will not find elsewhere, and its size means short walks matter. Here is how we would plan the photography of your day.

Where the Ceremony Happens

Paradisus Cancun lists five indoor and outdoor wedding venues under its Romance by Paradisus collections, with outdoor spots facing either the sea or the Nichupté Lagoon. That single choice shapes your photos more than anything else. A sea-facing ceremony gives you the open Caribbean behind you and soft, even light, because the sun is going down behind the building. A lagoon-facing ceremony can catch the actual sunset over the water.

Our advice is simple: if you want colour in the sky, ask for the lagoon; if you want turquoise water, ask for the sea and start earlier. Either way the glass pyramids are worth ten minutes of portraits on the way back, while your guests head to cocktails.

Seaweed and Weather at Paradisus Cancun

Sargassum usually builds from April and is heaviest from June to August, and November to March is normally the clearest stretch. Because Paradisus Cancun is on the open Caribbean, it is exposed in a heavy year, and one stretch of sand can be piled with seaweed one week and clean the next. Our backup is an early start, when large resorts have just raked, or the Nichupté Lagoon across the boulevard, where the water stays clear. Our month-by-month sargassum guide has the detail and the outlook for the season. Summer and early autumn bring afternoon showers that tend to pass quickly. We keep an eye on the forecast and adjust the hour with you the day before.

Bringing Your Own Photographer

Most all-inclusive resorts on this coast charge outside wedding photographers for access, as a vendor fee or a per-person day pass, and the amount moves with season and package. Ask Paradisus Cancun for the exact figure in writing before you sign, and ask whether it applies per vendor or per person, because a photographer and a second shooter are two people. We send our insurance and identification paperwork to your coordinator ourselves. Our guide to outside photographers explains how these charges work.

Close to Paradisus Cancun

The nearest public beaches are Playa Ballenas (km 14, about 1 km away) and Playa Delfines (km 18, about 3 km away). Mexican law keeps every beach public, so a session at the water's edge does not depend on the hotel's grounds policy.
