Caribbean Cruise or Cancun All-Inclusive With Kids?

In short

Big ships don't dock in Cancun: Western Caribbean cruises call at Cozumel, 60 km (37 miles) south, or at Costa Maya and Progreso. In Cozumel's port calendar for November 1 to 22, 2026, the median call lasts 10.5 hours and most ships sail before sunset. Carnival and Royal Caribbean only board babies from 6 months. For little ones and unhurried photos, we lean Cancun; for teens, the cruise.

Both trips put your family on the same turquoise water. The difference is time: a cruise gives you a day per port, a resort gives you the whole week. We are a Cancun studio, so every cruise rule below comes from the lines' and governments' own pages, read on November 14, 2026.

Why Ships Call at Cozumel, Not Cancun

Look for a cruise to Cancun and you land in Cozumel. Royal Caribbean's list of Mexican ports, for example, has Cozumel, Puerto Costa Maya at Mahahual and Yucatan (Progreso), and no Cancun. The terminal that Quintana Roo's port authority, APIQROO, runs in Cancun is Puerto Juarez, 5 km (3 miles) north of the city, the main boarding point for boats to Isla Mujeres.

Its cruise calendar is for Cozumel, an island 60 km south of Cancun and 20 km (12 miles) off the coast, where ships tie up at three terminals: Punta Langosta, Puerta Maya and SSA Mexico. So a cruise is a Cozumel day, not a Cancun day.

How Many Hours You Really Get Ashore

On November 14, 2026, APIQROO's Cozumel calendar listed 91 calls scheduled from November 1 to 22, each with arrival and departure times: most ships arrive between 6:00 and 7:30 am, 62 of the 91 sail between 4:00 and 5:00 pm, and the median call lasts 10.5 hours at the pier. Carnival's 25 calls run 8.5 to 9.5 hours, such as 6:30 am to 4:00 pm; Royal Caribbean's 27 run 9.25 to 11.25 hours. Disney Treasure and Disney Destiny are listed at Punta Langosta from 6:30 am to 5:00 pm.

Time ashore is shorter than time at the pier, because you must be back on board before the ship sails, and Carnival's ticket contract warns that shipboard and shore clocks may differ.

Then there is the light. Between November 1 and 22, the US Naval Observatory puts sunrise in Cozumel at 6:50 to 7:02 am and sunset at 6:06 to 6:13 pm, and 78 of the 91 calls were set to sail by 6:00 pm. Most of a port day falls between the soft morning and evening light.

Plans of your own in port

Royal Caribbean guarantees that the ship waits for its own delayed tours, not for guests out on their own. Anything you book yourself, a photographer included, has to end with time to spare, on the ship's clock.

Price the whole party, not the headline fare. Royal Caribbean says infants and children pay normal guest pricing, with reduced third- and fourth-guest rates and Kids Sail Free offers on some sailings, and that Mexico's non-resident tax is now in the taxes and fees of voyages calling in Mexico. Carnival's suggested gratuity alone, 17 USD a day for every guest age 2 and up, comes to 476 USD for two parents and two kids aged 2 or older in a standard cabin on a seven-day cruise.

Toddlers or Teens: Which Trip Is Easier?

Babies and toddlers. Under 6 months, the decision is made for you: Carnival and Royal Caribbean require infants to be at least 6 months old when they board, and 12 months on ocean crossings and other voyages with three or more sea days in a row. Royal Caribbean limits children in diapers to Baby Splash areas, which only some ships have. Carnival's Camp Ocean starts at age 2, included in the fare. A resort keeps you in the same room all week, with no departure to race, so we lean Cancun for the littlest ones.

Teens. Here a cruise shines: Carnival includes Circle C for ages 12 to 14 and Club O2 for 15 to 17, Royal Caribbean's Adventure Ocean runs to 17, and every port is a new day. A resort suits teens who want sleep, a beach and a little independence.

Ship Photographers or a Private Session

Royal Caribbean says each ship carries many professional photographers, from gangway shots in port to formal portraits, with up to 40 photo opportunities onboard. Packages can be bought before or during the cruise and single photos onboard, but nothing after the cruise, and purchased photos go online within 4 to 7 days after the voyage. If you want those onboard portraits, buy them before you disembark.

A port day rarely leaves room for an unhurried hour in soft light: a session has to fit inside the ship's day, ideally early. The Keepsake, our 45-minute collection, is the one we suggest when time is short, and our Cozumel cruise day guide does the timing math.

Our Verdict, Family by Family

  • Baby under 6 months. Cancun. Carnival and Royal Caribbean won't board them yet.
  • Toddlers and nappers. Cancun: one room, one routine, no departure clock.
  • Kids 6 to 12. Either: the ship for variety, the resort for a slower week.
  • Teens. The cruise, if they want a new port each day and their own club; the resort, if they want sleep and the beach.
  • Photos as a priority. Cancun, where you choose the morning, the beach and the light; our list of the best Cancun resorts for family photoshoots helps you pick.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do cruise ships go to Cancun?

Big cruise ships don't dock in Cancun. Western Caribbean itineraries call at Cozumel, an island 60 km (37 miles) south, or at Costa Maya and Progreso. The Cancun terminal run by Quintana Roo's port authority, Puerto Juarez, is the main boarding point for boats to Isla Mujeres.

How long do cruise ships stay in Cozumel?

In the port authority's calendar for November 1 to 22, 2026, the median call lasts 10.5 hours, and most ships arrive between 6:00 and 7:30 am. Carnival's calls run 8.5 to 9.5 hours, Royal Caribbean's 9.25 to 11.25. Your time ashore is shorter, because you must be back on board before the ship sails.

Is a cruise or a Cancun all-inclusive easier with a baby or toddler?

Carnival and Royal Caribbean require babies to be at least 6 months old at boarding, and 12 months on some longer voyages, and Royal Caribbean limits children in diapers to Baby Splash areas, which only some ships have. A resort keeps you in the same room all week, so we lean Cancun for the littlest ones.

Do kids need passports for a cruise or for Cancun?

To fly back from Cancun, US Customs and Border Protection asks every US citizen, children included, for a passport or a trusted traveler card such as NEXUS. On a cruise that starts and ends at the same US port, US citizens can return with a birth certificate and government-issued photo ID, and children under 16 need only a birth certificate. Carnival still recommends a passport.

Is a cruise photo package worth it?

It covers life onboard: Royal Caribbean lists up to 40 photo opportunities, sells packages before or during the cruise but not after, and posts purchased photos within 4 to 7 days after the voyage. A private session is a different product: our Chapter collection is 60 minutes and 35 edited photos for 475 USD, with a preview within 24 hours.
