In short

At NIZUC, propose on the adults-only beach in the last half hour of light, or privately at your garden pool villa after dark. Coverage starts at 245 USD.

A private point with two beaches and pool villas makes NIZUC one of the easiest places in Cancún to keep a proposal secret. Here is our plan.

The Plan at a Glance

  • ResortLuxury resort on about 29 acres at Punta Nizuc
  • Size274 suites and villas, including 29 garden pool villas
  • BeachesTwo: one adults-only, one family
  • From the airportAbout 15 minutes
  • Beach facesEast, at the southern tip of the Hotel Zone

Where to Ask

The adults-only beach. Quiet and private in the last light.

Your villa. Garden pool villas are completely private; lanterns by the pool photograph beautifully.

Hiding the Photographer and After the Yes

Our photographer arrives early, checks in as your guest and waits 20 to 40 metres from the spot with a long lens, looking like any other guest. We agree three signals: a message when you leave your room, a marker where you stop, and the moment you turn your partner around. You never need to look for us. For a staying guest, a short private session is very seldom charged the outside vendor fee used for weddings, and we confirm the current policy with the resort in writing.

After the yes we take 20 to 40 minutes of portraits while the light lasts. Proposal coverage uses our session collections: Spark, 245 USD for 35 minutes and 15 photos; Glow Hour, 325 USD for 45 minutes and 25 photos; and Essential Flow, 395 USD for 60 minutes and 35 photos. The gallery arrives in one to three business days. More on our surprise proposal photographer page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where should I propose at NIZUC?
On the adults-only beach in the last half hour of light, or privately at your garden pool villa with lanterns after sunset.
What time is best for a proposal at NIZUC?
About 25 minutes before sunset, which leaves the best light for photos after the yes. Sunset here ranges from 6:06 pm in mid-winter to 7:32 pm in mid-summer, and Quintana Roo does not use daylight saving.
How does the photographer stay hidden?
The photographer checks in as your guest and waits 20 to 40 metres away with a long lens. You walk to an agreed marker and turn your partner around; the photographer only comes over after the yes.
How much does proposal photography at NIZUC cost?
IVAE Studios proposal coverage starts at 245 USD for 35 minutes and 15 photos. Glow Hour, 325 USD for 45 minutes and 25 photos, is the most common choice. The gallery arrives in one to three business days.
