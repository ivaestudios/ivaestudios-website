In short

At Cancun's official weather station, June to August average highs of 34.0 to 35.1 °C (93 to 95 °F), and July is the driest of the three. Hurricane season starts June 1, with most activity from mid-August to mid-October; Atlantic seaweed typically peaks in June or July. Canada Day (Thursday, July 1) and the Fourth of July (observed Monday, July 5) share one week in 2027. For photos, book 6:30 am or ask us on WhatsApp for an evening start: the sun sets between 7:04 and 7:33 pm.

School is out and the days are long, but summer in Cancun with kids also brings heat, early hurricane season and peak seaweed. Here are June to August 2027 in real numbers, the week when Canada Day and the Fourth of July meet, and how we would plan your family photos.

The heat barely moves, and nights hold around 25 °C (77 °F). The rain does move: June gets almost three times July's rain, and July is the driest of the three, with rain on 8.5 of its 31 days on average. August sits in between. A Summer Day Built Around the Heat

With highs like these, a summer day with kids works best in three parts: the beach early, shade and a rest at midday, the water again late. Midday is also the hardest time for photos, with overhead light, squinting eyes and hot sand, so family sessions belong at either end of the day.

Pack hats, water and snacks for every outing, and when you compare hotels, look at the shade as closely as the pool. Our heat plan for family sessions covers session length, water and fabrics.

Storms and the First Months of Hurricane Season

The Atlantic hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30. The US National Hurricane Center puts its peak on September 10, with most activity between mid-August and mid-October, so June and July come early in the season and mid-August is where its busiest stretch begins.

In practice, check the hurricane center's outlook in the days before you fly, choose flights and rates you can change, and read what your travel insurance covers for named storms.

For your photos, a stormy forecast costs you nothing: there is no deposit, and cancellation is free until one day before. If the forecast looks stormy the day before, cancel at no cost or message us to look at another day of your trip.

Seaweed at Its Summer Peak

Sargassum is a floating seaweed that drifts in from the Atlantic and washes up on Caribbean beaches. The University of South Florida's Optical Oceanography Lab, which tracks it in its Sargassum Watch System, says that across the Atlantic seaweed belt it typically peaks in June or July, with the least in winter.

In its July 31, 2026 bulletin, the lab said 2026 was set to be the second largest Sargassum year, behind only 2025. Its latest bulletin, dated October 31, 2026, expects amounts to keep falling until possibly the end of November or December, and there is no outlook for summer 2027 yet. Bulletins come out by the last day of each month, and they are regional outlooks, not forecasts for a particular beach.

So read the May and June 2027 bulletins before you fly, and check the beach itself the night before: our guide to Cancun beach conditions today shows where to look, from the state's seaweed map to the Navy's daily bulletin and a webcam. If the seaweed is heavy on your session day, the photos can move to spots where the waterline stays out of frame, such as a pool deck, a garden or a covered walkway.

Canada Day to the Fourth: One Holiday Week

Canada Day is July 1, moving to July 2 only when July 1 is a Sunday under Canada's Holidays Act; in 2027 it is a Thursday. July 4 falls on a Sunday in 2027, so the US federal holiday is observed on Monday, July 5, on the US Office of Personnel Management's list. Both fall within Thursday, July 1 to Monday, July 5.

For families on these school calendars, it is also the first week of summer break: the last day of school for NYC Public Schools students is Monday, June 28, 2027, and the Toronto District School Board year runs to June 30, 2027. Mexico's school year for preschool, primary and secondary ends on Friday, July 9, 2027, under the SEP calendar in the Diario Oficial de la Federación. Earlier in June, the US federal Juneteenth holiday is observed on Friday, June 18.

If your trip falls on the holiday week, send us your dates as soon as your flights are booked, and plan the session for your first full morning: the preview arrives within 24 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is summer a good time to visit Cancun with kids?

Yes, with a plan for heat, rain and seaweed. At the SMN's Cancun station, June to August average highs of 34.0 to 35.1 °C (93 to 95 °F) and 8.5 to 11.4 days with rain a month. Hurricane season starts June 1, and Atlantic seaweed typically peaks in June or July. Keep the beach, and any photos, for early morning or the last hour before sunset.

Which summer month is the driest in Cancun?

July. At the SMN's Cancun station it averages 67.0 mm (2.6 in) of rain and 8.5 days with rain, against 184.4 mm (7.3 in) and 11.4 days in June and 102.2 mm (4.0 in) and 10.9 days in August.

Is August a good time to go to Cancun?

It can be, with flexible plans. August has the highest average high of the summer, 35.1 °C (95 °F), and 10.9 days with rain. The US National Hurricane Center says most Atlantic hurricane activity falls between mid-August and mid-October, so late August asks for the most flexibility.

When are Canada Day and the Fourth of July in 2027?

Canada Day is Thursday, July 1, 2027. July 4 falls on a Sunday, so the US federal holiday is observed on Monday, July 5. Both holidays fall within Thursday, July 1 to Monday, July 5.

What time should a summer family photo session start in Cancun?

From June to August 2027 the sun rises between 6:05 and 6:30 am and sets between 7:04 and 7:33 pm. Our 6:30 am online slot starts in soft morning light. For an evening session that ends at sunset, start about an hour before it, between about 6:05 and 6:30 pm; we arrange those times on WhatsApp.

What if a storm is forecast for our session day?

There is no deposit, you pay at the end of the session, and cancellation is free until one day before. Check the forecast the day before; if it looks stormy, cancel at no cost or message us to look at another day of your trip.
