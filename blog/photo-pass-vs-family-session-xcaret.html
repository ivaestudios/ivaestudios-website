In short

On November 14, 2026, the parks' Photo Pass calculator showed 99.99 USD online for one park, 199.99 USD for two and 299.99 USD for three to five, the same for 1 to 10 people. The pass buys the action: photos from the parks' cameras and photographers, ready in 24 hours, and every sale is final. A portrait is a different product: our Chapter collection is 60 minutes and 35 edited photos for 475 USD, with no deposit. Buy the pass for the rides; book the portrait for another morning.

Xcaret, Xel-Ha and Xplor sell a Photo Pass, much as Walt Disney World sells Memory Maker. Both record a day out; neither is a family portrait. Here is what the pass includes, what it costs, and how to fit a portrait around a park day.

Is the Xcaret Photo Pass Worth It?

For the action, usually yes. The pass collects the photos you could not take yourself: your kids on the rivers, rafts and zip lines, caught by cameras installed along the activities and by the parks' own photographers. At Xplor, the park's cameras are the practical way to get photos of the activities, because carrying a camera or phone on them is not allowed.

The price favors families because it is per group, not per person. One park costs the same for 2 people as for 10, and the 299.99 USD price for three parks also covers four or five. A family of five visiting Xcaret and Xplor pays 199.99 USD.

As a portrait, it is weaker. The photos come from points each park has chosen along its route and arrive as the original camera files. A calm portrait of all of you is a different job: arranging the group, waiting for soft light, coaxing a smile out of a tired four-year-old. Still choosing parks? See Xcaret parks with kids, by age.

What the Photo Pass Includes

From Grupo Xcaret's Photo Pass FAQ and the Photo Pass pages for Xcaret, Xel-Ha and Xplor, read November 14, 2026:

  • Online price99.99 USD one park, 199.99 USD two, 299.99 USD three to five
  • People1 to 10, one price
  • Valid atXcaret, Xel-Ha, Xplor, Xplor Fuego, Xoxi, Xenses, Xavage, Catamaran Xailing Cancun, Xenotes Tour
  • PhotosXelfie stations, park cameras, park photographers
  • Not includedDolphin swims, drone, Xunset and ATV photos, optional-activity videos, archaeological sites
  • Ready24 hours after your visit
  • DownloadsHigh-resolution JPGs; code valid 30 calendar days from purchase
  • RefundsNone; valid only on the date of your visit

Xcaret has dozens of automatic Xelfie points plus the parks' professional photographers, per the FAQ. Xel-Ha's page describes dozens of professional cameras at key points that you activate yourself.

Xplor fits your helmet with a chip that activates the cameras along the route, says Xplor's FAQ. Its Photo Pass page says carrying a camera or phone is not allowed during the activities, and a locker for two comes with the ticket.

Download with your code at xcaret.photos, ideally from a computer, or in the Xcaret App. Buy the pass with your tickets: the Photo Pass FAQ says at least 12 hours before your visit and the terms of use say 24, though the Xcaret page also mentions buying at the park before check-in.

Staying at Hotel Xcaret México, Hotel Xcaret Arte or La Casa de la Playa? The pass is not available to hotel guests, and the hotels' FAQ says photos are not included in your stay. It lists no price, sends you to your Guest Service Manager and mentions a special discount at Hotel Xcaret Arte.

If You Know Disney's Memory Maker

Memory Maker is the closest comparison. The prices in the table come from Walt Disney World's Memory Maker page, read November 14, 2026. It covers PhotoPass photos and videos from more than 100 locations, select attractions included, downloadable for 45 days from the date each is taken; Capture Your Moment sessions are sold separately.

So is the Disney photo package worth it? Over several park days, often; for a single visit, One Day fits. Xcaret prices by park instead.

On a cruise, the package covers the line's own photographers. Disney Cruise Line's photo terms, updated January 22, 2026, say a digital package holds photos that include at least one member of the purchasing stateroom, leave out Port Adventures and third-party operators, and sell private portraiture separately. See also a Caribbean cruise or a Cancun all-inclusive with kids.

Cameras, Phones and Your Own Photographer

Your own camera. Not on Xplor's activities, and Xel-Ha says carrying objects in hand is not allowed in some areas. Xcaret's Photo Pass page says some attractions do not let you carry small objects that might fall, and the Photo Pass FAQ suggests leaving your things in a locker.

A hired photographer. On November 14, 2026 we found no rule published either way in Grupo Xcaret's park FAQs, Photo Pass pages or terms of use. Do not plan a session inside a park on a guess: ask Grupo Xcaret in writing first. For the family portrait, a different day and place is simpler, such as your resort's beach in the soft first light.

Pairing a Park Day With a Portrait

Put them on different days: a park day is long and wet, and few children want to pose at the end of one.

  • The portrait on a non-park day, early in the trip while outfits are fresh, or the morning after a park day. Online booking at ivaestudios.com/book offers 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am and 5:00 and 5:30 pm, with other times on WhatsApp.
  • The park day with the pass, in swimsuits and water shoes.
  • The place: your resort beach or one nearby. At Hotel Xcaret México, our Hotel Xcaret México photoshoot guide covers its rivers, inlets and coast. Some hotels charge a fee for an outside photographer, paid by you to the hotel; we confirm your hotel's rule first.

When Each Is Worth Paying For

  • Buy the Photo Pass for Xplor and the rivers, for a big group, since 10 people pay what 2 pay, or for three to five parks.
  • Skip it at a Hotel Xcaret property, where it is not available to guests, or if your children will sit out most of the activities.
  • Book a session for one image of everyone together: for the wall, the year-end card or the grandparents.
  • Do both when the trip has a park day and a slower beach morning: the pass keeps the adventure, the session keeps the family.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Xcaret Photo Pass worth it?
For the action, usually yes: it collects the parks' shots of the rivers, rafts and zip lines, and Xplor allows no cameras or phones during its activities. One price covers 1 to 10 people. For a calm portrait of everyone, book a separate session.
How much does the Xcaret Photo Pass cost?
On November 14, 2026, the online price was 99.99 USD for one park, 199.99 USD for two and 299.99 USD for three to five, for 1 to 10 people. It is valid only on the date of your visit, and all sales are final.
How do I download my Xplor or Xcaret photos?
Photos are ready 24 hours after your visit. Enter your download code at xcaret.photos or in the Xcaret App; it is valid for 30 calendar days from purchase, and the files are high-resolution JPGs.
Is there a photo package for Hotel Xcaret guests?
The Photo Pass is not available to guests of Hotel Xcaret México, Hotel Xcaret Arte or La Casa de la Playa. The hotels' FAQ says photos are not included in your stay, lists no price and sends guests to their Guest Service Manager.
Is the Disney photo package worth it?
Over several park days, often yes. On November 14, 2026, Memory Maker was 185 USD bought at least 3 days ahead or 210 USD otherwise, and Memory Maker One Day was 85 USD. Like the Xcaret pass, it records the trip; it is not a private portrait session.
Can we bring our own photographer into Xcaret?
We found no rule published either way in Grupo Xcaret's FAQs, Photo Pass pages or terms of use on November 14, 2026, so ask Grupo Xcaret in writing first. For a family portrait, a morning on your resort beach on a non-park day is simpler.
