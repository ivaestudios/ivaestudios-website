In short

In 2027, MLK Day is January 18; Presidents' Day and Family Day share Monday, February 15; Ontario's March Break runs March 15 to 19; the US spring breaks we checked fall between March 8 and April 30; Easter Sunday is March 28; Canadian Thanksgiving is October 11 and US Thanksgiving November 25. Winter weeks bring Cancun's earliest sunsets and, usually, its clearest water; our prices are the same on every date.

Most families don't choose their travel dates; the school calendar does. So here is 2027 in one table: each main US and Canadian break, Cancun's sunset that week, what the sea usually does and the session time that fits, plus the guide for each week.

Best Light and Calmest Sea

Given a choice, winter wins on water. Every break from New Year's week to March Break falls in the stretch our sargassum season guide calls the clearest of the year, roughly November to March. The seaweed usually starts to arrive in April and peaks from June to August.

If you can choose one week

Presidents' Day and Family Day weekend, February 13 to 15: winter water, a 6:45 pm sunset that leaves time for dinner after a session, and extra days off in Toronto, Vancouver and New York City. Our best months for photography guide rates February the best month overall.

Winter has one catch, the norte. From November to February, a cold front can bring a windy day with choppy water, and the air after one is often the clearest of the year, as our guide to Cancun in December explains. In any month, the sea tends to be calmest and cleanest at sunrise, before the wind builds.

The light favors winter for young kids, too. From Thanksgiving week to Presidents' Day the sun sets between 6:04 and 6:45 pm, so a 5:00 or 5:30 pm session ends before dinner. Summer sunsets come as late as 7:33 pm, so mornings often suit little ones better; our sunset and golden hour times by month cover the whole year.

Storms run the other way: the Atlantic hurricane season lasts from June 1 to November 30, with most activity between mid-August and mid-October, according to the US National Hurricane Center. Our Cancun hurricane season guide explains how to plan around it.

Long Weekends and Clock Changes

US law sets Martin Luther King Jr. Day on the third Monday of January, Washington's Birthday, or Presidents' Day, on the third Monday of February and Columbus Day on the second Monday of October. Alberta sets Family Day on the third Monday of February and Thanksgiving on the second Monday of October, so both always fall on the same Monday as a US federal holiday; in 2027 Ontario and British Columbia also mark Family Day on February 15. Edmonton Public Schools adds one more long weekend, with no classes on Thursday and Friday, February 25 and 26, for a teachers' convention.

Clocks matter in March. US clocks spring forward on March 14, the second Sunday of the month, while Cancun stays on UTC-5 all year under Mexico's time zone law. Until then Cancun shares New York's clock; afterward it runs an hour behind, so a 7:00 am start feels like 8:00 am to East Coast kids.

Spring Break and Easter Crowds

Between March 1 and April 3 the breaks in our sample come one after another, and Mexico's school break, March 22 to April 3, covers Holy Week, which our best-months guide flags for crowds. In weeks like these our first choice is sunrise, when the sand is quiet and the sea at its calmest. In the Hotel Zone the clubs cluster around Punta Cancun, so look farther along the beach. Our spring break and Easter 2027 guide goes week by week, and our March Break guide covers the Canadian side.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is spring break 2027?
It depends on the school. In the official calendars we checked, Houston ISD is off March 8 to 12, Chicago Public Schools March 22 to 26 and NYC Public Schools April 22 to 30, 2027. In Canada, Ontario's March Break runs March 15 to 19 and Vancouver's spring break March 15 to 25.
When is March Break 2027 in Ontario?
March 15 to 19, 2027. The Toronto District School Board lists those dates as set by Ontario's Ministry of Education. In the Cancun Hotel Zone the sun sets between 6:56 and 6:58 pm that week.
When are Presidents' Day weekend and Family Day in 2027?
Both fall on Monday, February 15, 2027, the third Monday of February. It is Washington's Birthday, the US federal holiday known as Presidents' Day, and Family Day in Ontario, British Columbia and Alberta, so the long weekend runs from Saturday, February 13. In Cancun the sun sets at 6:44 to 6:45 pm.
What is the best time to visit Cancun with kids?
If your school calendar allows it, the winter breaks, from New Year's week to March Break, fall in the stretch our sargassum guide calls the clearest of the year, roughly November to March, and their early sunsets let a photo session end before dinner. Our best-months guide rates February the best month overall. Summer brings the seaweed peak and the heat, and most hurricane activity comes between mid-August and mid-October.
What time is sunset in Cancun during spring break 2027?
In the Cancun Hotel Zone the sun sets at 6:54 pm on March 8, 6:56 pm on March 15 and 7:00 pm on Easter Sunday, March 28, and by April 30 it sets at 7:12 pm. Cancun stays on UTC-5 all year, so these times do not jump when US clocks change on March 14.
Do your photo session prices go up during school breaks?
No. Our six collections cost 295 to 895 USD at a fixed price on every date, holiday weeks included. There is no deposit: you pay at the end of the session, by card or in cash, and cancellation is free until one day before.
