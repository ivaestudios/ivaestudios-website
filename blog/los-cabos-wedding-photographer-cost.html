In short

IVAE Studios publishes its wedding prices: half-day coverage of up to four hours is 1,800 to 2,800 USD, a full day of eight to ten hours with a second photographer is 3,500 to 5,500 USD, and photography plus a wedding film is 5,500 to 8,000 USD. In Los Cabos we travel from Cancún and travel is arranged as part of the coverage. Some resorts charge outside vendors a fee, paid to the resort. The gallery arrives in one to three business days.

Most wedding photographers in Los Cabos ask you to message them for a price. We would rather you see it before you write, so here it is, with the things that move the number up or down.

What Changes the Price

  • Hours. A small ceremony and portraits fit in half a day; a full reception needs the full day.
  • A second photographer. Essential from about 50 guests, so someone is with the couple and someone with the guests.
  • Film. A wedding film adds a videographer and editing time.
  • Several events. A welcome dinner or a day-after session adds coverage across the weekend.

Resort Vendor Fees

Some Los Cabos resorts charge outside wedding vendors an access fee or day pass. It is paid to the resort, not to us, and it changes by property and package. Ask for it in writing before you sign your venue contract. Our resort guides, such as Viceroy, Grand Velas and Montage, explain each property's process.

What You Get

Editorial coverage planned against your sunset, bilingual coordination with the resort, the planner and the officiant, and a gallery in days rather than months. We are based in Cancún and travel to Los Cabos, and we arrive with time to walk your venue in the same light you will have on the day.
