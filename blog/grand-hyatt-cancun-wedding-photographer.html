In short

Grand Hyatt Cancún Beach Resort is set to open in December 2026 in Puerto Cancún, a gated marina community at the start of the Hotel Zone, as the first Grand Hyatt in Cancún and a fully all-inclusive resort. It has 500 rooms, nine pools, a private beach club and about 30,000 square feet of indoor event space, including a Great Ballroom that divides into eight. Sessions start at 295 USD; wedding coverage starts at 1,800 USD.

A new resort is exciting and a little uncertain at the same time: the renderings are beautiful, but nobody has photographed a wedding there yet. This guide collects what Hyatt has announced about Grand Hyatt Cancún, what it means for photos and the questions we would ask before booking a wedding or a session there.

Weddings: What the Venues Suggest

Hyatt describes about 30,000 square feet of flexible indoor event space on a single level, a Great Ballroom that can be divided into eight sections, two breakout rooms and a boardroom, plus terraces, beach venues and poolside settings. That is the profile of a resort that can host both an intimate ceremony and a wedding of well over a hundred guests.

For photos, the combination we would look for is a beach or terrace ceremony in the late afternoon and a ballroom or terrace reception after dark. A single-level event space also makes the night easier to photograph, because guests, speeches and the dance floor stay close together. If your guest list is large, our guide to weddings of 20, 50 or 100 guests explains how coverage changes with size.

Photoshoots: Art, Marina and Beach

Grand Hyatt Cancún opens with an installation by the artist Teresa Solar at the entrance and tiles designed by María Pizarro throughout the property, both of which promise strong, graphic backgrounds for editorial portraits. Every room has a terrace, and some suites have private pools. Puerto Cancún adds a marina, with yachts and sailing trips that make a memorable setting for a couple or a proposal, and the private beach club has its own pool and restaurant.

Puerto Cancún sits at the northern start of the Hotel Zone, on the calm Bahía de Mujeres rather than the open sea, so the water is usually gentle. We would plan the beach part of a session for the golden hour and keep the art and architecture for the softer light just before.

Families

With a Grand Kids Club, nine pools and suites with private pools, the resort is clearly designed for families as well as couples. For a family session we would start at the beach club, where small children can play near calm water, and finish with a group portrait on a terrace. Our tips for photos with toddlers and our family photography page have more.

Questions to Ask Before You Book

  • The opening date, in writing, and what happens to your booking if it moves.
  • Which venue for which size: the capacity of each terrace, beach area and ballroom section.
  • The outside vendor policy for photographers and videographers, and any fee. Our guide to outside photographer fees explains how they usually work.
  • The rain plan for an outdoor ceremony, especially from June to November.
  • Day passes for guests staying elsewhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does Grand Hyatt Cancún open?
Hyatt's announcement of September 15, 2026 says Grand Hyatt Cancún Beach Resort is expected to open in December 2026. Opening dates can move, so confirm yours with the resort.
Where is Grand Hyatt Cancún?
In Puerto Cancún, a private, gated marina community at the northern start of the Hotel Zone, about 25 minutes from Cancún International Airport.
Can you get married at Grand Hyatt Cancún?
The resort has about 30,000 square feet of indoor event space on one level, including a Great Ballroom that divides into eight, plus terraces, beach venues and poolside settings. Ask the events team for wedding packages and capacities.
Is Grand Hyatt Cancún all-inclusive?
Yes. It is the first Grand Hyatt in Cancún and a fully all-inclusive resort, with 14 restaurants and bars, nine pools, a private beach club and a spa with a traditional temazcal.
Can we bring an outside photographer to Grand Hyatt Cancún?
The resort has not published its vendor policy yet. Ask in writing before you book. Staying guests booking a private session usually register the photographer at the front desk; weddings often involve a vendor fee.
How much does a wedding photographer cost at Grand Hyatt Cancún?
IVAE Studios' wedding coverage runs 1,800 to 2,800 USD for a half day, 3,500 to 5,500 USD for a full day with a second shooter and 5,500 to 8,000 USD with a highlight film. Sessions start at 295 USD.
