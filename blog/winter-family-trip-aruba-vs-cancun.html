Aruba or Cancun for a Winter Family Trip?

By Vianey Díaz · Published November 14, 2026 · 8 min read

In short

From December to March, Aruba is warmer at night, much drier from January and windy all day. Cancun's nonstops are shorter from every US airport we checked except Boston, a tie; it shares New York's clock until mid-March and has calmer mornings, with the odd cold front. Neither is in hurricane season then.

We are a Cancun studio, so we are not neutral. This comparison sticks to official numbers, checked on November 14, 2026, then shows how we would plan winter family photos on our side of the Caribbean.

Days tie in the mid to high 80s°F. Nights do not: Cancun's lows run 68 to 71°F (20 to 21°C), while Aruba's stay at 77 to 78°F (25°C). December is closer than Aruba's dry reputation suggests: its weather service puts the rainy season from September to January, with 11 days of at least 1 mm of rain in an average December. From January the gap opens, and Aruba's outlook through March 2027 leans drier and warmer than normal. More on our side in Cancun in December.

Hurricanes and Cold Fronts

Hurricanes are not part of this decision: NOAA's Atlantic season runs June 1 to November 30, so a December to March trip misses it in both places. Aruba's weather service places the island on the southern fringes of the hurricane belt, which matters more in summer and fall.

Cancun's winter variable is the cold front, which Mexico's weather service describes as cold air pushing under warmer air, with clouds that can bring showers and a quick drop in temperature. It forecasts 9 fronts entering Mexico in December, 8 in January, 7 in February and 6 in March, counted nationwide, not for Cancun alone. Our guide to the coolest time to visit Cancun shows a front day and a plan for kids.

The Wind, Hour by Hour

Aruba's weather service describes sustained moderate to fresh easterlies, averaging 24 to 28 km/h (15 to 18 mph) from December to March in its 1991 to 2020 normals. To see when it blows, we averaged NOAA's hourly records at both airports for the five winters from December 2020 to March 2025.

Aruba's calmest hour, around 7 am, still averaged more wind than Cancun's windiest, in mid-afternoon; at that hour, 60% of Cancun's readings were under 10 km/h, against 5% of Aruba's. Airports are not beaches, so read it as a pattern: Aruba's trade wind barely rests, while Cancun's builds by late morning and eases after sunset.

Cancun saves about 15 minutes from New York, an hour from Atlanta, Charlotte or Miami and over two hours from Houston; from Boston it is a tie. In those four months, 41 US airports had at least ten nonstops to Cancun, the West Coast included, and 15 to Aruba, none west of Dallas. The data covers US airports only; from Canada, see Cancun with kids from Canada, and for a third option, Punta Cana or Cancun.

Neither place changes its clocks. Until March 14, 2027, Cancun matches New York and Toronto and Aruba runs an hour ahead; once the US and Canada spring forward, Aruba matches them and Cancun runs an hour behind.

Entry Forms, Fees and Safety

Before you fly to Aruba

Each traveler, children included, needs an online ED card from edcardaruba.aw: one form per person, within 7 days before arrival, with a 20 USD Sustainability Fee per person by credit card; children under 8 are exempt from the fee. Aruba also asks for a passport valid for your stay and a return or onward ticket.

Mexico. Per Canada's travel advice, air arrivals get a passport stamp or use an e-gate kiosk, and complete the digital FMMD form on arrival. Quintana Roo adds its Visitax for every foreign visitor, infants included, paid online.

Safety. On paper, Aruba rates safer: the US State Department puts it at Level 1, exercise normal precautions, and Mexico at Level 2, exercise increased caution, noting that conditions vary widely by state. Canada advises normal precautions in Aruba and a high degree of caution in Mexico, and Quintana Roo is not among its regional advisories. More in our Cancun safety guide for families.

What Kids Can Do

Aruba's tourism authority calls Baby Beach, a shallow half-moon beach on a calm lagoon near San Nicolas, ideal for families with small kids; it is about a 45-minute drive from the Palm Beach hotels, whose water it also calls calm. Most of Aruba's sea turtle nests are on Eagle Beach. Cancun's day trips include cenotes, Maya ruins, the Xcaret parks and the ferry to Isla Mujeres; Cozumel's ferries leave from Playa del Carmen, as our guide to Cozumel or Isla Mujeres explains.

Family Photos: Wind and Light

The coasts face opposite ways. Palm Beach and Eagle Beach line Aruba's western shore, so the sun sets over the water, and the trade wind keeps hair and dresses moving all session: lovely in a few frames, harder with little ones and blowing sand. Aruba's tourism authority even lists windscreens among the Baby Beach rentals.

Cancun's long Hotel Zone beach faces east, so the sun rises out of the sea in what were the calmest hours at the airport. How we would plan a winter session:

Our Verdict, Family by Family

  • End of year and New Year. The weather is close, so let your airport decide: Cancun from the South, Midwest or West; either from New York or Boston, with warmer nights in Aruba.
  • February and March. For the driest trip, Aruba, with about a third of Cancun's February rain and a quarter of its March rain. Pack hair ties: its winter wind peaks then.
  • Babies and toddlers. Cancun for most US families: a shorter flight, the East Coast's clock until mid-March and calmer mornings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Aruba or Cancun better in December?

It is close. December's 1991 to 2020 normals give Aruba 80 mm of rain and Cancun 97 mm, with similar highs and warmer nights in Aruba. Cancun is the shorter flight from most US airports.

Is Aruba safer than Mexico?

On paper, yes. The US State Department rates Aruba Level 1 and Mexico Level 2, with conditions varying by state. Canada advises normal precautions in Aruba and a high degree of caution in Mexico, where Quintana Roo is not among its regional advisories.

Is Aruba windier than Cancun?

Yes. Aruba's normals average 24 to 28 km/h (15 to 18 mph) from December to March. In NOAA's airport records for five winters, Aruba's calmest hour, around 7 am, averaged 21 km/h, against 9 km/h in Cancun.

Do kids need an ED card for Aruba?

Yes. Every traveler needs an online ED card, one form per person, within 7 days before arrival, with a 20 USD Sustainability Fee per person; children under 8 are exempt from the fee.

When should we book winter family photos in Cancun?

Early morning, when the sun rises over the sea and the wind is usually lightest: our online slots at 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am fall around sunrise all winter. You can cancel free until a day before.
