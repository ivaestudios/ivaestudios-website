Spring Break and Easter 2027 in Cancun With Kids: Week by Week

In short

In 2027, spring break in Cancun comes in waves. In the official calendars we checked, college breaks fall in the weeks of March 8, 15 and 22, and Ontario's March Break runs March 15 to 19. The week of March 22, Holy Week, stacks the most breaks, including the start of Mexico's school break (March 22 to April 3). After Easter Sunday, March 28, only schools are off in our sample. For a quieter stay, families look away from Punta Cancun.

Spring break is not one week in Cancun. Universities, school districts, Canadian provinces and Mexico each pick their own, and in 2027 most land between March 8 and April 4. We lined them up from official calendars, then added where to stay and when the light is best for your family photos.

Every date below comes from the school's or university's own published calendar, checked on November 14, 2026. It is a sample, not a complete list, so check your own school's calendar before you book.

On paper, Holy Week is the fullest week: two universities, three school systems in the US and Canada and Mexico's school break all land on it. The week after Easter, with no college on the list, is the most family-centered of the four.

For scale: in 2026 the City of Cancun counted about 30,000 spring-break visitors, above the state tourism board's forecast of 21,000, with hotel occupancy over 90 percent. Book flights, rooms and your photo session early.

Holy Week and Mexico's School Break

Easter comes early in 2027: Palm Sunday is March 21, Good Friday March 26, Easter Sunday March 28 and Easter Monday March 29. Mexico's Secretariat of Public Education (SEP) has set the spring school break from March 22 to April 3, the two weeks Mexicans call Semana Santa and Pascua. In Toronto, Good Friday and Easter Monday are both school holidays, a four-day weekend.

For Holy Week 2026, the City of Cancun said it expected a high influx of visitors and readied its public beaches, Playa Delfines among them, for visitors and locals alike, with 45 lifeguards on duty from 9 am to 7 pm.

So in these two weeks we suggest a family session on your resort's own beach or in its gardens, or at sunrise, long before the lifeguards start their day. We love Playa Delfines for photos, but over Semana Santa and Pascua we suggest it only at sunrise; our guide to where to photograph in Cancun covers Delfines and the quieter beaches north and south of the city.

Where Families Stay Away From the Party

Cancun's nightlife centers on Punta Cancun, the bend in the Hotel Zone around kilometer 9, where Coco Bongo and other clubs stand. For a quieter week with children, look farther away:

  • Costa Mujeres and Playa Mujeres, the resort corridor north of the city; see our Costa Mujeres family session guide and Costa Mujeres or Riviera Cancun with kids.
  • Riviera Cancun, the resort corridor south of the airport toward Puerto Morelos, not the Riviera Maya around Playa del Carmen; see what Riviera Cancun is.
  • The south end of the Hotel Zone, well past Punta Cancun, facing the open Caribbean. Playa Delfines is one of its public beaches, so plan around Mexico's school break.
  • Playacar, the resort area at the south end of Playa del Carmen; compare it in Playa del Carmen or Cancun as your family base.

Ask your resort two things before you book: whether it expects student groups on your dates, and its rule for outside photographers. Some hotels charge a fee for one, paid by the guest to the hotel; we check your resort's rule before your session. Our guide to where to stay in Cancun with kids compares every area.

Traveling with another family? We have no limit on people and no per-person charge, so two families can share one session; two families, one photo session explains how we share the hour.

Sunrise, Sunset and Your Photo Session

Across March 2027, sunrise moves from 7:07 to 6:41 am and sunset from 6:51 to 7:01 pm. From Palm Sunday to the Sunday after Easter (March 21 to April 4), the sun rises between 6:50 and 6:38 am and sets between 6:58 and 7:03 pm. These are our calculations with NOAA's solar method for the Hotel Zone (21.135 N, 86.748 W), and the sunrise and sunset times match the US Naval Observatory's.

Golden light means the sun sits less than 6 degrees above the horizon. Online booking at ivaestudios.com/book offers five start times, 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am and 5:00 and 5:30 pm. In these weeks:

  • 6:30 am. From mid-March this start begins in the blue light before sunrise, and the sun comes up during your session (6:49 am on March 22).
  • 7:00 am. You meet the sunrise while the beach is still quiet, with golden light until 7:09 to 7:31 am, depending on the date. It is our pick for the busiest weeks.
  • Golden hour. We start about an hour before sunset, near 6:00 pm, so the session ends as the sun goes down. That start is not an online time, so we arrange it on WhatsApp; the 5:00 and 5:30 pm starts work too, in brighter light.

On the Caribbean side the sun rises over the sea in front of you and sets behind the hotels; see our sunset and golden hour table for every month, and why sunrise beats sunset with young kids.

Cancun does not change its clocks: Quintana Roo stays on UTC-5 all year, while US clocks spring forward on March 14, 2027. From then on, Cancun is one hour behind New York and on the same clock as Chicago and Houston, so 7:00 am here feels like 8:00 am to East Coast kids. Our March Break guide covers the Canadian side.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is spring break in Cancun in 2027?
There is no single week. In the official calendars we checked, college breaks run from March 7 to 13 (Penn State) to March 20 to 28 (UW-Madison), Ontario's March Break is March 15 to 19, and Mexican schools are off from March 22 to April 3. Easter Sunday is March 28.
Is Cancun crowded during spring break?
It is busy. In 2026 the City of Cancun counted about 30,000 spring-break visitors and hotel occupancy above 90 percent. In our 2027 sample, the week of March 22, Holy Week, stacks the most college and school breaks.
When is Semana Santa 2027, and are Mexican schools off?
Holy Week runs from Palm Sunday, March 21, to Easter Sunday, March 28, 2027. Mexico's SEP calendar closes schools from March 22 to April 3, covering Holy Week and the week after Easter.
Where should families stay in Cancun during spring break?
Away from Punta Cancun, the nightclub stretch of the Hotel Zone: Costa Mujeres to the north, Riviera Cancun south of the airport, the south end of the Hotel Zone or Playacar in Playa del Carmen.
What time are sunrise and sunset in Cancun in March 2027?
Sunrise moves from 7:07 am on March 1 to 6:41 am on March 31, and sunset from 6:51 to 7:01 pm. From March 21 to April 4, sunrise is between 6:50 and 6:38 am and sunset between 6:58 and 7:03 pm.
Do photo session prices go up during spring break?
Not with us. Our collections cost the same every week, from 295 USD, with no deposit and free cancellation until one day before. For families we recommend The Chapter: 60 minutes and 35 edited photos for 475 USD.
