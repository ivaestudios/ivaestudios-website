How Much Does a Wedding Videographer Cost in Cancún? (2026)

In short

In Cancún, film is usually priced alongside photography. Our published full-day photography runs 3,500 to 5,500 USD, and the same day with a cinematic highlight film runs 5,500 to 8,000 USD, so the film itself sits in the 2,000 to 2,500 USD band for a full day. What moves it: hours, a second camera, drone, whether you want a highlight film or a full-length edit, and how fast you need it.

The question arrives almost every week, usually phrased the same way: how much should we budget for video, and is it worth it? Here is the honest answer from a Cancún studio that shoots both, with the numbers we actually publish.

What moves the price of a wedding film

Wedding video is priced on five things, and once you see them the quotes stop looking random. Hours of coverage is the first: a ceremony and golden hour is a different job from a full day with getting ready, first look, ceremony, portraits and the party. The second is crew. One operator can cover a ceremony; a reception with a first dance, toasts and the whole dance floor needs a second camera or you lose half the story. Third is drone. Cancún resorts on the beach photograph beautifully from the air, but drone work needs the right permissions at each property and adds time. Fourth is the edit you want, which we cover below. Fifth is delivery speed: a highlight film in weeks is standard in the industry; a same-week turnaround is not, and it costs more because it takes an editor off everything else.

Our published numbers

We publish our ranges because most studios on this coast do not, and couples end up negotiating blind. Photography for a half day, up to four hours built around the ceremony and golden hour, runs 1,800 to 2,800 USD. A full day, eight to ten hours with a second shooter and venue scouting, runs 3,500 to 5,500 USD. The same full day with a cinematic highlight film added runs 5,500 to 8,000 USD. Read the last two together and the film sits in the 2,000 to 2,500 USD band for a full day. Every collection includes the complete edited gallery in one to three business days through a private 4K gallery. We do not publish a film-only price, because we shoot film alongside photography; if you want video only, send the date and venue and you get a real number the same business day.

Highlight film vs full-length edit

A highlight film is three to six minutes: the vows, the first look, the light, the party, cut to music. It is what couples actually rewatch and share. A full-length edit is the ceremony and the speeches in their entirety, usually thirty to sixty minutes, and it exists so nothing is lost. Most couples marrying in Cancún want the highlight film first and the full edit as an archive. Ask which one a quote includes, because the word film means both, and the difference in editing hours is large.

Resort videographers and outside vendor fees

Most all-inclusive resorts in Cancún and the Riviera Maya have an in-house video vendor and charge an outside vendor fee if you bring your own. The fee varies by property and it is worth asking for it in writing before you decide, because it changes the math: sometimes the in-house option is genuinely competitive for a short ceremony edit, and sometimes the fee is small next to the difference in the final film. We work as an outside vendor at properties across the coast and handle that arrangement with the resort directly.

Is video worth it for a destination wedding?

The honest answer is that it depends on who is not there. Destination weddings are small by design, and the guests who could not travel are the ones who watch the film. Couples who skipped video rarely regret it in the first month; the regret arrives around the first anniversary, when the voices matter more than the frames. If the budget forces a choice, we tell couples to protect photography first and add a highlight film only if it does not compromise the hours of coverage.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a wedding videographer cost in Cancún?

Priced alongside photography at our studio: a full day with a cinematic highlight film runs 5,500 to 8,000 USD in 2026, against 3,500 to 5,500 USD for photography alone, so the film adds roughly 2,000 to 2,500 USD for a full day.

Do you offer video without photography?

We shoot film alongside photography and do not publish a film-only price. Send the date and venue and you get a real number the same business day.

How long does the wedding film take to deliver?

Photo galleries arrive in one to three business days. Ask about film timing when you inquire, because the edit depends on the length you choose.

Do resorts charge a fee for an outside videographer?

Many do. Ask the coordinator for the outside vendor fee in writing before you compare quotes. We handle that arrangement with the property directly.

Highlight film or full-length edit?

A highlight film is three to six minutes and is what couples rewatch. A full-length edit keeps the whole ceremony and speeches. Confirm which one a quote includes.
