In short

The St. Regis Costa Mujeres opened in late June 2026 as one of the most refined resorts on this shore, with about 163 rooms and suites and no all-inclusive plan. It is quiet and new, and it faces the calm water of Costa Mujeres. Our sessions start at 295 USD, and the gallery arrives in one to three business days.

A very new, low-key luxury resort is a rare thing on this coast of large all-inclusives. With so few rooms on the sand, it should feel calm and unhurried. Since the resort has only just opened, we confirm the rules for each space before your date.

Seaweed and Weather at St. Regis Costa Mujeres

Sargassum usually builds from April and is heaviest from June to August, and November to March is normally the clearest stretch. This shore has an advantage over the Riviera Maya, because the seaweed comes from the southeast and Isla Mujeres gives St. Regis Costa Mujeres some shelter. It is a probability, not a promise, since the heaviest years have reached almost everywhere on this coast. Our month-by-month sargassum guide has the detail and the outlook for the season. Summer and early autumn bring afternoon showers that tend to pass quickly. We keep an eye on the forecast and adjust the hour with you the day before.

Bringing a Photographer to St. Regis Costa Mujeres

For guests of St. Regis Costa Mujeres there is no complicated process: register the photographer with the front desk a day or two ahead and we check in on arrival. Hotels set their own rules for outside vendors and revise them, so we get the current answer in writing for your date. Staying elsewhere usually means a day pass. Frequently Asked Questions

Can we bring an outside photographer to St. Regis Costa Mujeres?
Usually yes, as your guest: give the front desk the photographer's name and the session time in advance. Each resort sets its own rules for outside vendors and changes them, so we confirm the current policy with St. Regis Costa Mujeres in writing before your date. The beach itself is public.
Where are the best photo spots at St. Regis Costa Mujeres?
We work with The beach, The new architecture and Your suite. We choose the order by the light on your date and how busy each area is at that hour.
What is the best time for photos at St. Regis Costa Mujeres?
The hour before sunset for most families and couples, or sunrise with children under five. Sunset here ranges from 6:06 pm in November to 7:32 pm in July, and sunrise from 6:05 am in June to 7:27 am in January.
How much does a photographer at St. Regis Costa Mujeres cost?
IVAE Studios collections run from 295 USD for 35 minutes and 15 photos to 895 USD for 90 minutes and the full gallery. Most families choose The Chapter at 475 USD. Galleries arrive in one to three business days.
Is there sargassum at St. Regis Costa Mujeres?
Sargassum usually builds from April and is heaviest from June to August, and November to March is normally the clearest stretch. This shore has an advantage over the Riviera Maya, because the seaweed comes from the southeast and Isla Mujeres gives St. Regis Costa Mujeres some shelter. It is a probability, not a promise, since the heaviest years have reached almost everywhere on this coast. We check conditions close to your date and plan around them.
Is The St. Regis Costa Mujeres all-inclusive?
No. Reports describe it as a European plan, with rooms booked separately from meals. It is a different property from St. Regis Kanai, further south in the Riviera Maya.
