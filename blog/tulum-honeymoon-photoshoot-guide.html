Here is where a session usually happens, what the ruins and the national park allow, when to start, which collection fits and what it costs. Every third-party rule, fee and schedule below was checked on an official source on November 15, 2026.

Where honeymoon sessions usually happen

Usually on your own hotel or resort beach. On a honeymoon it is the easy choice: there is no transfer to arrange, and if your collection includes a second look, your room is close by for the change. The other option is a place you choose. Either way, send us the name of your hotel with your dates, because it decides which stretch of sand is yours, whether a hotel fee applies and whether the park rules below apply too.

The ruins and the national park: what the rules say

INAH's page for the Tulum archaeological zone, updated August 10, 2026, lists hours of 8:00 am to 5:00 pm, last entry 3:30 pm, and tickets of 265 pesos for foreign visitors and 80 pesos for Mexican nationals, fees that include the CONANP and/or Grupo Mundo Maya charges. Those hours begin after sunrise and end before sunset every day of the year. Taking photographs, filming or recording video with special or professional equipment at INAH zones, monuments and museums also requires an authorization, procedure INAH 01-001.

Visitors reach the archaeological zone through the Parque Nacional Tulum, a protected natural area whose 2024 management program, published by CONANP, notes that the walled zone itself lies outside the park's boundary. Inside the park, the program requires an authorization from SEMARNAT, through CONANP, for photography or filming with commercial purposes (rule 20), and for activities at archaeological sites it requires INAH's authorization first (rule 25). Day visitors may enter from 8:00 am to 5:00 pm and stay on the public beaches until 7:00 pm (rule 16). The program also notes businesses offering lodging, food and drinks inside the park, so it is worth knowing whether your hotel is one of them.

Our suggestion: visit the ruins together on your own, early in the day, and keep the session for your hotel beach at the right hour. For the paperwork in more depth, read INAH photo permits at Tulum and Chichen Itza.

When to start, month by month

Start right after sunrise or about an hour before sunset. The light is softer then, and it matches what Mexico's Health Ministry advised on May 14, 2026: avoid the sun during its most intense hours, mainly between 11:00 am and 5:00 pm, and do outdoor activities early in the morning or late in the afternoon.

Online booking at ivaestudios.com/book offers 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am and 5:00 and 5:30 pm; for any other start, message us on WhatsApp. One morning slot begins within about half an hour after sunrise all year. In the evening, from October to January, one online slot starts about 45 to 75 minutes before sunset; from February to September the sun sets later, between about 6:40 and 7:35 pm, so ask us for a later start.

Plan for heat and wind as well. Bring water, and on a warm evening a 35-minute Postcard is easy to enjoy. If it is windy, hair will move, so keep a hair tie in a pocket. For seaweed, CONABIO's satellite sargassum early warning system publishes a seven-day forecast of sargassum drift and expected arrivals on the Quintana Roo coast, updated daily and labeled experimental. And if the forecast for your day looks poor, cancellation is free up to one day before.

Planning around a resort vendor fee

Some hotels charge a fee for an outside photographer. If yours does, you pay it to the hotel, not to us, and we confirm your hotel's rule before the session. Three habits keep it simple:

  • Put the name of your hotel in your first message, next to your dates.
  • Budget the fee apart from the collection price, since it goes to the hotel.
  • If the fee changes your plans, pick another place for the session, with the park rules above in mind.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a honeymoon photo session in Tulum cost?

IVAE Studios collections have fixed prices in USD, from 295 USD for The Postcard (35 minutes, 15 edited photos, made for couples) to 895 USD for The Heirloom (90 minutes, the complete edited gallery). The Story (545 USD) and The Editorial (645 USD) add a second look. There is no deposit: you pay at the end of the session, by card or cash.

What time should our Tulum honeymoon session start?

Right after sunrise or about an hour before sunset. By our calculation for the 15th of each month in 2027, Tulum's sunrise runs from about 6:10 am in June to 7:30 am in January, and sunset from about 6:10 pm in November to 7:35 pm in July. Online start times are 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am and 5:00 and 5:30 pm; for other times, message us on WhatsApp.

Can we take our honeymoon photos at the Tulum ruins?

INAH lists the archaeological zone as open from 8:00 am to 5:00 pm, last entry 3:30 pm, which misses sunrise and sunset, and photography with special or professional equipment at INAH sites needs an authorization (procedure INAH 01-001), checked November 15, 2026. We suggest visiting the ruins on your own and photographing on your hotel beach.

Does our Tulum hotel charge a fee for an outside photographer?

Some hotels do. If yours does, you pay the fee to the hotel, not to IVAE Studios, and we confirm your hotel's rule before the session. Send us the name of your hotel with your dates.

When do we receive our honeymoon photos?

A preview arrives within 24 hours and the full gallery in 1 to 3 business days, in a private 4K gallery with unlimited downloads and print rights. Book the session early in your stay and you can share the preview before you leave Tulum.
