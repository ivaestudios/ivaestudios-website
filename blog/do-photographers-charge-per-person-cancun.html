Some do. Groups in Cancun meet five pricing models: per adult with a child rate, per guest, packages capped by headcount, per photo, and one price per session. Ask three photographers to quote a family of sixteen and you can get three kinds of answer: a price per adult, a price per guest, and a package that depends on how many people turn up. None of them counts the same thing. So on November 14, 2026, we read the public listings a group meets in Cancun and priced one example family wherever a price was published: ten adults and six children, one beach session.

Five Ways Photographers Price a Large Group

Before comparing totals, look at what each quote multiplies.

  • Per adult, with a child rate. Found on tour and activity sites, a hotel loyalty program's among them, where a photoshoot is sold like an excursion ticket.
  • Per guest. Some marketplace packages for large groups charge for every person in the frame.
  • Capped by headcount. Each package admits a maximum number of people, and people above the limit cost extra.
  • Per photo. You see the images, then pay for each one you keep.
  • One price per session. Time and photos set the price, whatever the headcount. This is how we work.

Per adult, with a child rate

A private “luxury photoshoot” on a hotel loyalty program's activities site lists 150 USD per adult and 45 USD per child aged 2 to 17, for 1 hour to 90 minutes, meeting at a public beach. Our group comes to 1,770 USD for 20 high-resolution images per group plus all RAW files. Editing may take up to 7 days, gratuities are not included, and all sales are final, with no refund for cancellations. Every person adds to the total; the gallery stays at 20 images.

Per guest

A “large group package” from a Cancun-based photographer on a vacation-rental platform's services marketplace lists 101 USD per guest for 90 minutes, described as ideal for groups of 12 or more, with a personalized quote on request. At that rate, 16 guests come to 1,616 USD. It does not state a photo count.

Capped by headcount

A global vacation-photography marketplace uses one price table everywhere, Cancun included: 30 minutes for up to 6 people (325 USD, 20 photos), 60 minutes for up to 10 (425 USD, 35), 90 minutes for up to 15 (590 USD, 50) and 2 hours for 16 or more (715 USD, 65). If more people arrive than booked and the group passes its package's limit, each extra person costs 75 USD while time and photos stay the same: book 90 minutes for 15, bring 16, and you pay 665 USD for 50 photos. Galleries arrive within 5 business days. Refunds are 90% up to 15 days before, 50% from 15 to 7 days and none after, except when weather makes the shoot impossible, and some photographers add travel fees. More in marketplace versus booking a studio directly.

Per photo

Paying per image suits a couple; in a group of sixteen, every household wants its own set. On the resort pages we read on November 14, 2026, we found no per-image prices, so ask the photo desk for its price list before anyone poses. One resort chain with a Cancun resort offers a package with all the roaming photos linked to one reservation, immediate family only. One Cancun studio lists 15 USD per photo with a 10-photo minimum, for one or two people; groups book its packages instead.

What Else a Big Group Should Budget

Two costs can sit outside the collection price:

  • The hotel's fee for outside photographers. Some hotels charge one; you pay it to the hotel, and we confirm your resort's rule beforehand. Our guide to a resort photographer or an outside photographer lists what to ask.
  • Transportation. For groups of up to 16, we can arrange a full van to take everyone to the session location; ask us for its price. See our photo session with pickup for groups.

Terms That Matter With Sixteen People

With a big group, the fine print weighs as much as the total.

  • No deposit, so nobody has to gather money from several households months ahead; you pay at the end of the session, by card or in cash.
  • Free cancellation until one day before.
  • A preview within 24 hours, so the first photos can be in the family chat by the next day.
  • The private 4K gallery in 1 to 3 business days, with unlimited downloads and print rights: every household downloads its own photos instead of buying images one by one. For sizes and copies, see large family portrait prints for every home.

How to Compare Two Group Quotes

Whether you call it extended family photos or family reunion photography, comparing prices comes down to five questions. Ask every photographer, in writing:

  1. The total for your exact headcount, children included.
  2. How many edited photos, at what resolution.
  3. How many minutes of photography.
  4. When the gallery arrives, and what you may do with the files.
  5. Cancellation terms and every extra fee: travel, extra people, the hotel.

Then divide each total by your headcount and by the edited photos; together, the two answers say more than either price. For the rest of the trip, see family reunion cost per person in Cancun.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do photographers charge per person?

Some do. On November 14, 2026, Cancun listings we read charged 150 USD per adult and 45 USD per child, or 101 USD per guest, and one marketplace charges 75 USD for each person above a package's limit. Other studios, ours included, charge one price per session.

How much are extended family photos in Cancun for 16 people?

For 10 adults and 6 children, the per-person and headcount listings we read on November 14, 2026 came to 715 to 1,770 USD. Our Editorial is 645 USD for 70 edited photos, about 40 USD per person; The Heirloom, with the complete edited gallery, is 895 USD, about 56 USD per person.

Do children count toward the price of a group photo session?

It depends on the model. One listing we read charged 45 USD per child aged 2 to 17, and a per-guest package charges for every guest. With us, children do not change the price, because there is no per-person charge.

How much do photographers charge per photo?

On November 14, 2026, one Cancun studio listed 15 USD per photo with a 10-photo minimum, for one or two people, and the resort pages we read showed no per-image prices. Our Editorial works out to about 9 USD per edited photo.

Are there extra costs for a big group with IVAE Studios?

No per-person charge and no deposit: you pay at the end of the session, by card or in cash, and cancellation is free until one day before.
