In short

Los Cabos is one of the shortest trips to a Mexican beach from Western Canada, with direct winter flights from cities such as Calgary, Edmonton, Vancouver, Toronto and Montreal, and in winter it keeps the same clock as Alberta. The dry, sunny season runs roughly November through April, which is also whale season. Getting There

Los Cabos International (SJD) has direct winter service from several Canadian cities, with WestJet and Air Canada as the main carriers and other airlines adding seasonal routes from cities such as Toronto, Ottawa, Edmonton and Montreal. Western Canada is the shortest trip. Routes and frequencies change every season, so check the current schedule before you set a date, and give guests a few airports to choose from.

From the airport, San José del Cabo and the Puerto Los Cabos resorts are 15 to 25 minutes away, the Tourist Corridor 25 to 35 minutes, and Cabo San Lucas or the East Cape around 45 minutes. Book private transfers for the wedding party.

Same Clock as Alberta

Baja California Sur keeps Pacific time all year, UTC-7, with no daylight saving. In winter that is exactly Alberta's time, so a Calgary or Edmonton family lands with no time change at all. Vancouver is one hour behind Los Cabos in winter, and Toronto and Montreal are two hours ahead. After Canada changes its clocks on March 14, 2027, Alberta moves one hour ahead of Los Cabos.

Legal or Symbolic Ceremony

A legally binding marriage in Mexico is a civil act performed by a local registry official, with document translations, witnesses and a schedule set by the registry. Many Canadian couples instead sign the legal paperwork at home before they fly and hold a symbolic ceremony in Los Cabos, which gives complete freedom over the time, the place and the officiant.

If you do marry legally in Mexico, the marriage is valid in Canada, and the step to remember is the apostille. Mexico and Canada are both parties to the Hague Apostille Convention, so an apostilled Mexican marriage certificate, with a certified English or French translation, is what you present at home. Have your planner arrange both before you leave Mexico. Our Canadian guide to a Cancún wedding covers this in more detail.

Budgeting in USD

Los Cabos resorts and most wedding vendors price in US dollars, so your real cost moves with the Canadian dollar between deposit and final payment. Confirm the currency of every quote in writing, ask whether the rate is locked at deposit, and keep a cushion for exchange drift. A no-foreign-fee credit card saves a little on every payment. Our Mexico wedding cost breakdown has real numbers.

The Best Months

November through April is the dry, sunny stretch in Los Cabos, with warm days and cool evenings, and it is peak season for both weddings and resort rates. It is also whale season: humpback whales pass along the coast in winter, and on some days you can see them from the beach. Summer is hotter and more humid, and late summer into fall is the hurricane season to plan around.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Canadians get legally married in Los Cabos?
Yes. A civil marriage in Mexico is valid in Canada. Bring home the Mexican certificate with an apostille and a certified English or French translation. Many couples sign legally at home and hold a symbolic ceremony in Cabo.
Is Los Cabos on the same time as Calgary?
In winter, yes. Los Cabos is UTC-7 all year, the same as Mountain Standard Time. After Canada changes its clocks on March 14, 2027, Calgary is one hour ahead until November.
What is the best month for a Los Cabos wedding?
November through April is dry and sunny, with whale season in winter. It is also peak season, so book venues and vendors early.
