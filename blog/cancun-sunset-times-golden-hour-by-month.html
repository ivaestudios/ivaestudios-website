In short

In 2027 the sun sets in Cancun between 6:04 pm, around late November, and 7:33 pm, in early July. It rises between 6:05 am, in early June, and 7:27 am, in mid January. Quintana Roo stays on UTC-5 all year, with no daylight saving time since 2015. Evening golden hour lasts 29 to 33 minutes on the 15th of each month. The Caribbean beaches face east, so the sun sets behind the hotels; to watch it go down over water, go to Nichupté Lagoon.

When we plan a session in Cancun, the first thing we look at is the light on your dates. Here it is for every month of 2027: when the sun comes up, when the soft light starts and ends, where the sun actually goes down, and how we turn those minutes into family, couples and proposal photos.

Sunset and Golden Hour by Month

These are the times for the Cancun Hotel Zone on the 15th of each month of 2027, calculated with the NOAA solar algorithm for 21.135 N, 86.748 W, in Cancun time (UTC-5). In other years the same dates shift by about a minute or two.

We count golden light as the time when the sun sits less than 6 degrees above the horizon. In the morning it runs from sunrise until the sun climbs past 6 degrees; in the evening, golden hour starts when the sun drops below 6 degrees and ends at sunset.

Less than an hour and a half separates the earliest and latest sunsets of the year, but that is enough to matter when you plan around dinner, a toddler's bedtime or a surprise.

Where the Sun Actually Sets

Cancun's Caribbean beaches face east: the sun comes up out of the sea and goes down behind the hotels and the lagoon. If you pictured it sinking into the turquoise water, this is the part to know.

On the beach at sunset, the color happens in the sky behind the hotels while the sea turns cool and soft. Because the hotels stand between the sand and the setting sun, the beach in front of them can fall into shade before sunset, and in winter, when the sun travels lower across the sky, that happens earlier in the afternoon. It is one reason we choose the spot for your date, not just the hour.

To watch the sun go down over water, cross the boulevard to Nichupté Lagoon. It faces west, with calm water that reflects the sky and mangroves on the far shore. If you are staying on the sea side, we go to a public spot on the lagoon a short drive away. Our guide to sunset photos at Nichupté Lagoon has the details.

Mornings work the other way around. From Punta Cancun south, the open Caribbean faces the sunrise directly, while the north shore beaches get a softer sunrise from the side. For the sun itself rising out of the sea behind you, morning is the only option on these beaches. See sunrise photos in the Cancun Hotel Zone.

Through the year the sunset also moves along the horizon, toward the southwest in December and January and toward the northwest in June and July, which changes what sits behind you in the frame.

What Time to Start Your Session

The best time for beach sunset photos is the last hour of the day. For an evening session we usually start about an hour before sunset, so the session ends in golden hour: our collections run from 35 to 90 minutes, and a 60-minute session such as The Chapter, the one we recommend for families, finishes as the sun goes down.

  • November to January. Sunset falls at 6:06, 6:08 and 6:26 pm on the 15th, so the 5:00 and 5:30 pm starts sit close to golden hour, and the 7:00 and 7:30 am starts sit around sunrise (6:58, 7:16 and 7:27 am on the same dates).
  • February to April, September and October. Sunset moves between 6:23 pm (October) and 7:06 pm (April). In October, 5:30 pm is just under an hour before sunset; in the other months, aim for about an hour before your month's sunset and message us if that is not an online time.
  • May to August. The sun sets between 7:17 and 7:32 pm, so a 5:30 pm start would come close to two hours early. For a summer sunset session, message us on WhatsApp for a later start. Mornings are calm and cooler: the sun is up between 6:05 and 6:26 am, and a 6:30 am start still catches the end of the morning golden light.

A Note on Cancun Time

Quintana Roo stays on UTC-5 all year and has not used daylight saving time since 2015, so sunrise and sunset only move with the seasons, with no jump in spring or fall. The state moved to the Southeast Zone in February 2015, and Mexico's current time zone law keeps it on the 75th meridian west, with seasonal clock changes only along the northern border. In practice, Cancun shares the clock of the US East Coast in winter and runs one hour behind it while the US is on daylight saving time, from the second Sunday of March to the first Sunday of November.

If an older forum post or guidebook gives you different sunset times, check when it was written; this page assumes today's all-year UTC-5. Cancun is two hours ahead of Los Cabos all year, and if your trip takes in the Pacific too, we keep a month-by-month sunset table for Los Cabos as well.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time is sunset in Cancun?
It depends on the month. In 2027 the earliest sunset is 6:04 pm, around late November, and the latest is 7:33 pm, in early July. On December 15 the sun sets at 6:08 pm and on June 15 at 7:30 pm. In other years these times shift by about a minute or two.
What time are sunrise and sunset in Cancun in December and January?
On December 15, 2027 the sun rises at 7:16 am and sets at 6:08 pm. On January 15 it rises at 7:27 am, the latest sunrise of the year, and sets at 6:26 pm.
Does Cancun change its clocks for daylight saving time?
No. Quintana Roo stays on UTC-5 all year and has not used daylight saving time since 2015, so sunrise and sunset only move with the seasons.
What time is golden hour in Cancun, and how long does it last?
Evening golden hour starts when the sun drops below 6 degrees above the horizon and ends at sunset. On the 15th of each month in 2027 it lasts 29 to 33 minutes: for example, 5:35 to 6:08 pm on December 15 and 6:58 to 7:30 pm on June 15.
Can you see the sunset over the ocean in Cancun?
Not from the Caribbean beaches of the Hotel Zone. They face east, so the sun goes down behind the hotels. Nichupté Lagoon, across the boulevard, faces west, and that is where you can watch the sun set over water.
What time should a sunset photo session start in Cancun?
We usually start about an hour before sunset, so the session ends in golden hour. Online booking offers 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am and 5:00 and 5:30 pm, Cancun time. For a later evening start, which summer sunsets call for, message us on WhatsApp.
