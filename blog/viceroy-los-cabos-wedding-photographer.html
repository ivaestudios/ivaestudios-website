In short

Viceroy Los Cabos is one of the most architectural wedding settings in Los Cabos: white cantilevered towers by Miguel Ángel Aragonés rising from still reflecting pools on the beachfront of San José del Cabo. It photographs best in the last hour of light and again at blue hour, when the white walls and the water hold the color of the sky. Wedding coverage starts at 1,800 USD; plan the ceremony about 90 minutes before sunset.

Most Los Cabos resorts sell the view. Viceroy sells the building, and the building is extraordinary: stark white volumes, long reflecting pools and the Sea of Cortez behind them. For a photographer that means clean lines, strong geometry and a wedding that looks like an editorial spread with very little styling. It also means the light has to be right, because white walls show every harsh shadow.

Viceroy Los Cabos at a Glance

  • WhereHotel zone of San José del Cabo, on the Sea of Cortez
  • DesignMiguel Ángel Aragonés; the resort first opened as Mar Adentro
  • RoomsAbout 190 rooms and suites
  • Signature lookWhite towers over still reflecting pools
  • BeachWide sand with strong currents; the pools are for swimming
  • AirportAbout 20 minutes from Los Cabos International (SJD)

The resort sits on the beachfront of San José del Cabo, the quieter, older town of Los Cabos, a few minutes from its plaza, galleries and mission church. For Catholic couples, the resort's wedding team notes that the ceremony can be held at the church in downtown San José with the celebration back at the resort.

Photographing the Architecture

Viceroy's white towers and pools are the reason couples choose it, and we build the gallery around them. Symmetry works here: a couple centered between two volumes, reflected in the pool, with the sea as a strip of blue at the end. So does scale, with small figures against a large white wall. We save close portraits for the softer corners of the gardens and the beach.

Blue hour, the 20 minutes after sunset, is Viceroy's secret. The pools and the walls pick up the color of the sky and the lights of the resort, and a few frames then are often the most striking of the night.

Ceremony Settings

Viceroy's wedding team offers ceremonies on the beach and on the resort's terraces and lawns, with dinners by the water or around the pools. A private ceremony is tied to a minimum block of rooms that depends on the date and the size of the wedding, so ask for it in writing early. For photographs, a ceremony facing the sea with the architecture behind your guests gives both of Viceroy's looks in one frame.

We plan ceremonies to start about 90 minutes before sunset, so that family photos and portraits fall in the warm last light. At Viceroy that means a ceremony around 4:05 pm in December and 5:35 pm in June. The beach faces the Sea of Cortez to the south, so the sun goes down over the land to the west; the light on the white walls in that last hour is soft and warm rather than direct.

Outside Photographers

Like most luxury resorts in Los Cabos, Viceroy coordinates outside wedding vendors through its wedding or events manager, and some resorts here charge outside vendors an access fee. Ask for the current rule and any fee in writing before you sign. We send our insurance and paperwork to the resort ourselves and walk the property ahead of the day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you get married at Viceroy Los Cabos?
Yes. The resort hosts ceremonies on the beach and on its terraces and lawns, with a dedicated wedding manager. A private ceremony requires a minimum room block that depends on the date and size.
What is the best time for wedding photos at Viceroy Los Cabos?
The last hour before sunset and the blue hour right after it, when the white architecture and the reflecting pools take on the color of the sky. Plan the ceremony about 90 minutes before sunset.
Can you swim at the beach at Viceroy Los Cabos?
The beach in San José del Cabo is known for strong currents, so the resort's pools are the place to swim. For photos, the waterline is beautiful, but we keep you on the sand.
How much is a wedding photographer at Viceroy Los Cabos?
IVAE Studios wedding coverage starts at 1,800 to 2,800 USD for up to four hours, with full days from 3,500 USD. The gallery arrives in one to three business days.
