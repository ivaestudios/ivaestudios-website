In short

Park Hyatt Riviera Maya is accepting reservations for stays from February 15, 2027. It will be the Park Hyatt brand's first all-inclusive, with 148 rooms of 52 to 214 square meters, next to Waldorf Astoria Cancún on the coast south of the airport. Its opening has already moved twice, so for a wedding or honeymoon, plan with a written backup. Sessions start at 245 USD.

Few openings on this coast have been awaited as long as Park Hyatt's. Planned first for 2025, then 2026, it is now taking reservations for February 2027. If you are dreaming of a honeymoon, a proposal or an intimate wedding there, here is what has been announced, what it suggests for photos and how to protect your plans while the resort is still under construction.

Details are from One Mile at a Time's report on the opening, which also describes a Beach Club Grill with seafood and Yucatecan flavors, an all-day restaurant, a steakhouse, a pool bar and a patisserie. The same report puts early cash rates at about $1,500 a night.

A Small Resort on a Big Coast

With 148 rooms, Park Hyatt will be one of the smaller resorts on a coast where 400 or 500 rooms is common, and smaller usually means quieter paths, more private beach areas and staff who remember your name. For photos, that is a gift: fewer people in the background and more freedom to wander.

Its neighbor, Waldorf Astoria Cancún, sits on the coast south of Cancún's airport, on the stretch between the Hotel Zone and Puerto Morelos. That coast faces east to the open Caribbean, so the sun rises over the sea and the late-afternoon light falls on the water from behind you.

What It Suggests for Photos

A honeymoon or anniversary session. Rooms of up to 214 square meters suit an unhurried, editorial session that moves from the suite to the beach. Our honeymoon photoshoot guide explains how we plan it.

A proposal. A small resort makes it easier to find a private corner of the beach at sunset. See our surprise proposal photography page.

An intimate wedding. Event spaces have not been announced yet, but a resort this size naturally suits a small celebration. Our guide to micro weddings has ideas.

Booking a Celebration at a Resort That Has Not Opened

  • Get the opening date in writing, with what happens to your deposit if it moves. This resort's opening has already moved twice.
  • Choose a backup resort nearby with similar style for the same dates; the neighboring Waldorf Astoria is one option to discuss with your planner.
  • Ask for the vendor policy for photographers before you sign anything.
  • Plan the photos last. We hold your date and adapt the plan to the resort when it opens; the light table above will not change.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does Park Hyatt Riviera Maya open?
It is accepting reservations for stays from February 15, 2027. The opening was first planned for 2025 and then 2026, so confirm your dates in writing.
Where is Park Hyatt Riviera Maya?
Next to Waldorf Astoria Cancún, on the coast south of Cancún International Airport, between the Hotel Zone and Puerto Morelos.
Is Park Hyatt Riviera Maya all-inclusive?
Yes. It will be the Park Hyatt brand's first all-inclusive resort, with 148 rooms, several pools, a full-service spa and restaurants including a beach club grill.
Can you get married at Park Hyatt Riviera Maya?
Event spaces have not been announced yet. A resort of 148 rooms naturally suits an intimate wedding; ask the resort for its plans and keep a written backup while it is under construction.
