In short

At Amàre Cancun, the former Breathless Cancun Soul, the two strongest places to ask are a rooftop at the last light and the flat water of the bay. Ask about 30 minutes before sunset. Our sessions start at 245 USD, and the gallery arrives in one to three business days.

A new name on an old favourite: the rooftops and the calm bay are still the reason to choose this tower. Here is how we would plan the moment at Amàre Cancun.

Where to Ask

The rooftop at the last light. Each tower has a rooftop pool, with long views over the sea and the lagoon. If the hotel allows it at your hour, it is the most dramatic place to ask.

The edge of the bay. Flat water and soft light, away from the busier pools. From about May to August the sun sets close to the bay, so you can stand at the water and let it go down behind the question.

Your balcony. Most suites have an ocean view. A very private question there, followed by portraits on the sand, is a simple plan that always works.

This stretch of shore looks north over the Bahía de Mujeres, which is why the water stays so flat. In summer the sun drops toward the west-northwest and can set near the water; in winter it goes down behind the hotels and the beach falls into soft, even light. From about May to August the sun sets to the west-northwest, close to the bay, so this is the season for a true sunset frame from the sand.

How We Keep the Surprise

We arrive before you do and stay out of sight, dressed like any other guest with a camera, at a spot we agree with you beforehand. You walk your partner to the place you chose, we photograph the question from a distance with a long lens, and we only come closer once you look for us. Then we stay for portraits of the two of you, so the same gallery has the moment and the relaxed photos after it.

Everything is arranged by WhatsApp or email ahead of time, including a code word in case plans change at the last minute. Our guide to planning a surprise proposal from abroad has the full checklist.

Getting the Photographer In

If you are staying at Amàre Cancun, the photographer comes in as your invited guest: give the front desk our name and the time of the session a day or two ahead, and we check in at the lobby. Rules for outside photographers belong to each resort and they change, so we confirm the current policy with the hotel in writing before your date. Guests of another hotel should expect a day pass for the photographer. Our guide to bringing an outside photographer to a resort covers the usual rules.

Close to Amàre Cancun

The nearest public beaches are Playa Langosta (km 5, under a kilometre away) and Playa Las Perlas (km 2.5, about 1 km away). Beaches in Mexico are public up to the high-water line, so the sand itself needs no resort pass; loungers, gardens and pools are the hotel's to manage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the best place to propose at Amàre Cancun?
A rooftop at the last light if the hotel allows it, the edge of the calm bay, or your own ocean-view balcony for a very private moment.
What time should I propose at Amàre Cancun?
About 30 minutes before sunset. Sunset here ranges from 6:06 pm in November to 7:32 pm in July, so the question falls near 5:35 pm in November and near 7:00 pm in July.
Can a photographer hide for a surprise proposal at Amàre Cancun?
Yes. We arrive first, stay out of sight at the spot we agreed with you, and photograph the moment from a distance with a long lens. As with any outside vendor, we confirm access with the resort beforehand.
How much does a proposal photographer at Amàre Cancun cost?
IVAE Studios collections start at 245 USD for 35 minutes and 15 photos, enough for the question and a few portraits. Many couples choose The Glow Hour at 325 USD or The Essential Flow at 395 USD for more time together afterwards.
