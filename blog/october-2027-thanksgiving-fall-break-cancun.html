In short

In 2027, Canadian Thanksgiving and the US federal holiday Columbus Day share Monday, October 11, and some US school districts take fall break that week. October is Cancun's rainiest month and still hurricane season, yet all-day rain is rare: in nine Octobers of hourly airport reports, only 4 of 265 days had rain in half or more of their reports. Sunset moves from 6:35 to 6:12 pm, so our 5:30 pm start falls 42 to 65 minutes before it.

Here are the dates, the weather and the light for Cancun in October, from official sources, so you can decide whether the month suits your family and plan your photos around it.

One Monday, Two Holidays

Canadian Thanksgiving falls on Monday, October 11, 2027: British Columbia lists it as a statutory holiday that day, and Ontario names Thanksgiving Day among its nine public holidays. The same Monday is Columbus Day on the US Office of Personnel Management's list of 2027 federal holidays. Arrive on Saturday, October 9, and the long weekend gives you three days in Cancun.

Fall break is set district by district. Three approved calendars for the 2027 to 2028 school year that we checked give students a week off in three different weeks:

  • Gwinnett County Public Schools, Georgia: fall break from Monday, October 11, to Friday, October 15.
  • Fulton County Schools, Georgia: fall break from September 27 to October 1, with students off again on October 11.
  • Cherry Creek School District, Colorado: no school for students or staff from October 18 to 22.

Check your own district's calendar before you book flights.

How Wet Is October, Really?

Mexico's National Meteorological Service (SMN) publishes 30-year averages, called normals, for its Cancun weather station, number 23155. In the 1991 to 2020 set, October is the rainiest month of the year: 277.1 mm (10.9 in) on 16.4 days with rain, about half the month. It is milder than summer, with an average high of 32.2°C (90°F), down from 35.1°C (95°F) in August, and an average low of 23.7°C (75°F).

Does it rain all day? Rarely. The normals count days, not hours, so we turned to the hourly reports from Cancun International Airport in NOAA's Integrated Surface Database. In the Octobers of 2016 to 2024, 265 days had at least 22 routine reports. Rain showed up in at least one report on 105 of them, and on just over half of those in only one or two. Only 4 of the 265 days had rain in half or more of their reports.

Mornings were not drier: rain was falling in 6.0% of the reports between 6 and 9 am and in 3.6% of those between 5 and 7 pm. Showers are local, so read this as the shape of the month, not a forecast for your beach. Our guide to whether it rains every day in Cancun covers the rest of the year.

Hurricane Season and Seaweed

October is still hurricane season. The US National Hurricane Center dates the Atlantic season from June 1 to November 30, with its peak on September 10 and most activity between mid-August and mid-October, so the October 9 to 11 weekend falls near the end of that busiest stretch.

Later in the month is not off the hook. In the SMN station's 1991 to 2020 record, the wettest October was 2005: 1,188.8 mm, 770 mm of it on October 21 alone. That was Hurricane Wilma, which, in the Hurricane Center's report, made landfall on Cozumel on October 21 as a category 4 hurricane and battered the northeastern tip of the Yucatan Peninsula on October 22. Book with free cancellation where you can, read your travel insurance terms for named storms, and follow the Hurricane Center before you fly. Our Cancun hurricane season guide goes further.

Seaweed usually eases in October. The University of South Florida's Optical Oceanography Lab wrote on October 31, 2026 that, as in all previous years, the amount declined substantially in October, more than halving in most regions, though it was still high for the season, and that it should keep falling until possibly the end of November or December. Its outlook is not a forecast for any one beach, so check the beach the day before; our sargassum season guide has the usual pattern.

If You Stay Into November

Dia de Muertos comes next. UNESCO, which lists it as intangible cultural heritage of humanity, says it takes place each year at the end of October and the beginning of November. Families help the souls of their dead find the way home with flower petals, candles and offerings laid along the path from the cemetery, and set out the favorite dishes of the dead around the home shrine and the grave. For local families it is a time of remembrance, so if you come across an altar, enjoy it as a guest and ask before you take a picture.

Rain eases, too: in the SMN normals, November gets 131.4 mm (5.2 in) on 12 days with rain. US clocks fall back on November 7, which puts Cancun on New York time, and US Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 25, 2027; our guide to Cancun in November and Thanksgiving week picks up from there.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Canadian Thanksgiving in 2027?

Monday, October 11, 2027, the same day as Columbus Day, a US federal holiday. Some US districts take fall break that week: Gwinnett County Public Schools in Georgia is off from October 11 to 15.

Does it rain all day in Cancun in October?

Rarely. October is the rainiest month in Mexico's official 1991 to 2020 normals for Cancun, with 277.1 mm (10.9 in) on 16.4 days, but in nine Octobers of hourly reports from Cancun's airport, only 4 of 265 days had rain in half or more of their reports.

Is October still hurricane season in Cancun?

Yes. The US National Hurricane Center dates the Atlantic season from June 1 to November 30, with most activity between mid-August and mid-October. Hurricane Wilma hit Cozumel and the northeastern Yucatan on October 21 and 22, 2005.

Is there sargassum in Cancun in October?

Usually less than earlier in the season: on October 31, 2026, the University of South Florida reported that sargassum had declined substantially in October, as in all previous years. Check the beach the day before.

What time is sunset in Cancun in October?

In October 2027, sunset moves from 6:35 pm on October 1 to 6:12 pm on October 31, and sunrise from 6:38 to 6:49 am, Cancun time, by our NOAA-method calculation for the Hotel Zone.
