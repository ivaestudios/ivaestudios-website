In short

Pick the coast your children can swim at and reach easily. In Los Cabos, beachfront does not always mean swimmable: the tourism board warns of rough surf and rip currents on some beaches and lists swimmable beaches such as Medano, Chileno, Santa Maria and Palmilla. From the West, Los Cabos is closer; from the East, Cancun. Los Cabos keeps UTC-7 and Cancun UTC-5 all year.

When families weigh Cabo against Cancun, the questions are practical: can the kids swim, how long is the flight, how hot is it, and which trip is easier with a toddler. We photograph families on both coasts, so we checked each answer against official sources on November 14, 2026, then added what each coast means for photos.

Where Your Kids Can Actually Swim

The biggest difference is in Los Cabos. Its tourism board notes that many resorts sit on the beach, yet some stretches are unsuitable for swimming because of rough surf, rip currents and undertows. For swimming it points to protected bays and coves such as Medano Beach in Cabo San Lucas and Lovers Beach near the Arch, and to the generally calm water at Acapulquito and Chileno on the Tourist Corridor. Its list of swimmable beaches adds Santa Maria, Las Viudas, La Ribera and Palmilla, whose shallow water it calls ideal for families. Green flags mark safe water; red or black flags warn of unsafe water.

Choose the beach first

In Los Cabos, pick the bay before the resort. If sea swimming matters to your children, stay on a swimmable beach; if not, plan on the pool. Our guide to swimmable beaches in Los Cabos has the details.

Cancun has contrasts too: the Mexican Caribbean tourism board describes Playa Chac Mool as known among surfers for its waves. Our guide to the safest Hotel Zone beaches for kids names the calm north-shore picks.

Two Clocks, All Year

Mexico's time zone law puts Baja California Sur, home of Los Cabos, on the 105th meridian west and Quintana Roo, home of Cancun, on the 75th, with clock changes only on the northern border. So Los Cabos keeps UTC-7 and Cancun UTC-5 all year. US clocks change on March 14 and November 7, 2027, so your gap from home shifts:

Los Cabos winter evenings are cool: January lows average 12.8 °C (55 °F) in Cabo San Lucas against 20.0 °C (68 °F) in Cancun, so pack a sweater. By July both coasts are hot, with highs of 95 °F in Cancun and 94 °F in Cabo San Lucas. The real gap is rain: Cancun averages 116 days with rain a year, Cabo San Lucas 13.5. June to September: Seaweed, Storms and Heat

The University of South Florida reports that large amounts of sargassum from the tropical Atlantic have reached the Caribbean every summer since 2011 except 2013. Its outlook covers the Caribbean Sea and Gulf of Mexico; Los Cabos, on the Pacific, is outside it. See our Cancun seaweed season guide.

The US National Hurricane Center dates the Atlantic season, which includes the Caribbean, from June 1 to November 30, with most activity from mid-August to mid-October. The eastern Pacific season, west of Mexico, runs from May 15 to November 30, with most storms from late June to early October. From June to September, Cancun averages 8.5 to 14.4 days with rain a month, Cabo San Lucas 0.1 to 4.0. See our Cancun hurricane season guide.

On both coasts, summer sessions belong to early morning or the last hour before sunset. On July 15, 2027, the sun rises at 6:14 am and sets at 7:32 pm in Cancun, and at 5:43 am and 7:08 pm in Cabo San Lucas. In Cancun our 6:30 am online slot fits that early light; later evening times are arranged on WhatsApp.

Which Is Easier With a Toddler?

The easier trip is usually the one closest to home, on your own clock, with calm water at the door: from the West Coast or Arizona, a Los Cabos resort on a swimmable bay; from the East Coast or Southeast, Cancun, which shares New York's clock in winter. Frequently Asked Questions

Is Cabo or Cancun better for families?

It depends on your home airport and how your children like the water. Cancun is closer to the US East and Southeast, Los Cabos to the West and Arizona. In Los Cabos, swim at the beaches the tourism board lists as swimmable, such as Medano, Chileno, Santa Maria or Palmilla.

Is there sargassum in Cabo?

The sargassum that reaches Cancun comes from the tropical Atlantic, and the University of South Florida's outlook for it covers the Caribbean Sea and Gulf of Mexico. Los Cabos, on the Pacific, is outside that outlook.

Is it cheaper to fly to Cancun or Cabo?

Fares change daily, so compare the same dates into Cancun (CUN) and Los Cabos (SJD). Distance shapes flight time: per Southwest, Los Angeles is 911 miles from Los Cabos and 2,119 from Cancun; Atlanta is 880 from Cancun and 1,695 from Los Cabos.

What is the time difference between Cabo and Cancun?

Two hours, all year: Los Cabos keeps UTC-7 and Cancun UTC-5, with no clock changes. In winter that matches US Mountain and Eastern time; from March 14 to November 7, 2027, US Pacific and Central daylight time.

Which is better in summer, Cabo or Cancun?

Both are hot: July highs average 95 °F in Cancun and 94 °F in Cabo San Lucas. Cancun adds more rain, sargassum and the Atlantic hurricane season from June 1; Los Cabos has the eastern Pacific season from May 15. Plan beach time and photos for early morning or the last hour before sunset.
