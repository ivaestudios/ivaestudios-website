In short

A Cancun family package bundles flights and a resort into one booking, usually priced per person for two people sharing a room. Many all-inclusive packages include airport transfers, but kids-free deals, resort credits and cancellation rules have conditions, and tips, excursions and the state tourist tax can sit outside the price. Price the identical trip separately, and if you want photos, check your resort’s rule on outside photographers first.

If you are pricing a Cancun trip for the whole family, you have met the package: flights, a resort and sometimes transfers in one price. We are a photography studio in Cancun, not a travel agency, so this guide sticks to what each seller publishes, read on November 14, 2026, plus the part we know best: where photos fit in.

How a Package Really Works

A package is one booking for your flights and hotel, sold by an airline’s vacation brand, a tour operator, a warehouse club or an online travel agency. The headline price is usually per person, based on double occupancy, which means two people sharing a room: Transat prints that line on its package pages, and Air Canada Vacations and Costco Travel label their prices the same way. Children, extra beds and bigger rooms all change your family’s total, so compare only quotes with your exact family in them.

From Canada: WestJet, Sunwing, Air Canada and Transat

WestJet Vacations says its all-inclusive packages include transfers, meals, drinks and most resort activities, and its Cancun page says there are more than 120 resorts to choose from. Its terms add that airport transfers are not included unless they appear on the itinerary, so check that line on a flight-and-hotel booking. Sunwing Vacations still sells under its own name, but it belongs to the WestJet Group, and its customers have flown on WestJet since May 29, 2025. Sunwing lists airport transfers in its all-inclusive packages, and kids stay and eat free at select resorts.

Air Canada Vacations includes a first checked bag and a carry-on per person, matches a lower price on an identical package by up to 50 Canadian dollars per person, and offers a full refund or no change fees if an Air Canada flight in your package is cancelled more than 48 hours before departure. Transat’s all-inclusive packages list an economy flight, one 23 kg checked bag, round-trip airport transfers, meals, and hotel taxes and service fees. For this winter’s routes, see our guide to new flights from Canada to Cancun and Tulum.

From the US: Airline Brands and Costco

Delta Vacations says its Cancun packages include round-trip Delta airfare and round-trip transfers between the airport and your hotel, including the ferry if you stay on Isla Mujeres. JetBlue Vacations adds airport transfers worth up to 50 USD, a meet and greet and 24/7 local support to flight-and-hotel packages to Cancun, though select hotels are not eligible for the transfers.

Costco Travel is offered as a service of your Costco membership, and you can book without a Costco Travel account by giving your membership number and last name. It says that airfare is an add-on unless a package notes otherwise, and that prices typically include accommodations plus airport transfers or a rental car. Its Mexico brochure, valid from November 1, 2026 to February 28, 2027, tags Cancun packages with extras such as a Digital Costco Shop Card, a spa credit or a resort credit. Online travel agencies sell bundles too, and the same checks apply to every seller.

Kids, Rooms and Babies

Kids stay free is real, with conditions. Transat’s Family Collection, where kids stay and eat free, offers that in a limited number of rooms, and the age rules and number of free children vary by resort. Palace Resorts, on its own site, applies its kids and teens offer to ages 0 to 17 at its family-oriented resorts, with one paying adult in the room and proof of age at check-in. And the kids-stay-free terms on Air Canada Vacations’ Hilton Cancun page spell out that parents still pay the flight portion of a child’s trip, including taxes and fees.

Rooms have limits too. On Air Canada Vacations’ Hilton Cancun page, every room type listed sleeps three or four people at most, and WestJet Vacations keeps a Family of five or more collection with family suites, two-bedroom units and villas. Book every child from the start: WestJet’s terms treat a change in room occupancy as a cancellation and rebooking at the new price. With a baby, Air Canada Vacations gives infant pricing only if the child is under 2 for the whole trip, return flight included.

What Packages Leave Out

Air Canada Vacations’ terms give the clearest list we found of what sits outside the package price: tips, excursions, administration, environmental sanitation or resort fees paid to the hotel in local currency, hotel transfers unless stated, and tourist levies such as Quintana Roo’s tourist tax, which our guide to Visitax and tourist fees for families explains. WestJet Vacations adds that all-inclusive privileges begin at check-in and end at check-out, times that may not match your flights. And none of the package inclusions we read listed photography.

Package or Separate: How to Compare

Are vacation packages worth it? Only a like-for-like price tells you. Air Canada Vacations’ price-match rule makes a good checklist, because it only matches an identical trip: same departure city, flights, hotel, room category, meal plan, occupancy, transfers, dates and length of stay. Price exactly that separately, then add whatever the separate booking lacks, such as bags and transfers. Check the resort’s own site too: when we looked, Palace Resorts gave direct bookings of four nights or more, made by November 30, 2026, a 250 USD resort credit, free stays for kids and teens and shared airport transfers, and excluded tour operator rates from the offer.

Then compare the rules, not just the totals. WestJet Vacations refunds in full within 24 hours of booking; after that it charges 250 Canadian dollars per guest to cancel 45 days or more before departure (Premium and Business excepted), keeps half of what you paid from 44 to 21 days out and all of it inside 21 days. If the separate total is lower and its rules are no stricter, book separately; otherwise, take the package.

Resort Credits and the Resort Photo Team

A resort credit sounds like free money, so read its list. The Palace Resorts credit above covered selected tours, romantic dinners, spa services, beauty salon services and golf, with a 16% service fee on top, no more than 50 USD toward the spa and no cash value, and it had to be used during that stay. Photography was not on the list. Other credits differ, so read yours.

Many resorts also have a photo team on site, which is convenient. Ask how they price, by the photo or as a set, and when you choose your images. Then ask the question from our comparison of a resort photo studio and an independent photographer: what do 35 edited photos cost in total? Our answer is The Chapter: 60 minutes, 35 edited photos, 475 USD, in a private 4K gallery with unlimited downloads and print rights.

Why Your Resort’s Photographer Rule Matters

If you want your own photographer, the resort you choose shapes the cost. Some hotels charge a fee for an outside photographer, which you pay directly to the hotel, not to us. We confirm your resort’s rule before your session, and if you are still choosing between packages, ask about each resort before you book. Frequently Asked Questions

Are Cancun vacation packages worth it for families?

Sometimes. Price the identical trip both ways: the same flights, hotel, room category, meal plan, occupancy, transfers, dates and length of stay. If the separate total is lower and its rules are no stricter, book separately; if not, the package is the better buy.

Do Cancun packages include airport transfers?

Many do, but check your booking. On November 14, 2026, WestJet Vacations, Sunwing and Transat listed transfers in their all-inclusive packages, Delta Vacations included them in its Cancun packages, and JetBlue Vacations covered transfers worth up to 50 USD at eligible hotels. Air Canada Vacations and WestJet Vacations exclude transfers unless your booking lists them.

How does child pricing work in a package?

Prices are usually per person, based on two people sharing a room, so ask for a quote with every child and their ages. Kids-stay-free offers come with conditions, such as a limited number of rooms, age rules that vary by resort or one paying adult per room. Air Canada Vacations gives infant pricing only to a child under 2 for the whole trip.

Can we use a resort credit to pay for family photos?

Only if photography is on the credit's list. The 250 USD credit Palace Resorts advertised for direct bookings on November 14, 2026 covered selected tours, romantic dinners, spa services, beauty salon services and golf, not photos. Our sessions are paid at the end, by card or in cash, from 295 to 895 USD.

Can we bring an outside photographer to our package resort?

It depends on the hotel. Some hotels charge a fee for an outside photographer, which you pay directly to the hotel. We confirm your resort's rule before your session, so if you are still choosing between packages, ask about each resort before you book.
