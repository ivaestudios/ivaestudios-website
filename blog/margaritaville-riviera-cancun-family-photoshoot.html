In short

Two Margaritaville Island Reserve resorts share Bahía Petempich in Puerto Morelos, and only one takes children: Riviera Cancun is all ages, with a kids club for 4 to 12, while Riviera Maya is adults only. At Riviera Cancun the beach faces east, behind the reef, so with small kids book sunrise. We recommend The Chapter: 60 minutes, 35 edited photos, 475 USD, no deposit.

Search for Margaritaville Riviera Cancun with kids and the confusion arrives fast: two Karisma resorts carry the Margaritaville Island Reserve name on the same bay in Puerto Morelos, with opposite age policies. If your family is booked at the all-ages one, this is our guide to a family session there: where we shoot, when the light is kindest to small faces, and what to know about the kids club, seaweed and the resort's rules.

Which Margaritaville Is Yours?

Read the end of the name

Margaritaville Island Reserve Riviera Cancun. All ages and all-inclusive, says Karisma, which runs both resorts. Its address is Km 27.5, Bahía Petempich, Puerto Morelos, and it has a kids club for ages 4 to 12. This guide is for you.

Margaritaville Island Reserve Riviera Maya. Adults only, by Karisma's own label, so not an option with children, and Karisma's address puts it on the same bay, Bahía Petempich. If that is your booking, see our Margaritaville Riviera Maya guide.

The mix-up is easy to make, and not only for guests: on November 14, 2026, the Riviera Cancun resort's own groups page carried a line about “the new, adults-only Margaritaville Island Reserve Resort Riviera Maya.” If anything seems to say your kids cannot come, check the resort name on your confirmation.

Where We Shoot

The water's edge. The heart of a session here. The Mexican Caribbean tourism board says the reef of Puerto Morelos Reef National Park lies just offshore, only a few hundred meters out at the town's main beach, and calls the beach at Bahía Petempich calm, shallow and sheltered by the reef, a good choice for families with small children. Calm water changes the pictures: a toddler can sit at the edge and splash, a parent can let go of a hand, and the frames show a family playing, not bracing for waves. Calm still means an adult within arm's reach, and the beach flag has the final word.

The grounds. The resort lists four pools: License to Chill is for adults only, and the other three are described as family pools. Where the resort allows, the grounds give a second backdrop. Karisma posts the property map as a downloadable image on the resort's Resort Map page.

Puerto Morelos town. If you want the town in your gallery as well, our Puerto Morelos and Akumal family guide covers it.

Sunrise and Sunset by Month

The reef park's management program, published in Mexico's official gazette, places the park in front of Puerto Morelos, a town on Quintana Roo's northeastern coast facing the Caribbean, and cites waves of about 0.14 meters (5.5 inches) measured at the beach, inside the reef. The beach looks east over that sea, so the sun rises out of the water and sets inland, behind the resort.

With small children we lean toward the morning. It comes before the heat of the day, and with the sea behind your family, the low sun sits behind you rather than in small eyes.

The Kids Club and Your Photo Day

The resort's all-inclusive list includes the Parakeets Kid's Club, with supervised activities for children 4 to 12, open 9 am to 5 pm; certified babysitting costs extra. A 7:00 am start with The Chapter ends at 8:00, an hour before the club opens. A 5:00 pm start lines up with closing time, so on photo day collect the kids earlier, let them rest, and dress them just before we meet. Under 4, plan around naps, as our guide to family photos with a toddler and a baby explains.

The resort's website has a sensory-friendly travel page, and Autism Double-Checked has published a visitor guide for it, with an optional Autism Passport you can share with the resort before arrival. A photo shop and service is among the resort's extras; our comparison of resort and outside photographers lays out both options.

Seaweed on This Stretch

The reef calms the water; seaweed is the variable no one controls. In its October 31, 2026 outlook, the University of South Florida's Optical Oceanography Lab found sargassum more than halved in most regions during October, though still high for the season, and expected further decreases until possibly the end of November or December, with Caribbean beachings likely to continue at much lower severity. It adds that the bulletin is not meant to predict conditions at a specific beach.

Closer to your date, Mexico's Navy publishes a daily sargassum bulletin, and our guide to checking beach conditions today shows how to read it with the state's traffic-light map, the beach flag and a webcam. Look the night before; cancellation with us is free up to one day before.

How the Photographer Gets In

We found no rule for outside photographers on the Margaritaville Island Reserve Riviera Cancun website: its weddings page, its FAQ and Karisma's reservation policies, which we checked on November 14, 2026, do not mention one. Some hotels charge a fee for outside photographers. If this one does, you pay it to the hotel, and we confirm the resort's rule with you before your date.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Margaritaville Riviera Cancun adults only?

No. Karisma lists Margaritaville Island Reserve Riviera Cancun as all ages and all-inclusive, with a kids club for children 4 to 12. The adults-only resort is its sister on the same bay, Margaritaville Island Reserve Riviera Maya.

Does Margaritaville Riviera Cancun have a kids club?

Yes. The Parakeets Kid's Club, for ages 4 to 12, is on the resort's all-inclusive list and is open from 9 am to 5 pm. Certified babysitting is an optional service at extra cost.

Where is Margaritaville Island Reserve Riviera Cancun?

On Bahía Petempich in Puerto Morelos, at Km 27.5 of the Cancun to Tulum highway, by Karisma's address; its adults-only sister, Margaritaville Island Reserve Riviera Maya, is on the same bay. The Mexican Caribbean tourism board describes the beach there as calm, shallow and sheltered by the reef, a good choice for families with small children.

What time is best for family photos at Margaritaville Riviera Cancun?

Sunrise, especially with small children. The beach faces east, and on the 15th of each month in 2027 the sun rises between 6:06 am in June and 7:27 am in January. It sets inland, behind the resort.

Can we bring an outside photographer to Margaritaville Riviera Cancun?

We found no rule for outside photographers on the resort's website. Some hotels charge a fee; if this one does, you pay it to the hotel, and we confirm the resort's rule before your date.

How much is a family photo session there?

We recommend The Chapter: 60 minutes, 35 edited photos and one look for 475 USD. Collections run from 295 to 895 USD, with no deposit and no per-person charge.
