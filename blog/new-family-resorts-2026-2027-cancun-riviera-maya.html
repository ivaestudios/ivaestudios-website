In short

Since November 2025, Iberostar Selection Riviera Cancun, Hotel Riu Ventura, Majestic Mirage Costa Mujeres, the St. Regis Costa Mujeres, Mondrian Cancun and Kimpton Tres Rios have opened around Cancun and the Riviera Maya. Park Hyatt Riviera Maya, Casa Nizuc and Grand Hyatt Cancun are due in December 2026, and Finest Riviera Cancun opens on February 11, 2027. JW Marriott's all-inclusive in Costa Mujeres, announced for 2026, has no date yet. Mondrian and Park Hyatt have not published a children's policy, so ask before you book. Adults-only openings are left out, and every date was checked on November 14, 2026.

A resort that has just opened has a lot going for it: new rooms, new pools and a kids' club nobody has worn out yet. Here are the openings we could date around Cancun and the Riviera Maya, from November 2025 to February 2027, that are not adults-only: when it opened or opens, where that date comes from, what it says about children, and what a brand-new resort means for your family photos.

Where we could not find a date from either, we used Caribbean Journal, a travel publication that follows Caribbean openings closely, and the table says so. Several of these dates moved at least once in 2026, so confirm yours with the resort before you book flights.

Open Now: Six New Resorts

Iberostar Selection Riviera Cancun. Iberostar announced on November 4, 2025 that this five-star all-inclusive near Puerto Morelos, 20 minutes from Cancun International Airport, had joined its portfolio on November 1, with 444 suites that include family rooms and swim-up suites. Its own page lists the Star Camp kids' club, for ages 4 to 6 and 7 to 12, and a splash park with slides. Our Iberostar Selection Riviera Cancun guide covers the beach and the photographer's access.

Hotel Riu Ventura. RIU announced on December 9, 2025 that it had just opened the 705-room Riu Ventura beside Playa Delfines, its sixth hotel in Cancun. Its own page calls it a family resort, with the RIU Land kids' club, a children's pool with slides and two-bedroom family rooms, and the announcement describes an adults-only pool and Sky Bar on the sixth floor. Our Riu Ventura guide has the spots and the best hours.

Majestic Mirage Costa Mujeres. Majestic Resorts lists a soft opening on December 12, 2025 and a grand opening on January 15, 2026 for this all-suite, all-inclusive resort just north of Cancun. It has separate sections for adults and for families: The Club is for adults 18 and over, and families with children can use the Kids Club, the Teens Club and the on-site water park. Our Majestic Mirage Costa Mujeres guide covers the beach and the light.

The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort. Marriott announced its opening on June 23, 2026: 163 guestrooms and 50 suites in the quiet enclave of Costa Mujeres, just north of Cancun, with a Kids Club and programming for families and multigenerational travelers. Marriott does not describe it as all-inclusive, which matters when you compare rates. See our St. Regis Costa Mujeres guide.

Mondrian Cancun. Ennismore announced on August 25, 2026 that Mondrian Cancun had opened as the brand's first all-inclusive: 400 rooms on Grand Island, between the Caribbean and Nichupté Lagoon, about 15 minutes from the airport. Neither that announcement nor the resort pages we read mention a kids' club, and the Mondrian+ rooms, their lounge and the rooftop pool are for adults, so ask the resort about children before booking. Our Mondrian Cancun guide covers the lagoon at sunset.

Kimpton Tres Rios. Hotel Equities, which operates Kimpton's first all-inclusive, announced its opening on September 23, 2026, and on November 8 Caribbean Journal reported a kids' club for ages 4 to 12. The resort has a guide of its own: our Kimpton Tres Rios family guide covers it in detail.

Opening This Winter

Park Hyatt Riviera Maya. Hyatt said on June 25, 2026 that Park Hyatt Riviera Maya, an all-inclusive with 148 rooms and suites in the private AMAI enclave between Cancun and Puerto Morelos, would open by the end of 2026. On November 10, Caribbean Journal reported that Hyatt is taking reservations from December 3, after earlier dates of February 15 and January 8, 2027. Hyatt has not published a children's policy, and the same report says one of the four pools is reserved for adults. Our Park Hyatt Riviera Maya guide has the light and the questions to ask.

Casa Nizuc. This 235-room Tribute Portfolio resort sits in the gated Aldea Nizuc community, at km 23.5 of the Hotel Zone, and it is not all-inclusive, according to Caribbean Journal, which also reported a separate children's pool and water slides. Marriott said on September 29, 2026 that it expected a November debut; on October 3, Caribbean Journal reported December 8.

Grand Hyatt Cancun Beach Resort. Hyatt said on September 15, 2026 that its 500-room all-inclusive in Puerto Cancun, a gated community with a marina about 25 minutes from the airport, is expected to open in December, with a Grand Kids Club, 47 suites and nine pools, one of them for adults only. Caribbean Journal reported an opening date of December 10 the next day; in its earlier reports, the first bookable date had moved from September 8 to November 17. Our Grand Hyatt Cancun guide has a section for families.

Finest Riviera Cancun. Finest Resorts lists the opening as February 11, 2027. Its November 2026 fact sheet describes an all-ages resort in Puerto Morelos with family suites that have a separate bedroom with two single beds, the Imagine Kids Club, a splash park, a water slide park and three child-care lounges near the restaurants, while the Excellence Club areas are for adults 18 and over. See our Finest Riviera Cancun guide.

Announced, Not Yet Dated

Marriott announced the 283-room JW Marriott All-Inclusive Costa Mujeres in November 2022, with an opening in 2026. Marriott has not announced its opening: on October 6, 2026, Marriott called JW Marriott Costa Elena, in Costa Rica, the brand's first all-inclusive resort worldwide, and its Mexico update of September 29 did not list Costa Mujeres among the coming openings. Marriott has not said whether it will take children. Our JW Marriott Costa Mujeres guide covers the beach in the meantime.

Further out, Marriott said in October 2024 that The Ritz-Carlton, Cancun, Punta Nizuc, a 131-room hotel about 10 minutes from the airport, would open in 2027. It gave no month and no children's policy, and its newsroom shows no later update.

Searching for a new Dreams? Hyatt's newsroom, read on November 14, 2026, lists no new Dreams resort around Cancun or the Riviera Maya since January 2025; the latest Dreams opening it lists is Dreams Playa Esmeralda, in the Dominican Republic, on September 2, 2025.

We left out adults-only openings, airport and city hotels, existing resorts that reopened after a renovation or under a new name, and announcements we could not confirm.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the newest family resorts near Cancun?
Iberostar Selection Riviera Cancun opened in November 2025, Hotel Riu Ventura and Majestic Mirage Costa Mujeres in December 2025, the St. Regis Costa Mujeres in June 2026, Mondrian Cancun in August 2026 and Kimpton Tres Rios in September 2026. Casa Nizuc, Park Hyatt Riviera Maya and Grand Hyatt Cancun are due in December 2026, and Finest Riviera Cancun on February 11, 2027. Mondrian and Park Hyatt have not published a children's policy.
Which new resorts near Cancun have a kids' club?
Of the resorts that opened since November 2025: Iberostar Selection Riviera Cancun (Star Camp, ages 4 to 12), Hotel Riu Ventura (RIU Land), Majestic Mirage Costa Mujeres (Kids Club and Teens Club), the St. Regis Costa Mujeres (Kids Club) and Kimpton Tres Rios (ages 4 to 12, as reported by Caribbean Journal). Of those still to open, Grand Hyatt Cancun plans a Grand Kids Club and Finest Riviera Cancun the Imagine Kids Club.
When does Grand Hyatt Cancun open?
Hyatt said on September 15, 2026 that Grand Hyatt Cancun Beach Resort, an all-inclusive in Puerto Cancun with 500 rooms and a Grand Kids Club, is expected to open in December 2026. Caribbean Journal reported an opening date of December 10, after earlier reports of first stays on September 8 and then November 17, so confirm yours with Hyatt.
Is Kimpton Tres Rios open?
Yes. Hotel Equities, which operates it, announced the opening on September 23, 2026, and Caribbean Journal reported on November 8, 2026 that Kimpton's first all-inclusive is open, with a kids' club for ages 4 to 12. IHG lists it at km 54.5 of the Cancun-Tulum highway, Playa del Carmen.
Which new resorts open near Cancun in 2027?
Finest Riviera Cancun, in Puerto Morelos, is the one with a date: February 11, 2027. Marriott said in October 2024 that The Ritz-Carlton, Cancun, Punta Nizuc would open in 2027, without a month. Park Hyatt Riviera Maya, once reported for January 8, 2027, is taking reservations from December 3, 2026, according to Caribbean Journal.
What are the newest Dreams resorts near Cancun?
None of the openings on this page is a Dreams. Hyatt's newsroom, read on November 14, 2026, lists no new Dreams resort around Cancun or the Riviera Maya since January 2025; the latest Dreams opening it lists is Dreams Playa Esmeralda, in the Dominican Republic, on September 2, 2025.
When does Finest Riviera Cancun open, and does it take children?
Finest Resorts lists the opening as February 11, 2027. Its November 2026 fact sheet describes an all-ages resort in Puerto Morelos with the Imagine Kids Club, a splash park and a water slide park, plus Excellence Club areas for adults 18 and over.
Can we bring our own photographer to a resort that just opened?
That depends on the resort's rule, which we confirm before your session. Some hotels charge a fee for an outside photographer; if yours does, you pay it to the hotel, not to us, and we confirm the rule with you first.
