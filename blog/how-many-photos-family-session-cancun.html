In short

The number of photos comes from the collection you book, not from the size of your family: 15, 25, 35, 50 or 70 edited photos, or the complete edited gallery. For a family of four the usual choice is The Chapter: 60 minutes, 35 edited photos, 475 USD. A first preview arrives within 24 hours and the private 4K gallery in 1 to 3 business days.

“How many photos will we actually get?” is a fair question to ask before you plan a session around naps, sunscreen and the light. The answer is simple, and you can have it before you book: the number of edited photos is set by the collection you choose. It does not change with how many people are in the frame, and it does not change with how the session goes.

IVAE Studios is led by Vianey Díaz, who has spent more than ten years behind the camera. The studio has delivered more than 500 sessions and holds 5.0 across 73 Google reviews. Below are the six collections side by side, which one usually fits which family, and when your photos arrive.

Which Collection Fits Your Family

For a family of four, the usual choice is The Chapter: 60 minutes and 35 edited photos for 475 USD. It is the collection we recommend for families. To see how an hour with children actually unfolds, from the warm-up to the last of the light, read how long a family photoshoot takes with kids.

Larger families who want every combination usually take one of the two 90-minute collections: The Editorial, 70 edited photos with 2 looks for 645 USD, or The Heirloom, the complete edited gallery with 2 looks for 895 USD. Multi-generation reunions, and families who want both a beach set and a set on the resort grounds, usually book one of these too, so nothing feels rushed.

Between The Chapter and the 90-minute collections sits The Story: 75 minutes, 50 edited photos and 2 looks for 545 USD, which adds fifteen minutes, fifteen photos and a second look to The Chapter. The two shortest collections are The Keepsake, 45 minutes and 25 photos, and The Postcard, 35 minutes and 15 photos, which is made for couples.

Whatever the size of the family, a lot rides on keeping the children happy. We photographed Danielle P.’s family of four, two energetic kids included, on the beach outside their hotel. From her review:

“She knew exactly how to keep them engaged and smiling naturally, which is no small task on a sandy beach in the sun!”

Danielle P., Google review

How the Photos Spread Across a Family

The count covers the whole session, not each person, so there is no set number of photos per person. What changes from one family to the next is how many combinations those photos have to cover.

For a family of four, the list is short: all of you together, the children, and the pairings that matter to you, perhaps each parent with the kids or a few frames of the two of you as a couple.

Now picture three households: grandparents traveling with two adult children and their families. The list grows quickly: the full group, each household on its own, the grandparents with all the grandchildren, the adult siblings together, the cousins, each couple. The Chapter would still hold 35 photos, now spread across many more combinations. That is why larger families who want every combination usually move up to the 70 photos of The Editorial or the complete edited gallery of The Heirloom, both with 90 minutes to work through the list calmly.

Before the trip, we build the list of groupings with you. On the day, we work from the biggest frame down: the full group first, while everyone is fresh, then each household, then the grandparents with the grandchildren, then the cousins. We photograph the grandparents and the smallest children early, while patience is highest, so they can rest or play nearby while we finish. Stephanie F.’s family was fourteen people, five of them under ten:

“They did the best job of photographing 14 people with 5 littles under 10 years old.”

Stephanie F., Google review

The Price Does Not Count Heads

There is no limit on the number of people and no per-person charge. A collection costs the same for a family of four and a reunion of fourteen; a bigger family changes how much time you will want, not a fee per head. So when you price photography for a family reunion, the number to look at is the collection.

The hotel may add a cost of its own: some hotels charge a fee for outside photographers, which you pay directly to the hotel, and we confirm your resort’s rule before the session. Sessions need no deposit. You pay at the end of the session, by card or in cash, and you can cancel free of charge up to one day before.

Fall in love with more photos than your collection includes? You can move up to a larger collection anytime and pay only the difference between the two prices.

Comparing photographers?

Ask each one for two things in writing before you book: how many edited photos you receive, and how fast they arrive. Ours are on this page.

What “Edited” Means Here

Every photo we deliver is edited one by one: color-graded, skin tones balanced, the Caribbean blues kept true rather than oversaturated, small distractions cleaned up. Each one arrives in full-resolution 4K. The Heirloom has no fixed count: it includes the complete edited gallery of your session.

No amount of editing makes a child smile, though. That part happens during the session itself:

“They made our son smile in almost every single photo and were so patient with him.”

Elizabeth K., Google review

When the Photos Arrive

A first preview arrives within 24 hours of your session, so you have something to share while you are still on the trip. The full gallery follows in 1 to 3 business days: a private 4K gallery with unlimited downloads and print rights, so you can send copies to every household in the family. Mor A.’s review puts it simply: “The photos were delivered super fast”.

We explain what the 24-hour preview is for in its own guide, and once the gallery is home, how to choose which photos to print for an album. Booking a couples session too? The same collections apply; see how many photos you get from a couples session.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many photos do you get from a one-hour family session?

With The Chapter, our 60-minute collection and the one we recommend for families, you receive 35 edited photos for 475 USD. The number is set by the collection, not by the size of your family or by how the session goes.

How many edited photos should a photographer deliver?

Ask for the number in writing before you book. Ours is fixed by collection: 15, 25, 35, 50 or 70 edited photos, or the complete edited gallery with The Heirloom, from 295 to 895 USD.

Does a bigger family pay more or get more photos?

There is no limit on the number of people and no per-person charge, and the number of photos comes from the collection. Larger families who want every combination usually take The Editorial, 90 minutes and 70 edited photos for 645 USD, or The Heirloom, 90 minutes with the complete edited gallery for 895 USD.

When will we receive our family photos?

A first preview arrives within 24 hours of the session, and the full private 4K gallery in 1 to 3 business days, with unlimited downloads and print rights.

Can we get more photos after the session?

Yes. You can move up to a larger collection anytime and pay only the difference between the two prices.
