In short

We read the kids club pages of 11 family all-inclusives around Cancun and the Riviera Maya on November 14, 2026. Seven take children from 4 to 12, the main clubs open between 8:30 and 10 am and most close between 5 and 6 pm, and wherever a resort states the cost of its main club, it is included. For babies and toddlers, Club Med Cancun stands out, with clubs from 4 months at extra cost. A 60-minute family session that starts at 6:30, 7:00 or 7:30 am is over by 8:30, before most clubs open.

The kids club can shape a family's whole week at an all-inclusive: who can stay without you, for how long, and whether it costs extra. We read each resort's own pages and put the answers in one table, with every gap marked. After it, we show how to fit a family photo session around the club, so your children arrive fresh.

What the Table Tells You

Ages 4 to 12 are the standard. Seven of the 11 clubs take exactly that range, and Moon Palace's Playroom also starts at 4. Club Med splits it, with Mini Club Med+ for 4 to 10 and teen clubs from 11, and Finest Playa Mujeres runs a Mini Club for 3 to 6 and a Maxi Club for 7 to 11.

Under 4, plan to stay with them. Moon Palace welcomes under-4s only with a parent or adult relative, Royalton Riviera Cancun does not let them be left in the club and offers babysitting at extra cost, and Finest's Mini Club takes babies up to 2 with a parent. Club Med Cancun is the clear exception, with Baby Club Med from 4 months and Petit Club Med for 2 and 3 year olds, both bookable online at extra cost, listed from 82 USD and 74 USD; Finest also registers 3 year olds in its Mini Club. Grand Palladium Select Costa Mujeres lists its club from 3, but the same answer says children under 18 must always be accompanied by family, so ask how drop-off works. Our guide to toddler-friendly all-inclusives in Cancun goes further.

Days run from about 9 to 5. The main clubs open between 8:30 and 10 am, and most close between 5 and 6 pm. For evenings, Club Med runs its Petit, Mini and teen clubs again until 9 pm, Finest Playa Mujeres runs its Imagine Lounge at dinner, from 5 to 10:30 pm, for potty-trained children from 3, up to 90 minutes at a time, and Hilton's posted schedule ran until 8 pm. Check the days too: Club Med lists its clubs as closed on Saturdays.

Teens get a space, not always a schedule. Eight of the 11 resorts mention something for teenagers, but only three publish both ages and hours: Club Med Cancun, Royalton Riviera Cancun's Teen Lounge and SLS Playa Mujeres' Teen Lounge. Our teen-friendly all-inclusives guide covers what teens want from a resort.

The main club is included; babysitting is not. Six resorts say their club comes with the stay or is free: Club Med for ages 4 to 17, both Royaltons, Margaritaville, SLS Playa Mujeres and Grand Palladium Select. None of the 11 publishes a fee for its main club. Babysitting is extra at Royalton Riviera Cancun and Margaritaville, and costs 20 USD an hour at Grand Palladium Select Costa Mujeres. Club Med's clubs for under-4s cost extra too, listed from 82 USD for Baby Club Med and 74 USD for Petit Club Med.

Clubs Named Without Details

Some well-known resorts name a club but publish no ages, hours or rules on their own sites: the new Coral Kidz Club at Grand Fiesta Americana Coral Beach, Club Nick at Nickelodeon Riviera Maya, which the resort calls its hub for free play, RIU Land at Riu Palace Costa Mujeres, and the Kids Club at Hotel Xcaret Mexico. Hard Rock Hotel Cancun's Roxity Kids Club page gives two schedules: 10 am to 1 pm and 2 to 5 pm with limited availability, and 9 am to 5 pm daily.

For these, and for any blank in the table, ask the resort five things before you book: the youngest age accepted and whether children must be potty trained; whether a parent stays, signs in or can leave; the hours on your dates; whether care for under-4s or evenings costs extra; and the babysitting rate.

Family Photos Around the Kids Club

If your children love their club, plan the photos around it rather than against it. Online booking at ivaestudios.com/book offers five start times, all in Cancun time: 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am, and 5:00 and 5:30 pm. Any other time is arranged on WhatsApp.

Sunrise, before the club. The Chapter, the collection we recommend for families, runs 60 minutes with 35 edited photos for 475 USD. Started at 7:30 am, it ends at 8:30, so the children go from the beach to breakfast and on to the club, and the afternoon stays theirs. Early, before the pools and slides, little ones tend to have more patience.

Late afternoon, after a rest. The 5:00 and 5:30 pm starts fall just as many clubs close: 5 pm at Finest Playa Mujeres, Margaritaville, Grand Palladium Select and Hard Rock Riviera Maya, and 5:30 pm at Club Med. A full day at the club can leave small children tired, so on a photo day collect them after lunch, let them nap, and dress them just before we meet. At Club Med, where the clubs list Saturday as closed, a Saturday session costs them no club time.

Where on the resort. Frequently Asked Questions

What age can children join a kids club at a Cancun all-inclusive?
At most resorts in our table, from 4 to 12. A few take younger children: Club Med Cancun runs Baby Club Med from 4 months and Petit Club Med for ages 2 and 3, both on demand at extra cost; Finest Playa Mujeres takes 3 to 6 year olds with registration and babies up to 2 with a parent; Grand Palladium Select Costa Mujeres lists its club from 3.
Are kids clubs free at Cancun all-inclusives?
Yes for the main club, wherever a resort says so. Royalton Riviera Cancun says there is no additional charge for ages 4 to 12, SLS Playa Mujeres lists its kids club as included with the stay, Grand Palladium Select Costa Mujeres says access is free, and Club Med Cancun includes child care for ages 4 to 17. Extras cost more: Club Med lists Baby Club Med from 82 USD and Petit Club Med from 74 USD, and Grand Palladium Select Costa Mujeres charges 20 USD an hour for babysitting.
What are kids club hours at Cancun resorts?
The main clubs in our table open between 8:30 and 10 am, and most close between 5 and 6 pm. SLS Playa Mujeres runs 10 am to 6 pm; Club Med Cancun's Mini Club Med+ runs 8:45 am to 5:30 pm plus 7 to 9 pm and lists Saturdays as closed; Finest Playa Mujeres adds mealtime care until 10:30 pm.
Which Cancun all-inclusives have a teen club?
Club Med Cancun has teen clubs for 11 to 13 and 14 to 17, Royalton Riviera Cancun a Teen Lounge for 13 to 17 open 3 to 11 pm, and SLS Playa Mujeres a Teen Lounge for 12 to 17 open noon to 9 pm. Hilton Cancun and Finest Playa Mujeres have teen spaces without published ages or hours.
When should we book family photos if our kids go to the kids club?
Book a sunrise start. Online times are 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am, so a 60-minute session ends by 8:30 am, before most clubs open. The 5:00 and 5:30 pm starts come as many clubs close, so collect the children early enough for a rest first.
Does the resort charge a fee for an outside photographer?
Some resorts do. If yours does, you pay it to the hotel, and we confirm the hotel's rule with you before the session.
