Florida or Cancun for Spring Break With Kids?

In short

From the Northeast, Atlanta and Chicago, Florida is the shorter trip and needs no passport for US citizens. Cancun has the warmer sea: 80°F in March on average from 2016 to 2025, against 77°F off Miami Beach and 70°F off Clearwater. For photos, Florida's Gulf beaches face the sunset, and Cancun's Caribbean beach faces the sunrise.

Spring break with kids often comes down to Florida's beaches and parks or an all-inclusive in Cancun. We are a Cancun studio, so we kept this fair, with official data from the US Department of Transportation, NOAA and the State Department, checked on November 14, 2026. Then we add what we know best: the light.

From New York, Boston, Washington, Atlanta and Chicago, Orlando and Tampa save you an hour to an hour and a half each way. From Texas the gap nearly closes, and from Los Angeles only Tampa beat Cancun. For the Keys, nonstops to Key West averaged 3 h 35 from New York and 1 h 50 from Atlanta. Our flight times to Cancun from US cities cover more airports.

Documents are the bigger difference. On flights within the United States, TSA asks adults 18 and older for an acceptable ID, such as a REAL ID, and no ID from children under 18. To fly to Mexico, the State Department says you need a passport book; a passport card will not get you on the plane. If one parent stays home, it advises a notarized consent letter from that parent. Canadians need a valid passport for Mexico, and to fly to the US every Canadian, children included, needs a passport or a NEXUS card. Quintana Roo also charges every foreign tourist, infants included, a state fee, the Visitax, paid online. Our guide to Mexico's entry rules for US travelers has the details.

Cancun and Florida Time Difference

Cancun does not change its clocks: Mexico's time zone law keeps Quintana Roo on UTC-5 all year. Most of Florida runs on Eastern time and springs forward on the second Sunday of March, March 14 in 2027.

  • Until March 14, 2027: Cancun shows the same time as Miami, Orlando, Tampa and Key West.
  • March 14 to November 7, 2027: Cancun is one hour behind them, on the same clock as Chicago.
  • The Panhandle: most of Florida west of the Apalachicola River, including Pensacola, Destin and Panama City Beach, runs on Central time, an hour behind Cancun until March 14 and the same after.

So for most spring-break weeks, East Coast kids gain an hour in Cancun: a 7:00 am start feels like 8:00 am.

Cancun's sea was the warmest of the six: about 3°F above Miami Beach, 4°F above Key West, 10°F above Clearwater and 14°F above Destin. It was also the steadiest, 79.5 to 80.7°F across the ten years, while Clearwater swung from 67 to 73°F.

Off the Hotel Zone the Caribbean photographs turquoise, and for the 2025 to 2026 season the City of Cancun listed Blue Flag certification for five of its public beaches there: Las Perlas, Chac Mool, Marlin, Ballenas and Delfines.

Spring Break Crowds and Holy Week

Whichever coast you pick, March is shared with college students: in 2027, Penn State is off March 7 to 13, UT Austin March 15 to 20 and UW-Madison March 20 to 28. In 2026, the City of Cancun counted about 30,000 spring-break visitors and hotel occupancy above 90 percent. The party centers on the clubs around kilometer 9 of the Hotel Zone, where Coco Bongo stands; our guide to the Hotel Zone during spring break shows where families go instead.

Cancun also has a wave Florida does not: Mexico's school break, set by the SEP for March 22 to April 3, 2027, around Holy Week (Semana Santa), from Palm Sunday, March 21, to Easter Sunday, March 28. For Holy Week 2026, the city staffed its public beaches with 45 lifeguards, from 9 am to 7 pm, for visitors and residents alike. Those weeks, we suggest your resort's own beach or a sunrise start. See our 2027 spring break guide for every week and our March Break guide for Canadian dates.

Off the beach, Florida's big pull is the theme parks around Orlando. A Cancun week centers on the resort, and its day trips lean to nature and history: cenotes, Maya ruins and the ferry to Isla Mujeres.

Gulf Sunsets, Caribbean Sunrises

Here is the difference that ends up in your photos. Florida's Gulf beaches, from Clearwater to Naples, face west, so the sun sets over the water: at 7:44 pm in Clearwater on March 22, 2027, late in the day for little ones.

Cancun's Caribbean beach in the Hotel Zone faces east, so the sun rises out of the sea: at 6:49 am that day, which is 7:49 am on an East Coast body clock and later than Miami Beach's 7:23 am sunrise. Our 7:00 am online start meets the sun just above the horizon; the 6:30 am start begins in the blue light before it. In the evening the sun sets behind the hotels, over the lagoon, at 6:59 pm; our sunset and golden hour guide has every month.

Our Verdict, Family by Family

  • Shortest trip, no passports: Florida, from the Northeast, Atlanta or Chicago, or if a child's passport will not be ready.
  • Warmest water in March: Cancun, at 80°F; in Florida, Miami Beach comes closest, at 77°F.
  • From Texas or Los Angeles: flight times within half an hour, so let the water or the parks decide.
  • Theme parks first: Orlando.
  • Photos: sunsets over the water on Florida's Gulf Coast; sunrise and turquoise water in Cancun.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Cancun or Florida better for spring break with kids?

It depends on your airport and your passports. From New York, Boston, Washington, Atlanta and Chicago, nonstops to Orlando and Tampa were an hour or more shorter than to Cancun in March 2026, and US citizens need no passport for Florida. Cancun has the warmer March sea: 80°F against 77°F off Miami Beach and 70°F off Clearwater.

What is the time difference between Cancun and Florida?

Cancun stays on UTC-5 all year. Until March 14, 2027, it matches Miami, Orlando and Tampa; from March 14 to November 7, 2027, it is one hour behind them. Most of Florida's Panhandle west of the Apalachicola River, Destin included, runs on Central time.

Is the water warmer in Cancun or Florida in March?

Cancun. NOAA satellite data for 2016 to 2025 put the March sea at 80°F off Cancun's Hotel Zone, 77°F off Miami Beach, 76°F off Key West, 73°F off Naples, 70°F off Clearwater Beach and 66°F off Destin.

Is Cancun or the Florida Keys better in March?

Cancun for warmer water: 80°F in March against 76°F off Key West, in NOAA data for 2016 to 2025. The Keys for a shorter flight: in March 2026, New York nonstops averaged 3 h 35 to Key West and 4 h 15 to Cancun.

Do kids need a passport to go to Cancun?

Yes. The US State Department says you need a passport book to fly to Mexico; a passport card will not get you on the plane. On flights within the United States, TSA does not require ID from children under 18.

When should we book a family photo session in Cancun during spring break?

Before you fly, for a morning early in your stay. Our prices are the same every week, from 295 USD, with no deposit and free cancellation until a day before. On March 22, 2027, the sun rises over the Hotel Zone at 6:49 am.
