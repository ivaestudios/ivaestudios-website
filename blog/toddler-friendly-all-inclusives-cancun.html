In short

With a baby or toddler, choose the resort for what happens before age four. On the resorts' own websites, checked November 14, 2026: Club Med Cancun takes babies from 4 months in its paid Baby Club Med, 15 to 20 minutes from the airport. Family Selection at Grand Palladium Select Costa Mujeres lists a free club from age 1, a kids pool 40 cm deep and babysitting at 20 USD an hour. Finest Playa Mujeres lists a crib, bottle warmer and baby monitor for the suite. Many kids clubs start at 4.

Most lists of the best Cancun resorts for kids rank water slides and kids clubs. With a one-year-old, much of that is out of reach, because at many all-inclusives the kids club starts at age four. What makes a week easy with a baby or toddler is plainer: calm, shallow water, a baby pool with shade, a crib in the room, someone to watch a sleeping child, a short walk to the sand and a short ride from the airport. Here are those six things, and four resorts whose own websites show how they cover them.

The Gap Under Age Four

Royalton Riviera Cancun's kids club takes ages 4 to 12, and its FAQ says younger children may not be left there unsupervised; for infants and toddlers under 4 it offers babysitting on request, at an additional cost. Grand Velas Riviera Maya's Kids' Club is for ages 4 to 12, and RIU Land at Riu Caribe and Riu Palace Costa Mujeres starts at 4. Even Club Med's all-inclusive price covers child care from 4 to 17; its clubs for younger children cost extra.

So the first question is not how big the kids club is, but who can watch your two-year-old, where, and at what cost. Our guide to kids clubs at Cancun all-inclusives covers ages and hours for older children; this page is about the years before.

What Matters With a Baby or Toddler

Six things decide whether a resort works for an under-four:

  • Care that takes under-fours. A club or nursery for your child's age. Ask whether a parent must stay, what it costs and if places are limited; failing that, ask about babysitting.
  • Baby gear in the room. A crib, bottle warmer, sterilizer and high chair save half a suitcase. Request them when you book; some resorts lend them subject to availability.
  • A shallow pool with shade. A baby pool, or a kids pool your toddler can stand in, matters more than the lazy river. Pack disposable swim diapers: Club Med, for one, requires them for children under three who are still potty training.
  • A calm, shallow sea. Small waves knock toddlers over. Read what the resort or the state tourism board says about the water, and look at the sea each morning.
  • A short walk. Long, hot paths wear out a toddler and whoever pushes the stroller, so ask for a building near the family pool or beach. Size is a clue: Royalton Splash Riviera Cancun has 1,005 suites, Finest Playa Mujeres 450 and Family Selection 180 rooms.
  • A short transfer. After a flight with a baby, 15 minutes and 45 minutes are very different rides, both ways.

Resorts That Meet Them

These four set out on their own websites what they offer babies and toddlers, some in far more detail than others. Amenities and prices change, so confirm them when you book.

Club Med Cancun: care from four months

Baby Club Med takes children from 4 to 23 months and Petit Club Med 2 and 3 year olds; both are booked online at extra cost, places are limited, and the clubs close on Saturdays. The all-inclusive covers a baby bath, bottle warmer and cot in the room, a stroller on request, purees and high chairs, and a 24-hour baby feeding room with sterilizers and a microwave. In the Aguamarina family area, two-bedroom rooms sit right next to a pool reserved for parents and children: the short walk a toddler needs. The resort sits on a private peninsula at Punta Nizuc, with three beaches and a lagoon.

Family Selection at Grand Palladium: a club from age one

Inside the Grand Palladium Costa Mujeres resort, this 180-room hotel lists a free club for children from 1 to 12, open daily from 9 am to 5 pm, though the same FAQ answer says children under 18 must always be accompanied by family, so ask how drop-off works for the youngest. Babysitting costs 20 USD an hour. The kids pool is 40 cm deep, about 16 inches, and the hotel describes its private beach as patrolled by lifeguards, with parasols, and a safe swimming area. Quintana Roo's tourism board describes Costa Mujeres as a shallow sea without surf, about 30 minutes from the airport, the time the hotel gives too. In the same resort, Grand Palladium Select Costa Mujeres lists its club from age 3.

Finest Playa Mujeres: a baby kit for the suite

Finest Playa Mujeres calls its baby amenities included; its fact sheet lists them on request, subject to availability: sterilizer, electric kettle, bottle warmer, baby monitor, high chair, baby bath and crib, plus homemade purees. The Mini Club, 9 am to 5 pm, takes babies up to age 2 with a parent and children from 3 to 6 with registration, and a lobby daycare at breakfast and dinner takes potty-trained children from 3 for up to 90 minutes. Add a resting area for babies with their parents, a babies and toddlers pool and a water playground with slides. Its fact sheet puts the airport 40 minutes away and its FAQ about 45, the longest ride here.

Royalton Splash Riviera Cancun: the short ride

Royalton Splash centers on what it calls one of the Caribbean's largest on-site water parks, but its features FAQ keeps key parts of it for older children: the splash pad is for ages 5 to 12, and the Monster Slides need a minimum height of 1.20 m (4 ft). For a toddler, that leaves the wading pool, which has no age limit, and the kids club, which starts at 4 and lets younger children in only with an adult. The real draw is the ride: 15 minutes on its main page, 20 to 25 by car in its FAQ. It lists babysitting but no baby gear on the pages we checked, so ask, and request a room near the wading pool.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Cancun all-inclusives take children under 4 in the kids club?

Club Med Cancun runs Baby Club Med from 4 to 23 months and Petit Club Med for ages 2 and 3, at extra cost. Family Selection at Grand Palladium Select Costa Mujeres lists a free club from age 1, though it also says children must always be accompanied by family, so ask how drop-off works. Finest Playa Mujeres takes babies up to age 2 with a parent and children from 3 with registration. Many other clubs start at 4.

Do Cancun resorts provide cribs and baby gear?

Three of the four resorts in this guide list them. Club Med Cancun includes a baby bath, bottle warmer and cot in the room; Finest Playa Mujeres lists a crib, sterilizer, bottle warmer and baby monitor, subject to availability; Family Selection at Grand Palladium lists a cot, sterilizer, high chair and stroller. Request them when you book.

Which toddler-friendly resort is closest to Cancun airport?

Of the resorts in this guide, Club Med Cancun, at 15 to 20 minutes, and Royalton Splash Riviera Cancun, at 15 to 25, by their own figures. Family Selection at Grand Palladium gives 30 minutes and Finest Playa Mujeres 40 to 45.

Can we get a babysitter at a Cancun all-inclusive?

Where resorts list it, yes, at extra cost. Family Selection at Grand Palladium lists babysitting at 20 USD an hour, and Royalton Riviera Cancun offers it on request for infants and toddlers under 4, at an additional cost.

What is the best time for family photos with a toddler at a Cancun resort?

Early morning, around naps. Online we offer 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am starts, before the heat, plus 5:00 and 5:30 pm. For families we recommend The Chapter: 60 minutes and 35 edited photos for 475 USD, with no deposit and free cancellation until a day before.
