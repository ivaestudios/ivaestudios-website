Where the Ceremony Happens

Ask the resort's wedding team for the current ceremony venues and capacities; planners describe a quiet stretch of sand and suites suited to wedding groups, including penthouses for the couple. For photos, the beach is the star: from here, Isla Mujeres is close across the channel, and the Cancún skyline sits on the horizon, a view no other resort on this shore has.

Large suites and penthouses also make excellent getting-ready locations, with room for the whole wedding party and natural light from the terraces.

Seaweed and Weather at Villa del Palmar Cancún

Sargassum usually builds from April and is heaviest from June to August, and November to March is normally the clearest stretch. This shore has an advantage over the Riviera Maya, because the seaweed comes from the southeast and Isla Mujeres gives Villa del Palmar Cancún some shelter. It is a probability, not a promise, since the heaviest years have reached almost everywhere on this coast. Our month-by-month sargassum guide has the detail and the outlook for the season. From June to October afternoon showers are common and usually pass quickly, so we watch the forecast with you and move the hour if needed.

Bringing Your Own Photographer

Most all-inclusive resorts on this coast charge outside wedding photographers for access, as a vendor fee or a per-person day pass, and the amount moves with season and package. Ask Villa del Palmar Cancún for the exact figure in writing before you sign, and ask whether it applies per vendor or per person, because a photographer and a second shooter are two people. We send our insurance and identification paperwork to your coordinator ourselves. Our guide to outside photographers explains how these charges work.

About 90 minutes before sunset, so the portraits fall in the warm last light. Sunset here ranges from 6:06 pm in November to 7:32 pm in July, which puts a ceremony near 4:35 pm in November and 6:00 pm in June. Quintana Roo does not use daylight saving.
