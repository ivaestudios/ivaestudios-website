In short

This is the index of our resort guides: 549 guides covering 324 hotels and resort groups from Costa Mujeres to Tulum. Each guide is written from the photographer's side: where to shoot on the property, the sunrise and sunset times for that beach, how outside photographers get access and what our coverage costs.

Most photography advice for this coast is generic: golden hour, white sand, turquoise water. The details that actually decide your photos are specific to the resort, from which way the beach faces to where a ceremony gazebo sits and whether the hotel charges outside vendors. We are writing a guide for every major resort between Costa Mujeres and Tulum, and this page collects them by zone. It grows every week.

Costa Mujeres and Playa Mujeres

The Coast, Through Our Lens

Real IVAE Studios sessions and weddings from Playa Mujeres to Tulum, so you can see how the light changes along the coast.

How to Use These Guides

Every guide follows the same logic so you can compare resorts quickly. The light table is calculated for that exact beach, using Quintana Roo's year-round clock with no daylight saving. The access section explains whether a photographer comes in as your guest or needs a day pass. And the prices are our own, published: sessions from 245 to 725 USD and weddings from 1,800 USD for a half day.

If your resort is not listed yet, write to us. We will tell you what we know about it, and the guide will usually follow within a few weeks. For the wider picture, start with our Cancún photographer and Riviera Maya photographer pages, or the venues we photograph.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which resorts near Cancún do you have photography guides for?
We currently have 549 guides covering 324 hotels and resort groups, from Costa Mujeres and Playa Mujeres through the Cancún Hotel Zone, Mayakoba and Playa del Carmen to Tulum. New guides are added every week.
Can I bring an outside photographer to an all-inclusive resort in Cancún?
In almost every case, yes. A staying guest booking a private session adds the photographer's name at the front desk. Weddings are different: most resorts charge outside vendors an access fee or day pass, which each resort guide explains.
What does IVAE Studios charge for a resort session or wedding?
Sessions run from 245 USD for 35 minutes to 725 USD for 90 minutes with the full gallery. Wedding coverage starts at 1,800 to 2,800 USD for up to four hours, and a full day with a second photographer is 3,500 to 5,500 USD. Galleries arrive in one to three business days.
