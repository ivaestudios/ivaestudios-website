In short

Quintana Roo's government announced new and returning winter routes from Canada for 2026-27: to Cancún from Calgary, Edmonton, Montreal, Halifax, Fredericton and Windsor, and to Tulum from Montreal, Toronto, Calgary and Quebec City. Most start between late October and mid-December. In winter Cancún is on the same clock as Toronto and Montreal, sunset is around 6:05 to 6:25 pm, and a family or couples session starts at 295 USD.

In October 2026 the governor of Quintana Roo, Mara Lezama, announced the airline routes that will connect Cancún and Tulum with Canada, the United States, Europe and the rest of Mexico this winter. For Canadian travellers the list is good news: more cities with a direct flight and more flights a week. Here are the Canadian routes, and how to turn the trip into photographs you will keep.

Time Zones: No Jet Lag from Ontario and Quebec

Quintana Roo keeps the same time all year, UTC-5, with no daylight saving. In winter that is exactly Eastern Standard Time, so Toronto, Windsor, Montreal and Quebec City are on the same clock as Cancún and Tulum. Halifax and Fredericton are one hour ahead of Cancún, and Calgary and Edmonton two hours behind. When Canada moves its clocks forward on March 14, 2027, Ontario and Quebec go one hour ahead of Cancún until November.

For photographs this matters more than it sounds: children from Ontario and Quebec keep their normal bedtime, which makes a sunset session easy. Families from Alberta find a sunset session especially easy in the first days: at 6 pm in Cancún, their bodies still think it is 4 pm.

Cancún or Tulum Airport?

Fly into Cancún if you are staying in the Cancún Hotel Zone, Costa Mujeres, Playa Mujeres, Puerto Morelos or the northern Riviera Maya, including Mayakoba. Tulum's airport makes sense for Tulum itself and the southern Riviera Maya. Our guide to Cancún versus Tulum airport (in Spanish) compares the transfer times, and our entry requirements guide covers what Canadians need at arrival.

Planning Your Photo Session

Book the session for your second or third day, once everyone has rested from the trip and before the last-day packing. In December and January the sun sets between about 6:05 and 6:30 pm in Cancún, so we start around 5 pm; in March, around 5:50 pm.

Winter is also the best season for photographs on this coast: dry, clear and, in most years, before the sargassum season builds. Our guides to Cancún in December and to March Break family photos go month by month.

Frequently Asked Questions

What new flights from Canada to Cancún start in winter 2026-27?
Calgary (Air Canada and Flair), Edmonton and Montreal (Flair), Halifax (Porter), Fredericton and Windsor (Air Transat), starting between December 11 and 19, 2026, according to the Quintana Roo government.
Which Canadian cities fly direct to Tulum this winter?
Montreal (Air Transat and Air Canada), Toronto (Air Canada and WestJet), Quebec City and Calgary, with routes starting between October 25 and 31, 2026.
Is Cancún on the same time as Toronto?
In winter, yes. Cancún is UTC-5 all year, the same as Eastern Standard Time. From March 14, 2027, when Canada changes its clocks, Toronto is one hour ahead until November.
