Planning a photoshoot on vacation comes down to two questions: what it costs, and how much of your evening it takes. With us, a couples photoshoot takes 35 to 90 minutes, fixed by the collection you book. What changes with the minutes is what fits inside them: one look or two, one spot or two, a quick hello from the Caribbean or an unhurried evening. Here is what each length holds, and how it lines up with Cancun's short golden light.

IVAE Studios is led by Vianey Díaz, who has more than ten years behind the camera. The studio has delivered more than 500 sessions and holds 5.0 across 75 Google reviews.

What fits in each length

35 minutes: one look, one spot

The Postcard is made for couples. Thirty-five minutes is close to the length of Cancun's golden light itself, so nearly the whole session can sit inside it. There is time to settle in, walk a stretch of beach and make portraits together, but not to change clothes or move to a second setting. You leave with 15 edited photos.

45 minutes: room to relax, or to propose

The Keepsake keeps one look and adds ten minutes, for 25 edited photos. That is time to relax and laugh together, and it is one of the two lengths we suggest for a surprise proposal.

60 minutes: a full hour, one look

The Chapter gives you a full hour with one look and 35 edited photos, with room for a slow start before the best light. It is also the other length we suggest for proposals.

75 minutes: a second look

The Story is where a change of clothes comes in: 75 minutes, 2 looks and 50 edited photos. The extra time also makes room for a second setting at an unhurried pace.

90 minutes: the unhurried version

The Editorial and The Heirloom both run 90 minutes with 2 looks. The Editorial delivers 70 edited photos and is our best value; The Heirloom delivers the complete edited gallery. Ninety minutes suits couples who want two locations without watching the clock, or a cenote or a second resort setting in the plan.

Walking between spots

Moving between settings takes minutes out of the light, even when the second spot is close. That is why, for two locations and a change of clothes, we suggest 75 or 90 minutes.

Cancun's golden light lasts about half an hour

Photographers talk about golden hour, but its deepest, warmest part is shorter than the name suggests. In Cancun, the stretch from the moment the sun sits about six degrees above the horizon until it sets lasts only about 29 to 33 minutes, depending on the month. Cancun lies in the tropics, where the sun goes down at a steep angle, so the low light passes quickly.

That half hour is why length matters. A 35-minute session can live almost entirely inside it. A 60-minute session begins in the softer late-afternoon light and walks into the gold. At 75 and 90 minutes there is room for a second look and a second setting around that same half hour. Whatever the length, we build the session around the sunset on your date and confirm the exact time for your beach. Mornings work the same way; our guide to sunrise vs sunset for couples photos in Cancun compares the two.

A 60-minute session, step by step

Here is how an hour lines up with the light on December 15, when the sun sets at 6:08 pm:

  1. 5:08 pm, we meet. A short walk to set the rhythm, with no instructions yet.
  2. Until about 5:35 pm. We walk while you talk, then slow down for quieter portraits: hands, eyes, the two of you sitting close.
  3. 5:35 pm, the golden light begins. These are the frames we plan the whole session around.
  4. 6:08 pm, sunset. We close on the last light as the sun goes down.

In December, the 5:00 pm slot you can book online is the closest to that start. In summer everything moves later: on June 15 the golden light begins around 6:58 pm and the sun sets around 7:30 pm, so the same hour starts around 6:30 pm. That is later than the evening slots online, so message us on WhatsApp and we set the time to your date. Every month is in our Cancun sunset and golden hour table.

So is 35 minutes enough? For one look in one place, yes. It is close to the length of the golden light, it runs five minutes longer than a 30-minute mini, and it ends with 15 edited photos, with the first previews within 24 hours. It is not enough if:

  • You are proposing. The hidden moment and the reveal come before any portraits, so we suggest 45 or 60 minutes.
  • You want a second outfit. Two looks start at 75 minutes.
  • You want two settings, such as a beach and a cenote. For that we suggest 75 or 90 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a couples photoshoot take?

With us, 35 to 90 minutes, depending on the collection: The Postcard is 35 minutes, The Keepsake 45, The Chapter 60, The Story 75, and The Editorial and The Heirloom 90. The Chapter, a full hour with 35 edited photos for 475 USD, leaves room for a slow start before the golden light.

Is a 30 or 35 minute mini session enough?

For one look in one place, yes. The Postcard gives you 35 minutes and 15 edited photos for 295 USD, close to the length of Cancun's golden light. For a proposal, a second outfit or a second location, choose a longer collection.

How long is an engagement photoshoot?

Engagement sessions use the same collections, from 35 to 90 minutes. For two locations and a wardrobe change we suggest 75 or 90 minutes: The Story, The Editorial or The Heirloom, each with 2 looks.

How long should a proposal photoshoot be?

We suggest The Keepsake, 45 minutes and 25 edited photos for 375 USD, or The Chapter, 60 minutes and 35 photos for 475 USD. That leaves time for the hidden moment, the reveal and portraits after the yes.

What time should our session start?

We build an evening session backward from the sunset on your date. On December 15 the sun sets at 6:08 pm, so a 60-minute session starts at about 5:08 pm; on June 15 it sets around 7:30 pm, so it starts around 6:30 pm. Online times are 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am and 5:00 and 5:30 pm; other times are arranged on WhatsApp.

Can we upgrade our collection after booking?

Yes. You can upgrade to a larger collection anytime, paying only the difference. There is no deposit: you pay at the end of the session, by card or in cash, and you can cancel free of charge up to one day before.
