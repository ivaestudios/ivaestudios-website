Mexico's time zone law keeps Quintana Roo five hours behind UTC all year, with no seasonal change. A Passport for Every Child

Canada's rule is simple: Canadian children need a Canadian passport to travel outside Canada, and Canada's travel advice for Mexico says your passport must be valid for the expected length of your stay. A child passport, for ages 0 to 15, is valid for a maximum of five years, so the one from your last trip may have run out. Check every date in the family the day you book flights.

If a child travels with only one parent, in the care of grandparents or friends, or with a group, the Government of Canada recommends a consent letter signed by each parent or guardian who is not travelling, and strongly recommends that a notary public witness it. Visas and the digital entry form are in our entry requirements guide for Canadians.

What Your Provincial Plan Pays Abroad

Canada's travel advice for Mexico is blunt: it says most provincial and territorial health care programs are extremely limited in their coverage abroad, and it tells travellers to get insurance that includes medical evacuation and hospital stays. The published limits show why:

  • Ontario (OHIP). Up to 400 CAD a day for the highest levels of hospital care, 200 CAD for lower levels and up to 50 CAD a day as an outpatient, only for acute, unexpected conditions that begin outside Canada. A transfer home is not covered.
  • Alberta (AHCIP). At most 100 CAD a day in hospital, not counting the day of discharge, and 50 CAD a day as an outpatient, one visit a day. Air and ground ambulances are not covered.
  • British Columbia (MSP). Up to 75 CAD a day for hospital care, doctors paid no more than B.C. rates and nothing toward ambulances.
Before you fly

Look up your own province's limits, then insure every member of the family, babies included.

Winter Coats and One Light Layer

In the 1991 to 2020 climate normals of Mexico's National Meteorological Service (SMN), Cancun's lows from December to February average 20 to 21 °C and its highs 29 to 30 °C; the coldest readings on record for those months are 12 to 13 °C. Parkas are dead weight for a week like that. If someone drives you to the airport, send the coats home with them and ask for them back at pickup. Travel in layers that fold into a carry-on, and pack one light sweater per child for the evenings.

For an evening session in winter, choose that layer in your photo palette, and keep the photo outfits in your carry-on, so a late suitcase cannot cost you the session. The full list by age is in our packing list for Cancun with kids.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Canadian children need their own passport to visit Cancun?

Yes. The Government of Canada says Canadian children need a Canadian passport to travel outside Canada, and Canada's travel advice for Mexico says it must be valid for the expected length of your stay. A child passport, for ages 0 to 15, is valid for up to five years. If only one parent is travelling, Canada recommends a consent letter.

Does OHIP cover my family in Mexico?

Only in part. For acute, unexpected conditions that begin outside Canada, OHIP pays up to 400 CAD a day for the highest levels of hospital care, 200 CAD for lower levels and up to 50 CAD a day as an outpatient. It does not cover a transfer home, and Ontario calls travel medical insurance essential.

Is Cancun on the same time as Toronto?

In winter, yes. Quintana Roo stays five hours behind UTC all year, the same as Toronto's standard time. From March 14 to November 7, 2027, Toronto is on daylight saving time and one hour ahead of Cancun.

When are Family Day, March Break and Thanksgiving in 2027?

Family Day is Monday, February 15, and Toronto and Vancouver public schools have no classes on Friday, February 12. March Break at the Toronto District School Board is March 15 to 19, and Vancouver's spring break is March 15 to 25. Thanksgiving is Monday, October 11, 2027.
