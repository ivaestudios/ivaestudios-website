Our couples guide to a Cozumel port day is about light and time. With children, the questions change: which pier, which taxi, where a three-year-old can splash safely, and how photos fit between breakfast and the nap without risking the ship. Every port time, fare and rule below comes from official pages we read on November 14, 2026.

Start With Your Pier, Not the Map

Cozumel has three cruise terminals. Quintana Roo's tourism board places Punta Langosta in the center of San Miguel, beside the malecon, and Puerta Maya and the International Pier to the south, with taxis waiting at their exits. APIQROO, the state port authority, lists the International Pier as SSA Mexico; its calendar held 91 calls from November 1 to 22.

The Port-Day Clock, With Kids

Under Mexico's time zone law, Quintana Roo keeps UTC minus 5 all year. US daylight saving time runs from the second Sunday of March to the first Sunday of November, and during it a ship on US Eastern time runs an hour ahead of the island. Carnival's ticket contract warns that ship and shore clocks may differ, so tell us which clock your all-aboard time is on.

The US Naval Observatory has the sun rising in Cozumel at 7:00 am on November 20, 7:26 am on January 15 and 6:56 am on March 15, highest between about 12:30 and 1:00 pm. Your first hours ashore bring the softest light and the coolest sand.

Work backward from all-aboard. On the most common Puerta Maya call, 6:30 am to 4:00 pm, a 45-minute session early in the day leaves the rest of it for the beach, lunch and the nap, with hours to spare. Every hour later thins that margin, and the ship sets the deadline.

Our online times, 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am and 5:00 and 5:30 pm, mostly fall before you are ashore or after you sail, so port-day times are set on WhatsApp. If your ship sails at 7:00 pm or later, as 11 of the 91 November calls were set to, ask about a late afternoon start.

The ship will not wait

Royal Caribbean's ships wait for delayed tours booked through the line, not for guests ashore on their own; Carnival's contract leaves the cost of rejoining the ship to the guest.

Taxis With Kids: Fares, Seats, Safety

Quintana Roo's mobility law has taxi fares approved by the state mobility institute, published in the official gazette and displayed in vehicles and terminals. For scale, the foundation that runs Chankanaab park lists reference fares to the park of 15 USD for one to four people from Puerta Maya or the International Pier and 17 USD from Punta Langosta, doubling for five to eight, a family of five included.

The same law obliges drivers to use a child seat whenever a child under five rides along and to seat minors in the back. Plan on packing your own light travel seat, or choose a photo spot you can walk to.

Are taxis safe? Canada's travel advice for Mexico, updated October 19, 2026, says to use only officially marked taxis, avoid hailing them on the street, carry small bills and avoid paying by card in the car. A marked taxi from your pier's stand fits that advice; agree on the fare before anyone climbs in.

Water and Sand for Small Kids

The piers, the town and Chankanaab sit on Cozumel's west side, facing the mainland. The 1998 management program for the island's reef national park, posted by CONANP, explains why that helps: the prevailing east and southeast winds push the waves onto the east coast, and the west stays sheltered most of the year, except when winter nortes blow from the north. It also notes counter-currents near Chankanaab.

So the west side suits little ones, with care: Canada's advice notes that beaches in Mexico are not always supervised and many lack warning flags. During the session, small children stay at the water's edge with a parent close; swimming comes after. Leave the exposed east coast for a trip with no ship to catch.

For the rest of the day, Chankanaab, inside the national park, has a lagoon, a beach and a reef close to shore for snorkeling. Its foundation puts it about 10 minutes by road from Puerta Maya, 15 from the International Pier and 30 from Punta Langosta; admission runs 8:00 am to 4:00 pm, at 589 pesos for adults and 418 for ages 4 to 12, under 4 free. The Island Museum, on the waterfront, has interactive spaces children usually enjoy, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, 209 pesos from age 4. Chankanaab closes on Sundays unless an opening is announced, the museum on Mondays.

Plan B: Late Ship, Wind or Tears

Late ship. Carnival's contract lets the line change arrival and departure times or skip a port, weather among the reasons. If you dock late, the session moves or shrinks; the margin does not. If the call is cancelled, there is no deposit to lose.

Wind. A winter norte can roughen even the sheltered side: photos on the sand, not in the sea.

Tears. A tired child on a parent's shoulder can be the most tender frame of the day; our guide to family photos with toddlers covers the rest.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we do family photos on a Cozumel cruise day with kids?

Yes, if the session is short and close to your pier. In the port authority's calendar for November 1 to 22, 2026, 79 of 91 calls were set to dock by 7:30 am and 67 to sail by 5:00 pm, so plan 45 minutes early in the day. What can kids do near the Cozumel cruise port?

Chankanaab, about 10 minutes by road from Puerta Maya, has a lagoon, a beach and a reef close to shore, and opens Monday to Saturday. The Island Museum, on the waterfront, has interactive spaces children usually enjoy and closes on Mondays.

Are taxis safe in Cozumel with kids?

Canada's travel advice for Mexico says to use officially marked taxis, not ones hailed on the street, to carry small bills and to avoid paying by card in the car. Quintana Roo law requires a child seat for children under five, so bring your own.

Is Cozumel good for families with small children?

For a port day, yes, with care. The piers sit on the west side, sheltered most of the year according to the national park's management program, except when winter nortes blow. Beaches are not always supervised, so keep little ones within reach.
