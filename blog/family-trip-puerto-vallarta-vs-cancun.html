Puerto Vallarta or Cancun for a Family Trip?

In short

Cancun is easier to reach: from January to August 2026 it had nonstops from 48 US airports, Puerto Vallarta from 23, and it keeps more of them in summer. Vallarta wins the winter, with whale season from mid-December to the end of March and two rainy days a month or fewer from November to May. Sargassum is a Caribbean issue, and Vallarta runs one hour behind Cancun all year.

Mexico's two big family beach regions face different oceans: Cancun and the Riviera Maya on the Caribbean, Puerto Vallarta and the Riviera Nayarit on the Pacific. We are a family photography studio based in Cancun, so we weigh a trip by its water and light, not only its flights. Here is how the two compare, checked on official and primary sources on November 14, 2026.

Water, Sand and Swimming

Cancun's Hotel Zone is shaped like a seven. North of Punta Cancun the beach faces the Bahia de Mujeres: flat, shallow, pale turquoise water where small children can stand. South of the point it faces the open Caribbean: deep blue, with real waves and wide sand; our calm bay or open sea guide maps both sides. In Vallarta, beaches of golden sand wrap around a bay framed by green mountains, a warmer, greener backdrop than Cancun's blues. On either coast, conditions change by beach and by day, so ask your resort about the water before the kids swim.

Sargassum Is a Caribbean Question

Sargassum is the brown seaweed that in some seasons lines Caribbean beaches. The University of South Florida's outlook of October 31, 2026 tracks a narrow belt of it from the coast of West Africa to the Gulf. As in every previous year, amounts fell in October, by more than half in most regions, though in every region but the eastern Atlantic they were still higher than in most past Octobers. The lab expected them to keep falling until possibly the end of November or December, with some beaching around the Caribbean, much lighter than in earlier months. Banderas Bay is on the Pacific, outside that belt. Our sargassum season guide covers the Cancun months and the beaches that stay cleanest.

Whale Season in Banderas Bay

Vallarta's winter brings humpback whales. NOAA Fisheries says Mexico's humpback population breeds along the country's Pacific coast, and the Riviera Nayarit tourism board puts the best whale watching from mid-December to the end of March, when the whales winter in Banderas Bay to feed, mate or give birth. It lists viewing spots from Nuevo Vallarta to Bucerias, Punta Mita, Sayulita and Guayabitos. If whales are on your children's wish list, those dates decide the trip.

Rainy Months and Hurricane Season

Mexico's National Weather Service (SMN) normals for 1991 to 2020 show two different rain calendars. At Puerto Vallarta's El Cuale station, June to September average 15 to 22 days with rain a month and bring about 59 of the year's 70 inches, while November to May average two rainy days a month or fewer. Winter nights are cooler there, too: January lows average 57 °F, against 68 °F in Cancun.

Cancun's rain is spread out, from 3.8 days with rain in April to 16.4 in October, the wettest month at 10.9 inches, followed by June and September. Our month-by-month Cancun guide turns that into travel dates.

Both coasts have a hurricane season: the National Hurricane Center dates the Atlantic one, Caribbean included, June 1 to November 30, busiest from mid-August to mid-October, and the eastern Pacific one May 15 to November 30, busiest from late June to early October.

Flights and Clocks

In the US Department of Transportation's T-100 data for January to August 2026, Cancun had scheduled nonstops from 48 US airports and Vallarta from 23, and the 24,804 flights on Cancun's routes were about four and a half times the 5,443 on Vallarta's. Vallarta's list shrinks in summer, from 23 US airports in January to 15 in July (Cancun: 44 and 40): its nonstops from New York JFK, Minneapolis and Detroit stopped after April, and Chicago O'Hare went from 82 flights in January to 8 in July.

From the West, Vallarta is closer; from the East, Cancun is. Average gate-to-gate times on those nonstops:

From Canada, distance tells the same story (our great-circle calculation from NOAA airport coordinates): Vancouver is 3,545 km from Vallarta and 4,475 km from Cancun, Calgary 3,470 and 4,092 km, Toronto 3,486 and 2,605 km, and Montreal 3,980 and 2,969 km.

Mexico's time zone law keeps Quintana Roo on UTC-5 and Jalisco, along with the Nayarit municipality of Bahia de Banderas, on UTC-6, with no daylight saving time; the rest of Nayarit and Baja California Sur, home of Los Cabos, run on UTC-7. So Vallarta is always one hour behind Cancun. In US winter, Cancun matches Eastern Standard Time and Vallarta Central; from the second Sunday of March to the first Sunday of November, US clocks move forward an hour and Cancun's and Vallarta's do not.

Sunrise in Cancun, Sunset in Vallarta

For photos, the biggest difference is where the sun meets the sea. Cancun's open beaches face east: the sun rises out of the Caribbean and sets behind the hotels, and our sunset and golden hour guide shows where to watch it go down over water, at Nichupte Lagoon. So our best family frames in Cancun come at sunrise and in early golden hour. Banderas Bay opens to the west, so in Vallarta the sun sets over the bay. Which One Fits Your Family

Choose Cancun if you fly from the East, the Midwest or eastern Canada, travel in summer, or want calm, shallow water for small swimmers. Choose Puerto Vallarta if you fly from the West or western Canada, travel from mid-December to March, or want sunsets over the bay.

Vallarta or the Riviera Nayarit? They share the bay and its whales, the municipality of Bahia de Banderas keeps Vallarta's clock, and its Las Gaviotas weather station logs the same summer rains. Weighing Los Cabos as well? Read our Cancun vs Los Cabos comparison. For a day off the resort, Cancun has Isla Mujeres across the bay, cenotes and Mayan sites; Vallarta has its Malecon boardwalk, the bay's beach towns and, in winter, whale-watching boats.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Cancun or Puerto Vallarta better for a family vacation?

Cancun is easier to reach, with nonstops from 48 US airports against Vallarta's 23 from January to August 2026, and calm, shallow water on its north shore. Vallarta is closer to the US West Coast and shines from mid-December to March, with whales and little rain.

Does Puerto Vallarta get sargassum like Cancun?

The sargassum that reaches Cancun comes from an Atlantic belt that the University of South Florida tracks from West Africa to the Gulf. Puerto Vallarta is on the Pacific, outside that belt. In Cancun it is seasonal, and the October 31, 2026 outlook expected it to keep falling until possibly the end of November or December.

When is whale season in Puerto Vallarta?

The Riviera Nayarit tourism board puts the best whale watching from mid-December to the end of March, when humpback whales winter in Banderas Bay. NOAA Fisheries says Mexico's humpback population breeds along the country's Pacific coast.

Which is rainier, Cancun or Puerto Vallarta?

Vallarta gets more rain, about 70 inches a year against Cancun's 54, but mostly from June to September; November to May averages two rainy days a month or fewer. Cancun's rain is spread out, from 3.8 rainy days in April to 16.4 in October (SMN normals, 1991 to 2020).

What is the time difference between Cancun and Puerto Vallarta?

One hour. Cancun stays on UTC-5 and Puerto Vallarta on UTC-6 all year, with no daylight saving time. The Nayarit municipality of Bahia de Banderas, on the bay's north side, keeps Vallarta's clock; the rest of Nayarit is an hour behind it.
