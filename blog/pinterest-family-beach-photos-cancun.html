Can You See the Sunset in Cancun?

Yes, but not over the sea from the Hotel Zone's Caribbean beaches. They face east, so the sun comes up out of the water in front of the sand and goes down behind the hotels, over Nichupté Lagoon. On the beach at sunset, the sky above the buildings takes the color while the sea turns soft and cool.

So where does the sun set in Cancun? It moves with the seasons: at 6:08 pm on December 15, 2026, at 245 degrees on the compass (west-southwest), and at 7:30 pm on June 15, 2027, at 295 degrees (west-northwest). Sunrise swings from 115 degrees in December to 65 in June. On the long Caribbean stretch south of Punta Cancun, every sunrise comes up over the water, and no sunset goes down into it.

Cancun keeps the same clock all year, UTC-5. For your own dates, see our Cancun sunset and golden hour table by month and our kilometer-by-kilometer Hotel Zone guide.

Four Pinterest Looks, and Where Each One Works

Here are four classic family beach looks, and where this coast gives you each one.

1. The sun sinking into the sea behind you

Not in the Hotel Zone. Almost, on Isla Mujeres.

At the north end of the island, Playa Norte curves around to face west across the bay, like its neighbor Playa Sol, so the sun goes down in front of you, over the water. One honest detail: the far shore is the mainland, about four miles (6 to 7 km) away, so the sun drops toward a thin line of coast, not an open ocean horizon. On a clear evening the water in front of you still turns gold. Coming from Cancun, check the ferry timetable for your way back. See our Isla Mujeres photographer page.

2. Glowing hair, sun flare and silhouettes

Yes, at sunrise.

This is the backlit beach family photo: the sun low behind you, light on the edges of hair and shoulders. Since the sun rises out of the Caribbean, your family stands with the water at its back, and nobody squints. From April to September, sunrise on the 15th falls between 6:05 and 6:34 am, so our 6:30 am start catches the sun while it is still low; from October to March it falls between 6:43 and 7:27 am, so book 7:00 or 7:30 am. More in our guide to sunrise family photos with kids, and from Costa Mujeres, see when the sun rises behind Isla Mujeres.

3. A pink sky reflected in calm water

Yes, at sunset, on Nichupté Lagoon.

The lagoon lies west of the Hotel Zone, so from its shore you face the setting sun across water that mirrors the sky on a calm evening. With the lagoon behind your family, the backlit look works here at sunset too. Put on repellent before you dress: dusk by the mangroves brings mosquitoes. Details in our guide to sunset photos at Nichupté Lagoon.

4. Soft, even light and nobody squinting

Yes, late in the afternoon, on your hotel's beach.

Once the sun drops behind the buildings, the sand sits in open shade while the sea stays bright. Faces look smooth, and children can keep their eyes open. From November to January, our 5:00 and 5:30 pm starts fall in the last hour and a half before sunset, which on the 15th of those months comes between about 6:05 and 6:26 pm.

Why the Beach Is in Shade by Late Afternoon

Spend a winter afternoon on a Hotel Zone beach and you may watch the hotel's shadow cross the sand long before sunset. That is the low winter sun, sitting behind the buildings. On December 15, 2026, at 4:00 pm, the sun is about 25 degrees above the horizon in the southwest, so a building throws a shadow about twice its height. By 5:00 pm it is 13 degrees up, and the shadow is about four times the height. On June 15, 2027, at 5:00 pm, the sun is still 32 degrees up, for a shadow about one and a half times the height.

How early your sand goes into shade depends on your hotel's height and its distance from the water, but in December and January it comes much earlier than in summer. For photos, that shade is the soft light of look four; for winter sun on the sand, come in the morning.

Sunrise or 5:00 pm Shade: Let the Kids Decide

Both looks are beautiful, so let your children make the call.

  • Choose sunrise if your kids wake early anyway, if you have toddlers who are happiest before breakfast, or if you want the backlit glow and a quieter beach. You are back at your hotel with the whole day ahead.
  • Choose 5:00 or 5:30 pm if your kids sleep in, if you have teenagers, or if an early alarm on vacation would start a mutiny. Shade is easy on eyes and skin, and in winter you finish around sunset.

Summer changes the math: from May to August, sunset on the 15th falls between 7:17 and 7:32 pm, so a 5:00 pm start comes more than two hours early, maybe in full sun. Ask us on WhatsApp for a later start, or take the 6:30 am start.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you see the sunset in Cancun?

Yes, but not over the sea from the Hotel Zone's Caribbean beaches, which face east: the sun sets behind the hotels, over Nichupté Lagoon. For a sunset over water, go to the lagoon shore or to Playa Norte on Isla Mujeres, which faces west.

Where does the sun set in Cancun?

Toward the west-southwest in winter and the west-northwest in summer: 245 degrees at 6:08 pm on December 15, 2026, and 295 degrees at 7:30 pm on June 15, 2027. From the Hotel Zone beaches, that is behind the hotels.

What is the best time for beach sunset photos in Cancun?

For color over water, the last half hour before sunset on Nichupté Lagoon or Playa Norte. On the Hotel Zone beach from November to January, our 5:00 and 5:30 pm starts usually fall in soft shade before sunset. In summer, ask us on WhatsApp for a later start.

Why is the beach in shade in the afternoon in Cancun?

The hotels stand between the beach and the setting sun, and the winter sun is low: at 5:00 pm on December 15 it is about 13 degrees up, so a building's shadow is about four times its height. Your hotel's height and distance from the water decide how early.

Can we get backlit beach family photos in Cancun?

Yes, in the morning, with the sea and the rising sun behind your family. From April to September, book the 6:30 am start; from October to March, when sunrise on the 15th falls between 6:43 and 7:27 am, book 7:00 or 7:30 am.

Is sunrise or 5:00 pm easier with kids?

It depends on your kids. Early risers and toddlers who are happiest before breakfast tend to do well at sunrise; kids who sleep in, teenagers included, do better at 5:00 or 5:30 pm in soft shade. For families we recommend The Chapter: 60 minutes, 35 edited photos, 475 USD.
