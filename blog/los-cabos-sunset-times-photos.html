Los Cabos Sunset Times for 2027, and Where the Sun Actually Sets Over the Ocean

In short

In 2027 the sun sets in Cabo San Lucas between about 5:35 pm in late November and early December and 7:08 pm in late June and July. Baja California Sur keeps UTC-7 all year with no daylight saving. Only the Pacific side gets the sun setting straight into the ocean; on the Sea of Cortez side and the Corridor, it sets over the land, which gives softer light. Golden hour starts about 30 minutes before sunset.

Ask anyone who has photographed in Los Cabos and they will tell you the same thing: the light in the last half hour is the reason to come. Here is exactly when it happens each month, and where to stand.

San José del Cabo is about a minute earlier, and the East Cape about two. The direction column matters for photographs: in December the sun sets toward the southwest, in June toward the northwest, which changes what is behind you.

Pacific or Sea of Cortez

The Pacific side, from Pedregal north past Diamante and Quivira, faces west. The sun drops straight into the ocean, the color is intense, and the waves add drama. The beaches are not for swimming, so sessions stay on the sand.

The Sea of Cortez side and the Tourist Corridor face south and southeast. The sun sets over the land to the west, so the beach falls into soft, even light that is kind to faces, and the sky over the sea turns pink after sunset. Several of the calm, swimmable bays are on this side.

The Best Sunset Spots

  • Pedregal and the Pacific resorts: ocean sunsets, especially strong in winter. See Waldorf Astoria Pedregal, Nobu and Pueblo Bonito Pacifica.
  • El Arco from the bay: from November to January the sun sets toward Land's End, close to the line of the arch from the Cabo San Lucas side. See The Cape and our El Arco guide.
  • The Corridor bays: soft light and calm water at Chileno Bay and Santa María Bay, best for families.
  • San José del Cabo: the beach for the last light, then the historic center at blue hour.

Planning a Sunset Session

We start sessions about an hour before sunset: around 4:35 pm in December and 6:05 pm in June. Wedding ceremonies start about 90 minutes before sunset, so that portraits fall in the last light. In winter, evenings cool quickly after sunset, so bring a layer for after the photos.

A Note on Time Zones

Los Cabos is on UTC-7 all year, which is Pacific time for most of Mexico's west coast. In winter it matches Alberta and Arizona; in summer, when the US and Canada move their clocks, Los Cabos matches Pacific Daylight Time on the US west coast. Cancún is two hours ahead of Los Cabos all year. Check your phone's clock against the hotel's on arrival.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time is sunset in Cabo San Lucas?
Between about 5:35 pm in late November and early December and 7:08 pm in late June and July. On the 15th of December 2027 it is about 5:37 pm; on the 15th of June, about 7:06 pm.
Does Los Cabos change the clocks for daylight saving?
No. Baja California Sur keeps UTC-7 all year, so sunset times only move with the season.
Where does the sun set over the ocean in Los Cabos?
On the Pacific side, from Pedregal north through Diamante and Quivira. On the Corridor and in San José the sun sets over the land to the west.
When should a sunset photo session start in Los Cabos?
About an hour before sunset: around 4:35 pm in December and 6:05 pm in June.
