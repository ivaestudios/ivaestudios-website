Someone you love is getting married in Cancun, and for once the whole family is in one place: cousins who rarely share a beach, grandparents who do not often make a trip like this, children who have grown since everyone was last together. Everyone packed their best clothes. Then the wedding weekend begins, and the photographer on the sand is, quite rightly, busy with the couple. Here is how we plan destination wedding guest family photos without taking a minute from the couple's day, from choosing the day to what the kids wear.

Why the Wedding Photographer Is Not Your Family Photographer

The couple's photographer works for the couple, and their day is already full: getting ready, the ceremony, portraits, the family formals the couple listed, the toasts, the first dance. Your branch of the family may appear in a group frame or on the dance floor, but a relaxed portrait of your children with their grandparents is rarely on that list, and asking for “just one quick photo” pulls the photographer away from moments the couple is counting on.

The kind answer, for the couple and for you, is a session of your own on a day that does not belong to the wedding: a wedding guest photoshoot in Cancun, with the same good clothes and a photographer there only for your family.

The Day Before or the Day After: Which Works Better

Family photos at a destination wedding work best on the days around it, not during it.

The day before the wedding

Most of the family has arrived, everyone is rested, and nobody has danced until midnight yet, which matters a lot with small children. In your wedding outfits, stay on dry sand or a garden path, away from the waterline: a splash of seawater is hard to fix overnight. If there is a welcome dinner that evening, a morning slot keeps it free.

The preview arrives within 24 hours, so you may see your first family photos on the wedding day itself. A gracious touch: keep them off social media until after the wedding, so the couple's day comes first.

The day after the wedding

The pressure is off, the outfits can come out once more, and family photos the day after the wedding have a relaxed, happy-tired charm. After a late reception, though, a 6:30 am start is a lot to ask of anyone, so the 5:00 or 5:30 pm slot is the kinder choice. Check flights first: if some of you fly home that day, choose the day before. Plan around any farewell brunch.

Not on the wedding day

The wedding day belongs to the couple, and a session squeezed in before the ceremony adds one more clock to a day that already has plenty. Our guide to a three-day destination wedding weekend shows the usual anchors, a welcome dinner, the wedding day and a farewell brunch; your session fits in the open hours between them.

Wedding Clothes or Beachwear: What to Wear

The Keepsake and The Chapter each include one look, so pick one of two moods.

  • Your wedding outfits. The one time everyone is polished at once. Follow the couple's dress code; unless they asked for white, the usual advice is to leave white and ivory to the wedding party. Our guide to what to wear to a beach destination wedding decodes beach formal and tropical chic.
  • Beachwear. Linen, cotton, bare feet and a little wind in everyone's hair: easier with small children, and nobody worries about the wedding outfit. White, like the family at the top of this page, is a classic for a session of your own.

If you would love both, The Story includes two looks: 75 minutes and 50 photos for 545 USD.

What should kids wear to a destination beach wedding?

Breathable and easy wins: a cotton or linen dress, or a linen shirt with shorts or light trousers, in clothes your child has worn before. Stiff new shoes and sand do not mix, so pack sandals or plan on bare feet for the beach frames, and bring a change of clothes for after. Chosen this way, one outfit can serve the wedding and your session.

Staying Out of the Couple's Timeline

A few habits keep your session invisible to the wedding:

  • Ask for the schedule first. Before you book, ask the couple or their planner for the weekend plan, and choose a slot that collides with nothing.
  • Stay clear of the setup. When you write to us, mention where the ceremony and reception will be, so the session stays well away from both.
  • Let us check the hotel's rule. Some hotels charge their own fee for outside photographers, paid by you to the hotel, and the resort hosting the wedding may be one of them. We confirm your hotel's rule before the session. Our resort photographer guide goes hotel by hotel.

Cousins and Grandparents on One Beach

Weddings gather people who rarely share a beach, and that is what makes these sessions worth it. Agree on a short list of groupings before the day, and it stays relaxed:

  • everyone together, first, while clothes are clean and attention is fresh;
  • each household on its own;
  • the grandparents with all the grandchildren;
  • the cousins, from the tallest to the smallest;
  • the grandparents with their grown children.

There is no limit on people and no per-person charge, so the aunt who joins only for the big frame costs nothing extra. For one household, The Keepsake (45 minutes, 25 photos) covers a lot; with several households and a list like this one, The Chapter (60 minutes, 35 photos), the collection we recommend for families, leaves room for more groupings. Booked The Keepsake and want more time? You move up by paying only the difference. Our guide to a large family photoshoot in Cancun goes further for big groups.

If a grandparent finds soft sand hard going, tell us when you write; firm ground near the beach, like a garden path, works beautifully. One family of 14 wrote:

“They did the best job of photographing 14 people with 5 littles under 10 years old.”

Frequently Asked Questions

Can wedding guests book their own family photo session in Cancun?

Yes. Book a session of your own, separate from the couple's photographer, ideally the day before or after the wedding: The Keepsake (45 minutes, 25 photos, 375 USD) or The Chapter (60 minutes, 35 photos, 475 USD), the one we recommend for families. There is no deposit; you pay at the end of the session.

Is it better to take family photos the day before or the day after the wedding?

The day before, everyone is rested and the outfits are fresh; keep wedding clothes away from the waterline. The day after, the pressure is off, and the 5:00 or 5:30 pm slot is kinder after a late reception. Leave the wedding day itself to the couple, and check flight times before you choose.

Can we wear our wedding outfits for the family session?

Yes. The Keepsake and The Chapter include one look, so choose your wedding outfits or beachwear. If you want both, The Story includes two looks: 75 minutes and 50 photos for 545 USD.

Will the resort hosting the wedding allow an outside photographer?

It depends on the hotel. Some hotels charge their own fee for outside photographers, which you pay directly to the hotel. We confirm your hotel's rule before the session.

How fast will we get the photos?

A preview arrives within 24 hours. The full private 4K gallery follows in 1 to 3 business days, with unlimited downloads and print rights.

Is there a limit on how many relatives can join?

No. There is no limit on the number of people and no per-person charge, so grandparents, aunts, uncles and cousins can all join the big group frame.
