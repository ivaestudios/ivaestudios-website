Which Part of Mexico Is Best for a Family Vacation?

In short

For most US and Canadian families, Cancun and the Riviera Maya is the easiest choice: from January to August 2026 Cancun had nonstops from 48 US airports, the most in Mexico, and the beaches at the start of its Hotel Zone have little surf. From the West, Los Cabos and Puerto Vallarta are closer and drier in winter; Mexico City and Merida trade sand for museums and Mayan sites.

With children, the region shapes the whole trip. As a family photography studio based in Cancun, we judge each place as parents do: flight, water, heat, then light. Figures checked on November 14, 2026.

Cancun and the Riviera Maya: The Easy First Trip

Cancun is the best-connected region: scheduled nonstops from 48 US airports from January to August 2026, still 40 in July. The average nonstop took 1 h 56 min from Miami, 3 h 48 min from Chicago O'Hare and 4 h 12 min from New York JFK. Cancun matches New York's clock in winter and runs an hour behind it from March to November.

Choose the beach as carefully as the hotel. Quintana Roo's tourism board says the beaches at the start of the Hotel Zone, such as Las Perlas, Langosta, Tortugas and Caracol, have little surf and suit children; farther south, Chac Mool, Marlin, Ballenas and Delfines have more.

February to April are driest, with 3.8 to 5.9 days of rain a month; October is wettest, with 16.4, in the Atlantic hurricane season, June 1 to November 30, busiest from mid-August to mid-October, says the US National Hurricane Center. Sargassum is the other summer question: the University of South Florida reports large amounts in the Caribbean every summer since 2011 except 2013, and the daily bulletin of Mexico's Navy (SEMAR) read "escaso", scarce, on November 13, 2026.

For photos, Cancun's Caribbean beaches face east, so a sunrise session gives turquoise water, soft light and a quieter beach while the children are fresh.

Los Cabos: Dry Winters, If You Pick the Bay

Los Cabos is the short flight from the West: the average nonstop took 2 h 05 min from Phoenix and 2 h 25 min from Los Angeles. Nonstops came from 31 US airports, 22 in July; it is the closest region to Vancouver and Calgary and keeps Phoenix's clock all year. It is also the driest: Cabo San Lucas averages 13.5 days of rain a year.

Choose the beach first

The Los Cabos Tourism Board warns that some beaches are unsuitable for swimming because of rough surf, rip currents and undertows. It points to protected bays such as Medano Beach and Lovers Beach near the Arch, calls Chileno and Playa Acapulquito generally calm, and describes Palmilla's shallow water as ideal for families. Look for green flags.

This winter's whale-watching season in Los Cabos runs December 15 to April 30, set by Mexico's environment ministry; the tourism board mentions gray and humpback whales. Pack sweaters: January nights average 55 °F. The eastern Pacific hurricane season runs May 15 to November 30. For photos: desert, granite and the Arch before sunset. Puerto Vallarta and the Riviera Nayarit: Whales and Pacific Sunsets

Puerto Vallarta, in Jalisco, shares Banderas Bay with the Riviera Nayarit to the north. At Vallarta's El Cuale station, November to May average two days of rain a month or fewer, against 14.6 to 21.5 from June to September, and average highs stay between 80 and 89 °F all year, the mildest of the three beach regions.

Winter is whale time: the federal season in the bay runs December 8 to March 23, and the Riviera Nayarit visitors bureau says whales can be watched from Nuevo Vallarta to Bucerias, Punta Mita and Sayulita. Vallarta's official tourism guide says most of its beaches are great for swimming and suggests the Malecon arches, Cuale River Island and the zoo for family days.

Flights favor the West: 2 h 33 min on average from Phoenix, 3 h 00 min from Los Angeles. Nonstops came from 23 US airports in January but 15 in July. The bay opens to the west, so the sun sets over the water. See our Puerto Vallarta family photographer page.

Mexico City and Merida: Culture Over Beach

Mexico City trades sand for museums and parks. At 2,240 m (about 7,350 ft), per Canada's travel advice, it stays mild: at Tacubaya, average highs run from 72 °F in December and January to 82 °F in April, with 47 °F winter nights. July and August bring 21 to 22 days of rain each, and Canada notes that air pollution peaks from December to March. Papalote, a children's museum in Chapultepec, and the National Museum of Anthropology open Tuesday to Sunday. See our Mexico City family photographer page.

Merida, in Yucatan, is the hot one: average highs of 96 °F in April and 97 °F in May, but 86 to 89 °F from December to February. Yucatan's tourism site describes the beaches of Progreso, 30 km away, as shallow, with calm waves and no currents, and features Chichen Itza, Uxmal and cenotes. The catch: nonstops from five US airports, only four of them every month: Dallas-Fort Worth, Houston, Miami and Orlando. See our Merida family photographer page.

Which One Fits Your Family

  • First trip, or toddlers. Cancun and the Riviera Maya: the most nonstops, beaches with little surf, and New York's clock in winter.
  • Flying from the western US or Canada. Los Cabos for dry winters on a swimmable bay; Puerto Vallarta for milder heat and Pacific sunsets.
  • Whales on the wish list. December 15 to March 23, when both Pacific bays are in season.
  • Summer break. Cancun kept nonstops from 40 US airports in July. Los Cabos is driest, with 1 day of rain in July against 8.5 in Cancun and 19.5 in Vallarta, but both average 94 to 95 °F highs: plan mornings.
  • Culture and older kids. Mexico City all year; Merida from December to February.

Frequently Asked Questions

What part of Mexico is best for families?
For most US and Canadian families, Cancun and the Riviera Maya: from January to August 2026 Cancun had nonstops from 48 US airports, the most in Mexico, and beaches with little surf at the start of its Hotel Zone. From the West, Los Cabos and Puerto Vallarta are closer; Mexico City and Merida suit a culture trip.
Cancun, Cabo or Puerto Vallarta: which is best with kids?
Cancun for the most flights and calm beaches; Los Cabos for the driest weather, on a swimmable bay; Puerto Vallarta for the mildest heat, with average highs of 80 to 89 °F all year. Both Pacific bays have whales in winter.
Which beaches in Mexico are best for small children?
In Cancun, Las Perlas, Langosta, Tortugas and Caracol, which Quintana Roo's tourism board says have little surf and suit children. In Los Cabos, beaches the Los Cabos Tourism Board points to for swimming, such as Medano, Chileno and Palmilla; it calls Palmilla's shallow water ideal for families.
Is Mexico kid friendly?
In our experience, yes: 29 of our 75 Google reviews mention children. Still, read official advice first. Canada's, updated October 19, 2026, urges a high degree of caution across Mexico; its regional warnings leave out Quintana Roo, Baja California Sur, Mexico City and Yucatan, and in Jalisco and Nayarit cover only border areas far from Banderas Bay. Americans should also read the US State Department's advisory.
