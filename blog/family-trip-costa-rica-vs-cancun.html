In short

Choose Costa Rica for rainforest, volcanoes and wildlife, Cancun for easy beach days with calm, shallow water on the bay side of the Hotel Zone. Guanacaste is dry from December to March; Cancun is driest in March and April and wettest in October. The U.S. rates both countries Level 2. Our Cancun family sessions cost 295 to 895 USD.

Costa Rica or Cancun with kids? On paper they look alike: warm water, beaches, a rainy season. On the ground, Costa Rica is a nature trip with beaches and Cancun is a beach trip with nature a drive away. We are a Cancun family studio, so the climate data, travel advice, fees and rules below come from official sources we checked on November 14, 2026.

Wildlife and Adventure, or Easy Beach Days

Costa Rica's draw is the outdoors. Guanacaste, in the northwest, sits in what Costa Rica's National Meteorological Institute (IMN) calls the Pacific North, a long coast rich in tropical forests, dry forests and wetlands. Canada's travel advice lists Arenal and Rincon de la Vieja among the country's active or potentially active volcanoes, says national parks require a permit, and warns that rafting, canopy tours and other adventure sports can be dangerous if you go unprepared.

Cancun is the easier beach trip. The Hotel Zone is shaped like a 7: its short arm faces north onto the Bahia de Mujeres, with flat, pale turquoise water where small children can stand in the sea on a calm day, and its long arm faces the open Caribbean, deeper blue and with real waves. Rainy Months, Side by Side

Costa Rica's green season depends on the coast. For the Pacific North, IMN's regional study (1961 to 1990 baseline) puts the dry season from December to March, with just 4% of the year's rain. April is a transition; rains set in around the last ten days of May and peak first in June; July and August bring a drier spell called the veranillo; and September and October are the wettest. The Caribbean side has no defined dry season, IMN says.

Cancun peaks at nearly the same time. In the 1991 to 2020 normals of Mexico's National Weather Service (SMN), October is the wettest month, with 277.1 mm (10.9 in) over 16.4 days with rain, followed by June and September; March and April are the driest. Winter is the big difference: Guanacaste is in its dry season, while Cancun still averages about 100 mm in December and again in January.

Hurricane seasons overlap too. NOAA's National Hurricane Center dates the Atlantic season June 1 to November 30, busiest from mid-August to mid-October, and the eastern Pacific season May 15 to November 30. Our guides to Cancun month by month and to hurricane season planning go deeper.

Waves and Small Kids

Official advice is blunt about both. For Costa Rica, Canada warns that riptides are common, that very few beaches are supervised by lifeguards and that there are no warning signs of dangerous conditions, and it suggests asking residents and tour operators where it is safe to swim. For Mexico, it also calls riptides common and says many beaches have no warning flags.

The difference is the map. In Cancun the calm water is in a predictable place, the north-facing bay side of the Hotel Zone, where a toddler can splash in the shallows on calm days; the Caribbean side is for watching the waves. Anywhere, keep small children within arm's reach.

Airports, Roads and Clocks

For Guanacaste's beaches, fly into Liberia: Guanacaste Airport (LIR) sits 12 km west of downtown Liberia, and its destinations page lists 20 destinations in the United States and 6 in Canada. Flying into San Jose (SJO) instead means a much longer drive to that coast. Canada calls road safety poor in both countries, and for Costa Rica adds that most roads are not paved, that driving can be hazardous especially in the rainy season, and that you should avoid travel at night. With car seats, plan transfers in daylight.

Passports and children

For Canadians, Canada says the passport must be valid for the whole stay in either country, and Costa Rican customs will ask for a return or onward ticket and proof of funds. Costa Rica requires an exit permit for its citizen and legal-resident minors, and Mexico a consent form for children who are its citizens or residents. U.S. families should check the State Department's country pages.

The clocks differ by an hour. Mexico's time law keeps Quintana Roo on UTC-5 all year, which is U.S. Eastern Standard Time. Costa Rica's official clock, kept by ICE, reads UTC-6, U.S. Central Standard Time, and the IANA time zone database shows no daylight saving time there since 1992.

Cost and Safety

Is Cancun or Costa Rica cheaper? No official source compares the two trips, so price the same week in both. Published extras differ: Costa Rica's departure tax is 29 USD per person, which Guanacaste Airport says most airlines include in the fare, and Quintana Roo charges its Visitax, which the state says all foreign tourists must pay. Canada also notes that Canadian dollars are extremely difficult to exchange in Costa Rica.

Is Cancun or Costa Rica safer? Neither government ranks one above the other. The U.S. State Department rates both Costa Rica and Mexico at Level 2, Exercise Increased Caution, noting that conditions in Mexico vary widely from state to state. Canada asks for a high degree of caution in both, and its regional warnings for Mexico do not include Quintana Roo.

Two Coasts, Two Photo Looks

On camera, the two coasts barely resemble each other. Guanacaste faces the Pacific, so on its west-facing beaches the sun sets into the ocean, and early: between 5:17 and 6:09 pm all year, by our calculation with NOAA's solar equations. Cancun's Caribbean side faces east, so the sun rises from the sea and sets behind the hotels, between 6:04 and 7:33 pm.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Costa Rica or Cancun better for a family trip with young kids?

Costa Rica suits families who want rainforest, volcanoes and wildlife, with more time on the road. Cancun is the easier beach trip: the bay side of the Hotel Zone has calm, shallow water, and cenotes and Tulum add jungle.

When is the rainy season in Guanacaste compared with Cancun?

The National Meteorological Institute of Costa Rica gives Guanacaste a dry season from December to March and the most rain in September and October. In the normals of the Mexican weather service, Cancun is wettest in October, with 277.1 mm, and driest in March and April.

Is Cancun or Costa Rica safer for families?

At the country level, official advice treats them alike. The U.S. State Department rates Costa Rica and Mexico at Level 2, Exercise Increased Caution, and Canada asks for a high degree of caution in both. Canada also warns that riptides are common in both countries.

Is Cancun or Costa Rica cheaper?

No official source compares the two trips, so price the same week in both. Costa Rica has a departure tax of 29 USD per person, which most airlines include in the fare, and Quintana Roo charges foreign tourists its Visitax. Our Cancun family sessions cost 295 to 895 USD.

What time is it in Costa Rica compared with Cancun?

Costa Rica runs on UTC-6 all year, like U.S. Central Standard Time, and Cancun on UTC-5 all year, like U.S. Eastern Standard Time. Costa Rica is one hour behind Cancun, and its Pacific sunsets fall between 5:17 and 6:09 pm.
