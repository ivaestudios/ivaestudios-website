In short

Finest Playa Mujeres is one of the best resorts near Cancún for a family photoshoot: it welcomes children, and its beach is shallow and calm enough for toddlers to stand in the water. Book 45 to 60 minutes, start about an hour before sunset, or go at sunrise if your children wake early. Sessions start at 295 USD and the gallery arrives in one to three business days.

Family photos at a resort only work if the children are comfortable, and children are comfortable where the water is calm and nobody is rushing them. Finest Playa Mujeres has both. This is how we plan family sessions there: which spots suit toddlers and teenagers, which hour of the day keeps everyone happy, how the photographer gets in, and which of our collections fits a family your size.

Where We Shoot With Children

The water's edge. The sea in front of Playa Mujeres is shallow and calm, often with barely a ripple. That is the most important fact for a family session: small children can stand ankle-deep, splash and run without a wave knocking them over, and the photos come out full of real play instead of stiff poses. We start here while energy is high.

The stone gazebo and the gardens. When little ones need a break from the sun, the stone arch gazebo above the beach and the planted areas around it give shade and a different background. It is also where we take the classic group portrait with grandparents, who are happier on solid ground than in soft sand.

The Sky Bar steps and terrace. At the end of the session, a few frames from the two-storey Sky Bar show the whole family with the sea and the resort behind. We use it with older children and teenagers, who tend to enjoy the height and the view.

Isla Mujeres. For a second look, the ferry from Puerto Juárez takes about 20 to 25 minutes. Our Isla Mujeres family session guide covers the calm shallows of Playa Norte and the painted streets in town.

With children under five, we usually recommend sunrise. They wake early anyway, the beach is empty, the heat has not started, and nobody is tired from a day in the pool. From this part of Playa Mujeres the sun also rises from behind Isla Mujeres for most of the year, roughly August through April. Our piece on why sunrise beats sunset with young kids goes into the detail.

With older children and teenagers, the hour before sunset is easier. Start about an hour before sunset. The beach faces east, so the sun goes down behind the resort and the shoreline falls into soft, even light: no squinting and no harsh shadows on faces.

Toddlers, Teenagers and Grandparents

Every age needs something different, and a good session plans for all of them. Toddlers get the first ten minutes, before they lose interest, and we photograph them playing rather than posing; a small bucket or a favourite toy helps. School-age children love being given a job, like racing to the water or carrying the hat. Teenagers do best when we treat them like adults, keep posing minimal and let them choose one photo idea. Grandparents stay in the shade and on firm ground, and we bring the family to them for the group portraits.

For outfits, soft neutrals, white, sand and one muted colour across the family look best against this pale beach and turquoise water. Our guide to what to wear for a family photoshoot in Mexico has palettes that work here.

How the Photographer Gets In

If you are staying at Finest Playa Mujeres, the photographer comes in as your invited guest: give the front desk our name and the session time a day or two ahead and we check in at the lobby. For a private family session, a staying guest is very seldom asked for the outside vendor fee that resorts charge for weddings, but we confirm the current policy with the resort in writing before your date. If you are staying elsewhere, a day pass usually applies, and a public beach or Isla Mujeres may be the better plan. More in our guide to bringing an outside photographer to a resort.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Finest Playa Mujeres good for a family photoshoot?
Yes. Finest welcomes children, and its beach is shallow and calm enough for toddlers to stand in the water safely, which gives natural, playful photographs. The stone arch gazebo and gardens add shade and a second background for group portraits with grandparents.
What is the best time for a family photoshoot at Finest Playa Mujeres?
Sunrise for children under five, because they wake early, the beach is empty and it is cool. The hour before sunset for older children; sunset ranges from about 6:05 pm in December to 7:32 pm in July. From roughly August through April the sun rises from behind Isla Mujeres.
Can I bring an outside photographer to Finest Playa Mujeres for family photos?
Yes. As a staying guest, give the front desk the photographer's name and session time in advance. A staying guest booking a private family session is very seldom charged the outside vendor fee used for weddings, but we confirm the current policy with the resort in writing.
How long should a family photoshoot be?
Forty-five minutes is enough for a family of three or four. Larger families or several generations are better served by 60 minutes, which leaves time for the group portrait, each household and the grandparents with the grandchildren without tiring the children.
How much does a family photoshoot at Finest Playa Mujeres cost?
IVAE Studios family sessions start at 295 USD for 35 minutes and 15 photos. The Keepsake is 375 USD for 45 minutes and 25 photos, and The Chapter, the family favourite, is 475 USD for 60 minutes and 35 photos. Galleries arrive in one to three business days.
