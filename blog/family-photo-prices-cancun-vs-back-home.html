How Much Do Family Photos Cost Back Home?

In the United States, Thumbtack's photographer price guide, updated October 28, 2026, draws on the estimates its pros give customers and on its own research. It puts the national average for family and children portraits at $269, and for portrait photography as a whole at $277. Its family portrait guide, dated August 26, 2020, adds that sessions in smaller towns and rural areas generally cost less than in metropolitan areas, so where you live moves the number.

For Canada, we found no dated guide just for family sessions. Bark, a marketplace for local pros, shows a nationwide average of C$300 an hour for portrait photographers, with no separate family figure and no date on the page. We have left that figure in Canadian dollars, as Bark lists it.

The averages also leave out what is included. The same Thumbtack family guide warns that receiving every photo from a shoot is not always part of the price, and that having to buy the rights to them separately can raise the total sharply. It also tells families to plan on a deposit, noting that some photographers require a nonrefundable 50 percent.

Is There a Mini Session in Cancun?

Back home, the mini session is the short, lower-priced option. Thumbtack and HoneyBook both describe minis of 15 to 30 minutes, and Zenfolio's guide for photographers calls them a shorter version of a traditional session, scheduled back to back and priced below a standard one. Photo counts vary: HoneyBook says some photographers report around 25 edited images from a 20-minute mini, while one of Zenfolio's package ideas is 5 to 8 full-resolution downloads.

Our list has no mini session. The shortest collection, The Postcard, runs 35 minutes with 15 edited photos for 295 USD, and it is made for couples. For a family, the shorter option is The Keepsake: 45 minutes and 25 edited photos for 375 USD. The one we recommend for families is The Chapter, 60 minutes and 35 edited photos for 475 USD. You can move up to a larger collection by paying only the difference.

The extra minutes go where families need them: time for small children to warm up, and time for the frames on your list, like everyone together, each child, or the grandparents with the grandchildren. What It Comes To Per Family Member

Because the collection sets the price, with no limit on the number of people and no per-person charge, the cost per family member drops as more of the family joins in. The Chapter, 475 USD, works out to less than 120 USD each for a family of four. A group of twelve on The Editorial, 645 USD for 90 minutes and 70 edited photos, comes to under 54 USD each.

A bigger group does need more minutes. If you want every combination (each household, the cousins, the grandparents with all the grandchildren), a 90-minute collection gives you room: The Editorial, or The Heirloom with the complete edited gallery for 895 USD. Do Photographers Charge More in Peak Season?

Back home, often yes. Thumbtack's guide suggests finding out when the photographers you are considering have their peak and off seasons, because booking during a local photographer's peak season will almost always cost more.

Our collection prices are the same on every date, holiday weeks included, whether you come for spring break, Thanksgiving or New Year. A busy week can change how quickly the online times fill, not what they cost. The cost that isn't ours is the hotel's own fee for outside photographers, where a resort charges one, and it goes to the hotel.

Online, sessions start at 6:30, 7:00 or 7:30 am, or at 5:00 or 5:30 pm, Cancun time; other times are arranged on WhatsApp. Cancellation is free up to one day before.

Outside the price: the fee some hotels charge for outside photographers, paid by you to the hotel. Whoever you book, at home or here, these questions bring out the full price:

  1. How long is the session, and how many edited photos come with it?
  2. Is there a deposit, and is it refundable?
  3. Does the price change with the date or with the number of people?
  4. Are all the photos, downloads and print rights included, or sold afterward?
  5. Does the hotel or venue charge a fee of its own?

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a family photoshoot?

In the US, Thumbtack's national average for family and children portraits is $269, in a guide updated October 28, 2026. With us in Cancun, sessions cost 295 to 895 USD by length; The Chapter, the one we recommend for families, is 60 minutes and 35 edited photos for 475 USD.

How long are mini photo sessions?

Usually 15 to 30 minutes, according to Thumbtack and HoneyBook. We don't offer mini sessions: our shortest collection is The Postcard, 35 minutes, made for couples. For a family, the shorter option is The Keepsake, 45 minutes and 25 edited photos for 375 USD.

Do photographers charge more in peak season?

Back home it is common: Thumbtack notes that booking during a photographer's peak season almost always costs more. Our collection prices are the same on every date, holiday weeks included. Some resorts charge their own fee for outside photographers, which you pay to the hotel.

Do you charge per person for a big family?

No. There is no limit on the number of people and no per-person charge; the collection sets the price. A large group that wants every combination can take 90 minutes: The Editorial, 70 edited photos for 645 USD, or The Heirloom, the complete edited gallery for 895 USD.

Is there a deposit or any hidden fee?

There is no deposit: you pay at the end of the session, by card or in cash, and cancellation is free up to one day before. Outside our price, plan for the fee some hotels charge for outside photographers, paid directly to the hotel; we confirm your hotel's rule beforehand.
