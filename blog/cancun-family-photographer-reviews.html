In short

As of November 13, 2026, 29 of our 73 Google reviews (5.0) mention the children, and 10 describe the team as patient. Below is one verbatim excerpt from each English family review, grouped by situation.

If you are planning family photos in Cancun, the question under all the others is usually the same: will it work with our kids? Other parents have answered it in public. As of November 13, 2026, the IVAE Studios Google profile holds 73 reviews with a 5.0 rating; 29 mention the children (25 in English, 4 in Spanish) and 10 describe our team as patient. Every quote below links back to that public Google profile, where you can read the full review.

How We Read the Reviews

We read all 73 reviews on November 13, 2026 and kept every one that mentions a child, plus a few other family reviews. Each English reviewer gets one excerpt, copied exactly as written and never over 30 words, credited by first name and last initial or by the name shown on Google. The 4 Spanish reviews appear in our own words, because a translation would not be theirs.

Reviews versus policy

A toy, a ride or a second session in a review is what happened for that family. For every family session: no deposit, payment at the end by card or in cash, free cancellation up to one day before, no group limit or per-person charge, a preview within 24 hours and the full gallery in 1 to 3 business days.

Our guide to family photos with toddlers in Cancun explains how we plan for little ones: book around the nap, photograph the youngest first, and get the parents playing so we can photograph the child reacting. If a meltdown comes, we pause and let a parent scoop the child up.

With children we would rather follow than direct: instead of holding a pose, we set up a walk, a race or a parent pulled into the surf, and photograph what happens.

Teenagers, Shy or Not

Linh K.'s family had an energetic 2-year-old and two teenage boys:

“What really impressed me was how they handled my two shy teenage boys. They made them feel comfortable and guided them to pose naturally.”

Linh K., Google review

It rained on their shoot day; by Linh K.'s account the photos still came out beautiful, and that family was offered a second session on a sunny day. One Spanish review is about a teenager too: a family from Colombia reviewed their daughter's 15th birthday session.

With teens we give them a job instead of a pose, like choosing the location or the song, and promise about an hour; see our guide to family photos with teenagers in Cancun.

Resort, Rides, Languages and Delivery
