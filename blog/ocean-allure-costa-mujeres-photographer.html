Seaweed and Weather at Ocean Allure Costa Mujeres

Sargassum drifts in from the southeast, so it hits the coast further south hardest. Ocean Allure Costa Mujeres sits behind the shelter of Isla Mujeres and the bend in the coastline, so in a typical season it stays clearer than Playa del Carmen or Tulum. Sargassum usually builds from April and is heaviest from June to August, and November to March is normally the clearest stretch. Clearer is not the same as clear: in the heaviest years seaweed has reached almost everywhere here. Our month-by-month sargassum guide has the detail and the outlook for the season. Summer and early autumn bring afternoon showers that tend to pass quickly. We keep an eye on the forecast and adjust the hour with you the day before.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we bring an outside photographer to Ocean Allure Costa Mujeres?
Usually yes, as your guest: give the front desk the photographer's name and the session time in advance. Each resort sets its own rules for outside vendors and changes them, so we confirm the current policy with Ocean Allure Costa Mujeres in writing before your date. The beach itself is public.
Where are the best photo spots at Ocean Allure Costa Mujeres?
We work with The beach, Your swim-out suite and The new terraces. We choose the order by the light on your date and how busy each area is at that hour.
What is the best time for photos at Ocean Allure Costa Mujeres?
The hour before sunset, or sunrise if you want the beach to yourselves. Sunset here ranges from 6:05 pm in November to 7:32 pm in July, and sunrise from 6:05 am in June to 7:27 am in January.
How much does a photographer at Ocean Allure Costa Mujeres cost?
IVAE Studios collections run from 245 USD for 35 minutes and 15 photos to 725 USD for 90 minutes and the full gallery. Most couples choose The Glow Hour at 325 USD or The Creative Runway at 475 USD. Galleries arrive in one to three business days.
