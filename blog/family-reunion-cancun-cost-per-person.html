In short

We priced one real week for 16, July 10 to 17, 2027: ten adults and six kids from Chicago, Houston, Newark and Atlanta. With flights, seven nights, transfers, Visitax, Xcaret and one photo session, it came to about $1,281 per person in a beachfront villa before food, $2,308 all-inclusive and $3,386 in beachfront suites with business or first class. Checked November 14, 2026.

A family reunion in Mexico works best when every household can see its share before anyone books. So we priced one real week for 16 the way four households would book it, on the airlines', the resort's and the operators' own websites, and divided everything by 16.

The 16 People and the Week We Priced

  • DatesSaturday, July 10 to Saturday, July 17, 2027, seven nights
  • Who10 adults and 6 kids aged 6 to 10, in four households
  • Flying fromChicago, Houston and Newark on United; Atlanta on Delta
  • StayingA Hotel Zone villa, or Hilton Cancun Mar Caribe All-Inclusive Resort
  • CheckedNovember 14, 2026

Three households are two parents with two kids; the fourth is the grandparents with an aunt and uncle. Each airline showed per-traveler fares for a party of four, and we used that fare for everyone, kids included. Prices move daily: this is a dated snapshot, not a quote.

The stay works out to about $50, $197 and $292 per person per night, a starting point for five nights, though the villa's price included a $251.45 weekly discount. Standard fares differ by city, from $671 out of Newark to $802 out of Chicago.

Villa or All-Inclusive for 16?

Split 16 ways, the villa came to $349 each for the week and the five all-inclusive rooms to $1,376. That gap, $1,027 per person, is the villa family's budget for groceries, meals out and drinks: about $147 per person per day before the villa stops being cheaper. For reference, Take a Chef lists a private chef in Cancun at 1,100 pesos per guest for groups of 13 or more, about $59 each, shopping and cleanup included.

Among the more than 170 homes Airbnb showed for 16 guests on those dates were a house in town at $1,507 for the week and our six-bedroom oceanfront villa in the Hotel Zone at $5,581.74, with free cancellation before June 10. Choose the villa for one shared living room and one long table; choose the resort when nobody wants to cook for 16.

Room Blocks and Group Rates

Sixteen people sounds like a group, but at a resort it is five rooms: Hilton's Resort View room with two queen beds sleeps four, so we priced three family rooms, one for the grandparents and one for the aunt and uncle. A room block holds a set of rooms at one agreed rate; Hilton's groups page invites family reunions to book one, with planning help from 10 rooms. At five rooms, compare public rates first.

On our dates the public Kids & Teens Stay Free rate won: $629 a night for two adults and two kids, against $829 on the flexible rate, which charged $629 for two adults alone. It covers one kid or teen, 17 and under, per registered adult, two per room; you pay at the hotel and can change or cancel until June 26, 2027. Ask any hotel to beat that rate and to put your rooms together.

Getting 16 People From the Airport

For private transportation in Cancun, Happy Shuttle Cancun charges per vehicle, not per passenger, prepaid online. Its published Hotel Zone round trips: $164 for its group vehicle for 11 to 16 passengers, $94 for a private van for 4 to 6 and $178 for a full-size luxury SUV for 4 to 6.

One vehicle for all 16 is about $10 each, but only if everyone lands together. Our four flights were due at 8:55 am, 10:06 am, 2:26 pm and 4:45 pm, so we budgeted one van per household: $376, about $24 each. For luxury private transportation from Cancun airport, four SUVs come to $712, about $45 each. Car seats are free on request, in limited numbers, and cancelling at least 24 hours ahead refunds 90%. More in our guide to Cancun airport transfers.

Who Books What, and When

  • Each household: its own flights and transfer, matched to its arrival time. July 2027 was already on sale when we checked.
  • One organizer: the villa or the rooms. The Hilton rates are paid at the hotel and can be cancelled until June 26, 2027; the villa showed $0 due at booking.
  • Every traveler: the Visitax, 293 pesos, about $16, babies included, up to ten travelers per payment on the official portal.
  • Xcaret: online at least seven days ahead, $134.10 per adult instead of $149; kids 5 to 11 pay 25% less, under-5s go free. Our 16 came to $1,944.45.
  • Photos: one booking for all 16, paid at the end of the session.

Keep it in one shared spreadsheet: a row per household with travelers and ages, flights, room, transfer, Visitax receipts and its share of the shared costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a family reunion in Cancun cost per person?

For 16 people flying from four US cities for July 10 to 17, 2027, we came to about $1,281 per person in a beachfront villa before food, $2,308 all-inclusive and $3,386 in beachfront suites with business or first class: flights, seven nights, transfers, Visitax, Xcaret and one photo session, checked November 14, 2026.

Is a villa cheaper than an all-inclusive for 16 people?

For the stay itself, yes: our Hotel Zone villa came to $349 per person for the week and five all-inclusive rooms to $1,376. The villa leaves out food and drinks, so the gap, about $147 per person per day, is your food budget before the villa costs more.

Do 16 people get a group rate at a Cancun resort?

Not automatically. Sixteen people fill about five rooms, and Hilton's groups page offers planning help from 10 rooms. At Hilton Cancun Mar Caribe, the public Kids & Teens Stay Free rate was $629 a night for two adults and two kids, the same as the flexible rate for two adults.

How do 16 people get from Cancun airport to the hotel?

Happy Shuttle Cancun lists a group vehicle for 11 to 16 passengers at $164 round trip to the Hotel Zone, about $10 each, if everyone lands together. If households land hours apart, a private van for 4 to 6 is $94 round trip each, and a full-size luxury SUV $178.

Do photographers charge per person for a family reunion?

We do not. We charge per session with no limit on people, so The Editorial at 645 USD is about $40 per person for 16 and The Heirloom at 895 USD about $56. There is no deposit: you pay at the end of the session, by card or in cash.

Do children pay the Visitax?

Yes. The official portal, visitax.gob.mx, charges 293 pesos per traveler, about $16, and says all foreign tourists must pay, infants included. It takes up to ten travelers per payment, so 16 people pay at least twice.
