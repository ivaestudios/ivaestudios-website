How the Photographer Gets In

Access is simple for guests of Barceló Maya Grand Resort. Put the photographer's name on your reservation at the front desk with the time of the session, and we meet you in the lobby. Resorts usually reserve their outside vendor fee for weddings, so a private session for a staying guest is very seldom charged, though we always confirm the current rule in writing first. Visitors who are not staying at the resort normally need a day pass; this guide explains how it works.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are the best photo spots at Barceló Maya Grand Resort?
The water's edge in front of Barceló Maya Grand Resort, away from loungers, and the grounds for shaded frames.
What time is best for a photoshoot at Barceló Maya Grand Resort?
For most couples and families, the hour before sunset; for young children, sunrise. Sunset here ranges from 6:09 pm in mid-winter to 7:33 pm in mid-summer, and Quintana Roo does not use daylight saving. From roughly August through April the sun rises from behind Cozumel.
Can I bring an outside photographer to Barceló Maya Grand Resort?
Yes. As a staying guest, give the front desk the photographer's name and the session time in advance. A private session is very seldom charged the outside vendor fee used for weddings, but we confirm the current policy with the resort in writing. Non-guests usually need a day pass.
How much does a photoshoot at Barceló Maya Grand Resort cost?
IVAE Studios sessions start at 295 USD for 35 minutes and 15 edited photos. The Chapter, recommended for families, is 475 USD for 60 minutes and 35 photos, and the Heirloom is 895 USD for 90 minutes with the full gallery. Galleries arrive in one to three business days.
