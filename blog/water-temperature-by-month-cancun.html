Cancun Water Temperature by Month: Is It Warm for Kids?

In short

Cancun's sea stays warm all year. From 2016 to 2025, NOAA satellite readings just off the Hotel Zone averaged 26.6°C (80°F) in February, the coolest month, and 29.4°C (85°F) in September, the warmest. With kids, the chilly moment is getting out: from December to March the air at sunrise averages 70 to 73°F (21 to 23°C). Keep a towel or robe within reach, save the splashing for the end of a winter photo session, and go by the lifeguards' flag on windy cold-front days.

Will the water be warm enough for the kids? Here is the answer for all twelve months: the temperature of the sea just off Cancun's Hotel Zone, the air at sunrise and sunset, the two ends of a beach day, and what those numbers mean for babies, toddlers and big kids.

Sea and Air Temperature by Month

For the sea we used NOAA Coral Reef Watch, whose satellite product maps sea surface temperature every day at 5 km resolution, back to 1985. We took the square of open water just off the Hotel Zone beaches, centered at 21.125 N, 86.725 W, and averaged ten years, 2016 to 2025. NOAA describes the product as night-time sea temperature, so read each number as the water's baseline, before an afternoon of sun.

For the air we went to the hourly weather reports from Cancun International Airport, archived in NOAA's Integrated Surface Database. For every day from 2016 to 2024 we took the report closest to sunrise and the one closest to sunset, then averaged them by month. The airport sits a few miles inland from the beaches.

February is the coolest month in the water and September the warmest, and the whole year spans less than 3°C (about 5°F). In every month the sea is warmer than the air at both sunrise and sunset; from November to January it is about 10°F (6°C) warmer than the air at sunrise. Averages hide the cool mornings, though: at the airport, more than one January sunrise in five, 64 of 279 from 2016 to 2024, came in at 18°C (64°F) or below.

Two more comparisons from the same record. Against the 1991 to 2020 average, the last ten years ran 0.4 to 0.6°C (about 1°F) warmer in every month, so older charts read slightly cool. And the coast south of Cancun barely differs: off Playa del Carmen and Tulum, February averaged 26.8°C (80°F) and September 29.7°C (85°F). The Best Month to Swim

If the warmest water is the goal, August to October lead, at 29.2 to 29.4°C (85°F). Those are also the peak weeks of hurricane season: the US National Hurricane Center dates the Atlantic season from June 1 to November 30, with most activity between mid-August and mid-October. Mexico's weather service, the SMN, also gives October a normal of 277.1 mm (10.9 in) of rain in Cancun, the most of any month.

For warm water outside those weeks, May to July and November average 28.0 to 29.1°C (82 to 84°F). From December to April the sea sits at 26.6 to 27.5°C (80 to 82°F), the coolest stretch of the year. Is It Warm Enough for Kids?

In every month the sea averages about 80°F (26.6°C) or more, so the answer depends less on the month than on the child, and on the air. The fine print is for the youngest. For swim classes for children 3 and younger, the American Academy of Pediatrics recommends water heated to 87 to 94°F, since hypothermia is a greater risk at that age. Even the warmest months here average 85°F, so with babies and toddlers, think of the sea as a few short dips with a towel waiting each time.

The numbers also show where the chill comes from. Because the sea is warmer than the air at both ends of the day all year, the cold moment is stepping out wet into a breeze, above all on winter mornings, and that is when the towel matters most. Chattering teeth, goosebumps or lips turning a little blue mean it is time to wrap up and get dry.

What to bring to the water:

  • A towel or hooded robe for each child, within arm's reach of the water rather than back at the lounger.
  • A dry layer for winter, such as a hoodie or a long-sleeved linen shirt, for mornings and evenings from December to March.
  • A long-sleeved swim shirt. The AAP advises dressing children in clothing that covers the body and suggests looking for clothing labeled with an Ultraviolet Protection Factor (UPF); a rash guard with a UPF label does both.
  • Sunscreen to reapply every 2 hours and after swimming, sweating or drying off with a towel, as the AAP's sun safety advice puts it. The AAP also says to keep babies younger than 6 months out of direct sunlight.

Pool or sea? Ask your resort which pools are heated, and to what temperature. If a pool runs warmer than the sea's 80°F, it can be the gentler first dip for a baby on a January morning, and the sea can wait for the afternoon, when the air is warmer. For calm water, our guide to the safest Cancun beaches for kids compares the sheltered bay side of the Hotel Zone with the open sea. By the satellite record, the bay stays within 0.2°C of the open water in every month, a touch cooler in winter, so choosing calm water costs next to nothing in warmth.

Swimming in Winter, and Norte Days

Can you swim in Cancun in winter? Yes. From December to March the sea averages 26.6 to 27.5°C (80 to 82°F), less than 3°C below its September peak. What changes is the air. The SMN's 1991 to 2020 normals put Cancun's normal low at 20.0 to 21.0°C (68 to 70°F) from December to February, and the late afternoon is kinder than the dawn: from November to March, the air at sunset averages about 5 to 7°F (3 to 4°C) warmer than at sunrise, which makes it the warmer hour for a winter swim.

Winter is also cold-front season. A front can bring a norte, a spell of strong wind: during one on February 5, 2024, the city reported gusts of 70 to 90 km/h (43 to 56 mph). Wind like that churns up the sea. On Cancun's public beaches a red flag means entering the sea is prohibited, yellow means caution and green means safe to swim, according to a city release from October 2024. The same release puts municipal lifeguards on watch from 9 am to 5 pm, so a sunrise or sunset dip on a public beach happens outside those hours: keep it to the water's edge, with an adult within reach. Water in Your Family Photos

Splashing, running through the shallows, a toddler meeting a wave: water frames look best when the sea is warm and calm, and here it is warm in every month. In the cooler months, plan the wet part for the last minutes of the session, so nobody spends the rest of it damp and chilly, and bring a towel or a robe in a color that suits your outfits, since it may end up in a frame or two. On a norte day, when the sea turns rough or the flag goes red, the photos stay on the sand. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the water temperature in Cancun by month?
From 2016 to 2025, NOAA satellite readings just off the Hotel Zone averaged 26.6 to 26.8°C (80°F) from January to March, 27.1°C (81°F) in April, 28.0 to 29.1°C (82 to 84°F) from May to July, 29.2 to 29.4°C (85°F) from August to October, 28.4°C (83°F) in November and 27.5°C (82°F) in December.
Is the water warm in Cancun in December?
Yes. The sea off the Hotel Zone averaged 27.5°C (82°F) in December from 2016 to 2025, and its coolest December day in that decade was 26.5°C (80°F). The air is cooler, about 71°F (21.8°C) at sunrise and 77°F (25.0°C) at sunset, so keep a towel or robe ready for when the kids come out.
How warm is the water in Cancun in January?
About 26.8°C (80°F), the 2016 to 2025 average just off the Hotel Zone. In those ten years the coolest single day was 25.7°C (78°F), on January 21, 2018. January also has the coolest air at sunrise of the year, about 70°F (21.0°C).
Can you swim in Cancun in winter?
Yes. From December to March the sea averages 26.6 to 27.5°C (80 to 82°F). The exception is a windy cold-front day, a norte, when lifeguards can raise the red flag; on Cancun's public beaches a red flag means entering the sea is prohibited.
What is the best month to swim in Cancun?
For the warmest water, August to October, when the sea averages 29.2 to 29.4°C (85°F). Those months are also the peak of the Atlantic hurricane season, which runs from June 1 to November 30. For warm water outside the peak, May to July and November average 28.0 to 29.1°C (82 to 84°F).
Is the sea in Cancun warm enough for babies and toddlers?
It is cooler than the water the American Academy of Pediatrics recommends for swim classes for children 3 and younger, 87 to 94°F; the warmest monthly average here is 85°F. Keep their dips short, with a towel waiting, and keep babies younger than 6 months out of direct sunlight, as the AAP advises.
