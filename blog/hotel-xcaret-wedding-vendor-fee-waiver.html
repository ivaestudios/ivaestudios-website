Hotel Xcaret Vendor Fee and the Room-Block Waiver

Hotel Xcaret has one of the clearest outside-vendor policies in the Riviera Maya, and almost nobody explains it, because the photographer telling you about it is usually the one who benefits from you not knowing. The short version: Grupo Xcaret charges a per-vendor access fee that couples report between 870 and 1,500 USD, and it disappears entirely if that vendor sleeps three or more nights inside your room block. That is a real, documented mechanic, not a negotiating trick, and this guide walks through exactly how to use it, who it covers, and the paperwork window you cannot miss.

The Fee: What Xcaret and Xcaret Arte Charge Per Vendor

The number couples encounter at Hotel Xcaret Mexico and Hotel Xcaret Arte sits in a band of roughly 870 to 1,500 USD, and the word that matters most in that sentence is per. This is charged per outside supplier, not per wedding. A photographer, a videographer and a hair and makeup artist are three fees. Bring a second shooter and that is a fourth. Couples who budget for one fee and arrive with a four-person creative team get an invoice they did not plan for.

Where you land inside the band depends on three things: which of the Grupo Xcaret properties you are at, which wedding package you bought, and what category the resort assigns your supplier. Adults-only Xcaret Arte tends to sit toward the top of the range. As with every resort on this coast, treat any published figure as a reference and get your own number confirmed by your coordinator in writing before you sign. Fees are revised, and the version in your contract is the one that gets billed.

It is worth understanding why the charge exists at all, because that explains the waiver. Xcaret is all-inclusive. A supplier working a ten-hour wedding day eats, drinks, uses the property and occupies staff attention without having paid for a room. The fee is an occupancy and administration charge dressed as a vendor fee. Once you see it that way, the waiver stops looking like a loophole and starts looking like the resort's own logic applied consistently. We break down the same mechanic at a different property in our guide to the Moon Palace outside photographer fee.

The rule in one sentence

If your outside vendor books and genuinely sleeps three or more nights inside your wedding room block at Hotel Xcaret, the outside vendor fee for that person is waived.

The Three-Night Room-Block Waiver, Step by Step

The waiver is simple in principle and easy to lose in execution. This is the order we run it in.

  1. Raise it before you sign the wedding package. Ask your coordinator to state the waiver terms in the contract or in an email you can save: minimum nights, whether the booking must be under the group code, and the deadline for adding a vendor.
  2. Get your room block set up with a group code. The waiver depends on the vendor's reservation living inside your block, not on them booking the same hotel independently at a public rate. This is the step couples most often get wrong.
  3. Confirm the vendor's dates. Three or more nights, and they need to bracket the wedding day, typically the night before, the wedding night and the night after. Ask whether nights must be consecutive; assume yes unless told otherwise.
  4. Have the vendor booked under the block by name, with their identification exactly as it appears on their passport or INE. Mismatched names are the second most common failure.
  5. Submit the vendor to the wedding office with the waiver referenced explicitly. Do not assume the room booking triggers it automatically; the wedding office and the reservations office are different departments.
  6. Ask for written confirmation that the fee has been waived for that named vendor, and keep it. If anything is questioned during the wedding week, this is the document that settles it.
  7. Re-confirm about a week out. Staff change, files get rebuilt, and a two-minute check saves an awkward conversation on the day.

Do the arithmetic honestly before you commit. If room nights in your block run 350 to 500 USD and the vendor fee is 1,000 USD, three nights may cost roughly what the fee costs, which sounds like a wash. It is not, because those nights buy something real: a photographer on property before dawn, present for the welcome dinner, awake for the sunrise session, and not driving Federal 307 on the morning of your wedding. Some couples also route comped nights earned by their group's room count into this booking, which turns the whole thing into a genuine saving.

Which of Your Vendors the Waiver Can Cover

Anyone who can plausibly occupy a room can qualify. In practice that means photographer, videographer, second shooter, hair and makeup artist, and sometimes planner or florist staff who are on site across several days anyway. Each person needs their own qualifying nights, so a two-photographer team means either two waivers or one room shared between them, which is normal and usually accepted as long as both names sit on the reservation.

The waiver rarely helps suppliers who arrive for a few hours and leave: a DJ, a band, a live saxophonist, a fireworks crew. Three nights of accommodation to save a single fee does not pay for itself, so budget the fee for those and spend your energy on the creative team where the arithmetic works. There is also a practical ceiling. Resorts do not enjoy a wedding where twelve outside vendors all appear in the block, and coordinators will push back. Prioritise the people whose presence across multiple days actually improves your wedding.

"A photographer who sleeps on the property is not just cheaper. They are there for the sunrise, the welcome dinner and the moment your grandmother arrives, and none of those were on the timeline."

Xcaret vs Xcaret Arte: Different Properties, Different Photo Looks

Couples often treat these as one venue with two names. They are not, and the choice changes your pictures more than the fee does.

Hotel Xcaret Mexico is the larger, family-oriented original, built into the jungle south of Playa del Carmen with river channels running through the property, caleta inlets, dramatic stone architecture and access to the Grupo Xcaret parks. It is visually rich and genuinely unlike a standard beach resort. It is also busy: through the middle of the day there are families everywhere, and a portrait walk at 1 PM will have people in the background. We schedule around that rather than fighting it, working the river and cave areas early and the beach at the end of the day.

Hotel Xcaret Arte is adults-only and design-led, organised around artisan studios and a quieter, more contemporary palette. The architecture reads cleaner on camera, the property is calmer at every hour, and the light in the interior spaces is considerably more controlled. If your taste runs editorial and minimal, Arte is the easier property to photograph. If you want the jungle-and-water drama, Xcaret Mexico gives you frames Arte simply does not have.

Timeline: When the Resort Needs the Vendor List

Grupo Xcaret typically wants the final vendor list roughly 30 to 45 days before the wedding, with full names, identification and insurance documentation for each outside supplier. Waiver requests must be resolved before that window closes, and the room nights need to already be reserved under the group code when you submit.

Our working calendar looks like this. Twelve months out, the waiver terms go into the contract discussion. Six months out, the room block is open and the photographer's nights are booked by name. Ninety days out, we send the coordinator the vendor documentation package. Forty-five days out, we ask for written confirmation that the fee is waived. Seven days out, we re-confirm access times and locations. It looks like a lot of touchpoints, and it is precisely why nothing gets billed on the wedding day.

Late additions are usually still permitted, but the waiver becomes very hard to apply once the list has closed, and the fee reverts to the standard rate. If you are inside 45 days and only now thinking about an outside photographer, plan on paying the fee and treat any waiver as a bonus.

Best Photo Locations Inside the Property by Hour

Xcaret rewards a photographer who knows the property clock. Between roughly 6:15 and 7:30 AM the river channels and caleta are empty and the light comes in low and sideways through the jungle, which is the strongest hour on the entire property and the one only a resident vendor can use. Mid-morning, roughly 9 to 11 AM, belongs to getting ready: suites with filtered green light, then the stone corridors and architectural detail while they are still quiet.

Between 11 AM and 3 PM the sun is directly overhead and the property is at its most crowded. This is not a portrait window, and any photographer who books your couples session then is choosing convenience over your pictures. We use it for indoor coverage, shaded stone areas, or nothing at all. From about 4 PM the light softens; ceremonies that begin around 4:30 to 5 PM finish inside the golden hour, which is the timing we push for hardest.

Golden hour on this stretch of coast lands roughly 6:15 to 6:45 PM in summer and closer to 5:15 to 5:45 PM in December, so your ceremony time has to move with the season. After sunset, blue hour gives about twenty usable minutes over the water, and then the reception becomes a lighting problem rather than a light problem. There is more on how we plan a full day of this in our Riviera Maya wedding photographer guide, and the wider property list lives on our venues page.

What We Send Your Coordinator So Nothing Gets Billed on the Day

Couples should not be the ones chasing vendor paperwork during the month of their wedding, so we handle it directly. The package we send Grupo Xcaret includes the photographer's full legal name and identification, our Mexican tax registration, liability insurance details, the arrival and departure times we are requesting, the equipment list including whether any lighting stands will be used at the reception, and an explicit reference to the room-block waiver with the reservation number and dates.

We also ask the coordinator three questions in writing every time: what time can we enter the property, are there any areas closed to outside vendors, and is drone use permitted on the wedding date. Those three answers shape the timeline more than anything else, and getting them early means the schedule we build is the schedule that runs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the Hotel Xcaret wedding vendor fee?
Outside vendor fees at Hotel Xcaret and Hotel Xcaret Arte are reported in the range of 870 to 1,500 USD per vendor, with the position inside that band depending on the property, the wedding package and the vendor category. It is charged per supplier, so a photographer, a videographer and a hair and makeup artist can each carry their own fee. Confirm the exact figure in writing with your wedding coordinator before you sign, because it is revised periodically.
How does the Hotel Xcaret room-block vendor fee waiver work?
The waiver removes the outside vendor fee when the vendor books and sleeps three or more nights inside your wedding room block. The vendor has to be reserved under your group code rather than as an independent booking, the room nights must be genuinely occupied, and the request has to be made before your vendor list closes. Because Hotel Xcaret is all-inclusive, those nights also cover the vendor's meals, which is why the resort is willing to drop the access charge.
Which vendors can the room-block waiver cover?
In practice the waiver is applied to any outside supplier who can be booked into the block: photographer, videographer, second shooter, hair and makeup artist, and in some cases planner or florist staff. Each person needs their own qualifying room nights, so two photographers means two waivers or one shared room booked for both. Suppliers who only arrive for a few hours on the wedding day, such as a DJ or a band, rarely make the arithmetic work.
Is the fee different at Hotel Xcaret Arte?
Hotel Xcaret Arte is an adults-only property within the same Grupo Xcaret family and it sits toward the upper end of the reported 870 to 1,500 USD band, though the structure and the room-night waiver work the same way. The bigger practical difference for photography is the architecture: Arte is quieter, more design-led and adults-only, while Hotel Xcaret Mexico is larger, family-oriented and busier through the middle of the day.
When does Hotel Xcaret need the final vendor list?
Grupo Xcaret typically wants the vendor list and supporting documents roughly 30 to 45 days before the wedding, with names, identification and insurance details for every outside supplier. Waiver requests need to land before that window closes, and room nights must already be reserved under the group code. Late additions are usually still allowed but the waiver becomes much harder to apply, and the fee is then billed at the standard rate.
