In short

February in Cancun is sunny and dry: average highs of 30°C (86°F), lows of 21°C (69°F), only 59 mm of rain and a sea of about 26°C (79°F). Cold fronts still bring a windy day now and then. Sunset moves from 6:37 pm on February 1 to 6:51 pm on February 28. In 2027 the busiest days are February 13 to 15, when Valentine's Day, the Super Bowl and Presidents' Day fall on the same weekend.

February is the month we recommend most often to people who can choose their dates. It is drier than January, the evenings are a little warmer and the sunset is later every day. Here is what to expect, with Mexico's official climate normals, this winter's cold-front forecast and the light times we use to schedule sessions on this coast.

Temperatures and rain are the official 1991 to 2020 normals of Mexico's national weather service (SMN) for the Cancun station. Sea temperature is the typical February reading for this coast, and the light times are calculated for the Hotel Zone beach.

Dry, Sunny and a Little Warmer

February has almost half the rain of January: 59 mm in an average year against 104 mm. Days average 30°C (86°F) and nights 21°C (69°F), with low humidity. It is the kind of weather where you can plan an outdoor dinner a week ahead and expect it to happen.

Cold fronts, called nortes, are still part of the season. For 2026 to 2027 the SMN forecasts 56 across Mexico, above the average of 50, with seven expected in February. Not every front reaches Cancun. When one does, it brings a day or two of wind from the north, some cloud and rough water, and then some of the clearest skies of the year.

How we plan around a norte

We watch the forecast from 72 hours out and keep a backup evening in every February booking. On a windy day we move to the side of the coast that is sheltered from the north wind instead of cancelling.

The Sea in February

The Caribbean is at its coolest of the year in February, which here means about 26°C (79°F). That is comfortable for swimming and for photos in the shallows. On the day a norte arrives the waves build and some beaches fly a red flag; by the next day or the one after, the water is usually calm again.

Seaweed in February

February normally falls in the clean season, before the sargassum that drifts in from the Atlantic begins to build in spring. The caveat is recent history: 2026 was a record sargassum year and the seaweed started arriving in January. Esteban Amaro Mauricio, who directs the Quintana Roo Sargassum Monitoring Network, has warned that it could return as early as January 2027.

Our advice is simple: expect clean beaches and check the forecast the week you travel. If patches arrive, the beaches that face Isla Mujeres, such as Playa Mujeres and Costa Mujeres, are usually the last affected, and we move sessions to the shore that is clear that day. See our sargassum season guide and the beach-by-beach forecast.

Sunrise, Golden Hour and Sunset

By February the evenings are noticeably longer: sunset is 14 minutes later at the end of the month than at the start. Quintana Roo keeps the same clock all year, with no daylight saving, and in February that clock matches Eastern Time in New York and Toronto. These are the times for the Hotel Zone beach:

"Golden light" is when the sun drops below six degrees and the color turns warm; "last light" is the end of civil twilight, when the sky still glows. For a one-hour session we start about an hour before sunset, so around 5:40 pm in mid-February. Sunrise is still after 7 am, which keeps a morning session civilized. The Dates That Fill Up in 2027

January 30 to February 1. The first Monday of February is a public holiday in Mexico, so this is a long weekend for Mexican families and the resorts are livelier.

February 13 to 15. This is the weekend to plan around. Valentine's Day falls on Sunday, February 14, the same evening as Super Bowl LXI, and Monday, February 15 is Presidents' Day in the United States. Some school districts in the United States take that whole week off. Sunset sessions on the 13th and 14th are the first of the month to go. If a proposal is the plan, read our guide to Valentine's Day in Cancun.

The rest of the month. Outside those two weekends February is steady rather than crowded, and the hour before sunset on the beach is easy to enjoy. Ask your resort early about access for outside vendors; our guide to bringing an outside photographer to a resort covers the usual rules.

What to Wear in February

Days are warm enough for linen, slip dresses and bare shoulders, and the evening breeze is gentle on most days. Fabrics with a little weight move well; very light short skirts fight the wind. A light layer for after sunset is still worth packing.

February light is clear, so white, sand, soft blue and sage look fresh against the water, and one warm accent such as terracotta or rust photographs beautifully at golden hour. Our guides to family outfits and couples outfits have full palettes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is February a good time to visit Cancun?
Yes. February is one of the driest and sunniest months, with warm days, mild evenings and usually very little seaweed. The busiest days in 2027 are February 13 to 15, when Valentine's Day, the Super Bowl and Presidents' Day fall on the same weekend.
What is the weather like in Cancun in February?
The official SMN normals for Cancun give an average high of 30°C (86°F), an average low of 21°C (69°F) and 59 mm of rain in February. A cold front can still bring a windy day or two.
Is there seaweed in Cancun in February?
Usually very little, because sargassum normally builds from spring. In 2026 it arrived early, in January, and the director of the Quintana Roo Sargassum Monitoring Network has warned it could return as early as January 2027, so check the forecast the week you travel.
Is the water warm in Cancun in February?
The sea is about 26°C (79°F), the coolest of the year here and still comfortable for swimming. It can be rough on the day a cold front passes.
What time is sunset in Cancun in February?
On the Hotel Zone beach, sunset is at 6:37 pm on February 1, 6:45 pm on February 14 and 6:51 pm on February 28. Warm golden light starts about 30 minutes earlier. Quintana Roo does not use daylight saving.
How far ahead should we book a photographer for Valentine's weekend?
As soon as you have flights. Sunset sessions on February 13 and 14 are the first of the month to go, and resorts often host events that affect access for outside vendors on those evenings.
