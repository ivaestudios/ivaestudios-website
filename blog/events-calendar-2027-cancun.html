A few dates a year change a family trip to Cancun: long weekends, festivals, the seasons of whale sharks and sea turtles. Here is 2027 in one table, with what each means for crowds, kids and photos.

February: Carnival and a Long Weekend

The first Monday holiday of 2027 makes a long weekend from Saturday, January 30 to Monday, February 1, Constitution Day, a rest day under Article 74 of the Federal Labor Law and a school holiday.

Carnival moves with Easter. Easter Sunday 2027 is March 28, which puts Ash Wednesday on February 10 and carnival just before it. Cancun's city government announced its 2026 edition on January 31, 2026: February 12 to 17, with the burning of bad mood and comparsas in Parque de las Palapas, concerts at night on the Malecon Tajamar and a free closing concert at Beto Avila stadium. The city counted more than 88,000 people in 2025, describes the carnival as a space for families and kept a ban on alcohol sales at the 2025 edition. All of it is downtown, away from the Hotel Zone beaches. For Cozumel's carnival, we found no official 2027 dates yet.

March: Equinox, Holy Week and School Break

Monday, March 15 is the Benito Juarez holiday, so another long weekend runs March 13 to 15, during spring break season; our spring break and Easter 2027 guide lines up the breaks.

The equinox. The spring equinox falls on Saturday, March 20, at 3:25 pm Cancun time, per the U.S. Naval Observatory. On the days around it, says INAH, the federal institute that runs Chichen Itza, seven triangles of light and shadow form on the balustrade of the north staircase of the Temple of Kukulcan from about 3 to 5 pm and join the carved serpent head at its base. Chichen Itza is in Yucatan, one hour behind Cancun all year, so that is 4 to 6 pm Cancun time.

Crowds are large: 32,872 people came in 2025. In 2026 INAH closed the ticket windows at 4 pm local time and did not allow tripods, camera lenses, food or pets inside; drones were banned and professional photo gear needed a permit. If the 2027 rules are similar, with young kids that means a long drive, a hot crowd and no snacks. That year INAH suggested nearby sites such as Izamal, Ek' Balam, Uxmal and Kabah; its equinox operation also covered Tulum and Coba. See Mayan ruins with kids near Cancun and our INAH photo permits guide.

Holy Week. Palm Sunday is March 21, Good Friday March 26, Easter Sunday March 28 and Easter Monday March 29, and Mexico's Secretariat of Public Education set the school break from March 22 to April 3. With Mexican school vacation on top of spring break, we expect the busiest public beaches of the spring, so we plan sessions at sunrise or on your resort's beach.

May to September: Whale Sharks, Turtles and Summer

Whale sharks. The Whale Shark Biosphere Reserve, off the north coast of Quintana Roo, sets its season from May 15 to September 17 each year, with up to 160 boats and 1,350 visitors a day. Boats leave from Holbox and Chiquila and from ports south of Isla Contoy, such as Isla Mujeres and Cancun. Everyone in the water wears a life vest or a 4 mm wetsuit and stays at least two meters from the animals. Ask your operator about ages for children, and keep photos for another day. Staying on Holbox? See our Holbox family photo session guide.

Sea turtles. The City of Cancun says the nesting season runs from May to October or November each year. In 2025 its ecology department protected 10,132 nests in 52 beach corrals, nearly all of them green turtles. At a hatchling release, Mexico's federal standard NOM-162-SEMARNAT-2012 says visitors may not handle the hatchlings, must stay at least two meters behind them and may not use flash. Our sea turtle season guide covers photos near the nests.

Summer. Mexican schools close on Friday, July 9, 2027, so from July 10 we plan more sunrise sessions; our 2027 school breaks guide covers the rest of the year.

September to December: Holidays and Day of the Dead

Independence Day. September 16, a federal rest day, is a Thursday in 2027; we plan that week like a long weekend.

Day of the Dead. UNESCO has listed Dia de Muertos, celebrated at the end of October and the start of November, as intangible heritage since 2008. Grupo Xcaret held the 20th Festival de Vida y Muerte at Parque Xcaret, near Playa del Carmen, from October 30 to November 2, 2026, with altars, music, dance, food and a children's area; the 2027 dates were not out when we checked. See our Xcaret parks with kids guide.

Revolution Day. The holiday falls on Monday, November 15, making a long weekend from November 13 to 15.

Riviera Maya Jazz Festival. We found no official dates for a 2027 edition on November 14, 2026, so it is not in the table.

What are the main events around Cancun in 2027?

Carnival in early February; the Chichen Itza equinox on March 20; Holy Week, March 21 to 28, with Mexico's school break to April 3; whale sharks from May 15 to September 17; Independence Day on September 16; and Day of the Dead at the turn of October and November.

When is the spring equinox at Chichen Itza in 2027?

Saturday, March 20, 2027. INAH says the serpent of light and shadow shows on the days around it, from about 3 to 5 pm local time, which is 4 to 6 pm in Cancun.

When is Semana Santa 2027?

Holy Week runs from Palm Sunday, March 21, to Easter Sunday, March 28, with Good Friday on March 26. Mexican schools are off from March 22 to April 3, so we plan family photos at sunrise or on your resort's own beach.

When is whale shark season near Cancun?

From May 15 to September 17 each year, under the management program of the Whale Shark Biosphere Reserve, with up to 1,350 visitors a day. Ask your tour operator about ages for children.
