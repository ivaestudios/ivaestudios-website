In short

On November 14, 2026 we confirmed eight resorts around Cancun with a water park, splash area or lazy river on their own websites: Seadust and Crown Paradise Club in the Hotel Zone; Riu Dunamar, Planet Hollywood and Majestic Mirage in Costa Mujeres; Royalton Splash and Royalton Riviera Cancun on Highway 307; and Nickelodeon in the Riviera Maya. Lazy rivers among them: Royalton Splash, Aqua Nick and Planet Hollywood. Of the eight, only Aqua Nick sells day tickets. The Moon Palace and Hyatt websites did not open for our check. For photos, go before the slides open or on the beach that evening.

A water park is where kids are happiest on a Cancun vacation. Below: the resorts that list one on their own websites, with the age rules they publish, whether day visitors get in and where each one sits. Then the part we know best: fitting family photos around the slides.

Slides for the Kids, the Beach for Portraits

Water parks are built for play: bright slides, wet hair, swimsuits and other families everywhere. The kids' faces light up there, but it is the hardest place to make a portrait you will frame. So we treat the slides as the reward and plan the photos on the beach.

  • Before the slides open. Our online morning starts are 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am, Cancun time. The Chapter, the 60-minute session we recommend for families, ends by 8:30 am even from the latest start, and Seadust's website says its water park's family hours start at 10:00 am. With children under five, sunrise is our pick anyway: they are up early and the sand is still cool.
  • On the beach that evening. The 5:00 and 5:30 pm starts leave the day for the slides. Build in a break: the family hours Seadust publishes for its water park leave a gap from 3:00 to 4:00 pm, a natural window for showers, dry hair and clean clothes.
  • Check the sunset for your dates. In summer, sunset comes close to two hours after our 5:30 pm online start, so message us on WhatsApp for a later one; our sunset and golden hour table by month has the times.

A Short Candid Set by the Water

Some families want a few frames of the kids where they were happiest. That can work as a short closing set after the portraits, when three things line up.

  • The resort says yes. Pools and water parks belong to the hotel, so the resort decides whether and when we can photograph there. Some hotels charge a fee for an outside photographer, which you pay to the hotel; we confirm your resort's rule before your date.
  • The spot is quiet. Water play areas are shared, so the edge of a splash zone, or a slide entrance before it fills, makes a cleaner frame than the middle of the action.
  • The clothes fit the plan. For a swimsuit set, choose a collection with two looks: The Story (75 minutes, 50 photos, two looks, 545 USD), The Editorial or The Heirloom. You can move up a collection by paying only the difference.

In the water, keep an adult within arm's reach of each small child and follow the resort's height rules and lifeguards.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which resorts near Cancun have a water park?
Checked on their own websites on November 14, 2026: Seadust Cancun and Crown Paradise Club Cancun in the Hotel Zone; Riu Dunamar (shared with Riu Palace Costa Mujeres), Planet Hollywood Cancun and Majestic Mirage Costa Mujeres in Costa Mujeres; Royalton Splash Riviera Cancun and Royalton Riviera Cancun in Riviera Cancun; and Nickelodeon Hotels & Resorts Riviera Maya, home of Aqua Nick. The Moon Palace and Hyatt websites did not open for our check, so ask those resorts directly.
Which Cancun resorts have a lazy river?
Of the resorts whose websites we could open on November 14, 2026, Royalton Splash Riviera Cancun lists two lazy rivers, Aqua Nick at Nickelodeon Hotels & Resorts Riviera Maya has a 1,100-foot lazy river plus an adventure river, and Planet Hollywood Cancun, in Costa Mujeres, has one. The Moon Palace and Hyatt websites did not open for our check, so ask those resorts directly.
Can you use a resort water park in Cancun without staying there?
Of the eight resorts we checked, only Aqua Nick, at Nickelodeon Hotels & Resorts Riviera Maya, sells day tickets on its own website: adult tickets from age 10, child tickets for ages 3 to 9. The others did not mention day access, so ask the resort.
Are there age or height limits at Cancun resort water parks?
It depends on the resort. Aqua Nick says each attraction has its own height requirement and guests under 18 must enter with an adult. Seadust says children should be registered in its Kids Club, for ages 5 to 12. Royalton Splash Riviera Cancun has no age restrictions for general resort areas. The others list none.
When should we take family photos if we are staying at a water park resort?
Before the slides open or on the beach that evening. Our online times are 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am and 5:00 and 5:30 pm, Cancun time. The Chapter, the 60-minute collection we recommend for families, costs 475 USD with no per-person charge.
Can you photograph our kids at the water park itself?
Only if the resort allows it, because pools and water parks belong to the hotel, and some hotels charge an outside photographer fee that you pay to the hotel. We confirm your resort's rule before your date and suggest a short set after the portraits.
