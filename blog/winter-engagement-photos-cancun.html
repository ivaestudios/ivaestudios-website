Back home, winter engagement photos mean gloves, a scarf and twenty minutes before your hands go numb. Google Trends lists "winter engagement photos" among the fastest-rising engagement searches in the United States this fall, up 170 percent, and more couples are solving the cold the simple way: a long weekend in the sun. Here is how we plan a winter engagement session on this coast, from the weather to the save-the-date deadline.

Why Winter Is a Great Season Here

Winter is the dry season on the Mexican Caribbean. The humidity drops, the air is clear after each cold front and the water turns the bright turquoise people imagine. For photos, that means comfortable skin, hair that behaves and colors that look true. It is also a beautiful contrast for your save-the-dates: a warm beach photo in an envelope that arrives in January.

And it is short to get here. Cancún's airport has nonstop flights from most large US and Canadian cities, so a three-night trip can easily hold an engagement session, a couple of beach days and a celebration dinner. Our guide to a destination engagement trip has ideas for building the weekend.

March32°C / 90°F21°C / 70°F39 mm

These are the official 1991 to 2020 normals of Mexico's national weather service (SMN) for Cancún. December and January bring most of the season's cold fronts, the nortes: a windy day or two with clouds and rough water, then clear skies. For the 2026 to 2027 season the SMN forecasts nine fronts across Mexico in December, eight in January, seven in February and six in March. February and March are drier and warmer, but March is also spring break, and Holy Week 2027 runs from March 21 to 28, the busiest week on the beaches.

Our pick

For the calmest beaches and steady weather, aim for the first half of December, the second half of January or February. We always keep a backup day in case a norte arrives.

When to Take Engagement Photos

"When do you take engagement photos" is one of the fastest-growing engagement questions on Google this season. The honest answer depends on what the photos are for.

  • For save-the-dates: a destination wedding sends them 10 to 12 months ahead, as we explain in our save-the-date guide. So the session should be 10 to 14 months before the wedding. A winter session this year is perfect for a wedding in late 2027.
  • For the wedding website and welcome bags: any time up to three or four months before the wedding.
  • As a scouting trip: if you are getting married in Cancún or the Riviera Maya, a winter session lets you see your resort, meet your photographer and test the light before the big day.

Because our galleries arrive in one to three business days, even a trip in January leaves plenty of time to design and print.

Where to Shoot

The Hotel Zone beaches give the classic look: white sand and turquoise water, with soft dunes and palms. Isla Mujeres adds colorful streets and a quiet sunrise on its eastern edge. Playa del Carmen mixes beach and town for a more urban, editorial feel. And a cenote gives an unforgettable frame of stone, roots and clear water; see what to expect in our cenote couples photoshoot guide.

If you are already engaged and planning the proposal story too, our guide to a proposal followed by an engagement shoot shows how to fit both into one trip.

What to Wear

Winter here lets you dress up without overheating. Long dresses, tailored linen suits and light knits all work, and evenings around 20 to 21°C mean a second, warmer look is comfortable after sunset. Bring two outfits: one light and flowing for the beach, one more formal or more "you" for the second half. Fabrics with some weight look best in the breeze. Frequently Asked Questions

Is winter a good time for engagement photos in Cancún?
Yes. December to March is the dry season, with the lowest humidity of the year, clear air after each cold front and average highs of 29 to 32°C (84 to 90°F). Keep a backup day in December and January in case a windy norte arrives.
When should you take engagement photos for a destination wedding?
For save-the-dates, 10 to 14 months before the wedding, since destination weddings send them 10 to 12 months ahead. For a wedding website or welcome bags, any time up to three or four months before.
What time is sunset in Cancún in winter?
On the Hotel Zone beach, sunset is at 6:08 pm in mid-December, 6:26 pm in mid-January, 6:45 pm in mid-February and 6:56 pm in mid-March. Sessions usually start about an hour earlier.
What should we wear for winter engagement photos on the beach?
Two outfits: one light and flowing for the beach and one more formal or personal for the second half. Fabrics with some weight look best in the breeze, and a light layer helps after sunset, when it cools to about 20°C.
How much is an engagement photoshoot in Cancún?
IVAE Studios sessions start at 245 USD for 35 minutes. Essential Flow is 395 USD for 60 minutes and 35 photos, and Creative Runway, ideal for two outfits, is 475 USD for 75 minutes and 50 photos.
