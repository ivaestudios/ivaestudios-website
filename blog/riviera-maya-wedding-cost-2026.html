Riviera Maya Wedding Cost in 2026: Resorts, Mayakoba and the Real Line Items

In short

The Riviera Maya is the corridor from Puerto Morelos to Tulum, 45 to 55 minutes from Cancún airport at Playa del Carmen and Mayakoba, and it is the easiest place on the coast to budget: all-inclusive resorts bundle food, drink and coordination into tiered packages, so the three bands, 30,000 to 50,000 USD for 30 guests, 75,000 to 120,000 for 80 and 150,000 to 250,000 or more for an editorial week, hold with fewer surprises. Mayakoba is the luxury benchmark; outside vendor fees and guest rooms are the line items that move quietly.

Couples say Riviera Maya as if it were one place. It is a 130-kilometre corridor with five wedding worlds on it, and the cost of your wedding depends far more on which of them you choose than on the word Mexico. Here is the honest budget map.

One corridor, five wedding worlds

From north to south: Playa del Carmen, with rooftop receptions, the Playacar beachfront and the widest resort choice on the coast; Akumal and Xpu-Ha, protected turtle bays and the calmest water, quiet and private; Mayakoba, the private canal network with Banyan Tree and Rosewood; and Tulum at the southern end, boho and boutique, which we cover in its own cost guide. Playa del Carmen and Mayakoba sit 45 to 55 minutes from Cancún airport, which matters for every guest transfer and every vendor quote. The corridor's advantage over Tulum is polish and predictability; its advantage over the Cancún hotel zone is choice.

All-inclusive packages and what they really cover

Most Riviera Maya weddings happen at all-inclusive resorts, and that is why the budget is easier than in Tulum: the package bundles the ceremony setup, a reception menu, unlimited food and drink for guests staying on property, and an on-site coordinator. What the package rarely covers is the part that makes the wedding yours: upgraded flowers, a live band, a private dinner outside the standard menu, extended reception hours and, critically, your own photographer and videographer. Read the package as a floor, not a total, and ask for the list of what counts as an upgrade before you sign.

Mayakoba and the luxury benchmark

Mayakoba is the luxury benchmark of the corridor: Banyan Tree, Rosewood and Fairmont on a private canal network behind a protected beach. Weddings here sit firmly in the upper bands, with venue buyouts, private villas for the wedding party and vendor standards to match. It is also the corridor's most editorial setting: canals at first light, jungle boardwalks and a beach that stays calm. If the wedding is the editorial week at 150,000 to 250,000 USD or more, this is usually where it happens.

Outside vendor fees, rooms and the quiet line items

Two line items move Riviera Maya budgets quietly. The first is the outside vendor fee: most resorts charge it when you bring your own photographer, videographer or planner, and it varies by property, so get it in writing before you compare quotes. The second is rooms. Packages are priced assuming guests stay on property, often with a minimum room block, and a wedding that pulls guests from three hotels can lose the package pricing altogether. Transfers, welcome dinner, and a day-after brunch round out the list that couples forget until the last invoice.

Where photography sits in the budget

Photography and film sit at five to eight percent of a destination wedding total, and ours is one of the few line items on this coast with published numbers. A half day built around the ceremony and golden hour runs 1,800 to 2,800 USD. A full day with a second shooter and venue scouting runs 3,500 to 5,500. The full day with a cinematic highlight film runs 5,500 to 8,000. Every collection includes the complete edited gallery in one to three business days through a private 4K gallery, and we work as an outside vendor at properties along the whole corridor.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Riviera Maya wedding cost?

About 30,000 to 50,000 USD for an intimate 30-guest celebration, 75,000 to 120,000 for 80 guests and 150,000 to 250,000 or more for a 150-guest editorial week. All-inclusive packages keep the bands predictable.

Is the Riviera Maya cheaper than Tulum for a wedding?

Usually, at the same guest count. Resort packages bundle food, drink and coordination; Tulum's boutique venues price each element separately and sit two hours from the airport.

What is the outside vendor fee?

A charge most resorts apply when you bring your own photographer, videographer or planner. It varies by property. Ask for it in writing before comparing quotes.

How far is the Riviera Maya from Cancún airport?

Playa del Carmen and Mayakoba are 45 to 55 minutes. Tulum, at the southern end, is closer to two hours.

What does photography cost on the Riviera Maya?

Our published ranges: 1,800 to 2,800 USD for a half day, 3,500 to 5,500 for a full day, 5,500 to 8,000 with a highlight film, gallery in one to three business days.
