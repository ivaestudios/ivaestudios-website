In short

Winter is Holbox's most reliable season for photographs: dry, cooler and with fewer mosquitoes. The trade-off is the cold fronts from the north that cross the Yucatán a few times each winter, bringing a windy, cooler day or two and choppy water, so we keep a backup evening. Sunset is earliest in late November and December, around 6:05 to 6:10 pm. Sessions start at 295 USD plus travel.

Families and couples who come to Holbox in winter get the island at its best: clear skies, comfortable heat and long evenings at Punta Cocos. Here is what to expect, month by month, and how to plan around the one thing winter can bring, the wind.

The Weather

December to March is the dry season on Holbox: warm days, cooler nights, low humidity and far fewer mosquitoes than in summer. The sky is usually clear at sunset, and the water is calm on most days, which keeps the sandbars and the shallows at their best.

Cold Fronts and Wind

A few times each winter, a cold front from the north crosses the Yucatán. Because Holbox faces north, it feels them first: a day or two of strong wind, cooler air, choppier water and sometimes changes to the ferry schedule. They pass quickly. We always keep a second evening of your stay as a backup, and on a windy day we move the session to sheltered streets in the village or to the lagoon side.

Holidays and Crowds

The weeks around New Year and the spring school breaks are the busiest of the winter. Book your hotel and your session early, and expect more people at Punta Cocos at sunset; we arrive early to choose the spot. Our guide to new winter flights from Canada covers the Cancún side of the trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is December a good time to visit Holbox?
Yes. December is dry, cooler and has fewer mosquitoes. A cold front can bring a windy day or two, so keep a backup evening for photos.
What time is sunset on Holbox in winter?
Around 6:05 to 6:10 pm in late November and December, 6:28 pm in mid-January and about 7 pm by mid-March.
Is Holbox windy in winter?
Cold fronts from the north bring a few windy, cooler days each winter. They pass quickly, and we move sessions to sheltered spots when needed.
