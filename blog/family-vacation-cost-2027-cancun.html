In short

We priced one real week, July 10 to 17, 2027. With United fares from Chicago, seven nights all-inclusive, a private airport transfer, Visitax, a day at Xcaret and a family photo session, two parents and two kids came to about $7,692 at the value level, $8,782 mid-range and $13,479 for luxury; two parents and four kids came to about $12,606, $14,039 and $20,672. The kids paid full airfare and Visitax, a reduced or free resort rate, and three quarters of the adult price at Xcaret.

Most Cancun budgets are written for two adults. Families ask us something else: what does the week cost with the kids on the booking? So we priced one real week in July 2027, line by line, on the airline's, the resorts' and the park's own websites, for families of four and six, at three levels.

We priced every resort level on Hilton's own site, so the child rules and taxes compare like for like. Value is Hilton Cancun, an All-Inclusive Resort, on the Cancun to Tulum highway outside the Hotel Zone; mid-range is Hilton Cancun Mar Caribe All-Inclusive Resort, on Kukulcan Boulevard in the Hotel Zone; luxury is the same resort's Enclave section, with beachfront suites and lounge access.

Prices move daily, so read this as a dated snapshot, not a quote. Flying from another city? Swap in your own fares. Coming from Canada? Our guide to a family trip from Canada to Cancun covers that side of the planning.

What the Kids Actually Add

How each line treated the children on our dates:

  • Flights. United showed the same per-person fare for the kids as for the adults, so in the family of four they are half the airfare: $1,604 of $3,208 in the mid-range column.
  • Resort. At Hilton Cancun, the Sunset View room for two adults was $356.10 a night; with the 6- and 9-year-old it was $536.10, so the kids added $180 a night, $1,260 for the week. Hilton quotes these resorts per person, children included, and anyone 5 or older left off the booking is charged at check-in, so list every child.
  • Xcaret. Adults are 12 and older. Children 5 to 11 pay three quarters of the adult price, $100.57 against $134.10 online, and kids 4 and under get in free.
  • Visitax. There is no child rate: the official site says all foreign tourists must pay.
  • Transfers. Priced per vehicle, and the company we used lists child seats as free on request, in limited number.
  • Photos. We charge per session, not per person.

Where Each Number Comes From

Flights. On November 14, 2026 we searched United's site for the nonstop from Chicago O'Hare leaving at 10:33 am on July 10, returning July 17. Per-person round trips started at $702 in Basic Economy (non-refundable, no changes, no carry-on bag), $802 in Standard Economy (one carry-on) and $1,045 in Business for four travelers, $1,062 for six. Checked bags cost extra in economy.

Resorts. Rates come from Hilton's booking pages for Hilton Cancun, an All-Inclusive Resort and Hilton Cancun Mar Caribe, checked November 14, 2026, with taxes included in every total. Hilton Cancun's Early Booking Offer can be changed or cancelled up to 60 days before arrival; the Mar Caribe rates in our tables can be changed or cancelled until June 26, 2027, 14 days before arrival. Planning photos at the first resort? See our Hilton Cancun family photoshoot guide.

Transfers. Happy Shuttle Cancun listed these rates on November 14, 2026: a private van for 4 to 6 people to the Hotel Zone is $94 round trip, and a full-size luxury SUV for 4 to 6 is $178. Its private transfer page says each ride is one flat rate per vehicle, not per passenger. The company files Hilton Cancun under Puerto Morelos, where its van for 4 to 6 is $124 round trip. For taxis, Uber and car seats once you land, see our guide to getting around Cancun with kids.

Visitax. Article 51-Octies of Quintana Roo's fee law, as last amended on December 16, 2025, charges each foreign visitor 2.5 UMA, the unit Mexican law uses for fees. With the UMA at 117.31 pesos from February 1, 2026, according to INEGI, that is about 293 pesos, or about $15.75 at the 18.6188 pesos per dollar that Mexico's official gazette published for November 13, 2026. The UMA is updated every year, so the 2027 amount will follow the new value. You pay at visitax.gob.mx; more in our Visitax guide for families.

Xcaret. On November 14, 2026 the Xcaret price guide listed the park at $149 per adult, or $134.10 online, and the park's ticket page gives that price for purchases made at least seven days ahead, with 25% off for kids 5 to 11 and free entry for toddlers 0 to 4. Its quote tool gave $469.35 for two adults and two kids aged 5 to 11, and $569.92 for two adults, three kids and one under 5. See our guide to Xcaret parks with kids.

Meals out. An all-inclusive rate covers meals, snacks and drinks at the resort, so we left restaurants out; dinners outside go on top.

Ways to Lower the Total

  • Take the early rate if your dates are firm. At Hilton Cancun the Early Booking Offer was $536.10 a night against $689 for the flexible rate.
  • Look for a kids-stay-free rate, like the one at Mar Caribe.
  • Buy park tickets online at least seven days ahead: $134.10 instead of $149 per adult at Xcaret.
  • Fly Basic Economy only if you can skip carry-ons. It saved $100 per person on our flight.
  • Share one vehicle. Six people pay the same $94 to the Hotel Zone as four.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a family trip to Cancun cost?
For July 10 to 17, 2027, with flights from Chicago, seven nights all-inclusive, transfers, Visitax, a day at Xcaret and a photo session, a family of four came to about $7,692, $8,782 or $13,479 at the value, mid-range and luxury levels, and a family of six to about $12,606, $14,039 or $20,672. Prices checked November 14, 2026.
Do kids pay full price at Cancun all-inclusive resorts?
It depends on the resort and the rate. At Hilton Cancun, an All-Inclusive Resort, kids aged 6 and 9 raised our July 2027 room from $356.10 to $536.10 a night. At Hilton Cancun Mar Caribe, the Kids & Teens Stay Free rate lets one child 17 or under per registered adult stay and eat free, up to two per room.
Do children have to pay the Visitax?
Yes. The official Visitax site says all foreign tourists must pay, with no separate child rate. The fee is 2.5 UMA, about 293 pesos per person in 2026, or about $15.75 at the November 13, 2026 exchange rate.
How much is a private airport transfer for a family in Cancun?
Happy Shuttle Cancun publishes $94 round trip for a private van for 4 to 6 people to the Hotel Zone and $178 for a full-size luxury SUV. Rates are per vehicle, and the company lists child seats as free on request.
How much is Xcaret for kids?
Children 5 to 11 pay three quarters of the adult price, and kids 4 and under enter free. On November 14, 2026, tickets bought online at least seven days ahead were $134.10 per adult and $469.35 for two adults and two kids.
How much does a family photo session add to the trip?
Our collections run from 295 to 895 USD, with no charge per person. The Chapter, which we recommend for families, is 60 minutes and 35 photos for 475 USD, with no deposit: you pay at the end of the session.
