In short

Valentine's Day 2027 is Sunday, February 14. In Cancun the sun sets at 6:45 pm that day, with warm golden light from 6:14 pm, so a proposal or a couples session starts around 5:45 pm. It is also Super Bowl Sunday and the middle of the Presidents' Day long weekend, which makes February 13 to 15 the busiest days of the month. Book the sunset hour as soon as you have flights, or choose sunrise for an empty beach.

A Valentine's trip to Cancun is easy to picture and a little harder to schedule. Everyone wants the same dinner table and the same hour of light, and in 2027 the date lands on the busiest weekend of February. This is how we plan it for our own couples: the exact light, the calendar around the 14th and the four ways people use the day.

Why This Weekend Is Different in 2027

Three things land on the same weekend. Valentine's Day is Sunday, February 14. Super Bowl LXI is played that same evening in California; kickoff is traditionally around 6:30 pm Eastern, and in February Cancun is on the same clock as the East Coast, so the game starts close to sunset here. And Monday, February 15 is Presidents' Day in the United States, which turns the weekend into a three-day trip for many couples and families.

What that means for you: flights and rooms for February 12 to 15 fill early, restaurant tables for the 14th go first, and the hour before sunset is the most requested of the month. There is one small advantage. On Super Bowl Sunday many guests are in front of a screen at sunset, so the beach can be quieter than on a normal Sunday.

"Golden light" is when the sun drops below six degrees and the color turns warm; "last light" is the end of civil twilight. For a proposal we like the ring to come out about 20 minutes before sunset, around 6:25 pm, so there is warm light for the moment itself and for the portraits that follow. Quintana Roo does not use daylight saving, so these times do not shift.

Four Ways to Use the Day

1. A sunset session, then dinner. The classic plan. Start the session around 5:45 pm, finish with the last light just after 7 pm and book dinner for 7:30 or 8 pm. Reserve the table when you book the trip; on the 14th, the good ones are gone weeks ahead.

2. A sunrise session and the whole day free. Sunrise is at 7:18 am, late enough to be comfortable. The beach is nearly empty, the light is soft and you are at breakfast by 8:30 am. From the Hotel Zone the sun rises over the sea. Our comparison of sunrise and sunset sessions explains the difference in the photos.

3. The day before or the day after. Saturday the 13th and Monday the 15th have the same light and less pressure on restaurants. If the date itself matters less to you than the evening, this is the easiest way to get both the table and the hour you want.

4. A session as the gift. For an anniversary or a honeymoon, the session is the present: an hour together on the beach and a gallery a few days later. Our couples photography page shows how a session runs, and our anniversary session ideas has more to choose from.

If You Are Proposing

A Valentine's proposal is the least surprising date of the year, which is why the plan matters. Three things make it work.

A reason to be there. Your partner needs a believable reason to walk onto the beach dressed well at 6:15 pm. A dinner reservation, a "resort photo offer" or a sunset drink all work. We agree on the story with you beforehand.

A spot we have looked at. We choose the exact place with you, check where the sun will be and decide where we stand so you are not looking for us. Our proposal spots guide covers beaches, cenotes and rooftops, and for the Hotel Zone there is a dedicated list.

A signal. We agree on a simple signal, such as taking off your sunglasses, so we are already photographing before you kneel.

If you are planning from home, start with how to plan a surprise proposal from abroad and how to work with your resort's concierge. Our surprise proposal page explains how we stay out of sight.

Weather and a Backup Plan

Mid-February is dry-season weather: 30°C (86°F) by day and 21°C (69°F) at night in the official normals, with only 59 mm of rain in an average February. The one thing to plan for is a cold front, called a norte, which can bring a day or two of north wind and rough water. Mexico's weather service forecasts seven across the country this February; not every one reaches Cancun.

We watch the forecast from 72 hours out. If the 14th looks windy, we move to the side of the coast that is sheltered from the north wind, or to the evening before or after. That is the main reason to arrive at least a day before the date you care about.

What to Wear

Choose something you would wear to a good dinner and can walk in on sand. Flowing dresses and linen shirts photograph well and move in the breeze; very short, light skirts fight the wind. Solid colors work better than small patterns. Red is beautiful at sunset as one piece, not head to toe. Bring flat sandals for the walk and a light layer for after dark. Our couples outfit guide has full palettes.

For a proposal, keep the ring box in a jacket or a loose pocket, not a tight one, and tell us beforehand which side you plan to kneel on so we stand where both faces show.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time is sunset in Cancun on Valentine's Day 2027?
On Sunday, February 14, 2027, sunset on the Hotel Zone beach is at 6:45 pm. Warm golden light starts at 6:14 pm and the sky still glows until 7:07 pm. Sunrise is at 7:18 am.
Is Cancun busy on Valentine's Day?
In 2027, yes. February 14 is a Sunday, the same evening as the Super Bowl, and Monday, February 15 is Presidents' Day in the United States, so February 13 to 15 are the busiest days of the month. Book flights, dinner and the sunset hour early.
What is the best time to propose on the beach in Cancun in February?
About 20 minutes before sunset, around 6:25 pm in mid-February. The light is warm for the moment itself and there is time for portraits before the last light just after 7 pm. For an empty beach, propose at sunrise, around 7:20 am.
How far ahead should we book a photographer for Valentine's Day?
As soon as you have flights. Sunset sessions on February 13 and 14 are the first of the month to go. If those are taken, the 12th and the 15th have the same light.
What happens if the weather is bad on February 14?
Rain is rare in February. The usual issue is a cold front with north wind for a day or two. We watch the forecast from 72 hours out and move to a sheltered part of the coast or to the evening before or after.
