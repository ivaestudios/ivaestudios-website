In short

The sand closest to Cancun airport is at the south end of the Hotel Zone, along the Riviera Cancun resort strip and in Puerto Morelos, about 15 to 20 minutes away by the published figures. For small children, the reef-calmed water around Puerto Morelos is gentler than Playa Delfines. On your last morning, book 7:00 or 7:30 am in winter or 6:30 am in summer, and reach the airport at least 3 hours before an international flight, as Delta suggests.

Many families can only fit photos into the first or the last day of the trip, and the last morning works well when your beach is close to the airport. Here is where that sand is, how long the drive takes, which water suits small children, and how we would plan the morning around checkout. For the first evening instead, our flight times guide covers an arrival-evening session.

Which Beach Is Nearest to Cancun Airport?

The airport is south of the city, and Mexican Caribbean, the site of Quintana Roo's tourism promotion board, puts the Hotel Zone about a 20-minute drive away. The closest sand falls into three areas:

  • The south end of the Hotel Zone. Of the 12 public beaches on the city's beach dashboard, the two farthest south, toward the airport, are Playa Delfines, at kilometer 17.8, and Playa Coral, at kilometer 24.5. The state tourism site places Playa Nizuc at the far southern end; Club Med Cancun, whose address is Punta Nizuc, says it is 15 minutes from the airport, and its transfer listing says 20.
  • Riviera Cancun. Royalton Riviera Cancun, on Highway 307, gives about 18 km (11 miles), or 15 to 20 minutes, to the airport; Margaritaville Island Reserve Riviera Cancun gives its address as Bahía Petempich, Puerto Morelos.
  • Puerto Morelos. The airport is 18 km (11 miles) north of town on Highway 307, says the management program of the Puerto Morelos Reef National Park, and the state tourism site calls it about 20 minutes.

Those are easy-drive figures. Car seats, luggage and a hotel lobby all add minutes, which is why our airport transfers guide plans on 25 to 40 minutes to the Hotel Zone and about 30 to Puerto Morelos.

Which Beach Suits Young Kids

For gentle water, look to Puerto Morelos. The state tourism site says the reef there runs closer to shore than anywhere else in the Mexican Caribbean and works as a natural breakwater, keeping the town's beaches calm. It calls Bahía Petempich, north of town, calm, shallow and a good choice for families with small children; Punta Brava, also north of town, is open to the waves.

In the Hotel Zone, the same site places Delfines among the beaches with stronger waves and calls the water at Playa Nizuc calm and shallow. With toddlers at Delfines, stay at the water's edge and check the flag.

Sea turtles nest on Puerto Morelos beaches from May to October, the site adds, so give any marked nest room. See our Puerto Morelos and Akumal family session guide or, at Punta Nizuc, our Club Med Cancun guide.

A Departure-Morning Plan That Works

Work backward from your flight. Delta suggests arriving at least 3 hours before departure when you travel outside the United States, with check-in closing 1 hour before. Air Canada opens check-in and bag drop for international flights, Mexico and the Caribbean included, 3 hours before departure and closes it 60 minutes before. Your own airline's page has the final word.

A 2:00 pm flight, from a resort 15 to 20 minutes from the airport:

  • 7:00 am: the session on your resort's beach; The Chapter runs 60 minutes.
  • 8:15 am: breakfast.
  • 9:15 am: showers and packing.
  • 10:15 am: checkout.
  • 10:30 am: leave, with a cushion on the published drive time.
  • 11:00 am: at the airport, 3 hours before the flight.

Pick the start by the sun. By our calculation for these beaches, on the 15th of each month the sun rises at about 6:58 am in November, 7:16 in December, 7:27 in January and 7:18 in February, so book 7:00 or 7:30 am; at 6:34 to 6:56 am in March, September and October, so 7:00 am; and at 6:05 to 6:29 am from April to August, so 6:30 am. Our sunrise and sunset table has every month.

With a flight before about noon, the morning gets tight: book an earlier day, or read our last-minute family photographer guide if time is short.

Beach Clubs and Things to Do Nearby

Looking for a beach club near Cancun airport? Look to Puerto Morelos: the state tourism site says beach clubs line its coast, and at Ojo de Agua a couple of them rent umbrellas and lounge chairs and serve food and drinks. Near Playa Nizuc, it adds, resorts offer beach services such as Balinese beds. Check each club's own site for hours and fees.

With a few hours before a late flight:

  • Puerto Morelos town. Walk the pier beside the old lighthouse, tilted by Hurricane Beulah in 1967; snorkeling trips leave from the pier for the reef with local guides. At the entrance to town, the Dr. Alfredo Barrera Marín Botanical Garden has mangrove trails, a traditional Maya house and an apiary of stingless bees.
  • The south end of the Hotel Zone. The Cancun sign stands at the entrance to Playa Delfines, and El Rey, which the state tourism site calls the largest archaeological site in Cancun, is at kilometer 18.

Already checked out? Ask the front desk whether it can hold your bags.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the nearest beach to Cancun airport?

The south end of the Hotel Zone and the coast south of the airport. Of the city's 12 public beaches, Playa Coral (km 24.5) and Playa Delfines (km 17.8) are the farthest south; Club Med Cancun and Royalton Riviera Cancun each publish 15 to 20 minutes to the airport, and Puerto Morelos is about 20 minutes away.

Are there public beaches near Cancun airport?

Yes. Playa Delfines and Playa Coral are public, and the city's beach dashboard lists parking, restrooms and lifeguards at both, with operating hours of 9:00 am to 7:00 pm. In Puerto Morelos, about 20 minutes from the airport, the main beach is public and a short walk from the plaza.

Which beach near Cancun airport is best for young kids?

For gentle water, Puerto Morelos: the reef keeps the town's beaches calm, and the state tourism site calls Bahía Petempich calm, shallow and a good choice for families with small children. Playa Delfines has stronger waves, so stay at the water's edge with toddlers there.

Is there a beach club near Cancun airport?

Look to Puerto Morelos, about 20 minutes away. The state tourism site says beach clubs line its coast, and at Ojo de Agua a couple of them rent umbrellas and lounge chairs and serve food and drinks. Check each club's own site for hours and fees.

Can we do family photos on the morning we fly home?

Yes, if your flight leaves late enough. Book 7:00 or 7:30 am from November to February, 7:00 am in March, September and October, or 6:30 am from April to August, and reach the airport at least 3 hours before an international flight, as Delta suggests. Your preview arrives within 24 hours.
