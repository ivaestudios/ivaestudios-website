Bahamas, Turks and Caicos or Cancun With Kids?

In short

From New York, Boston or Washington, Nassau is 35 minutes to an hour closer than Cancun, with Providenciales in between; from Texas and Denver, Cancun is closer, and only Cancun had scheduled West Coast nonstops in the 2026 data. All three need passports and share one hurricane season. Cancun adds cenotes, Maya ruins and island ferries.

Cancun or The Bahamas with kids, or Turks and Caicos? Parents usually trade a short flight and calm water against price and things to do. We are a Cancun studio, so we have a side; the outside facts here were checked on official and primary sources on November 14, 2026.

Nassau is the short hop from the East Coast, Atlanta and Chicago; from Dallas, Houston and Denver, Cancun is closest, and no West Coast airport had a scheduled nonstop to Nassau or Providenciales. Schedules change: Beaches Turks & Caicos says United will start a weekly Denver nonstop to Providenciales in December 2026. Choice counts too: 46 US airports had ten or more scheduled nonstops to Cancun in those months, 21 to Nassau and 15 to Providenciales. In winter, all three keep New York time; from March 14 to November 7, 2027, Cancun, which never changes its clocks, runs an hour behind New York. For more cities, see our flight times to Cancun.

Beaches and Water for Small Kids

With small children, shelter matters most. Turks and Caicos' tourism board points to the barrier reef running 340 miles around the Caicos Islands, which it calls the planet's third largest, and calls Sapodilla Bay tranquil. On The Bahamas' official site, Paradise Island, linked to Nassau by bridge, is home to big resorts, including Atlantis, and a handful of lively beaches. Cancun has both moods: the Mexican Caribbean tourism board calls Playa Nizuc calm and shallow and the bay at Playa Las Perlas sheltered, while Chac Mool draws surfers.

Each coast has its own risk. The US and Canada warn of sharks in The Bahamas, and Canada in Turks and Caicos too: don't swim where fishermen use bait. For Mexico, Canada warns that riptides are common; on Cancun's public beaches, a green flag means safe to swim, yellow caution and red stay out.

The Resorts Parents Compare

Google's search suggestions pair Moon Palace and Hyatt Ziva with Beaches Turks & Caicos, and Club Med Cancun with Turks and Caicos. On Grace Bay, Beaches lists a 45,000-square-foot waterpark, Sesame Street characters, a Kids Camp with certified nannies and a teen disco. Club Med fans, note: its Turks and Caicos resort, Club Med Turkoise, is adults-only. Club Med Cancun lists kids clubs from babies to age 17, with child care for ages 4 to 17 included, on a private peninsula at Punta Nizuc with three beaches and a lagoon. On the Cancun side, our guides show where we photograph families at Moon Palace, Hyatt Ziva Cancun, Club Med Cancun and along Costa Mujeres.

Seaweed and Hurricane Season

Hurricane season is shared: NOAA's Atlantic basin covers the Atlantic, the Caribbean and the Gulf, with a season from June 1 to November 30 that peaks around September 10; most activity falls between mid-August and mid-October.

Seaweed is harder to compare. The University of South Florida's monthly sargassum outlook covers the Caribbean Sea and the Gulf, Cancun included, but doesn't name The Bahamas or Turks and Caicos, so ask your resort there. For 2026, which USF says is set to be the second-largest sargassum year after 2025, its January bulletin said Mexico's Caribbean coast should already have seen some beaching; amounts peaked in June, and its October 31 outlook expects them to keep falling until possibly the end of November or December.

Price, Passports and Fees

Is Cancun or Turks and Caicos cheaper? Resort rates move daily, and we found no official source pricing these trips side by side, so compare the same week, room and inclusions; our Cancun trip budget guide lists what to price. Turks and Caicos uses the US dollar; The Bahamas' official site says the Bahamian dollar is kept level with the US dollar and both are accepted on all islands; Cancun uses pesos and adds Quintana Roo's Visitax, which every foreign tourist pays, infants included. Taxis favor small parties: Turks and Caicos fares are usually per person, Canada notes, and The Bahamas' official site says surcharges often apply above two people.

Flying in, everyone needs a passport, babies included: US Customs and Border Protection requires children to carry their own when flying home, and for Americans, Mexico requires a passport book, not a card. Turks and Caicos' tourism board asks for six months of validity beyond your stay. Check every bag for ammunition: The Bahamas and Turks and Caicos ban even small amounts packed by mistake, and the US State Department warns of 12 years or more in prison in Turks and Caicos. It rates all three Level 2, exercise increased caution, and Canada advises a high degree of caution in all three, with regional advisories in Mexico.

Days Off the Beach

In Turks and Caicos, most days out happen on the water: its tourism board suggests Iguana Cay, where iguanas outnumber people, paddling Chalk Sound National Park, the reef and, in winter, humpback whales off Salt Cay. Nassau and Paradise Island offer Atlantis, which The Bahamas' official site credits with the world's largest open-air marine habitat and the Aquaventure water park, plus marching flamingos at Ardastra Gardens and downtown museums; the swimming pigs are a separate trip, on Big Major Cay in The Exumas. Cancun's region, says its tourism board, has archaeological sites, cenotes, underground rivers, islands and lagoons, and Ultramar ferries run to Isla Mujeres from Puerto Juarez and the Hotel Zone.

Our Verdict, Family by Family

  • Babies and toddlers. Fly the shortest nonstop and ask your resort about calm water: Nassau from the Northeast, Atlanta or Chicago; Cancun from Texas, Denver or the West.
  • School-age kids. For days off the resort, Cancun has the widest choice; for days on the water, Turks and Caicos.
  • Teens. Let them pick: Atlantis on Paradise Island, snorkeling in Turks and Caicos, or Maya ruins and cenotes near Cancun.
  • Three generations. Cancun, when relatives fly in from several cities: more than twice as many US airports had regular nonstops there as to Nassau.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Cancun or the Bahamas better for a family vacation?

It depends on your airport and plans. From New York, Boston or Washington, Nassau is 35 minutes to an hour closer; from Texas, Denver and the West, Cancun is. Cancun offers more days off the resort, with cenotes, Maya ruins and island ferries; Nassau adds Atlantis.

Is the flight shorter to the Bahamas, Turks and Caicos or Cancun?

In US Department of Transportation data for January to August 2026, New York nonstops averaged about 3 h 10 to Nassau, 3 h 35 to Providenciales and 4 h 10 to Cancun. From Houston, Cancun was quickest, at 2 h 20, and only Cancun had scheduled West Coast nonstops.

Is Cancun or Turks and Caicos cheaper?

Resort rates change daily, so compare the same week, room and inclusions. Turks and Caicos uses the US dollar and its taxis usually charge per person; Cancun uses pesos and adds Quintana Roo's Visitax, which every foreign tourist pays, infants included.

Does Club Med in Turks and Caicos take kids?

No. Club Med Turkoise, on Grace Bay, is adults-only. Club Med Cancun lists kids clubs from babies to age 17, with child care for ages 4 to 17 included.

How does a family photo session work in Cancun?
