How to Propose at Excellence Playa Mujeres: Rooftop, Beach and the Right Minute

In short

At Excellence Playa Mujeres you have two good options: a rooftop terrace proposal in your own suite, completely private, or the water's edge in the last half hour before sunset, with Isla Mujeres on the horizon. Either way the photographer waits out of sight and shoots from a distance. Coverage starts at 245 USD and the photos arrive in one to three business days.

Excellence Playa Mujeres suits a proposal because it gives you a choice most resorts do not: a public, cinematic setting on a calm beach, or a completely private one on a rooftop terrace above the palms. Both work, and they produce very different photographs. This is how we plan each one, including where the photographer hides, the minute to ask and what to do with the twenty minutes after the yes.

Option One: the Rooftop Terrace

Excellence Playa Mujeres has suites with private rooftop terraces, including two-storey categories with the terrace above the bedroom. If your partner is shy, or would hate an audience, this is the proposal we recommend: nobody else is there, the view runs over the palms to the sea, and you can take as long as you need.

The usual sequence: the resort, or a decorator you book through it, sets up flowers and candles on the terrace while you are both at dinner or the spa. Our photographer is let up a few minutes before you return and waits in the stairwell or behind a planter, wherever the layout hides them best. You bring your partner up "to see the sunset". The photographer shoots the reaction to the set-up, the kneel and the yes, then joins you for portraits on the terrace while the light is still warm.

Two things make or break it. First, timing: the terrace needs to be ready and the photographer in place before you walk up, so we agree a message you send when you leave dinner. Second, light: the sun sets behind the resort, so on the terrace you get warm side light in the last half hour. A few candles help after sunset, but string lights or lanterns photograph much better than dozens of small tea lights.

Option Two: the Water's Edge

If you want the ocean in the photograph, walk down to the beach in the last 30 minutes before sunset. The sea in front of Playa Mujeres is calm and shallow, the sand is wide and pale, and the horizon holds only the low line of Isla Mujeres. Choose a spot away from the beach bar and the pool, so that the other guests are behind the photographer, not behind you.

Because the beach faces east, there is no glare in your faces at that hour: the shoreline is lit softly and evenly, which is exactly what you want for a reaction you cannot repeat. We mark the spot in advance, a particular palm, lounger or post, and you simply stop there and turn your partner to face the sea.

Where the Photographer Waits

On the beach, our photographer arrives early, checks in as your guest and sits on a lounger 20 to 40 metres from the marker with a long lens, looking like anyone else enjoying the end of the day. On the terrace, they wait out of sight inside the suite's layout. In both cases they shoot without moving until after the yes, and only then come over.

We agree three signals with you: a message when you leave, the marker, and the moment you turn your partner around. You do not need to look for us. For access, the photographer's name goes on your reservation at the front desk; as with any adults-only resort, the photographer must be over 18. For a short private session, a staying guest is very seldom charged the outside vendor fee used for weddings, but we confirm the current policy with the resort in writing first.

After the Yes

Once the ring is on, we spend 20 to 40 minutes on relaxed portraits: on the terrace if you started there, then down at the water's edge as the sea turns blue, or the other way round. These are the frames with the ring, the ones you will send home that night.

Proposal coverage uses our session collections. Spark is 245 USD for 35 minutes and 15 photos; Glow Hour, 325 USD for 45 minutes and 25 photos, is the most common choice; and Essential Flow, 395 USD for 60 minutes and 35 photos, is ideal if you want both the terrace and the beach. The edited gallery arrives in one to three business days. For more ideas, see our surprise proposal photographer page and the guide to coordinating with a resort concierge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I propose on my rooftop terrace at Excellence Playa Mujeres?
Yes. If you have booked a rooftop terrace suite, it is the most private place on the property to propose. The resort can arrange decoration while you are at dinner, and our photographer waits out of sight on the terrace and shoots the moment and the portraits afterwards.
What is the best time to propose on the beach at Excellence Playa Mujeres?
About 25 minutes before sunset, which ranges from about 6:05 pm in December to 7:32 pm in July. The beach faces east, so the light is soft and even with no glare. For a quiet alternative, sunrise from roughly August through April comes up from behind Isla Mujeres.
How does the photographer stay hidden?
On the beach, the photographer checks in as your guest and waits on a lounger 20 to 40 metres away with a long lens. On a terrace, they wait out of sight inside the suite. We agree a marker and three simple signals in advance, so you never need to look for us.
Is there a fee to bring a proposal photographer into Excellence Playa Mujeres?
For a staying guest booking a short private session, the outside vendor fee charged for weddings is very seldom applied. Policies can change, so we confirm with the resort in writing before your date. The photographer must be over 18, as it is an adults-only resort.
How much does proposal photography cost at Excellence Playa Mujeres?
IVAE Studios proposal coverage starts at 245 USD for 35 minutes and 15 photos. Glow Hour, 325 USD for 45 minutes and 25 photos, is the most common choice, and Essential Flow, 395 USD for 60 minutes and 35 photos, covers both a terrace and the beach.
