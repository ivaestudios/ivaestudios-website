In short

Riviera Cancun is not a town. It is a name used by resorts on the Caribbean coast south of Cancun International Airport, most of them between the airport and Puerto Morelos, 18 km (11 mi) apart, and a few just south of that town. Their addresses say Puerto Morelos or Benito Juárez, the municipality that includes Cancun; none is in the Hotel Zone, and several also call themselves Riviera Maya. Offshore lies the Puerto Morelos Reef National Park. Below: nine resorts with the name, checked on their own websites on November 14, 2026.

If your resort has Riviera Cancun in its name, your transfer will leave the airport heading south, away from the city, toward a fishing town called Puerto Morelos. Nothing is wrong with the booking. Here is where Riviera Cancun really is, which resorts carry the name, what the water is like in front of them, and how we plan family photos there.

Riviera Cancun Is a Name, Not a Town

Search a map for Riviera Cancun and you will not find a town. The Mexican Caribbean tourism site lists Cancun, Costa Mujeres, Puerto Morelos and the Riviera Maya among its destinations, but no Riviera Cancun. The name belongs to resorts on the coast south of Cancun International Airport, and their addresses show where they really are: Royalton Riviera Cancun gives Puerto Morelos, while Royalton Splash Riviera Cancun, at the same kilometer of Highway 307, gives Benito Juárez, the municipality that includes Cancun, as does Haven Riviera Cancun, farther north.

When Mexico created the Puerto Morelos Reef National Park in 1998, the coast in front of Puerto Morelos belonged to Benito Juárez; today Puerto Morelos has its own municipal government, and resort addresses are split between the two. The park's management program puts the town 35 km (22 mi) south of Cancun, 34 km (21 mi) north of Playa del Carmen and 18 km (11 mi) south of the airport. Seven of the resorts in our table lie between the airport and the town, about 3 to 14 km (2 to 9 mi) north of it. Two lie just past it: Grand Residences Riviera Cancun, which says its beach is south of the town, and Finest Riviera Cancun, opening in 2027, whose website places it about 3 km (2 mi) south of the town as well.

It is not the Cancun Hotel Zone, the strip along Kukulcán Boulevard: no resort in our table gives an address there. Nor is it separate from the Riviera Maya, which the Mexican Caribbean site says stretches from Puerto Morelos to Tulum. The resorts use both names: Royalton places itself in Riviera Cancun and, on the same page, in the Riviera Maya, and Excellence Riviera Cancun gives Riviera Cancun as its locality while calling itself a Riviera Maya resort. Puerto Morelos, or Highway 307 with Benito Juárez, puts you on this coast; Kukulcán Boulevard, in the Hotel Zone; Zona Continental, the mainland part of Isla Mujeres, in Costa Mujeres, north of the city. What Beach Are They On?

The resorts all look east over the same Caribbean shore, most of them north of Puerto Morelos. The Mexican Caribbean site's guide to the beaches of Puerto Morelos names its stretches: the main beach and Ojo de Agua in town; Punta Brava, north of the main beach, the wilder end of town with open sea views; and Bahía Petempich, toward Cancun, which it calls the place where the big resorts are, calm and shallow behind the reef and a good choice for families with small children.

Iberostar Selection Riviera Cancun and Margaritaville Island Reserve Riviera Cancun both give Bahía Petempich in their addresses; the others do not name their beach. So the honest answer to "what beach is Royalton Riviera Cancun on" is its own stretch of this shore, about 11 km (7 mi) north of town, with Hideaway as its neighbor. Excellence Riviera Cancun's stretch is about 3 km (2 mi) north of town and Haven's about 14 km (9 mi), while Grand Residences Riviera Cancun says only that its beach lies south of the town.

The Reef and the Water

Offshore runs the Puerto Morelos Reef National Park, created by a presidential decree published on February 2, 1998, over 9,066 hectares (about 22,400 acres). A summary of its management program, published in Mexico's official gazette on September 18, 2000, places the park between latitudes 20°48′ and 21°00′ north, a span that takes in every resort in our table with published coordinates, and its zone for beach recreation and swimming covers the first 100 to 200 meters of sea.

The same document says waves inside the reef lagoon are low because the reef barrier works as an efficient dissipator of wave energy. It also says the reef is not uniform: it is most uniform between Puerto Morelos and Punta Tanchacté, while farther north, toward La Bonanza, it does not always break the surface, with gaps of up to 900 meters (about half a mile), and where the barrier is deeper the waves can be higher. South of the town, it adds, the reef does not reach the surface again until Punta Maroma, 16 km (10 mi) away, which matters for the two resorts in our table south of town. Calm water is common here, not guaranteed. Before planning in-the-water photos with a toddler, ask your resort about the sea in front of your building and check the beach flag; our guide to calm-water beaches for toddlers in the Riviera Maya goes further.

Family Photos on This Coast

Three things make this coast kind to family photos.

  • It faces east. The sun rises out of the sea, so morning is the hour for frames with the sunrise over the water, and the coolest time on the sand; in the evening the sun sets inland, behind the resorts. Online booking offers 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am and 5:00 and 5:30 pm; other times can be arranged on WhatsApp. Our sunrise and sunset table by month has the light for your dates.
  • The water is often calm. Where the reef shelters the shore, the shallows can be gentle enough for little ones at the water's edge, which is when splash photos work best.
  • The airport is close. The resorts give 15 to 25 minutes to the airport, so a session on your last morning, before an afternoon flight, is realistic.

Each resort sets its own rule for outside photographers, and some charge a fee; where they do, you pay it to the hotel, and we confirm your resort's rule before your session. For a second setting, Puerto Morelos town has a pier and an old lighthouse tilted by Hurricane Beulah in 1967; see our Puerto Morelos and Akumal family session guide. The tourism site notes that sea turtles nest on Puerto Morelos beaches from May to October.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Riviera Cancun?
A name, not a town. Resorts on the Caribbean coast south of Cancun International Airport put it in their names, most of them between the airport and the town of Puerto Morelos, 18 km (11 mi) apart, and a few just south of the town. Their own addresses place them in Puerto Morelos or in Benito Juárez, the municipality that includes Cancun, and none of them is in the Cancun Hotel Zone.
Is Riviera Cancun part of the Riviera Maya?
The names overlap. The Mexican Caribbean tourism site says the Riviera Maya stretches from Puerto Morelos to Tulum, and resorts with Riviera Cancun in their names, Royalton and Excellence among them, also describe themselves as Riviera Maya resorts. Playa del Carmen is 34 km (21 mi) south of Puerto Morelos.
What beach is Royalton Riviera Cancun on?
Its own stretch of the east-facing Caribbean shore, about 11 km (7 mi) north of Puerto Morelos town in a straight line, with the adults-only Royalton Hideaway Riviera Cancun as its neighbor. Royalton's website does not give the beach a name, and it puts the resort about 18 km, or 15 to 20 minutes, from Cancun International Airport.
What beach is Excellence Riviera Cancun on?
A stretch of the same east-facing shore about 3 km (2 mi) north of Puerto Morelos town in a straight line, going by the coordinates on its website, which does not name the beach. The resort is adults only, for guests 18 and over, and gives 15 to 20 minutes to Cancun International Airport.
Is the water calm enough for toddlers in Riviera Cancun?
Often, but not everywhere. The reef of the Puerto Morelos Reef National Park lowers the waves behind it, and the Mexican Caribbean tourism site describes Bahía Petempich, where the big resorts are, as calm and shallow. The park's management program notes gaps in the reef farther north and no reef at the surface for 16 km (10 mi) south of the town, and the tourism site says Punta Brava, north of town, is open to the waves. Ask your resort and check the beach flag on the day.

Yes, following your resort's rule for outside photographers. Each resort sets its own and some charge a fee, which you pay to the hotel; we confirm your resort's rule before the session.
