In short

Puerto Aventuras is a private marina community 15 minutes south of Playa del Carmen and about 60 minutes from Cancun airport, a shorter drive than from Tulum's. For families, the draws are Fatima Bay, the marina, kids clubs at Dreams Aventuras and Catalonia Riviera Maya, and condo rentals. Photograph the bay at sunrise and the marina in the last light. We recommend The Chapter: 60 minutes, 35 edited photos, 475 USD, no deposit.

Puerto Aventuras is not a beach strip like Cancun or a walking town like Playa del Carmen. It is a planned, private community begun in 1988 around a marina, with houses, condos, a few hotels, a golf course and a bay beach, and its website says it was designed to enjoy each day calmly. For parents of small kids, that calm is the appeal.

Where It Is and How to Get There

Puerto Aventuras sits on Federal Highway 307 between Xcaret and Xpu-Ha, in Dolphin Discovery's directions, so between Playa del Carmen and Akumal. The marina page on the town's website puts it 15 minutes south of Playa del Carmen and 60 minutes from Cancun Airport; Catalonia Riviera Maya, inside the complex, says 20 minutes by car from Playa.

OpenStreetMap's routing engine, checked November 14, 2026, gives about 78 km (48 miles) from Cancun International Airport (CUN) and about 85 km (53 miles), some 80 to 85 minutes without traffic, from Tulum International Airport (TQO). Cancun is the shorter drive; add time for immigration, bags and traffic. Our guide to CUN or TQO compares the two airports. Transfers aren't always included: Dreams Aventuras' FAQ says guests pay their own transportation, and its concierge can arrange it.

Fatima Bay, the Marina and the Rest of Town

Fatima Bay is the beach at the heart of town, beside the marina. Puerto Aventuras Hotel & Beach Club, a 30-room hotel on the bay, describes its water as calm; calm still means an adult within arm's reach.

On October 22, 2026, the homeowners association began installing a sargassum barrier to protect areas including Fatima Bay, the Yacht Club and Kantenah Lagoon, and in October it announced support from Mexico's Navy to collect sargassum on the beach and at sea. It is going in progressively, so check conditions near your dates.

The marina is the other playground: its page lists diving, catamaran, fishing and yacht outings, and the town map shows an open-air food court by the water. An evening walk along the boats is the easiest outing there is with small kids.

The Golf Park has pitch and putt, a driving range and FootGolf, and the town lists tennis, padel and pickleball courts. Golf carts come with rules: the homeowners association fines carts or vehicles driven by minors and speeding over 30 km/h, so teens ride rather than drive.

A Fair Note on the Dolphins

Dolphin Discovery, number 8 on the town map, sits by the marina. Its FAQ says the dolphin platforms are in a sea inlet in the heart of Puerto Aventuras, not the open sea, and that cameras and phones are not allowed in the water for safety reasons; it sells its own photos. Families feel differently about dolphin programs, and the decision is yours. If you book one, keep our session for the beach or the marina. For sea life in the wild, our Puerto Morelos and Akumal guide covers Akumal's turtle bay and its rules.

Where Families Stay

  • Dreams Aventuras Riviera Maya. Hyatt describes an all-inclusive on a secluded bay in the marina village, with 305 rooms and suites, Preferred Club Family Suites for up to six, the Explorer's Club for Kids and a Core Zone for teens. See our Dreams Aventuras photo guide.
  • Catalonia Riviera Maya. An all-inclusive with more than 400 rooms, five pools and a Kid's Club; its page says children up to 6 stay free and kids 7 to 11 pay half the adult price. See our Catalonia Riviera Maya guide.
  • Puerto Aventuras Hotel & Beach Club. Thirty rooms on Fatima Bay, two-queen rooms for up to four, and a day-pass phone line on its website.
  • Rentals. The town's lodging page lists condos and houses on the beach, the marina canals and the lagoon.

Looking for Omni Puerto Aventuras? Omni's website no longer lists a resort here: on November 14, 2026, its old Puerto Aventuras page sent us to its general beach page. Confirm names, day-pass rules and photographer policies with the hotel itself.

Is Puerto Aventuras Safe?

No one can promise safety; here is what official sources said on November 14, 2026. The US State Department's Mexico advisory of May 29, 2026 is Level 2: exercise increased caution, due to terrorism, crime and kidnapping, and some areas carry increased risk. It strongly advises US citizens to follow its own staff's rules, such as not traveling between cities after dark, which matters if your flight lands late, and using dispatched taxis or ride apps rather than hailing cabs on the street. Canada advises a high degree of caution across Mexico (updated October 19, 2026), and Quintana Roo, the state Puerto Aventuras is in, is not on its list of states to avoid for non-essential travel.

Inside, the marina's page says entry is by QR code, with 24-hour surveillance. The homeowners association's report for June 15 to September 15, 2026 counts 90 working cameras, 24 at the main entrance, notes surveillance at two beach access points and lists two home burglaries and several other thefts in that period. For the wider picture, see whether Cancun is safe for families.

Sunrise on the Bay, Golden Hour at the Marina

On this coast the sun rises over the Caribbean and sets behind the land, so morning and evening give you two different towns.

Sunrise is the beach hour: soft light on Fatima Bay, cooler sand and kids who were up anyway. On the 15th of each month in 2027, the US Naval Observatory puts sunrise here between 6:08 am in June and 7:28 am in January. Our online starts are 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am, so from December to February a 7:00 am start begins before sunrise and the sun comes up during the session, and from May to July the sun is up by 6:30.

Evening belongs to the marina, with the last light warm and low across the boats. Sunset runs from 6:08 pm in November to 7:33 pm in July. Our 5:00 and 5:30 pm online starts meet that light from November to January; from April to August, when the sun sets after 7 pm, ask us on WhatsApp for a later start.

Morning belongs to the bay. The last light belongs to the marina.

In a rental. Under Mexico's General Law of National Assets, sea beaches are goods of common use, open to everyone in Mexico within the limits of the law (Articles 7 and 8), so we meet you on the sand. Entry to the complex is by QR code, so ask your host how a visitor gets in.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is there to do in Puerto Aventuras with kids?

Fatima Bay, whose water a hotel on the bay calls calm; the marina, with boat outings and an open-air food court; a Golf Park with pitch and putt and FootGolf; and kids clubs at Dreams Aventuras and Catalonia Riviera Maya.

Is Puerto Aventuras safe?

On November 14, 2026, the US State Department's Mexico advisory of May 29, 2026 was Level 2: exercise increased caution, due to terrorism, crime and kidnapping, with increased risk in some areas. Canada advises a high degree of caution across Mexico without listing Quintana Roo, where Puerto Aventuras is, among the states to avoid.

How far is Puerto Aventuras from Cancun airport?

About 60 minutes, says the town's marina page, or roughly 78 km (48 miles). Tulum's airport is about 85 km (53 miles) away, some 80 to 85 minutes without traffic, so Cancun is the shorter drive.
