In short

At six of the eight Cancun all-inclusives we checked on their own websites on November 14, 2026, the standard family room stops at four guests, and children count. For five or more, look for a family suite (Finest Playa Mujeres for 5, Park Royal Beach Cancun for 6, Crown Paradise Club Cancun for 7), a two-bedroom suite (Garza Blanca for 6, Royalton Splash Riviera Cancun for 8), rooms sold as connecting (Hard Rock Hotel Cancun) or a residence or penthouse loft (Garza Blanca, 8 or 12). Most swim-up rooms we found stop at four. Booking two rooms? Get the connection in writing. One photo session covers everyone, at no per-person charge.

If you travel as five or more, you know the line under the room you liked: maximum four. Here is how room limits work at Cancun's all-inclusives, which room types fit five, six or eight, and what eight resorts list on their own websites. Then the part we know best: photographing everyone together, however many room keys you collect.

Why Four Is the Limit

All-inclusive rates are charged per person per night, as Royalton's FAQ and Garza Blanca's suite page both state, and every room type carries a maximum. At six of the eight resorts we checked, the standard family room or junior suite stops at four. Grand Fiesta Americana Coral Beach lists its two-queen suites for three adults, and only Crown Paradise Club Cancun starts higher: its Standard Plus room sleeps five, in a king bed and a three-bed bunk.

Children count toward that maximum. At Hard Rock Hotel Cancun, the room with two double beds takes four guests, children from 0 to 17 included, and no more than three adults; its two-bedroom Rock Suite takes six, infants included. Where a page lists only adults, as Grand Fiesta Americana's does, ask how children fit before you pay.

Age bands also change from resort to resort. Royalton's FAQ calls guests from 0 to 1 infants, 2 to 12 children and 13 to 17 teens, by age at check-in. Park Royal Beach Cancun's booking form counts guests as adults from 13, while the Family Selection at Grand Palladium Select Costa Mujeres applies the adult rate from 18. Grand Fiesta Americana's booking form marks children as 5 to 12, and Garza Blanca's as 4 to 12, with infants up to 3. The same 13-year-old can book as an adult at one resort and as a child at another.

Three questions before you pay

Does a baby in a crib count toward the limit? Are the connecting rooms guaranteed, or only requested? Which ages count as children, teens and adults? Ask the resort, and keep each answer in an email.

Which Room Fits Five, Six or Eight

  • Five. A family suite with a separate kids' room, like Finest Playa Mujeres' Family Suite: a king bed for the parents, two single beds next door. Crown Paradise's Standard Plus and Family Suite for 5 pair a king bed with a three-bed bunk, and Hard Rock's Deluxe Family is two connecting rooms, one with a king bed and one with double beds.
  • Six. Park Royal Beach Cancun's Family Suite, two rooms with two double beds, a living room with a sofa bed and a dining area; Garza Blanca's two-bedroom suites, with three bathrooms; or Hard Rock's two-bedroom Rock Suite.
  • Seven or eight. Crown Paradise's Family Suite for 7, on two levels with two bathrooms, or its three-bedroom Presidential Suite for 8; Royalton Splash's Two Bedroom Junior Suite for 8; Grand Fiesta Americana's penthouses for 7 or 8 adults; or Garza Blanca's 3 Bedroom Oceanfront Residence for 8, with a full kitchen and butler service.
  • More than eight. Garza Blanca's 4 Bedroom Penthouse Loft lists up to 12 guests and six bathrooms. Royalton Splash's Diamond Club Chairman Two Bedroom Suite shows a maximum of 10 on its card but describes room for eight, so ask. Otherwise, book two of the suites above, connected or close together if the resort can arrange it.

Count bathrooms as carefully as beds: Park Royal's Family Suite sleeps six around one private bathroom, Crown Paradise's Family Suite for 7 has two and Garza Blanca's two-bedroom suites have three. With teenagers and an early breakfast, that decides your mornings.

Swim-Up Rooms for Families

A swim-up room opens from its terrace straight into a pool, and most we found stop at four, including Finest Playa Mujeres' Junior Swim-Up Suites and Royalton Splash's Diamond Club Swim Out junior suites. The exception is Garza Blanca's 2 Bedroom Swim Up, for six, with three bathrooms; its FAQ adds that the resort has no private pools and that swim-up rooms give extra privacy.

Two other ways to have water at the door: Park Royal Beach Cancun's Family Suite with Terrace has a private plunge pool facing the lagoon, for six, and SLS Playa Mujeres connects two ground-floor swim-out rooms as Family Style Swim-Outs, with no guest count on its page, so ask. With toddlers, remember the terrace opens onto open water, and agree on who is watching.

Connecting Rooms, in Writing

The surest connecting room is one you buy as a room type, because then the connection is on your confirmation. Hard Rock Hotel Cancun's Deluxe Family categories are two connecting rooms by definition, and Grand Fiesta Americana's Family & Friends Suite combines two oceanfront suites, with two bathrooms and two living rooms. Book two ordinary rooms and the connection becomes a request: Grand Palladium's Family Selection says it has connecting rooms and asks you to note the preference in the booking comments, and Royalton's FAQ, asked about floors and buildings, says special requests are not guaranteed and depend on availability at arrival.

  1. Book both rooms at the same time, on one reservation if allowed, with an adult registered in each; at Royalton, the guest who checks in must be 18.
  2. Email the resort itself, not only your travel agent, with both confirmation numbers, every guest's name and age, and the words “connecting rooms with an inside door”: adjacent or nearby rooms can sit side by side with no door between them.
  3. Ask for a written reply that says whether the connection is guaranteed or only requested, and whether a crib counts toward the limit.
  4. Confirm again a week before you fly, and bring the email to check-in.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Cancun all-inclusive resorts have rooms for a family of five?
Of the eight resorts we checked on their own websites on November 14, 2026, Finest Playa Mujeres has a Family Suite for five, Crown Paradise Club Cancun has a Standard Plus room and a Family Suite for five, Hard Rock Hotel Cancun has Deluxe Family rooms for five, made of two connecting rooms, and Grand Fiesta Americana Coral Beach lists its two-bedroom Family & Friends Suite for five adults. Park Royal Beach Cancun, Garza Blanca and Royalton Splash Riviera Cancun have suites for six or more, and Garza Blanca's 4 Bedroom Penthouse Loft lists up to 12 guests.
Do children and babies count toward the room limit?
They can. At Hard Rock Hotel Cancun, children from 0 to 17 count toward the room limits, and the two-bedroom Rock Suite takes six guests, infants included. Age bands differ too: Royalton counts 2 to 12 as children, Park Royal Beach Cancun's booking form counts guests as adults from 13, and Grand Palladium's Family Selection applies the adult rate from 18. List every child's age when you book, and ask whether a crib counts.
Which Cancun resorts have swim-up rooms for families?
Most swim-up rooms we found take four guests, including the Junior Swim-Up Suites at Finest Playa Mujeres and the Diamond Club Swim Out junior suites at Royalton Splash Riviera Cancun. Garza Blanca's 2 Bedroom Swim Up takes six, and SLS Playa Mujeres connects two ground-floor swim-out rooms as Family Style Swim-Outs, without a published guest count.
How do we make sure we get connecting rooms?
When you can, book a room type that already joins two rooms, such as Hard Rock Hotel Cancun's Deluxe Family rooms or Grand Fiesta Americana's Family & Friends Suite. Otherwise, book both rooms together and email the resort with both confirmation numbers, every guest's name and age, and a request for connecting rooms with an inside door. Ask for a written reply that says whether the connection is guaranteed or only requested.
Does a photo session cost more for a family of eight in several rooms?
Not with us. There is no limit on people and no per-person charge. For families we recommend The Chapter (60 minutes, 35 photos, 475 USD), and for a bigger group or two outfits, The Story (75 minutes, 50 photos, two looks, 545 USD); you can move up by paying only the difference. There is no deposit: you pay at the end of the session.
