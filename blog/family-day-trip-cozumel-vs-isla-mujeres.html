Cozumel or Isla Mujeres: Which Day Trip With Kids?

By Vianey Díaz · Published November 14, 2026 · 8 min read

In short

Pick Isla Mujeres for an easy day with small kids: 20 to 30 minutes by ferry from Cancun, with boats from the Hotel Zone itself, shallow water at Playa Norte and a 7 km island you cross by golf cart. Pick Cozumel for reefs, ideally from Playa del Carmen, about 45 minutes away. Family photos fit best into the Isla Mujeres day.

Both islands make a wonderful day off the resort, but very different ones: a short hop to a small island of golf carts and shallow water, or a longer crossing to the largest island in the Mexican Caribbean, internationally known for its reefs. Every ferry time, fare and park rule below comes from operators and official sources we checked on November 14, 2026.

Ferries, Fares and Real Transit Time

Isla Mujeres has the easier map. Ultramar sails from Puerto Juárez, north of downtown Cancun, and from Playa Tortugas and Playa Caracol, at km 6.5 and 9.5 of Kukulcan Boulevard. Xcaret Xailing leaves from El Embarcadero, at km 4.5, roughly hourly from 9:00 am to 7:00 pm, with the last boat back at 8:00 pm, and puts the crossing at about 20 to 30 minutes.

Cozumel's ferries leave from central Playa del Carmen, where Ultramar and Xcaret Xailing both run the route; Xailing sails roughly hourly from the maritime terminal at Calle 1 Sur. Xailing and Quintana Roo's tourism board both put the crossing at about 45 minutes, and from Cancun, Xcaret's own directions start with the road to Playa del Carmen.

Count the whole trip. Xcaret Xailing recommends arriving 30 minutes early, so the ferry part alone is close to an hour each way to Isla Mujeres and an hour and a quarter to Cozumel, before the ride to the terminal.

Round-trip fares in pesos, read November 14, 2026. Ultramar: under 1 free, child fare from age 1 up to 1.20 m (3 ft 11 in). Xailing to Cozumel: child fare for ages 5 to 11, under 5 free.

Before you board

Babies under 1 ride free on Ultramar but need a boarding pass. In bad weather, Xcaret Xailing says, the harbor master decides whether boats can sail, and Ultramar says its departures can change without notice.

Beaches and Water for Small Kids

For toddlers and early swimmers, Playa Norte, at the north end of Isla Mujeres beside the town, decides it: Quintana Roo's tourism board describes its white sand, shallow water and generally gentle waves.

Cozumel's family water is about snorkeling. At Chankanaab, inside the Cozumel Reefs National Park, the reef is so close to shore that snorkeling is enough to explore it, and El Cielo is a shallow, clear sandbar reached by boat, known for its starfish. For splashing rather than snorkeling, Playa Norte wins.

Golf Cart or Taxi: Getting Around

Isla Mujeres is about 7 km (4.3 miles) from tip to tip, and the tourism board says you can cover it in half an hour. The usual way around is a golf cart, rented by the pier; the town is walkable, and taxis run south to Punta Sur, Mexico's easternmost point.

Cozumel is about 48 km (30 miles) long and 14.8 km (9 miles) wide, according to the state port authority, APIQROO. San Miguel, the town, faces the ferry pier, so its waterfront and island museum are a walk away. Beyond town, the tourism board suggests a rental car for the east coast and Punta Sur park, with its lighthouse museum and a boat tour of Laguna Colombia to look for crocodiles and birds, and taxis wait at the southern cruise piers.

Snorkeling: Reef Park or Island Sites

The reefs off Cozumel's west coast were declared a national marine park in a decree published July 19, 1996, and the tourism board singles out Palancar, Colombia and Santa Rosa for coral, underwater walls and clear water.

The park's management program, in the 1998 edition CONANP posts, has snorkelers go with an authorized guide and stay 1.5 meters (5 feet) from coral, and forbids touching or standing on the reef, feeding or chasing marine life, and wearing gloves.

Isla Mujeres has more than twenty snorkel and dive sites, 3 to 30 meters deep, says the tourism board, including the Manchones reef, El Farito and MUSA, an underwater museum with more than 500 sculptures. Garrafón park adds snorkeling in a reef cove and a zipline over the sea.

Snorkel and boat trips in the federal marine parks pay a fee per person per day, set for 2026 at 225.56 pesos in Cozumel's reef park and 125.32 in the park along Isla Mujeres' west coast, Punta Cancun and Punta Nizuc, rounded to the nearest 5 pesos when paid. Children under 12 are exempt, and from January 1, 2027, under-13s and adults 60 and over. Ask your tour operator whether its price includes it.

Where Family Photos Fit In

Isla Mujeres is where a family session fits into the day: Playa Norte faces north and west, so the sun sets over the water, and its shallows let small children be in the sea with you.

First light. A sunrise session, before the beach fills, needs an early boat: Xailing's first departure, at 9:00 am, is too late, so check Ultramar's first boat from Puerto Juárez, or stay the night on the island.

Last light. Spend the day, then shoot before the ride back. By our calculation, sunset at Playa Norte falls between 6:04 pm in late November and 7:33 pm in early July (see our Cancun sunset times by month). With Xailing's last boat at 8:00 pm and 30 minutes at the dock, that is comfortable from mid-September to March and tight from late April to late August; in June and July, with sunset at 7:25 pm or later, the session has to end before the sun goes down. Confirm Ultramar's last boat the day you go.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Isla Mujeres or Cozumel better for a day trip with kids?

For young children, Isla Mujeres: 20 to 30 minutes by ferry from Cancun and shallow water at Playa Norte. Cozumel suits families who snorkel, especially from Playa del Carmen, about 45 minutes away.

How long is the ferry to Isla Mujeres and to Cozumel?

Xcaret Xailing puts Cancun to Isla Mujeres at about 20 to 30 minutes and Playa del Carmen to Cozumel at about 45, and recommends arriving 30 minutes early. From Cancun, a Cozumel day starts with the drive to Playa del Carmen.

How much is the ferry for a family?

Ultramar lists round trips of 580 pesos per adult and 440 per child to Isla Mujeres, and 640 and 480 to Cozumel; babies under 1 ride free. Xcaret Xailing charges 580 and 460 pesos to Cozumel, with children under 5 free.

Which island has better snorkeling for kids?

Cozumel, for children who already snorkel: Chankanaab's reef is close to shore and El Cielo is a shallow sandbar reached by boat. Isla Mujeres has more than 20 sites, including MUSA and Garrafón's reef cove.

Can we fit family photos into an Isla Mujeres day trip?

Yes: on Playa Norte at first light if you catch an early boat, or at last light before the boat back, which on Xcaret Xailing leaves the island at 8:00 pm, so in June and July the session ends before sunset. Our sessions cost 295 to 895 USD, with no deposit.
