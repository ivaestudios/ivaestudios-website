In short

The Quintana Roo government's list of new winter routes includes three new nonstop routes from the US to Cancun, all with Breeze Airways: Tampa from December 19, 2026, on Wednesdays and Saturdays; Pittsburgh from January 7, 2027, on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Sundays; and Richmond from January 8, 2027, on Mondays and Fridays. The same list has Newark to Tulum, twice a week from December 17, without naming the airline. All four cities share Cancun's clock until March 14, 2027. Around those first flights the sun sets between 6:10 and 6:21 pm, so an early-afternoon landing can still make a 5:00 or 5:30 pm session, and a late one is better saved for the next morning.

New nonstop flights change how families plan a winter trip to the Mexican Caribbean, and they change how we plan your photos too. Here are the new US routes for winter 2026-27, taken only from official announcements that we read on November 14, 2026, followed by the part we know best: how the hour you land decides when your first session can be.

New Nonstop Routes to Cancun

On October 18, 2026, Quintana Roo's governor, Mara Lezama, announced the new routes and extra flights reaching Cancun and Tulum this winter from Canada, the United States and Europe. Three of the new Cancun routes start in the United States, and Breeze Airways flies all three; Breeze and the three departure airports had announced them on May 5, 2026, with the days of the week.

Same Clock as the East Coast

Tampa, Pittsburgh, Richmond and Newark all run on US Eastern time. Quintana Roo stays on UTC-5 all year: Mexico's time zone law places the state in its Southeast Zone, on the 75th meridian west, and allows seasonal clock changes only along the northern border. In winter that is exactly Eastern Standard Time, so from the first new flights in December until the US moves its clocks forward on March 14, 2027, there is no time difference at all. From that date until November, Cancun is one hour behind the East Coast.

For children, that makes this the gentlest kind of trip: dinner, bath and bedtime stay where they are at home, and a session at 5:00 pm feels like 5:00 pm. Our guide to the Cancun time zone and your kids' body clock goes further.
