In short

IVAE Studios photographs families in Playa del Carmen at your resort's beach or at a place you choose, with collections from 295 USD and no deposit. For families we recommend The Chapter: 60 minutes, 35 edited photos and one look for 475 USD. Online start times are 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am and 5:00 and 5:30 pm, there is no limit on the number of people, and a sneak peek arrives within 24 hours.

Some families stay in town, near Fifth Avenue, the pedestrian promenade that Quintana Roo's tourism board calls the heart of Playa del Carmen; others in Playacar, which continues south of the center, or at a resort outside town. Wherever you sleep, a family session comes down to four choices: where, what time, which collection and how many people. Here is how we plan each one, with every outside fact checked on official sources on November 15, 2026.

Your Resort's Beach, or a Place You Choose

We come to your resort or to a place you choose. With young children, the simplest stage is the beach at your own hotel: no transfer, no car seat, and your room, shade and a bathroom a few minutes away. If you would rather be somewhere else, such as the sand near your rental, the garden of your villa or a corner of town your children love, tell us when you book.

Your hotel's rule

Some hotels charge a fee for an outside photographer, which you pay to the hotel. Tell us where you are staying and we confirm the rule before the session.

Our resort-by-resort photography guides, from Costa Mujeres to Tulum, may already cover your hotel. Still choosing a base? Compare Playa del Carmen or Cancun as your family base, and if you are heading south, see our Tulum family photo session guide and Tulum family photographer page.

Start Times and the Light by Month

Online booking at ivaestudios.com/book offers five start times: 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am, and 5:00 and 5:30 pm. Other times can be arranged on WhatsApp. Which one suits you depends on the month. In Playa del Carmen the sun rises between 6:07 am, from late May to mid-June, and 7:27 am in January, and it sets between 6:06 pm in late November and 7:33 pm from late June to mid-July.

One of the three morning starts always falls within 25 minutes of sunrise: 6:30 am from April to September, 7:00 or 7:30 am from November to February, and 6:30 or 7:00 am in March and October, depending on the date. In the afternoon, from October to January, sunset comes 36 to 69 minutes after a 5:30 pm start, so a one-hour session ends close to sunset, or after it. From April to August the sun sets after 7:00 pm; for the last light then, ask us about a later time on WhatsApp.

Heat, Naps and the Youngest Travelers

Early and late starts also keep little ones out of the strongest sun. The U.S. Food and Drug Administration and the American Academy of Pediatrics recommend keeping newborns and babies younger than 6 months out of direct sunlight, and the FDA adds that it is especially important to keep babies out of the sun between 10 a.m. and 2 p.m. Every online start time keeps the session outside that window, even with our 90-minute collections. With a baby under six months, choose a spot with shade close by.

Then plan around naps, not the other way around. If your toddler sleeps after lunch, a morning start leaves the afternoon free; if mornings are slow in your family, 5:00 or 5:30 pm, after the nap, may suit you better. After a long flight, let body clocks choose: children who wake very early in the first days of a trip often do best in a morning session. One parent of a one-year-old wrote:

“They were so good at getting us to feel natural and getting our 1 year old to smile.”
