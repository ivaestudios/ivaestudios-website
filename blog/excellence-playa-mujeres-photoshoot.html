In short

Excellence Playa Mujeres gives a couples session three settings in one walk: a calm, pale beach facing Isla Mujeres, the resort's long shaded walkways, and, if you book one, a rooftop terrace suite where we can shoot in total privacy at golden hour. For most of the year the sun rises from behind Isla Mujeres. Sessions start at 245 USD.

Excellence Playa Mujeres is one of the resorts that put this quiet corner north of Cancún on the map: adults-only, all-inclusive, with a wide beach and a golf course instead of a busy road. For a couples session that means space and calm. It also offers something most resorts do not: suites with their own rooftop terraces, which turn into a private set above the palms. Here is how we plan a session there, spot by spot and hour by hour.

  • Private setRooftop terrace suites, including two-storey categories

If you only read one line: book the hour before sunset, start on your terrace if you have one, and finish at the water's edge as the sea turns blue.

The Rooftop Terrace: a Private Set

Excellence Playa Mujeres sells suites with their own rooftop terraces, including two-storey categories where the terrace sits above the bedroom. If you have booked one, it is the most private place on the property to be photographed, and the one we use first. Nobody walks through the frame, there is a view over the palms toward the sea, and the warm light of the last hour falls across the terrace from behind the resort.

On the terrace we work slowly: coffee or a glass of wine, the two of you on the daybed or at the railing, the soaking tub if it has one, then a few wider frames with the coastline behind. Fifteen minutes here relaxes most couples more than any amount of posing advice, and the photos have an intimacy the beach cannot give. Robes and bare feet are fine; this part of the session is meant to look like your holiday, not a production.

If your suite does not have a terrace, a sea-view balcony works on a smaller scale, and we can shoot the same quiet moments in the room by the window.

The Beach and the Walkways

From the suite we walk down to the beach, which is why we usually book this session for the late afternoon. The sand here is wide and pale and the water is calm and shallow enough to step into, with the low line of Isla Mujeres across the channel. Because the beach faces east, there is no sun in your eyes at the end of the day: the shoreline falls into soft, even light and the sea goes from turquoise to deep blue as we work.

Between the two, the resort's shaded walkways and colonnades give clean architectural frames, and they are our fallback if the sun is still strong. On the land side, the golf course designed by Greg Norman and the inland marina offer green and nautical backgrounds; both are working areas, so we use them only with the resort's agreement and outside of play or boat traffic.

From this end of Playa Mujeres, Isla Mujeres stretches across the eastern horizon, and for most of the year, roughly August through April, the sun comes up from behind the island. Around the summer months it rises over open water just to the north of it. Either way, a 6:30 am session gives you an empty beach and a glowing horizon, and it is the best choice for couples who hate the heat. For the evening, start about 50 minutes before sunset so that the rooftop and the beach both get good light.

Access for a Staying Guest

If you are staying at Excellence Playa Mujeres, our photographer comes in as your invited guest: you give the front desk the name and the time a day or two in advance, and we check in at the lobby. For a short private session for two, a staying guest is very seldom asked for the outside vendor fee that resorts apply to weddings, but we confirm the resort's current policy in writing before your date. Note that adults-only means the photographer must also be over 18, which we always are.

Not staying here? Then the photographer is a visitor and a day pass usually applies. Our guide to bringing an outside photographer to a resort covers both situations, and the Playa Mujeres vendor fee comparison explains how the charges work for weddings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we do a photoshoot on our rooftop terrace at Excellence Playa Mujeres?
Yes. If you have booked a rooftop terrace suite, the terrace is the most private place on the property for photographs, and we usually start the session there in the hour before sunset before walking down to the beach. The photographer comes in as your invited guest.
What is the best time for a couples photoshoot at Excellence Playa Mujeres?
The hour before sunset, which ranges from about 6:05 pm in December to 7:32 pm in July, or sunrise. For most of the year, roughly August through April, the sun rises from behind Isla Mujeres across the channel, and the beach is empty at that hour.
Do I have to pay a fee to bring a photographer to Excellence Playa Mujeres?
For a staying guest booking a short private session, the outside vendor fee that resorts charge for weddings is very seldom applied. Policies can change, so we confirm with the resort in writing before your date. Non-guests should expect a day pass for the photographer.
How much is a couples photoshoot at Excellence Playa Mujeres?
IVAE Studios sessions start at 245 USD for 35 minutes and 15 photos. Essential Flow is 395 USD for 60 minutes and 35 photos, and Creative Runway, which suits a rooftop plus beach session, is 475 USD for 75 minutes and 50 photos. Galleries arrive in one to three business days.
Is Excellence Playa Mujeres adults-only?
Yes, it is an adults-only all-inclusive resort, which keeps the beach and pool areas quiet in your photographs. Families travelling together often look at its sister resort next door, Finest Playa Mujeres, which has family and adults-only areas.
