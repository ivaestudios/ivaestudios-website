In short

January in Cancun is warm, dry-season weather: average highs of 29°C (84°F), lows of 20°C (68°F) and a sea of about 26°C (79°F). Cold fronts, called nortes, bring a few windy days with rough water, and sargassum is usually low in winter. Sunset moves from 6:17 pm on January 1 to 6:37 pm on January 31, so a one-hour session starts around 5:15 to 5:35 pm.

January is when the questions change from holiday plans to plain weather: is it warm enough to swim, will it rain, is the seaweed back and what time does the sun go down? Here are the answers we give our own clients, with Mexico's official climate normals, this winter's cold-front forecast and the light times we use to schedule sessions on this coast.

Temperatures and rain are the official 1991 to 2020 normals of Mexico's national weather service (SMN) for the Cancun station. Sea temperature is the typical January reading for this coast, and the light times are calculated for the Hotel Zone beach.

Warm Days, the Coolest Nights and the Nortes

January is the coolest month of the year in Cancun, and it is still beach weather. Days average 29°C (84°F) with low humidity and a steady breeze. Nights average 20°C (68°F), the lowest of the year, so the hour after sunset is the one time of year you may want sleeves.

The weather to know about is the norte. From November to March, cold fronts move down from the Gulf of Mexico, and when one reaches this coast it brings wind from the north, low cloud, a passing shower and choppy water. Most of January's 104 mm of average rain arrives this way, in short bursts between long sunny stretches. For the 2026 to 2027 season the SMN forecasts 56 cold fronts across Mexico, above the average of 50, with eight expected in January. Not every front reaches Cancun, and on this coast the windy part rarely lasts more than two days.

How we plan around a norte

We watch the forecast from 72 hours out and keep a backup evening in every January booking. On a windy day we move to the side of the coast that is sheltered from the north wind instead of cancelling. The day after a front passes often has the clearest air and the best sunset of the week.

Is the Sea Warm Enough to Swim?

Yes. The Caribbean here sits at about 26°C (79°F) in January, a degree or so cooler than in December and far warmer than any beach in the United States or Canada at this time of year. Children stay in the water for hours. The exception is the day a norte blows through: waves build, some beaches fly a red flag and small boats to Isla Mujeres can be delayed.

For photos in the water, such as walking in the shallows or a splash with the kids, calm days are the rule rather than the exception. If your dates are flexible, plan water photos for the first half of your trip so there is room to move them.

Seaweed in January

Winter is normally the cleanest season on this coast, and January is usually part of it. There is one honest caveat. In 2026, a record sargassum year, the seaweed began arriving in January, earlier than normal. Esteban Amaro Mauricio, the hydrobiologist who directs the Quintana Roo Sargassum Monitoring Network, has warned that it could return as early as January 2027. In mid-October 2026, Mexico's Navy rated the state's sargassum as very low.

What that means in practice: expect clean beaches, and check the forecast the week you travel. If patches do arrive, the beaches that face Isla Mujeres, such as Playa Mujeres and Costa Mujeres, are usually the last affected, and we move sessions to whichever shore is clear that day. Our sargassum season guide explains the full year, and the beach-by-beach forecast shows which shores stay clear.

Sunrise, Golden Hour and Sunset

January days are short but getting longer: sunset moves 20 minutes later over the month. Quintana Roo keeps the same clock all year, with no daylight saving, and in winter that clock matches Eastern Time in New York and Toronto. These are the times for the Hotel Zone beach:

"Golden light" is when the sun drops below six degrees and the color turns warm; "last light" is the end of civil twilight, when the sky still glows. For a one-hour session we start about an hour before sunset. January also has the latest sunrise of the year, around 7:27 am, which makes it the easiest month for a sunrise session: no 5 am alarm, and the sun comes up over the sea from the Hotel Zone. Crowds and the Quiet Weeks

The month starts busy. New Year's week runs into the first days of January while schools in the United States, Canada and Mexico are still on break. The second busy stretch is the Martin Luther King Jr. long weekend, January 16 to 18 in 2027, when many American families add a few days to make a short trip.

Between and after those dates, January is one of the calmer parts of the winter season: the weather is the same and the beaches have more room in the late afternoon. For photos, everyone wants the hour before sunset, so on the busy weekends book the session when you book your flights. Ask your resort early about access for outside vendors; our guide to bringing an outside photographer to a resort covers the usual rules.

What to Wear in January

Dress for a warm afternoon and a breezy evening. Linen, cotton and flowing dresses work well; fabrics with a little weight move beautifully in the wind, while very light short skirts fight it. Bring a light knit, a linen shirt or a wrap for after the session, when the temperature drops toward 20°C.

January light is clear and slightly cool in tone, so white, sand, soft blue and sage look fresh against the water. Our guides to family outfits, couples outfits and coordinating outfits by age have full palettes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is January a good time to visit Cancun?
Yes. January is dry-season weather: warm days, low humidity and usually very little seaweed. Expect a few windy cold fronts, called nortes, and cooler evenings. New Year's week and the Martin Luther King Jr. weekend are busy; the rest of the month is calmer.
What is the weather like in Cancun in January?
The official SMN normals for Cancun give an average high of 29°C (84°F), an average low of 20°C (68°F) and 104 mm of rain in January, most of it arriving in short bursts with cold fronts. January has the coolest nights of the year.
Can you swim in Cancun in January?
Yes. The sea is about 26°C (79°F). On the day a cold front blows through, the water can be rough and some beaches fly a red flag; the calm returns within a day or two.
Is there seaweed in Cancun in January?
Usually very little, because winter is the cleanest season. In 2026 the sargassum began arriving in January, earlier than normal, and the director of the Quintana Roo Sargassum Monitoring Network has warned it could return as early as January 2027, so check the forecast the week you travel.
What time is sunset in Cancun in January?
On the Hotel Zone beach, sunset is at 6:17 pm on January 1, 6:26 pm on January 15 and 6:37 pm on January 31. Warm golden light starts about 30 minutes earlier. Quintana Roo does not use daylight saving.
What time should we book a photo session in January?
For a one-hour sunset session, start about an hour before sunset: around 5:15 pm early in the month and 5:35 pm at the end. For sunrise, January has the latest sunrise of the year, about 7:27 am.
