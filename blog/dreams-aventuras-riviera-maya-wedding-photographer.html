Where the Ceremony Happens

Ask Dreams Aventuras's wedding team for the current ceremony and reception venues. On this coast the strongest settings are the beach and any raised terrace facing the sea, with pools and loungers out of the frame.

The coast here faces east onto the open Caribbean, so the sun rises over the sea and sets behind the resort, leaving the beach in soft, even light at the end of the day. We plan ceremonies to start about 90 minutes before sunset, so that family photos and portraits fall in the warm last light. From roughly September through April the sun rises from behind Cozumel, which makes a sunrise session here unusually striking.

Bringing Your Own Photographer

Expect Dreams Aventuras to charge outside wedding vendors for access. Resorts call it a vendor fee, an outside supplier fee or a day pass, and they revise it by season and wedding package, which is why we do not print a number here. The moment to negotiate it is before you sign your wedding package; afterwards coordinators have little room. Ask whether it is charged per vendor or per person, and get the answer by email. We handle the paperwork with your coordinator.

Coverage and Prices

What does it cost? Half-day coverage, up to four hours, is 1,800 to 2,800 USD. The full day, eight to ten hours with two photographers, is 3,500 to 5,500 USD, the right choice for larger weddings. With a wedding film added, 5,500 to 8,000 USD. We deliver every wedding gallery within one to three business days, not the weeks or months that are common elsewhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can you get married at Dreams Aventuras?
Ask Dreams Aventuras's wedding team for the current venues and capacities. The beach and raised terraces facing the sea give the best photographs.
What time is best for a wedding at Dreams Aventuras?
Start the ceremony about 90 minutes before sunset, so that portraits fall in the warm last light. Sunset here ranges from 6:08 pm in mid-winter to 7:33 pm in mid-summer, and Quintana Roo does not use daylight saving.
Does Dreams Aventuras charge for an outside wedding photographer?
Expect an access charge for outside wedding vendors, as a vendor fee or a per-person day pass, which the resort revises by season and package. Ask for the amount in writing before you sign, and ask whether it applies per vendor or per person.
How much does IVAE Studios charge for a wedding at Dreams Aventuras?
Half-day coverage of up to four hours is 1,800 to 2,800 USD, a full day of eight to ten hours with a second photographer is 3,500 to 5,500 USD, and coverage with a wedding film is 5,500 to 8,000 USD. The resort's vendor charge is separate.
