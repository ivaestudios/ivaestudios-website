In short

A Cancun proposal is three jobs: the setup, by a resort romance team or an independent planner; the photography, by a hidden photographer timed to the light; and your part, the ring and the walk. On pages we checked on November 14, 2026, one planner's Cancun setups started at $799 and a resort's Marry Me package cost 1,100 USD, both without a photographer. We photograph only, from 295 USD.

Search for a Cancun proposal planner and you will find arches, lanterns and giant letters on the sand. But a proposal also needs someone to photograph the question without being seen, and someone to get the ring, and the person you love, to the right spot at the right minute. We have photographed more than 100 surprise proposals in Cancun and the Riviera Maya, and our role in each plan is the photography. Here is who does what, and whether you need a planner at all.

One Proposal, Three Jobs

A proposal splits into three jobs, whoever does each one:

  • The setup. Decor, a table for two, music, permission to use the spot, the build and the teardown: a resort romance team or an independent proposal planner.
  • The photography. A photographer who stays out of sight until the question, works with the light, then steps in for portraits after the yes. That is our part.
  • Your part. The ring, the cover story and the walk to the spot.

A planner can add a photographer to a package, and we can tell you where the light falls and when to walk, but we do not build setups or sell decor, dinners or music. Our collections cover the photography only; the rest is paid to whoever provides it.

What Proposal Planners Include and Cost

Not every planner publishes prices, so on November 14, 2026 we read the published prices of two planners and one resort. These are other businesses' prices, not ours, and they change:

  • A proposal planning company with ready-made Cancun packages lists seven setups, from a lantern-lit deck to a flower heart arch or a private fire show, “starting at” $799 to $2,139, most with about an hour at the setup and the planner's coordination. On its $799 deck package, which we read in full, a photographer was a $575 add-on, a musician $519, and hotel fees were not covered.
  • A resort in Costa Mujeres, on an extras menu booked through its concierge, lists a Marry Me package at 1,100 USD: big letters, a red rose path, LED candles and petals in the sand, a red carpet and a room decoration. A beach gazebo dinner adds 311 USD; no photographer appears on the menu.
  • A company that plans destination proposals worldwide lists pre-set packages at $3,000 to $7,000, all inclusive with planning, photography and decor, custom designs from $8,000, and immersive productions at $20,000 to $150,000 or more. Its page is dated April 15, 2026 and gives no separate price for Mexico.

So “luxury proposal packages” can mean a resort menu or a five- or six-figure production, and the price follows the build. On our side, luxury means what we publish: collections up to The Heirloom (90 minutes, the complete edited gallery, 895 USD), editorial direction and a private 4K gallery, at the resort or villa you choose.

When a Planner Is Worth It

A venue you have never seen

If you cannot scout the resort or the beach and do not know its rules, a planner who works there takes out the guessing: which spots are allowed, what the hotel charges outside vendors, where a crew can build unseen. On the planner package we read, if your hotel has no beach or does not allow setups on it, building at an allowed beach instead cost $500 more.

A large setup

Arches, giant letters, a fire show, a musician: anything that needs a crew, permission and a teardown is a planner's job or the resort's, including keeping the build out of your partner's path.

A family surprise

If parents, siblings or grandparents fly in to appear after the yes, someone has to hide them and time their entrance; a planner can choreograph that. For the photos there is no limit on people and no per-person charge, so three generations can join the portraits after the yes. For groups of up to 16, we can arrange transportation in a full van to the location; its price is not published, so ask when you write. See our guides to a photo session with pickup for groups and multigenerational family photo ideas.

When a Resort Team or a Sunset Plan Is Enough

If you are staying at a resort and want a private dinner on its beach, its romance team, booked through the concierge or the events desk, already knows the property and its rules. You book the table and the decor as a guest; we photograph. See coordinating a proposal with your resort concierge.

A proposal can also need nothing built: the two of you, a quiet stretch of sand, the ring and a photographer your partner never notices. Nothing gives the plan away, and the evening light makes the setting. If your partner would hate an audience, start here, or browse our luxury proposal ideas in Cancun.

How to Read Planner Reviews and Photos

Stars alone tell you little. In proposal planner reviews, look for:

  • Proposals, not weddings. Reviews that name the place and say how the day went, including wind or delays.
  • Where they are posted. Google, Tripadvisor or WeddingWire, platforms the planner does not run, as well as its own site.
  • Real setups in real light. Ask for one they built at your resort or on your beach, at your hour, and who took those photos.
  • The terms. The planner package we read is non-refundable but can be rescheduled with 14 days' notice; the resort menu charges 50% for a romantic dinner cancelled less than 12 hours ahead.

How We Work With Your Planner

When a planner or a resort builds the setup, three things are settled between them and us before the day.

The signal

One cue everyone knows: reaching the end of the rose path, a phrase, a hand to your pocket. The crew, a musician, a server with champagne and the photographer all wait for the same one; with two signals, someone moves early.

The light

Setups are booked by the clock; photographs depend on the sun. We place the question in soft early morning or late afternoon light, with time for portraits after the yes while the setup still stands, so check that your booked hour covers both.

Which way the setup faces

The direction of an arch or a row of letters decides whether the light falls on your faces, whether MARRY ME reads from where we stand and where we can wait unseen. Send us the planner's layout, or put us in touch, in English or Spanish, before anything is built.

Some hotels charge a fee for outside photographers, which you pay to the hotel; we confirm your hotel's rule before the session. After the yes, we stop hiding and direct the portraits. Ben L. wrote this after a birthday and anniversary session:

“At no point were we left wondering what to do, where to be, or what was happening next.”

Ben L., Google review

Your Part: Ring, Cover Story, Walk

  • The ring. On you or in your carry-on, never in checked luggage.
  • The cover story. A believable reason to dress up and be at that spot at that minute: a sunset dinner, or a couples photo session, which also explains the camera.
  • The walk. Where to stop and which way to face, agreed before the day. Hold the ring up and stay on one knee a beat longer than feels natural.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a proposal planner in Cancun?

Not always. A planner is worth it for a venue you have never seen, a large setup or a family surprise. For a dinner at your resort, its romance team can be enough, and a sunset proposal on the beach needs only the two of you, the ring and a photographer.

How much does a Cancun proposal planner cost?

On pages we checked on November 14, 2026, one planner's Cancun setups started at $799 to $2,139, a Costa Mujeres resort's Marry Me package cost 1,100 USD, and a planner that works worldwide listed pre-set packages, photography included, at $3,000 to $7,000, with no separate price for Mexico. Prices change, so confirm in writing.

Does a proposal planner include the photographer?

Not always. On the pages we read, one planner's Cancun package listed a photographer as a $575 add-on, a Costa Mujeres resort's extras menu listed none, and a planner that works worldwide included photography in its pre-set packages. If your package includes one, ask to see their proposal work; if not, book your own and tell the planner early.

Can IVAE Studios plan the setup?

No. We photograph only: decor, dinners and music are booked with a planner or your resort and paid to them. We work with them on the signal, the light and which way the setup faces, in English or Spanish.

How much does the proposal photography cost, and is there a deposit?

From 295 USD (The Postcard, 35 minutes, 15 edited photos) to 895 USD (The Heirloom, 90 minutes, the complete gallery). There is no deposit: you pay at the end, by card or in cash, and can cancel free of charge until one day before.

Can the family join the proposal photos?

Yes. There is no limit on people and no per-person charge, so parents, siblings and grandparents can join the portraits after the yes. For groups of up to 16, we can arrange a full van to the location; ask for its price when you write.
