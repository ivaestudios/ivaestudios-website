Costa Mujeres or Riviera Cancun With Kids?

In short

Pick Riviera Cancun, around Puerto Morelos, for the shortest airport ride, 15 to 25 minutes in published figures, and reef-calm water on Petempich Bay. Pick Costa Mujeres, north of the city, for shallow water sheltered by Isla Mujeres and ferries to the island, 30 to 60 minutes from the airport. Check age policies first: Haven Riviera Cancun and both Excellence resorts are adults-only.

Families booking a newer all-inclusive outside the Hotel Zone often end up choosing between two corridors on opposite sides of Cancun: Costa Mujeres and Playa Mujeres to the north, and Riviera Cancun toward Puerto Morelos to the south, which many parents call the Riviera Maya. We photograph families on both, and every airport time, resort policy and seaweed reading below comes from official sources checked on November 14, 2026.

Two Corridors on Opposite Sides of Cancun

Costa Mujeres is the coast north of the city, past the ferry terminals of Puerto Juarez and Punta Sam, about 20 minutes from the Hotel Zone, says Quintana Roo's tourism board. Playa Mujeres is the gated community at its southern end, with a golf course and an inland marina; our guide to Costa Mujeres and Playa Mujeres untangles the names.

Riviera Cancun is the name resorts use for the coast south of the airport, around Puerto Morelos; the big ones line Petempich Bay, just north of the town. Several call themselves Riviera Maya resorts, and the tourism board calls Puerto Morelos the shortest ride from Cancun airport in the whole Riviera Maya. The Airport Ride, by the Clock

For Puerto Morelos the tourism board says about 20 minutes. Excellence Riviera Cancun says 15 minutes in its fact sheet and about 20 in its FAQ, and Royalton Splash Riviera Cancun says 15 minutes in one place and 20 to 25 by car in another. For Costa Mujeres the board says 30 minutes, Excellence and Finest Playa Mujeres say 40, and Riu Palace Costa Mujeres, about 35 km away, says 45 to 60 minutes depending on traffic; as the board notes, the transfer crosses the city.

With a baby or a late landing, that gap counts twice: arrival day and departure day.

The Water: Island Shelter or Reef

Both sides are known for calm water, for different reasons. The tourism board describes Costa Mujeres as wide white beaches on a shallow turquoise sea without waves, where people swim and snorkel close to shore, and the Excellence Playa Mujeres fact sheet describes the community's beaches as sheltered by Isla Mujeres, just off the coast.

To the south, the Puerto Morelos Reef National Park protects the stretch of the Mesoamerican Reef in front of the town, and the tourism board says the reef works as a natural breakwater, so the sea reaches the beach calm. The board singles out Petempich Bay, where the big resorts are, as calm, shallow and protected by the reef, a good choice for families with small children. Either way, check the flag each morning; our beach conditions guide shows how.

Sargassum on Each Side

Neither side is immune, so check the official maps for your week. Mexico's Navy, SEMAR, rated sargassum scarce (escaso) in its bulletin of November 13, 2026, the latest posted when we checked. The state environment ministry's traffic-light map of the same day rated Isla Blanca, Costa Mujeres and Playa Mujeres low and Punta Sam clear. To the south, its points from Riviera Cancun down to Petempich Bay and Punta Caracol were low, and the two Puerto Morelos town points moderate.

That is one day, not a forecast. Look at the latest map for your beach two evenings before a session: cancellation is free up to one day before, and there is no deposit to lose. More in Costa Mujeres sargassum and your photos.

Towns and Day Trips

From Costa Mujeres. Isla Mujeres is the easy outing. Ultramar's passenger ferries leave from Puerto Juarez at 580 pesos round trip per adult and 440 per child under 1.20 m, and babies under 1 ride free with a boarding pass; Punta Sam, a few minutes from the resorts, is the only crossing that takes cars. See our Isla Mujeres day plan from Costa Mujeres and our Cozumel or Isla Mujeres comparison.

From Riviera Cancun. Puerto Morelos is a fishing town with a square, cafes, a pier and a lighthouse leaning since Hurricane Beulah in 1967. Reef snorkel trips leave from the pier with local guides, in small groups under the park's rules. Inland, the Ruta de los Cenotes runs about 35 km to Leona Vicario, past cenotes such as Las Mojarras, which the board calls perfect for families, and the Tren Maya's Puerto Morelos station sits on the same road. Our Puerto Morelos family session guide and Tren Maya day trips with kids go further.

Riu Palace Costa Mujeres or Royalton Splash Riviera Cancun. Riu Palace runs the RIU Land kids' program and gives complimentary access to the Splash Water World park at Riu Dunamar next door; Royalton Splash has a kids' club for ages 4 to 12, a teens' club and what it calls one of the largest on-site water parks in the Caribbean. The airport ride is the clearest difference. Our Riu Palace and Royalton Splash guides show where we shoot.

Majestic Elegance Costa Mujeres or Haven Riviera Cancun. Only one takes children: Haven is adults-only, while Majestic Elegance has a Family Section with pools for the whole family and one just for children.

Excellence Playa Mujeres or Excellence Riviera Cancun. Both are adults-only. Their family siblings are Finest Playa Mujeres, with pavilions by age group for toddlers, children and teens, and Finest Riviera Cancun in Puerto Morelos, for all ages, which the resort says opens on February 11, 2027.

How Each Side Photographs

Both coasts face east, so the sun rises over the water and sets behind the resorts, leaving soft, even light on the sand in the last hour. The horizon is the difference. From the southern resorts around Playa Mujeres, by our calculation, the sun comes up behind Isla Mujeres for much of the year; further north it rises over open sea more often. Our sunrise behind Isla Mujeres page has the times, and the shallows let small children wade into the picture with you.

On Riviera Cancun, the calm water of Petempich Bay gives a sunrise straight out of the sea, and Puerto Morelos, with its pier and leaning lighthouse, makes a second setting a short drive away.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Costa Mujeres in Cancun?

It is the coast just north of the city, past Puerto Juarez and Punta Sam, about 20 minutes from the Hotel Zone according to Quintana Roo's tourism board. Some resorts there list Cancun in their addresses, but it is a separate, quieter corridor.

Which is closer to Cancun airport, Costa Mujeres or Riviera Cancun?

Riviera Cancun. Published figures put Puerto Morelos and its resorts 15 to 25 minutes from the airport, against 30 to 60 minutes for Costa Mujeres, where the transfer crosses the city.

Which side has calmer water for small children?

Both are known for it: Costa Mujeres faces a shallow sea sheltered by Isla Mujeres, and the big Riviera Cancun resorts sit on Petempich Bay, behind the Puerto Morelos reef. Check the beach flag each morning on either side.

Are Haven Riviera Cancun and the Excellence resorts for families?

No: Haven Riviera Cancun, Excellence Riviera Cancun and Excellence Playa Mujeres are adults-only. With children, look at Majestic Elegance Costa Mujeres, which has a Family Section, Finest Playa Mujeres, or Finest Riviera Cancun, which the resort says opens February 11, 2027.

Can you photograph our family on either side?

Yes, with the same collections. For families we recommend The Chapter: 60 minutes, 35 edited photos, 475 USD, with no per-person charge and a preview within 24 hours. If your resort charges an outside-photographer fee, you pay it to the hotel, and we confirm the rule first.
