In short

Waldorf Astoria Riviera Maya, also known as Waldorf Astoria Cancun, has one of the most private beaches near Cancun, which makes it a very easy place to keep a secret. Ask about 30 minutes before sunset. Our sessions start at 245 USD, and the gallery arrives in one to three business days.

With 173 rooms on more than 100 acres and jungle behind the sand, this resort almost does the hiding for us. Here is how we would plan the moment.

Where to Ask

The empty stretch of beach. The shoreline here feels remarkably private compared with the Hotel Zone. We set up down the sand from the resort and shoot from a distance, with nothing behind you but sea and palm trees.

Your terrace. Most rooms have an ocean-facing balcony, and some have a private pool, which makes a very personal setting for the question. We would then move to the beach for portraits in the last light.

The Waldorf Terrace. Polished and elegant, with warm late-afternoon light, if the hotel confirms it is free at your hour.

The coast south of the airport faces east, so the sun rises from the sea and sets inland over the jungle. The beach falls into even light early in the evening here, which gives a long, comfortable window for portraits.

How We Keep the Surprise

We arrive before you do and stay out of sight, dressed like any other guest with a camera, at a spot we agree with you beforehand. You walk your partner to the place you chose, we photograph the question from a distance with a long lens, and we only come closer once you look for us. Then we stay for portraits of the two of you, so the same gallery has the moment and the relaxed photos after it.

Everything is arranged by WhatsApp or email ahead of time, including a code word in case plans change at the last minute. Our guide to planning a surprise proposal from abroad has the full checklist.

Getting the Photographer In

For guests of Waldorf Astoria Cancun there is no complicated process: register the photographer with the front desk a day or two ahead and we check in on arrival. Hotels set their own rules for outside vendors and revise them, so we get the current answer in writing for your date. Staying elsewhere usually means a day pass. Our guide to outside photographers at resorts has the details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the best place to propose at Waldorf Astoria Cancun?
On the quiet beach, away from the resort's loungers, or on your own ocean-facing terrace if you want a very private moment. The Waldorf Terrace also works if the hotel confirms it is free.
What time should I propose at Waldorf Astoria Cancun?
About 30 minutes before sunset. Sunset here ranges from 6:06 pm in November to 7:32 pm in July, so the question falls near 5:35 pm in November and near 7:00 pm in July.
Can a photographer hide for a surprise proposal at Waldorf Astoria Cancun?
Yes. We arrive first, stay out of sight at the spot we agreed with you, and photograph the moment from a distance with a long lens. As with any outside vendor, we confirm access with the resort beforehand.
How much does a proposal photographer at Waldorf Astoria Cancun cost?
IVAE Studios collections start at 245 USD for 35 minutes and 15 photos, enough for the question and a few portraits. Many couples choose The Glow Hour at 325 USD or The Essential Flow at 395 USD for more time together afterwards.
