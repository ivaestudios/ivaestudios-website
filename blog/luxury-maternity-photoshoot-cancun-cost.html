How to compare maternity shoot prices

An average price for a maternity photoshoot hides more than it tells, because quotes count different things. Whichever luxury maternity photographer you choose, ask for these answers in writing:

  1. The total, and what it includes.
  2. The minutes, and at what hour.
  3. The number of edited photos and looks.
  4. When the preview and the gallery arrive, and what you may do with the files.
  5. The deposit, the cancellation terms and every extra fee, the hotel's included.

What Is Not in the Price

Our collections cover the photography: time, direction, edited photos and the gallery. Three things sit outside the price:

  • The gown. Wear your own or rent one. For a luxury maternity photoshoot dress, a long gown in a light fabric that moves, cut to follow the bump, in ivory, a soft neutral or one strong color, looks at home by the sea. More in what to wear for beach maternity photos.
  • Hair and makeup. Not part of our collections; book them separately if you want them.
  • The hotel's fee for outside photographers. Some hotels charge one. You pay it to the hotel, and we confirm your hotel's rule before the session.

Bring the Whole Family, Grandparents Included

A maternity session can be just you, the two of you, or everyone who will meet the baby. Older children, grandparents-to-be, siblings and cousins join at the same price: no per-person charge and no limit on people. For a big group of up to 16, several families or three generations, we can arrange a full van to take everyone to another location for the session, priced on request.

Booking Around Your Pregnancy

Plans can change when you are expecting, so our terms are simple: no deposit, payment at the end of the session by card or in cash, and free cancellation until one day before. Book at ivaestudios.com/book or message us on WhatsApp.

Flying while pregnant

When to travel and when to have the session are questions for your doctor or midwife, and each airline sets its own rules. On November 14, 2026, their pages said: Delta does not restrict flying while pregnant or require a medical certificate, and suggests checking with your doctor if you travel after your eighth month; Southwest recommends against air travel beginning at the 38th week; Air Canada accepts an uncomplicated pregnancy with no history of premature labor up to and including the 36th week, and asks for a medical clearance review if the pregnancy is high risk or at 36 weeks and beyond; WestJet asks expectant mothers more than 36 weeks along to review its health information with their physician. Check your airline's page again before you book.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a luxury maternity photoshoot cost in Cancun?

With us, 295 to 895 USD by collection: The Keepsake, 45 minutes and 25 edited photos, is 375 USD; The Story, 75 minutes with two looks, 545 USD; The Heirloom, 90 minutes with the complete edited gallery, 895 USD. There is no deposit: you pay at the end of the session.

What is included in the price of a maternity session?

The session time, our direction, the edited photos, a preview within 24 hours and a private 4K gallery in 1 to 3 business days, with unlimited downloads and print rights. The gown, hair and makeup and any hotel fee for outside photographers are not included.

Which collection should I book for two maternity looks?

The Story is the first collection with two looks: 75 minutes and 50 edited photos for 545 USD. The Editorial (645 USD) and The Heirloom (895 USD), both 90 minutes, also include two looks, and you can move up by paying only the difference.

Can my partner, children and parents join the maternity session?

Yes. There is no limit on the number of people and no per-person charge. For groups of up to 16, we can arrange a full van to take everyone to the session location; ask us for its price.

What time of day is best for maternity photos in the Cancun heat?

Online start times are 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am and 5:00 and 5:30 pm, away from the midday sun; for another time, message us on WhatsApp. On a hot day, The Keepsake, 45 minutes, keeps the session brief.

What happens if my plans change during the pregnancy?

There is no deposit, and cancellation is free until one day before. You pay only at the end of the session, by card or in cash.
