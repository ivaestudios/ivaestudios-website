In short

Ten luxury settings around Cancun: your resort's architecture and pool edge at sunrise, your suite terrace, a garden path, the Mayakoba lagoon, a marina at golden hour, a boat you charter, a villa you rent, an empty beach at first light, Playa Sol on Isla Mujeres at sunset and a cenote at opening time. One setting fits The Postcard (295 USD); two settings and two looks, The Story (545 USD) or The Editorial (645 USD).

A luxury photoshoot does not need a styled set: around Cancun the luxury is already there, in the resort you chose, the suite you booked, the water at the right hour. Looking for luxury places to take pictures near you? Start where you are staying.

Two Windows of Light

Our online starts are 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am and 5:00 and 5:30 pm; other times are arranged on WhatsApp. On the 15th of November to January, US Naval Observatory data puts Cancun's sunrise between 6:58 and 7:27 am and sunset between 6:06 and 6:27 pm: the 7:00 or 7:30 am start meets the sun, and the 5:00 or 5:30 pm start runs into the last hour of light. On the 15th of May to August the sun rises between 6:06 and 6:26 am and sets between 7:17 and 7:32 pm, so take 6:30 am or ask for a later evening start. Every month: our sunrise and sunset table.

At Your Resort

Resort photoshoot ideas start where you are staying. For all three, some hotels charge a fee for outside photographers, which you pay to the hotel; we confirm your hotel's rule before the session (see can you bring an outside photographer).

1. Architecture and the pool edge at sunrise

At first light a luxury hotel looks the way its architects drew it: long lines, empty loungers, still water. The frame: the two of you small against the building, then close at the pool's edge, the water holding the sky. A luxury photoshoot background that needs nothing added, so dress for quiet luxury.

Wear Solid colors, clean cuts, no logos: a column dress, a linen suit Best hour The morning start closest to sunrise Access Hotel grounds Collection The Postcard; The Story adds the beach

2. Your suite terrace

The most private setting is the one you booked: coffee at the terrace rail, a slow barefoot dance, the two of you framed by the open door. With a private pool, see the in-suite couples session.

Wear A silk slip dress, an open linen shirt Best hour Morning if it faces the sunrise, evening if it faces the sunset Access Still the hotel's rule Collection The Postcard; The Story adds the pool edge

3. A garden path

Gardens give shade, texture and a path that leads the eye to you: stone underfoot, palms or bamboo on both sides, the two of you walking toward the camera. The photograph at the top of this page was made in a garden.

Wear White, sand or terracotta, never head-to-toe green Best hour Early morning or the last hour of light Access Hotel grounds Collection With the beach, The Story or The Editorial

On the Water

4. The Mayakoba lagoon

In Playa del Carmen, Mayakoba's own site describes one lagoon connecting four resort hotels, with nature trails and 595 acres of mangrove forest. The frame: the two of you at the lagoon's edge or on a dock, mangroves behind, your reflection in the water. Our Mayakoba lagoon guide covers the light.

Wear White, sand, terracotta or rust; flat shoes for docks Best hour First light or the last hour before sunset Access Mayakoba says guests have access to the entire destination; your hotel's rule on photographers applies Collection The Story or The Editorial: lagoon, then beach

5. A marina at golden hour

Masts, white hulls and water turning gold: a marina at day's end looks like the opening scene of a film, and starts an evening well, walking the dock toward your boat. For sunset without a boat, see sunset photos at Nichupté Lagoon.

Wear Navy, white or cream; flat soles Best hour 5:00 or 5:30 pm from November to January; later from April to August, by WhatsApp Access Ask the marina or your charter company where visitors may walk Collection With the boat, The Story or The Editorial

6. A boat you charter

On the water the frames are the bow, the deck and the wind in your hair. You charter the boat and the photographer comes aboard; for a family on deck, see family photos on a chartered yacht.

Wear Fabrics that move without flying off; hair styled for wind Best hour Out on the water for the last hour of light Access Count the photographer as a passenger; send us the charter times Collection The Story or The Editorial, starting at the marina

Beyond the Resort

7. A villa you rent

A villa is a private set with rooms, the easiest place for two looks. Villa photoshoot poses that never feel posed: on the pool steps, feet in the water; in a doorway, one looking out, the other looking at them; at breakfast, mid-conversation; side by side on a daybed.

Wear One relaxed look in linen, one dressed for dinner Best hour Morning indoors; an evening start for the pool Access Your rental: tell the manager a photographer is coming Collection The Story or The Editorial: two looks, two corners

8. An empty beach at first light

Early, the white sand is yours: walking at the waterline, a dress catching the breeze. It is public, too: under Mexico's General Law on National Assets, maritime beaches and the 20-meter federal zone beside them, the ZOFEMAT, are goods of common use, and access may not be restricted except as the regulation allows.

Wear White, ivory or pale blue; bare feet Best hour The start closest to sunrise: 6:30 am in summer, 7:00 or 7:30 am in winter Access Public sand; hotel grounds keep their own rule Collection The Postcard; The Story adds the garden

9. Playa Sol, Isla Mujeres, at sunset

Isla Mujeres is a ferry ride from Cancun, and Quintana Roo's tourism promotion board describes Playa Sol, just south of Playa Norte, as facing west, the beach where the island watches the sun go down: the two of you at the water's edge as the sky turns orange, then pink. More on our Isla Mujeres photographer page.

Wear Warm tones: champagne, terracotta, white Best hour 5:00 or 5:30 pm from November to January; later from April to August, by WhatsApp Access A public beach; plan the ferry both ways Collection The Postcard

10. A cenote at opening time

Limestone, roots and still water while the cenote is quiet, with frames on the stairs, the platform and the water. Cenotes charge an entry fee and keep their own hours: Casa Tortuga, in Tulum, and Kin-Ha, in Puerto Morelos, open at 9:00 am, and Kin-Ha allows no sunscreen, not even biodegradable. See what to expect in a cenote.

Wear Swimwear plus a light dress for dry frames; minimal, waterproof makeup Best hour Opening time, arranged on WhatsApp Access Entry fee; each cenote sets its own rules Collection The Postcard or The Keepsake

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best luxury photoshoot locations in Cancun?

Start where you are staying: your resort's architecture and pool edge, your suite terrace or a garden path. Beyond it: the Mayakoba lagoon, a marina, a chartered boat, a rented villa, an empty beach at first light, Playa Sol on Isla Mujeres or a cenote.

What time is best for a luxury photoshoot in Cancun?

Online starts are 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am and 5:00 and 5:30 pm; other times are arranged on WhatsApp. From November to January the evening starts run into the last hour of light; from April to August the sun sets after 7:00 pm, so ask for a later start.

Do resorts charge a fee for an outside photographer?

Some hotels do. Where there is a fee, you pay it to the hotel, and we confirm your hotel's rule before the session. The beach itself is public in Mexico; a hotel's pool, gardens and terraces follow the hotel's rules.

Can the whole family or a group come?

Yes. There is no limit on people and no per-person charge. For groups of up to 16 people we can arrange transportation in full vans; its price is not published, so ask when you write.
