In short

IVAE Studios photographs families in both Cancun and Playa del Carmen, from 295 USD with no deposit. Choose Cancun's Hotel Zone for the shortest ride, about 20 minutes from the airport, and the low-surf beaches where it begins. Choose Playa del Carmen, about 45 minutes away, for a walkable town on pedestrian Fifth Avenue, the quiet beaches of Playacar and the ferry to Cozumel. From either base, the easiest family session is an early one on your own resort's beach.

Both towns sit on the same Caribbean coast, and both work beautifully with children. What changes is the shape of your days: a resort strip between the sea and a lagoon, or a town you can walk. We photograph families from both bases, so here is the comparison we give parents, built on official and operator sources we read on November 14, 2026.

From the Airport: 20 or 45 Minutes

Quintana Roo's tourism board puts Cancun's airport about 20 minutes from the Hotel Zone and about 45 minutes from Playa del Carmen on Federal Highway 307. Both are round numbers; your hotel's location and the traffic set the real time. With small children the gap counts twice, after the flight and again on the way home, so the short ride is Cancun's best argument.

The board lists private transfers from the airport to hotels in both places. Playa adds an easy public option: direct buses to the terminal on Fifth Avenue, one block from the sea and the Cozumel pier, within walking distance of the beach and most downtown hotels. In Cancun, ADO's airport buses run downtown or to a single Hotel Zone stop at Punta Cancun, so hotels farther south on the strip are still a taxi or city bus away. Our Cancun airport transfers guide covers booking the ride.

Beaches: Calm Water, Surf and Space

All of Cancun's beaches are public, and the tourism board sorts them by the water. Those where the Hotel Zone begins, Las Perlas, Langosta, Tortugas, Caracol and Gaviota Azul, have little surf and are the ones it recommends for children; farther south, Chac Mool, Marlin, Ballenas and Delfines have more waves and water sports. Delfines, one of the city's largest beaches, has no hotels on the sand. See which Cancun beach is safest for family photos with kids.

In Playa del Carmen the beach runs in front of the center, with beach clubs and the pier for snorkel, dive and catamaran boats. To the north, Punta Esmeralda has a freshwater spring running out into the sand; to the south lies Playacar, under the board's heading of quiet beaches. For space on the sand, Playacar is the easy answer.

Choosing an all-inclusive? For a week inside the resort, the shorter transfer tips it to Cancun, especially at a hotel near the calm beaches where the Hotel Zone begins. For a town within reach, look at Playacar, which runs south from the ferry terminal and has beachfront all-inclusives such as Riu Playacar.

Island Days From Each Base

Playa's ferry terminal is in the center, at Calle 1 Sur, and the crossing to Cozumel takes about 45 minutes; Xcaret Xailing asks passengers to arrive 30 minutes early. From Cancun, a Cozumel day starts with the drive to Playa.

Cancun's island is Isla Mujeres: Xcaret Xailing's ferry leaves from El Embarcadero, at km 4.5 of the Hotel Zone, for a crossing of about 20 to 30 minutes, and the tourism board lists Puerto Juárez, north of the city, as the shortest route, about 20 minutes. Our Cozumel or Isla Mujeres guide compares the two days with kids.

Seaweed: One Coast, One Map

Neither base escapes sargassum: both face the same sea, and the state rates their beaches on one map. On the November 11, 2026 edition of the traffic-light map from SEMA, Quintana Roo's environment ministry, every Hotel Zone point from Las Perlas to Punta Nizuc was green, the low level, and so was every Playa del Carmen point from Punta Esmeralda to Playacar. At the Hotel Zone's far south end, by Punta Nizuc, Playa Coral was orange, abundant: a reminder to read the map beach by beach. Of 100 beaches in the north of the state, 86 were low, 8 moderate, 3 abundant, 3 clear and none excessive.

Mexico's Navy rated sargassum scarce in its November 13 bulletin, with the only possible arrival south of Tulum, and the University of South Florida's October 31 outlook expected amounts to keep falling until possibly the end of November or December. Check the map the week you travel; our seaweed season guide shows the usual pattern by month.

Strollers, Walks and Evenings

Fifth Avenue, Playa's heart, is a pedestrian promenade parallel to the sea, lined with small hotels, shops and restaurants, so a stroller walk to dinner stays off the roadside. Parque Fundadores, by the sea at the start of the avenue, hosts afternoon shows such as Mayan dancers and the Papantla flyers. At sunset the avenue fills with live music, mariachis and DJs, and the bars and clubs of Calle 12 run until dawn, so with young children choose a hotel a few blocks away, or in Playacar.

Cancun's Hotel Zone, with its resorts, malls, restaurants and marinas, is made for staying put. Evenings out are usually a short ride: lagoon restaurants with mariachis, live music and shows, or the tourism board's pick for children, a dinner show aboard a ship. On the sand, in either town, a carrier beats a stroller.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Playa del Carmen or Cancun better for families?

Cancun's Hotel Zone is about 20 minutes from the airport, with low-surf beaches where it begins, easiest with babies and toddlers. Playa del Carmen, about 45 minutes away, is a walkable town on pedestrian Fifth Avenue, with quiet beaches in Playacar and the ferry to Cozumel.

What is the difference between Cancun and Playa del Carmen?

Cancun's Hotel Zone is a resort strip between the Caribbean and the Nichupté lagoon, where outings mean a taxi or the city bus. Playa del Carmen is a town built around Fifth Avenue, a pedestrian promenade parallel to the sea, with the ferry to Cozumel leaving from the center.

Which has better beaches for young kids?

Quintana Roo's tourism board recommends Cancun's low-surf beaches where the Hotel Zone begins for children: Las Perlas, Langosta, Tortugas, Caracol and Gaviota Azul. In Playa del Carmen, the quiet beaches are south of the center, in Playacar.

Is Playa del Carmen safe for families?

Playa del Carmen and Cancun are both in Quintana Roo, so official advice is the same for either base. Canada's advice for Mexico, last updated October 19, 2026, is to exercise a high degree of caution, and Quintana Roo is not among the states where it advises avoiding non-essential travel.

Does Playa del Carmen or Cancun get more sargassum?

It changes beach by beach with the wind and currents. On the state's November 11, 2026 traffic-light map, every Playa del Carmen point, Playacar included, was at the low level, and so was the Hotel Zone from Las Perlas to Punta Nizuc, while Playa Coral, at its far south end, was abundant. Check the latest map the week you travel.

Where would our family photo session happen?

From Playa del Carmen, on Playacar's quiet beaches or early on the beach north of the ferry pier; from Cancun, on the calm beaches where the Hotel Zone begins or the open-sea beaches farther south. Online start times are 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am and 5:00 and 5:30 pm, with no deposit.
