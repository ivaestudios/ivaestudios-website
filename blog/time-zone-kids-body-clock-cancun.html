In short

Cancun keeps UTC-5 all year. It matches Winnipeg all year, and New York and Toronto until March 13, 2027; from March 14 to November 6, 2027 it is an hour behind New York and level with Chicago. For kids from the West, our 6:30 am start is 3:30 am to a Los Angeles toddler in winter, while 5:00 pm feels like 2:00 pm. From the East Coast and Atlantic Canada, mornings are easy and summer sunsets run late.

Parents planning a trip usually have two questions about time: what time is it in Cancun, and what will our start time feel like to the kids? The first answer never changes. The second depends on where you live and when you travel, because most of the US and much of Canada change their clocks and Cancun does not.

What Time It Is in Cancun

Cancun runs on UTC-5 all year.

That is the offset of US Eastern Standard Time, and it never moves. Quintana Roo got its own Southeast Zone by a decree published on January 31, 2015, in force the next day, and Mexico's 2022 time zone law keeps it on the 75th meridian west, with seasonal clock changes only along the northern border. Mexico City stays an hour behind Cancun all year. If an older forum post gives you different times, check its date.

The United States changes its clocks at 2:00 am on the second Sunday of March and the first Sunday of November (NIST): March 14 and November 7 in 2027. Hawaii and Arizona, except the Navajo Nation, stay on standard time. A bill to make daylight saving time permanent passed the House on July 14, 2026; as of November 14, 2026 it has been referred to the Senate and is not law.

Canada changed in 2026. Most of British Columbia sprang forward for the last time on March 8, 2026 and keeps UTC-7 all year, like Yukon since 2020. Alberta (UTC-6) and Manitoba (UTC-5) did not turn their clocks back on November 1, 2026 and stay there, and Saskatchewan keeps UTC-6. Ontario's Eastern-time communities still change on the US dates, and so do the Maritimes and Newfoundland under their time laws (New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador). Quebec said on October 28, 2026 that it wants November 1 to be its last change but has not chosen which time to keep, so check before a spring or summer trip.

Jet Lag With Kids: How Long the Clock Takes

From the lower 48 states and Canada the gap is three hours at most. The CDC's Yellow Book, written for health professionals, describes jet lag disorder after crossing at least two time zones and gives an average adjustment of about 1 hour a day after flying east and 1.5 hours a day after flying west. It has no separate figures for children, so treat them as a rough guide.

At that pace, a Los Angeles family in winter, flying east across three hours, needs about three days to catch up; a Halifax family in summer, flying west across two, a little over one. From New York, Toronto or Winnipeg in winter, and from Chicago in summer, there is nothing to catch up. The CDC also suggests shifting sleep by an hour a day for two to three days before the trip: earlier if you fly east, later if you fly west.

Naps travel too. A toddler who naps from 1:00 to 3:00 pm in Los Angeles will want that nap from 4:00 to 6:00 pm in Cancun during the first days of a winter trip, right on top of the evening starts. If that is your child, book the session later in your stay, or send us the nap time on WhatsApp and we can look at another start. Frequently Asked Questions

Is Cancun on Eastern time?
In winter, in practice yes. Cancun keeps UTC-5 all year, the offset of US Eastern Standard Time, so it matches New York and Toronto until March 13, 2027 and again from November 7, 2027. From March 14 to November 6, 2027 it is an hour behind New York and on Chicago's clock.
Does Cancun observe daylight saving time?
No. Quintana Roo has kept UTC-5 all year since February 1, 2015, when it moved to its own Southeast Zone. Mexico's 2022 time zone law allows seasonal clock changes only along the northern border.
What is the time difference between Cancun and California?
Cancun is 3 hours ahead of California until March 13, 2027 and from November 7, 2027, and 2 hours ahead from March 14 to November 6, 2027. British Columbia stopped changing its clocks in 2026, so Cancun is 2 hours ahead of Vancouver all year.
Will my kids get jet lag in Cancun?
Probably not much: from the lower 48 states and Canada the gap is three hours at most. The CDC Yellow Book describes jet lag disorder after crossing at least two time zones, with an average adjustment of about 1 hour a day after flying east and 1.5 hours a day after flying west, and no separate figures for children.
What session time is best for kids from the West Coast or the East Coast?
From the West Coast, 5:00 or 5:30 pm: a 6:30 am start is 3:30 am for a Los Angeles child in winter, while 5:00 pm feels like 2:00 pm. From the East Coast, the morning: from March 14 to November 6, 2027, 6:30 am feels like 7:30 am, and the June sunset at 7:30 pm like 8:30 pm.
Are the online booking times in Cancun time?
