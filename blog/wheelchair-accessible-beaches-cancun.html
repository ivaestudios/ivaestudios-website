In short

In September 2024, Cancun made Playa Las Perlas, at km 2.3 of the Hotel Zone, an accessible beach: ramps, handrails, a deck, accessible restrooms, and amphibious chairs, walkers, loungers and mats to reach the water. The city's seven Blue Flag public beaches, Playa Delfines included, list access ramps, restrooms and free parking. On Isla Mujeres, the city placed free amphibious chairs at the ZOFEMAT modules on Playa Centro and Playa Norte in December 2024. Airport wheelchair help comes from your airline.

A standard wheelchair, a walker or a stroller bogs down in soft, dry sand, but that does not have to end the beach day. We went through the official records of Cancun and Isla Mujeres for beaches with ramps and amphibious chairs, how and when to ask for the equipment, and where family photos work with a wheelchair or a walker. All of it was checked on November 14, 2026.

Playa Las Perlas: Built for Wheels

Playa Las Perlas is a small public beach, 53 meters of shoreline at km 2.3 of Boulevard Kukulcán. On September 11, 2024, the Benito Juárez municipality, which governs Cancun, inaugurated it as an accessible beach, with ramps and handrails, a deck with its own palapas, a tactile guide strip, separate restrooms for people with disabilities, and signs with a QR code that opens a video in sign language. That December, the city called it the first beach of its kind accessible to swimmers.

For the sand and the sea, the head of ZOFEMAT, the municipal office that looks after the beaches, reported the purchase of three wheelchairs, three amphibious chairs, two amphibious loungers, two amphibious walkers and mats to reach the water. The city's beach board also marks parking, lifeguards and public transport at Las Perlas, with hours of 9:00 am to 7:00 pm, and an April 2026 release says parking at the Blue Flag beaches is free.

Before you go

The equipment list comes from the September 2024 announcement, which does not say how the equipment is lent. Ask the ZOFEMAT staff on the beach what is available that day.

Playa Delfines and the Blue Flag Beaches

The municipality looks after seven Blue Flag public beaches: Playa del Niño, Las Perlas, Chac Mool, Marlín, Ballenas, Delfines and Coral. Its December 27, 2025 release lists universal access ramps among their services, with restrooms, showers, lifeguards, palapas, parking and ZOFEMAT information modules, and entry is free.

Playa Delfines, known as El Mirador, is far longer than Las Perlas: 353 meters at km 17.8, with parking, lifeguards and restrooms the beach board marks as accessible. We found no official mention of amphibious chairs or mats there, so plan it as a beach to enjoy from firm ground, and ask at the ZOFEMAT module how far the ramp goes before unloading. Our Playa Delfines photoshoot guide covers its best hour.

Check the flag first: the city's beach board shows each public beach's flag, and our guide to Cancun beach conditions today explains the colors.

Isla Mujeres: Free Amphibious Chairs

In December 2024 the Isla Mujeres municipality, with CRIT Teletón Quintana Roo and the island's DIF family welfare office, put three restored amphibious chairs into service for people with motor disabilities at the ZOFEMAT modules on Playa Centro and Playa Norte, free of charge, from 9:00 am to 6:00 pm. In March 2025, after training staff to handle them, it described the service at Playa Centro as free, every day from 9:00 am to 6:00 pm: you go to the module and ask.

Can visitors borrow one? The releases invite members of the public who need a chair to ask, without saying whether that includes visitors, and a July 2026 release says the modules serve national and foreign visitors from 9:00 am to 4:00 pm. A June 2025 release adds that the modules point people to each beach's restrooms, showers and access ramps for people with disabilities. With three chairs for two beaches, go in the morning and keep a plan B. Ask your ferry line about boarding with a wheelchair; for island photos, see our Isla Mujeres family photo session guide.

What to Ask Your Resort Before You Book

Most beach time happens at the resort, so ask before you book, by email, and keep the reply:

  • Is there an accessible room with a roll-in shower, and will you confirm it in writing?
  • Is there an elevator or a ramp at every change of level between the room, the lobby, the restaurants and the beach?
  • Where does the path to the sea end, and do you lend a beach wheelchair or lay mats on the sand?
  • Can the airport transfer carry a wheelchair, and does the van have a ramp or a lift?

Looking at Moon Palace or a Riu? Send the same questions. Riu's own page for Hotel Riu Cancun says nothing about accessibility and lists it as adults only, 18 and over. The same ramps, decks and mats work for a stroller.

Wheelchair Help at the Airport

Wheelchair help at Cancun International Airport comes through your airline; the passenger pages we checked on the site of ASUR, the airport's operator, list no wheelchair service. Ask the airline when you book and again at check-in.

From the United States. The Department of Transportation's rule, 14 CFR Part 382, requires airlines to help from the terminal entrance to the gate, between gates on a connection, and on and off the plane, with ground and boarding wheelchairs, ramps or lifts as needed, without requiring advance notice and at no charge. It covers U.S. airlines on every flight and foreign airlines on flights to or from the U.S.

From Canada. The Accessible Transportation for Persons with Disabilities Regulations require large airlines to help on flights to or from Canada, on request and free, with check-in, reaching the boarding area, boarding and getting off, and moving between your wheelchair and theirs or your seat. A request made at least 48 hours before departure must be honored; with less notice, the airline must make every reasonable effort, and some services, such as help at check-in, are owed even then.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Cancun wheelchair friendly?
Parts of it are. In 2024 the city made Playa Las Perlas an accessible beach, with ramps, handrails, a deck, accessible restrooms and amphibious chairs, walkers and loungers, and its seven Blue Flag public beaches list access ramps. Soft sand still stops a standard wheelchair, so plan around ramps, decks and firm ground.
Can you rent a beach wheelchair in Cancun?
The public options we found are equipment on the beach, not rentals: the amphibious chairs bought in 2024 for Playa Las Perlas, which you ask about with the ZOFEMAT staff there, and the free ones Isla Mujeres placed at its ZOFEMAT modules on Playa Centro and Playa Norte in December 2024. Ask your resort whether it has a beach wheelchair before you book.
How do I get wheelchair assistance at Cancun airport?
Through your airline: ask when you book and again at check-in. U.S. rules require airlines to help from the terminal entrance to the gate, on connections and on and off the plane, without advance notice and at no charge. Canadian rules require large airlines to help free on request and to honor requests made at least 48 hours before departure.
Can visitors borrow the amphibious chairs on Isla Mujeres?
In December 2024 Isla Mujeres placed three free amphibious chairs, for people with motor disabilities, at its ZOFEMAT modules on Playa Centro and Playa Norte. Its releases do not say whether visitors qualify, but the modules serve foreign visitors, from 9:00 am to 4:00 pm as of July 2026. Go in the morning and ask.
Can we have beach family photos with a wheelchair or a walker?
Yes. We plan around firm ground, such as the ramps and deck at Playa Las Perlas, a resort boardwalk or the firm sand by the water, and whether the chair appears in the photos is up to the person who uses it. There is no limit on people and no per-person charge.
