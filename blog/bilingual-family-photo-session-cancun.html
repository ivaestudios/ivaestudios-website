In short

We work in English and Spanish, so a family that speaks both needs no interpreter: abuelos can be directed in Spanish, kids and in-laws in English, and the person who usually translates gets to be in the photos. English-only families book, plan and are directed in English. The price doesn't change: The Chapter, the collection we recommend for families, is 60 minutes and 35 edited photos for 475 USD, with no per-person charge, no deposit and a preview within 24 hours.

In some families, the conversation changes language halfway through a sentence. The grandparents are most at home in Spanish, the grandkids answer in English, and someone in the middle translates in both directions without noticing they are doing it. Other families speak only English and wonder whether a photo session in Mexico will mean a lot of pointing and nodding. Looking for a Spanish-speaking photographer in Cancun for your parents, or an English-speaking one for yourselves? You don't have to choose. Here is how a family session runs when the directions go both ways.

Our team, led by Vianey Díaz, brings more than 20 years of photography experience and has photographed thousands of families and couples over those years. Since IVAE Studios opened in 2023, we have delivered more than 500 sessions, we hold 5.0 across 75 Google reviews, and we work in English and Spanish.

Who a Bilingual Session Is For

A bilingual family photographer in Cancun makes the biggest difference for three kinds of families:

  • Latino families from the US and Canada whose abuelos speak only Spanish, or simply relax more in it, while the grandkids grew up speaking English.
  • English-only families who worry about a language gap once they land in Mexico.
  • Families with a side in each language: a Mexican-American family reunion, Spanish-speaking in-laws meeting the new baby, cousins who grew up in different countries.

In bilingual families there is usually one person who translates for everyone else. In a photo session, that person ends up half outside the frame, relaying instructions instead of being in the picture. When the photographer speaks both languages, nobody has to do that job, and the family translator gets to be part of the family photo again.

How Directions Work in Two Languages

Most of what we say during a family session is small: where to stand, who goes next to whom, chin down a touch, look at the baby, walk toward the water and back. Small directions only work when they land right away. If each one has to pass through a translator, the moment passes too: by the time the instruction reaches the grandparents, the toddler has wandered off.

So we give each direction in the language the person thinks in: Spanish for the grandparents, English for the grandkids, either one for the parents, and whatever mix suits your family when we speak to the whole group. You don't have to manage any of it.

It shows in the photographs. People who understand what is happening relax, and relaxed people look at each other instead of looking around for help. A grandmother who caught the direction the first time looks at her granddaughter, not sideways at whoever usually translates for her. Laura D. wrote after her family's session:

“Being able to communicate effortlessly in both English and Spanish was another huge plus.”

Laura D., Google review

Abuelos, Directed in Spanish

For many families, the grandparents are the reason for the trip, and the photo with them is often the one every household wants framed. When the abuelos speak only Spanish, a session run in English can leave them standing politely at the edge, smiling at instructions they didn't catch. Directed in Spanish, they are in the middle of it: walking the waterline with the grandkids, seated with the youngest on their lap, at the center of the big group where they belong.

Family photos with Spanish-speaking grandparents also go better when we know a little about them before the day. When you write, tell us who prefers Spanish, and anything that helps us plan around them: whether they walk easily on sand, how long they are comfortable standing, whether the heat tires them. The planning itself can stay in English with you.

For more ideas, see our guide to grandparents and grandkids beach portraits, and our multigenerational family photography page for sessions with three or four generations.

English Only? No Language Gap

If nobody in your family speaks Spanish, you don't need to. You can book, plan and be directed entirely in English. Where Spanish is needed, we take care of it: some hotels charge their own fee for outside photographers, paid by you to the hotel, and we confirm your hotel's rule before the session, so you know ahead of time. Niki K. wrote after her family's photos in Holbox:

“I don't speak Spanish, but everyone I communicated with was so helpful and made the entire process easy by speaking English with me.”

Niki K., Google review

Families with a side in each language get both at once. You hear the plan in English, your in-laws hear it in Spanish, and the photos look like one family rather than two groups taking turns.

Big Families, Reunions and Milestones

When the whole family flies in, from the great-grandparents to the newest cousin, the group grows fast, and so does the mix of languages. There is no limit on the number of people and no per-person charge, so adding the cousins, the in-laws or an aunt who decided at the last minute doesn't change the price. For groups of up to 16, we can arrange a full van to take everyone to another location for the session; ask us for its price when you write.

“They did the best job of photographing 14 people with 5 littles under 10 years old.”

Stephanie F., Google review

The Chapter (60 minutes, 35 edited photos, 1 look, 475 USD) is the collection we recommend for families. A big group has more combinations to get through, such as each household on its own, the grandparents with all the grandchildren and the siblings together, so The Story (75 minutes, 50 photos, 2 looks, 545 USD) or The Editorial (90 minutes, 70 photos, 2 looks, 645 USD, our best value) gives you more room. If you book one and want the next, you move up by paying only the difference.

Booking, Paying and the Preview

You can book in either language. Choose your collection, date and time online at ivaestudios.com/book, or in Spanish at ivaestudios.com/es/reservar. Online, sessions start at 6:30, 7:00 or 7:30 am, or at 5:00 or 5:30 pm. For any other time, message us on WhatsApp, in English or Spanish.

There is no deposit. You pay at the end of the session, by card or in cash, and cancellation is free until one day before, which helps when several households are lining up flights and one of them changes plans.

A preview arrives within 24 hours, so if the session falls early in the trip, the whole family can look at it together before everyone flies home. The private 4K gallery follows in 1 to 3 business days, with unlimited downloads and print rights, so grandparents who would rather hold a print than scroll a phone can have one made.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you direct our grandparents in Spanish and our kids in English?

Yes. We work in English and Spanish, so grandparents who prefer Spanish hear each direction in Spanish, while kids and in-laws hear it in English. Nobody in the family has to translate, so everyone can be in the photos.

No. You can book, plan and be directed entirely in English. Where Spanish is needed, such as confirming your hotel's rule for outside photographers, we take care of it before the session.

Does a bilingual session cost more?

No. Our six collections have fixed prices in USD, whatever language your family speaks. The Chapter, the one we recommend for families, is 60 minutes and 35 edited photos for 475 USD, with no per-person charge.

There is no limit on the number of people and no per-person charge. For groups of up to 16, we can arrange a full van to take everyone to another location for the session; ask us for its price.

Do we pay a deposit?

No. There is no deposit: you pay at the end of the session, by card or in cash, and cancellation is free until one day before. You can book online at ivaestudios.com/book or on WhatsApp, in English or Spanish.

When do we see the photos?
