In short

Check five things on the resort's own website: a teen space with published ages, included water sports, Wi-Fi throughout the resort, rules on where teens can go and the age at which adult rates start. When we checked on November 14, 2026, three resorts near Cancun listed teen club ages: Royalton Riviera Cancun (13 to 17), SLS Playa Mujeres (12 to 17) and Club Med Cancun (11 to 13 and 14 to 17). For the family photo, keep it to 45 or 60 minutes.

With a teenager between 13 and 17, the resort shapes more of the trip than it does with little ones. A kids' club that stops at 12 is no use to them, and a week with nothing to do and patchy Wi-Fi feels long for everyone. We read the teen pages of family all-inclusives around Cancun on November 14, 2026, and kept only what each resort publishes. Then comes the part we know best: photographing the family member who least wants to be photographed.

What Teens Want, and How to Check It

Teenagers want what they have at home, only better: a space of their own, things to do, Wi-Fi and some freedom. Here is how to check each one before you book.

  1. A teen space with ages and hours. Most of the kids' clubs we read stop at 11 or 12, so a 13-year-old needs a teen lounge or club. A club named without ages or hours is a question for the resort, not an answer.
  2. Sports they can just grab. At most resorts below, non-motorized water sports such as kayaks and paddleboards are included, while jet skis, scuba and some boat trips can cost extra.
  3. Wi-Fi everywhere, not only in the lobby. Look for wording like "throughout the resort" on the resort's own pages.
  4. Freedom with a net. Ask whether the teen club is supervised, how sign-in works and what the rules are for under-18s walking around on their own.
  5. What a teen costs. Some resorts price teens as adults: SLS Playa Mujeres charges guests 13 and older the adult rate, while the Family Selection page at Grand Palladium Select Costa Mujeres says the adult rate applies from 18. Compare the total for your family.

Resorts That Publish a Teen Program

Eight family all-inclusives around Cancun, with what their own websites said on November 14, 2026, starting with the most detailed. Programs change, so confirm with the resort before you book.

Royalton Riviera Cancun

The most detailed teen page we read. The Hangout Teens Lounge, for ages 13 to 17, is supervised and open from 3:00 to 11:00 pm, with PlayStation, movies, high-speed Wi-Fi, kayaking, beach volleyball, tennis and dance lessons, and the current program shows on the in-room TV and the Royalton app. Non-motorized water sports are included, though the teen page marks sailing, snorkeling and catamaran trips as possibly extra. The resort says its Wi-Fi works anywhere on the property.

SLS Playa Mujeres

The Teen Lounge takes ages 12 to 17, daily from noon to 9 pm, with billiards, video games and a program that runs from Fortnite tournaments to water polo. Paddleboarding, kayaking and catamaran sailing are complimentary, jet skis cost extra, and Wi-Fi is free throughout the resort. One budget note from its FAQ: guests 13 and older pay the adult rate.

Club Med Cancun

At Punta Nizuc, Club Med includes child care from 4 to 17. Its Teens Club runs separate programs for 11 to 13 and 14 to 17, and its Chill Pass lets teens sign in and out of group activities at will. Sailing, kayaking, paddleboarding, snorkeling classes and flying trapeze lessons are included, scuba costs extra, and Wi-Fi is complimentary.

Royalton Splash Riviera Cancun

Its on-site water park has 14 slides and two lazy rivers, and the resort pitches the slides at teens and adults as well as kids. It lists supervised kids' and teens' clubs, but no teen ages. Paddleboarding and kayaking come at no extra cost and Wi-Fi is free throughout. Its kids' club page marks laser tag, bowling and a trampoline park with a dollar sign, so ask the price.

Finest Playa Mujeres

Its FAQ lists an entertainment lounge for teens, with no ages or hours, beside a rock-climbing wall, a zip line and a water park with slides. Kayaking, snorkeling, sailing, windsurfing and paddleboarding are part of the all-inclusive, motorized water sports cost extra, and Wi-Fi is complimentary throughout.

Grand Palladium Select Costa Mujeres

The Family Selection page names a Teen Club next to the Mini Club, Kids Club and water park, with no teen ages or hours. Kayaking, paddleboarding and snorkeling are listed as non-motorized water sports, Wi-Fi covers the resort, and the resort lists one free hour a day per room at the Rafa Nadal Tennis Centre. The same Family Selection page says children under 18 must always be accompanied by family or legal guardians, so ask what that means for a teen walking to the club.

Seadust Cancun

In the Hotel Zone, Seadust has a Teen Playroom with video games and ping-pong, open 11 am to 6 pm, and a zip line for riders up to 14. Wi-Fi is free. The pages we read give no teen ages and list no water sports, so ask at booking.

Planet Hollywood Cancun

In Costa Mujeres. No teen club is named, only a STARS Kids Club for 4 to 12, but there is a FlowRider surf simulator and Planet Play, an indoor zone with trampolines, basketball courts, golf simulators and arcade games that the resort calls a playground for kids and teens. Wi-Fi is resort-wide. The page does not say whether the FlowRider and arcade cost extra.

Named, not described. Majestic Mirage Costa Mujeres and Crown Paradise Club Cancun name a teens' club without ages, hours or activities. This check does not cover Moon Palace, Hyatt Ziva Cancun or Hilton Cancun, so ask those resorts about teen programs directly. For younger siblings, our kids club guide compares ages and hours.

The Hardest Family Member to Photograph

Teenagers are usually the hardest people in a family to photograph on vacation. They are self-conscious, they do not want to look posed, and the pictures may end up in front of their friends. The resort can help. A scene they already like (a rooftop, a pier, the edge of a lazy river) makes a short session easier, because they start somewhere that feels like theirs: Royalton Riviera Cancun lists a rooftop terrace, and Royalton Splash has its two lazy rivers. Ask your teen to choose the spot.

Pools, rooftops and lazy rivers belong to the resort, which decides whether we can photograph there, and some resorts charge a fee for an outside photographer. If yours does, you pay it to the hotel, and we confirm the hotel's rule with you before the session. Frequently Asked Questions

Which all-inclusives near Cancun have a teen club?

When we checked on November 14, 2026, three listed teen club ages on their own websites: Royalton Riviera Cancun (13 to 17), SLS Playa Mujeres (12 to 17) and Club Med Cancun (11 to 13 and 14 to 17). Royalton Splash Riviera Cancun, Finest Playa Mujeres, Grand Palladium Select Costa Mujeres and Seadust Cancun list a teen club, lounge or playroom without ages.

Are water sports included for teens at Cancun all-inclusives?

Non-motorized ones usually are. Kayaks and paddleboards are included at Royalton Splash Riviera Cancun, SLS Playa Mujeres, Club Med Cancun, Finest Playa Mujeres and Grand Palladium Select Costa Mujeres. Jet skis and other motorized sports cost extra where resorts mention them, Club Med charges for scuba, and Royalton Riviera Cancun marks sailing, snorkeling and catamarans as possibly extra.

Do Cancun resorts have Wi-Fi everywhere?

Most we checked say so. Royalton Riviera Cancun, Royalton Splash Riviera Cancun, SLS Playa Mujeres, Finest Playa Mujeres, Grand Palladium Select Costa Mujeres and Planet Hollywood Cancun describe Wi-Fi throughout the resort. Club Med Cancun and Seadust Cancun list free Wi-Fi without saying where it reaches.

Can teens walk around a Cancun resort on their own?

Rules vary, so ask before you book. Club Med's Chill Pass lets teens sign in and out of group activities at will, Royalton Riviera Cancun's Teen Lounge is supervised, and Grand Palladium's Family Selection page says children under 18 must always be accompanied by family or legal guardians.

How long should a family photo session with teenagers be?

Short. The Keepsake runs 45 minutes with 25 edited photos for 375 USD, and The Chapter runs 60 minutes with 35 photos for 475 USD. There is no deposit, and a preview arrives within 24 hours, so your teen can post from the trip.
