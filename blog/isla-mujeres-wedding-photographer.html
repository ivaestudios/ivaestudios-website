In short

Isla Mujeres is a 20-minute ferry from Cancun and has something the mainland resorts do not: a beach that faces the sunset. A wedding here is usually small, held at Playa Norte or at one of the island's resorts, and timed so the ceremony ends in golden light. The ferry sets the schedule for guests and vendors, and wedding photography with IVAE Studios runs from 1,800 USD for half-day coverage.

Couples choose Isla Mujeres for the same reason photographers do. The island is small and quiet, the water on its sheltered side stays turquoise, and Playa Norte looks west, so the sun goes down into the sea in front of you instead of behind the hotels. What makes an island wedding different is logistics: every guest, every flower and every camera arrives by boat. This is how we plan it.

The light times are calculated for Playa Norte. Quintana Roo keeps the same clock all year, with no daylight saving. Ferry schedules and fares change, so confirm them the week of the wedding.

Why Couples Choose the Island

The sunset. Most beaches around Cancun face east, so the sun sets behind the buildings. Playa Norte faces north and west. It is one of the few beaches in the region with a real sunset over water, and that single fact decides the hour of almost every wedding here.

The water. The island is a narrow strip about seven kilometres long. Its eastern side takes the open Caribbean, and the western side, where Playa Norte and the town are, sits in the shelter. In the months when sargassum reaches the mainland, the west side of the island is usually the cleaner water. It is a strong tendency, not a guarantee, and our sargassum season guide explains the year.

The scale. Isla Mujeres suits small weddings and elopements. Guests walk or ride golf carts, the town is a few streets wide, and the day feels like a gathering rather than a production. If you are deciding how small to go, see our guides to a micro wedding and to eloping in Mexico.

Where Weddings Happen

Playa Norte. The northern tip of the island: wide sand, very shallow water and the sunset in front of you. It is a public beach and busy from late morning, so a ceremony here is planned for the end of the day and needs a permit, covered below. The beach is about a ten-minute walk from the ferry terminal.

The resorts. Several hotels on the island host weddings and events. Three of the best known: Zoëtry Villa Rolandi Isla Mujeres is the most intimate, with 35 oceanfront suites. Impression Isla Mujeres by Secrets is an adults-only all-inclusive with 125 suites. Almare, a Luxury Collection resort, is an adults-only all-inclusive with 109 suites. Each has its own rules for outside photographers, so ask your wedding coordinator early; our guide to bringing an outside photographer to a resort covers the usual questions.

Punta Sur. The southern tip, with low cliffs over open sea and a small Maya temple to Ixchel. The park is a paid attraction with opening hours, and the wind is constant, so we use it for portraits rather than ceremonies. The morning after the wedding, the cliffs outside the gate catch the first light of the day.

The Ferry Sets the Timeline

Everything on the island arrives by boat, so the timeline is built around the crossing.

From Puerto Juárez. The main terminal is just north of downtown Cancun. Passenger ferries run frequently from early morning until late at night, and the crossing takes about 20 minutes. Ultramar asks passengers to be there 30 minutes before departure, and its published fare in August 2026 was 290 pesos one way per adult.

From the Hotel Zone. The piers at Playa Tortugas, Playa Caracol and El Embarcadero are closer to the Hotel Zone resorts, but they start later in the morning, run fewer departures and take 25 to 35 minutes.

What this means for guests. Tell guests which pier to use and which boat to take, and put the return time on the invitation. If the party runs past the last ferry, anyone staying on the mainland needs a room on the island. The same applies to your vendors, which is why we ask about the end time before we quote.

Weather. Strong north winds, mostly between November and February, can suspend the ferries. For a winter wedding we suggest that the couple, the rings and the dress cross the day before. Our backup plan guide covers the rest of the year.

Beach Permit and the Legal Ceremony

The beach. Mexican beaches are federal land. A ceremony on the sand is authorized by the federal maritime zone office of the municipality, known as ZOFEMAT; Isla Mujeres has its own. The municipality publishes fees for certificates but no fixed fee for a beach event, so the cost is confirmed at the office and paid against an official receipt. A hotel or a wedding planner normally handles this for you. Ask for it in writing.

The paperwork. Many couples from abroad marry legally at home and hold a symbolic ceremony on the island, which needs no civil paperwork in Mexico. A legal civil ceremony is possible with documents and lead time. Our guide to a legal or symbolic wedding in Mexico compares the two.

What We Photograph

An island wedding day with us follows the boat and the light. We cross early, photograph the getting ready at the hotel, the ceremony, the family groups and the couple's portraits at golden hour, and stay for the dinner and the first dances. If you want the island itself in the story, a short session the next morning on the quiet east side or at Playa Norte before the day visitors arrive adds a second set of photos in completely different light.

For two people eloping, the day is simpler: a ceremony at the water's edge, portraits as the sun goes down and dinner in town. Our elopement photography page explains how that works, and the Isla Mujeres photographer page shows the settings we use most.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you get married on the beach on Isla Mujeres?
Yes. Ceremonies are held at Playa Norte and at the island's resorts. A ceremony on a public beach needs a permit from the municipal federal maritime zone office (ZOFEMAT) of Isla Mujeres, which a hotel or planner normally arranges.
What is the best time of day for a wedding on Isla Mujeres?
Late afternoon. Playa Norte faces north and west, so the sun sets over the water. Start the ceremony 75 to 90 minutes before sunset, which falls between 6:04 pm in late November and 7:33 pm in early July.
How do guests get to a wedding on Isla Mujeres?
By passenger ferry. From Puerto Juárez in Cancun the crossing takes about 20 minutes and boats run from early morning until late at night. The Hotel Zone piers have fewer departures and take 25 to 35 minutes. Give guests the pier, the boat and the return time.
How much does a wedding photographer on Isla Mujeres cost?
IVAE Studios wedding photography runs 1,800 to 2,800 USD for half-day coverage, 3,500 to 5,500 USD for a full day with a second shooter and 5,500 to 8,000 USD with a highlight film. The exact figure for the island is confirmed with your date and end time.
Is Isla Mujeres good for a small wedding or an elopement?
It is one of the best places near Cancun for it. The island is small, guests move on foot or by golf cart, and the sunset at Playa Norte gives a small ceremony a setting that needs very little decoration.
What happens if the ferry is cancelled?
It is uncommon, and it happens mostly with strong north winds between November and February. For winter weddings we recommend that the couple, the rings and the dress cross the day before, and we plan our own crossing with a margin.
