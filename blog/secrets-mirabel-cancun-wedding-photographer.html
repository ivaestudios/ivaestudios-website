Seaweed and Weather at Secrets Mirabel Cancún

Secrets Mirabel Cancún faces the open Caribbean, the side of this coast that takes the most seaweed in a heavy year. Sargassum usually builds from April and is heaviest from June to August, and November to March is normally the clearest stretch. It changes week to week with the wind. If the sand is heavy on your date, we go early, when large resorts have just raked, or move to the Nichupté Lagoon across the boulevard, where the water stays clear. Our month-by-month sargassum guide has the detail and the outlook for the season. Summer and early autumn bring afternoon showers that tend to pass quickly. We keep an eye on the forecast and adjust the hour with you the day before.

Bringing Your Own Photographer

Before signing, ask Secrets Mirabel Cancún one direct question: what do you charge an outside wedding photographer? Resorts on this coast call it a vendor fee, an outside supplier fee or a day pass, and they revise it by season and package, so we do not print a number here. Get the answer by email while your contract is still open, when there is still room to negotiate. We handle the paperwork with your coordinator.

Close to Secrets Mirabel Cancún

The nearest public beaches are Playa Delfines (km 18, under a kilometre away) and Playa Ballenas (km 14, about 5 km away). Mexican law keeps every beach public, so a session at the water's edge does not depend on the hotel's grounds policy.
