A trip with grandparents and grandchildren asks one resort to do three jobs at once. The grandparents want short walks, shade and a quiet pool. The parents want one dinner that is not a negotiation. The children want a slide, a club and someone their own age. Below: the questions to ask first, then six Cancun resorts whose own websites answer several of them.

What Three Generations Need From a Resort

Most family vacation ideas with grandparents come down to one decision: where everyone sleeps. Before you compare prices, send these questions to every resort on your list by email, so the answers arrive in writing.

  • Short, step-free walks. How far is it from your room to the beach, the main restaurant and the kids club, without steps? Ask for rooms near the lobby or an elevator, or whether the resort runs golf carts.
  • A calm beach and shade. Ask whether the sea in front of your building is good for swimming, and where the shade is.
  • A quiet pool near the kids club. A grandparent who can read by an adults' pool while the grandchildren play nearby gets a vacation too.
  • Connecting rooms or a family suite. Two rooms with a door between them, or a suite with separate bedrooms, keeps the generations close without one shared bathroom. Ask the resort to confirm the connection in writing.
  • À la carte dinners close by. Restaurants a short walk from the rooms, and a kids club that runs into the evening, make an adults' dinner possible.
  • Medical help on site. Ask whether there is a doctor or nurse on the property, and at what hours.
  • Firm ground for photos. A lawn, a garden, a boardwalk or a pool deck near the sea lets the grandparents stand, or sit, without sinking into deep sand. If someone uses a cane, a walker or a wheelchair, see family photos with less mobile grandparents.
What the websites leave out

On the pages we read, only Club Med mentions medical care, and none of the six resorts describes its elevators or step-free routes. Ask the resort directly.

Six Resorts, Checked on Their Own Websites

Not a ranking: each covers a different part of the checklist. Everything below comes from the resort's own website, read on November 14, 2026; ages, hours and room types change, so confirm them when you book. Being on this list says nothing about a hotel's rule for outside photographers, which we confirm separately for your dates.

Nizuc Resort & Spa, Punta Nizuc

Nizuc spreads 274 suites and villas over 29 acres. That sounds like long walks, but its Resort Suites, which the resort calls ideal for families, come with golf cart service to and from your suite around the clock, and select suites connect. Grandparents who would rather stay next to the main lobby can take an Ocean Suite (up to two guests, all over 16), steps from Nizuc Beach, Reef Beach and the main pool. There is a quiet adult pool, Winik's Kids Club (the page lists no ages, so ask) and six restaurants.

Club Med Cancun, Punta Nizuc

Club Med's page says a nurse is present on site. It also lists family and interconnecting rooms, and its Aguamarina Family Oasis has two-bedroom Deluxe rooms, an oceanfront pool and an all-day snack bar. Child care for ages 4 to 17 is part of the all-inclusive, with hours up to 9 pm and meals with the kids club team, which leaves the adults a buffet or à la carte dinner of their own; for children under 4, ask about Baby Club Med and Petit Club Med. The resort sits on a private peninsula with three beaches and a natural lagoon.

Grand Fiesta Americana Coral Beach, Hotel Zone

It suits a family that wants to stay in the Hotel Zone itself: the resort sits at km 9.5 of Kukulcan Boulevard, with views across the bay to Isla Mujeres. All 602 rooms are suites with balconies or terraces, and the Ocean Front Two Bedroom Family & Friends Suite joins two oceanfront suites, one with two queen beds and one with a king, with two bathrooms, living rooms and balconies. The Coral Kidz Club has indoor spaces designed for different ages and an outdoor playground with a splash pool and slides; teens get a Sports Lounge with table tennis and billiards.

Finest Playa Mujeres, North of Cancun

Built for all ages, with an adults-only resort within the resort: the Excellence Club has its own pool area, so grandparents who want quiet can stay there while the family takes a Family Suite (88.3 m², a king bed for the parents and a separate kids' bedroom with two single beds, up to five guests). The Imagine Kids Club takes babies with a parent and children up to 11, there is a lounge for teens, and the Imagine Lounge looks after potty-trained children 3 and up at breakfast and at dinner, for up to 90 minutes, near most of the restaurants. There are 11 dining options, most without reservations.

SLS Playa Mujeres, North of Cancun

Its Family Style Swim-Outs connect two ground-floor rooms that open onto a river pool through a private garden, which spares the grandparents the elevator. Four pools run from family-friendly to adults-only. Coati Kids Club takes ages 4 to 12, from 10 am to 6 pm, and the Teen Lounge ages 12 to 17, from noon to 9 pm. There are 17 chef-led restaurants and bars inside a double-gated community.

Garza Blanca Resort & Spa, North of Cancun

For families who would rather share one home than book three rooms. The two-bedroom suites sleep up to six, with three bathrooms; the three-bedroom Oceanfront Residence sleeps up to eight, with three and a half bathrooms, a full kitchen and butler service. The Kids' Club is free for ages 4 to 12, on a schedule that varies by season.

Family Beach Photos With the Grandparents

We photograph at the resort or villa you choose. Each hotel sets its own rule for outside photographers, so we confirm yours before the session; where a hotel charges a fee, you pay it to the hotel.

We photograph the grandparents first, seated if they like, while everyone is fresh. Once their frames are done, they can rest in the shade while we photograph the rest of the family. Book early or late, when it is cooler: online you can choose 6:30, 7:00 or 7:30 am, or 5:00 or 5:30 pm, and other times can be arranged on WhatsApp. We direct in English and Spanish, so a grandmother who prefers Spanish hears the same directions as her grandchildren.

There is no limit on people and no per-person charge (more on how group pricing works). There is no deposit: you pay at the end of the session, by card or in cash. Previews arrive within 24 hours and the private 4K gallery in 1 to 3 business days, with unlimited downloads and print rights.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best Cancun resort for families with kids and grandparents?

The one where nobody walks far and each generation has its own space: connecting rooms or a family suite, a kids club, a quiet adult pool, nearby restaurants and medical help on site. Of the six resorts we checked, Nizuc offers round-the-clock golf carts to the Resort Suites it calls ideal for families, and Club Med Cancun lists interconnecting rooms and a nurse on site.

Which Cancun resorts have connecting rooms or family suites?

Of the six we checked: Club Med Cancun lists family and interconnecting rooms, select Resort Suites at Nizuc connect, SLS Playa Mujeres has Family Style Swim-Outs that join two ground-floor rooms, Finest Playa Mujeres has a Family Suite with a separate kids' bedroom, Grand Fiesta Americana Coral Beach has a two-bedroom Family & Friends Suite, and Garza Blanca has two- and three-bedroom suites.

Can you photograph our family at our resort?

We photograph at the resort or villa you choose, but each hotel sets its own rule for outside photographers. We confirm your hotel's rule before the session; where a hotel charges a fee, you pay it to the hotel. If a group of up to 16 is split across hotels or wants another location, we can arrange a full van, priced on request.

When is the best time for family beach photos with grandparents in Cancun?

Early morning or late afternoon, when it is cooler. Online booking offers 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30 am and 5:00 and 5:30 pm; other times can be arranged on WhatsApp. We photograph the grandparents first, seated if they like, and where the resort allows, on firm ground such as a lawn, a boardwalk or a pool deck.

Do you charge per person for a big three-generation family?

No. There is no limit on people and no per-person charge. For a big group we suggest The Editorial (90 minutes, 70 photos, 645 USD) or The Heirloom (90 minutes, the complete edited gallery, 895 USD). There is no deposit: you pay at the end of the session.
