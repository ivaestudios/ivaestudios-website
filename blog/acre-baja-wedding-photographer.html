In short

Acre is a farm and treehouse resort on about 25 acres in the foothills of San José del Cabo, known for its mango orchard, its palm grove and its farm-to-table food. It is the opposite of a beach wedding: shade, greenery and filtered light, with ceremonies from very small to about 350 guests. Many couples pair it with a beach session another day. Wedding coverage starts at 1,800 USD.

Acre is the Los Cabos wedding for couples who do not want a resort. You drive inland from San José into a grove of palms and mango trees, with treehouses, a farm-to-table restaurant and long tables set among the trees. It photographs beautifully, but differently from the coast, and the plan should reflect that.

Acre at a Glance

  • WhereFoothills of San José del Cabo, inland
  • SizeAbout 25 acres of farm, orchard and palm grove
  • StayA small, adults-only treehouse hotel
  • FoodFarm-to-table restaurant and a cocktail program
  • WeddingsFrom intimate dinners to about 350 guests in the orchard
  • Not hereNo beach: plan a coast session on another day

The property mixes a working farm with a restaurant, a bar and a dozen or so treehouses raised among the palms. Wedding sources consistently list the mango orchard for celebrations of up to about 350 guests, with smaller rooms and tables for welcome dinners and intimate gatherings. Confirm the current spaces and capacities with Acre directly.

The Orchard and the Spaces

The mango orchard is where most large Acre weddings happen: a ceremony aisle between the trees, then dinner in the same grove as the light fades. The palm grove gives tall vertical lines and dappled shade for portraits. The treehouses, with their wood decks and open sides, are a natural place for getting-ready photos and a quiet first look, if your party is staying in them.

Light Under the Trees

Under the canopy, the light is filtered all afternoon, which is generous for faces and forgiving for guests in the sun. The risk is the opposite of the beach: it gets dark earlier under the trees than in the open. We plan portraits at the edge of the grove in the last 40 minutes, and we bring our own light for the reception after dark.

Adding the Beach

Acre has no ocean view, and many couples want both looks. The simplest plan is a beach session on another day of the trip, at sunset in San José or at a calm bay on the Corridor, so the wedding day stays relaxed. Our Los Cabos engagement and elopement pages show the coastal side.

Planning and Vendors

Acre's events team works with independent planners and vendors. Ask early about the current rules for outside vendors, music curfews and the timeline for setup, and get them in writing. We coordinate directly with your planner and the venue, in English and Spanish.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you get married at Acre in Los Cabos?
Yes. Acre hosts weddings in its mango orchard and other spaces on its farm in the foothills of San José del Cabo, from intimate dinners to about 350 guests.
Is Acre on the beach?
No. Acre is inland, in the foothills above San José del Cabo, among palms and orchards. Many couples add a beach session on another day of the trip.
What time should an Acre wedding ceremony start?
About 100 minutes before sunset, a little earlier than on the coast because it gets dark sooner under the trees: around 3:55 pm in December and 5:25 pm in June.
How much is a wedding photographer at Acre?
IVAE Studios wedding coverage starts at 1,800 to 2,800 USD for up to four hours, with a full day and second photographer from 3,500 USD.
