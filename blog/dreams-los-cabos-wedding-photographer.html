In short

Dreams Los Cabos is an all-inclusive resort on the Tourist Corridor, part of the Hyatt Inclusive Collection, and its signature wedding venue is the white oceanfront gazebo, listed for ceremonies of up to about 160 guests. Larger weddings use the beach, the pool terrace or the ballroom. Plan the ceremony about 90 minutes before sunset. Wedding coverage starts at 1,800 USD.

Dreams Los Cabos is the classic all-inclusive wedding in Los Cabos: a white gazebo on the edge of the sea, a reception on the terrace and guests who never need to leave the resort. Here is how we photograph it so it looks like your wedding and not the brochure.

Dreams Los Cabos at a Glance

  • WhereTourist Corridor, between Cabo San Lucas and San José
  • TypeAll-inclusive, Hyatt Inclusive Collection
  • GazeboWhite oceanfront gazebo, up to about 160 guests
  • Other venuesBeach, pool terrace and ballroom
  • Best lightThe last hour before sunset

The Gazebo

The gazebo is the photograph most couples picture when they book Dreams: a white structure with the Sea of Cortez behind the altar. It works best when we shoot the ceremony from the side as well as down the aisle, so the sea fills more of the frame and the guests are part of the story. After the ceremony we take family photos right there and move to the sand for portraits.

The Beach, the Terrace and the Ballroom

Wedding sites list a beach venue for larger groups, a pool terrace for receptions and a ballroom for the biggest weddings or a backup in case of wind. Confirm the current capacities and venue fees with the resort. Like most of the Corridor, the shoreline can have strong surf, so we keep portraits on the sand rather than in the water.

A good rule here: start the ceremony about 90 minutes before sunset, and keep the final half hour of daylight for portraits. At Dreams that means around 4:05 pm in December and 5:35 pm in June.

Outside Photographers

All-inclusive resorts in Los Cabos usually charge outside wedding vendors an access fee or a day pass, and the amount changes by season and package. Ask Dreams Los Cabos for the exact figure in writing before you sign, and ask whether it applies per vendor or per person, because a photographer and a second shooter are two people. We send our insurance and paperwork to the resort ourselves. Our guide to resort and outside photographers compares both options.

Coverage and Prices

Our wedding prices are public. Up to four hours of coverage costs 1,800 to 2,800 USD; a full day of eight to ten hours with a second photographer is 3,500 to 5,500 USD; photography plus a wedding film is 5,500 to 8,000 USD. The resort's access charge for outside vendors is separate and paid to the hotel. Your edited gallery arrives within one to three business days.

We are based in Cancún and travel to Los Cabos for weddings; travel is arranged as part of the coverage, and we arrive with time to scout. Sessions before or after the wedding, such as a welcome-dinner portrait or a day-after shoot, use our collections from 245 USD.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many guests fit in the gazebo at Dreams Los Cabos?
Wedding listings give the oceanfront gazebo a capacity of about 160 guests. Larger weddings use the beach, the pool terrace or the ballroom.
Can I bring my own photographer to Dreams Los Cabos?
Yes, coordinated through the resort's wedding team. All-inclusive resorts usually charge outside wedding vendors an access fee or day pass, so ask for the amount in writing.
What time should a Dreams Los Cabos wedding start?
About 90 minutes before sunset: around 4:05 pm in December and 5:35 pm in June, so portraits fall in the last light.
How much is a wedding photographer at Dreams Los Cabos?
IVAE Studios wedding coverage starts at 1,800 to 2,800 USD for up to four hours, with a full day and second photographer from 3,500 USD.
