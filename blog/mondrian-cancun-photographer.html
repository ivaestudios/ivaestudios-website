In short

Mondrian Cancún opened in August 2026 on Grand Island, between the Caribbean and the Nichupté Lagoon, as the brand's first all-inclusive. For photos it gives you two coasts: the open Caribbean at its Bungalow Beach Club on Playa Delfines, a short free shuttle away, and the sun setting over the lagoon behind the tower, which few Hotel Zone resorts can offer. Sessions start at 245 USD, and the gallery arrives in one to three business days.

Most resorts in the Cancún Hotel Zone face the sunrise: their beaches look east to the open Caribbean, and at sunset the sun drops behind the buildings. Mondrian Cancún is different. Its tower stands on the lagoon side of Grand Island, so the sun goes down over the water, and its beach club sits on one of the widest beaches of the coast. Here is how we would plan a session or a celebration there.

Two Coasts in One Session

The Cancún Hotel Zone is a long, thin strip of land with the Caribbean on one side and the Nichupté Lagoon on the other. Almost every resort sits on the sea side, which is why most Hotel Zone sunsets happen behind the hotels. Mondrian's tower and pools look over the lagoon instead, to the west, where the sun sets over open water: toward the west-southwest in December and the west-northwest in June.

That gives a session two completely different looks. We start at the beach club in the afternoon, when the light from the west falls on the sea and turns it turquoise behind you, then take the shuttle back for the golden minutes over the lagoon. For both, plan 75 to 90 minutes.

The Beach Club on Playa Delfines

Mondrian's beach is the Bungalow Beach Club on Playa Delfines, a few minutes away by complimentary shuttle, where guests have preferred access. Playa Delfines is one of the widest open-sea beaches of the Hotel Zone, with long, uninterrupted sand and the colorful Cancún letters at its lookout. The surf there is stronger than on the calm northern beaches, so we work at the waterline rather than in the water.

Like every beach in Mexico, Playa Delfines is public, so a session on the open sand does not depend on the beach club's rules. Inside the club area, we follow its policies.

The Lagoon and the Tower

Two of the resort's three pools face the Nichupté Lagoon, with mangroves and calm water behind them, and the third, The Observatory, is an adults-only rooftop pool with views across Cancún. For photos, the lagoon side works best in the last half hour before sunset, when the water reflects the sky and the tower's terraces catch the warm light. Access to rooftop areas reserved for particular guests is the resort's decision, so we confirm what is open to your session before the day.

Weddings and Celebrations

Mondrian Cancún describes flexible event spaces, with meeting rooms to follow, and offers bespoke celebrations as a premium add-on. Because the resort is new, its wedding offer is still taking shape: ask the events team in writing which spaces are available on your date, the capacity of each and the policy for outside vendors. For an intimate wedding or a proposal, the lagoon at sunset is the setting we would choose. Our guide to proposing on New Year's Eve and our surprise proposal photography page explain how we plan the moment.

Access for Guests

Staying at Mondrian? The photographer comes in as your invited guest: give the front desk our name and the session time a day ahead and we meet you in the lobby. For a private session, staying guests are seldom charged the outside vendor fees used for weddings, but we confirm the current policy with the resort in writing. Our guide to bringing an outside photographer to a resort covers the usual rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Mondrian Cancún?
On Grand Island in the Cancún Hotel Zone, between the Caribbean Sea and the Nichupté Lagoon, about 15 minutes from Cancún International Airport. Its tower and two of its pools face the lagoon.
Does Mondrian Cancún have a beach?
Its beach is the Bungalow Beach Club on Playa Delfines, a few minutes away by complimentary shuttle, where guests have preferred access. Playa Delfines is a wide open-sea beach with stronger surf than the northern Hotel Zone.
What is the best time for photos at Mondrian Cancún?
Sunset, because the sun sets over the Nichupté Lagoon behind the tower, which few Hotel Zone resorts can offer. For a two-coast session, start at the beach club about 90 minutes before sunset and finish at the lagoon.
Can I bring my own photographer to Mondrian Cancún?
Yes. As a staying guest, register the photographer at the front desk a day ahead. Staying guests are seldom charged outside vendor fees for a private session, but we confirm the current policy in writing.
Can you get married at Mondrian Cancún?
The resort offers flexible event spaces and bespoke celebrations as a premium add-on. As a new resort, its wedding offer is still taking shape, so ask the events team in writing for venues, capacities and the outside vendor policy.
How much is a photoshoot at Mondrian Cancún?
IVAE Studios sessions start at 245 USD for 35 minutes. Essential Flow is 395 USD for 60 minutes, and Creative Runway, ideal for the beach club and the lagoon, is 475 USD for 75 minutes.
