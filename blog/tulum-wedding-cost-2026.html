Tulum Wedding Cost in 2026: What Actually Moves the Budget

In short

A Tulum wedding follows the same three bands as the rest of the Mexican Caribbean, roughly 30,000 to 50,000 USD for an intimate 30-guest celebration, 75,000 to 120,000 for 80 guests and 150,000 to 250,000 or more for an editorial week, but Tulum spends the money differently: a transfer closer to two hours from Cancún airport, venues that run on generators and one congested beach road, boutique properties instead of all-inclusive packages, and vendors who travel in. Photography and film typically sit at five to eight percent of the total.

Tulum is the most photographed wedding town on this coast and the one where couples most often get the budget wrong, because it does not behave like a resort destination. Here is where the money goes, from a studio that drives down from Cancún for it.

The three budget bands, and where Tulum lands

Across Cancún and the Riviera Maya, a destination wedding lands in one of three bands, driven almost entirely by guest count and tier: an intimate 30-guest celebration at 30,000 to 50,000 USD, an 80-guest event over three or four days at 75,000 to 120,000, and a 150-guest editorial week at 150,000 to 250,000 or more. Tulum fits those bands, but it rarely sits at the bottom of one. The town trades the all-inclusive package, where food, drink and coordination arrive bundled, for boutique venues where every element is a separate contract. That is what gives Tulum its look, and it is also why two weddings of the same size can differ by tens of thousands.

Logistics: the line items Cancún does not have

Tulum sits at the southern end of the Riviera Maya, closer to two hours door to door from Cancún airport, against 45 to 55 minutes for Playa del Carmen or Mayakoba. Every guest, every vendor and every delivery pays that distance in transfer cost and time. The beach zone is a single coast road that congests badly in high season, so timelines need margin that a resort wedding never thinks about. Many beach venues run on generator power, which affects lighting, sound and anything that needs to be plugged in. None of this is a reason not to marry in Tulum; it is the reason the logistics line in a Tulum budget is real rather than decorative.

Venues: boho, boutique and priced by the night

Tulum venues are built from wood, stone and palm rather than resort marble: beach clubs, jungle chapels and boutique hotels along the beach road, with the cenotes minutes inland. They are priced very differently from an all-inclusive: a buyout of a small property for the wedding weekend, a venue fee plus outside catering, or a beach club rental with its own minimums. Ask early what the number includes, because in Tulum it often includes the space and little else. The upside is control. You choose the caterer, the florist and the music rather than picking from a resort menu.

Vendors, travel and the beach road

Most specialist vendors on this coast are based in Cancún or Playa del Carmen and travel to Tulum, so quotes carry travel and sometimes a night's stay. Build the day against sunset rather than against the clock: the golden hour is short, the beach road is slow, and a ceremony that starts late costs you the light no budget can buy back. We scout the venue in the same light you will have on the day, and we plan the portraits around the jungle edge, the open sand and, when the couple wants it, a cenote inland.

Where photography sits in a Tulum budget

Photography and film usually sit at five to eight percent of a destination wedding total. Our published ranges are the same for Tulum as for Cancún, with travel arranged as part of the coverage: a half day built around the ceremony and golden hour runs 1,800 to 2,800 USD, a full day with a second shooter and venue scouting runs 3,500 to 5,500, and the full day with a cinematic highlight film runs 5,500 to 8,000. The complete edited gallery arrives in one to three business days. Send the date and the venue and you get a real number the same business day.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Tulum wedding cost?

It follows the coast's three bands: about 30,000 to 50,000 USD for 30 guests, 75,000 to 120,000 for 80 guests and 150,000 to 250,000 or more for a 150-guest editorial week. Tulum tends to sit higher within each band because venues are boutique rather than all-inclusive.

Is Tulum more expensive than Cancún for a wedding?

Often, for the same guest count. Boutique venues price each element separately, vendors travel in, and the transfer is closer to two hours. Cancún's all-inclusive packages bundle much of that.

How far is Tulum from Cancún airport?

Closer to two hours door to door in normal traffic, against 45 to 55 minutes for Playa del Carmen or Mayakoba.

What does photography cost for a Tulum wedding?

Our published ranges apply: 1,800 to 2,800 USD for a half day, 3,500 to 5,500 for a full day, 5,500 to 8,000 with a highlight film. Travel from Cancún is arranged as part of the coverage.

When should the ceremony start in Tulum?

Plan it against sunset, not the clock. The beach road is slow in high season and the golden hour is short; a late start costs the light.
