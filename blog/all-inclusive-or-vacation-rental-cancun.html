All-Inclusive or Vacation Rental in Cancun With Kids?

In short

Pick an all-inclusive for meals, pools and a kids club handled for you; pick a rental for bedrooms, a kitchen and room for a big group. Compare totals with taxes: Quintana Roo charges a 6% lodging tax on rented homes and 5% at hotels, and hosts must register with the state. For photos, the beach in front of a beachfront rental is federal, with no hotel fee; at a resort, we confirm the hotel's rule first.

A condo or villa you rent, or an all-inclusive resort: it is one of the first choices a family makes for Cancun. We photograph families at both, so here is how they compare with kids, which Quintana Roo rules apply to rentals, and how a photo session works at each.

Which One Fits Your Family

An all-inclusive tends to suit parents who want a real break from cooking and dishes, kids old enough to enjoy a kids club, and short trips, where every hour spent shopping or cooking is an hour away from the pool.

A rental tends to suit babies and toddlers who nap better in a dark bedroom of their own, big or multigenerational groups who want one living room and one table, and families who cook around allergies, picky eaters or a long stay.

Or split the difference: a rental for the space and one resort day for the pools, covered below.

What Each Price Really Covers

Nightly rates do not compare until you add what each one leaves out. An all-inclusive rate covers your room, meals and drinks at the resort; a rental covers the home, and food, cleaning and transport are yours to add. Price the same dates for your exact family, every child included.

Two tax details matter. Quintana Roo's lodging tax law charges the guest 6% for a rented apartment, house or private villa and 5% at hotels. At an all-inclusive, the 5% applies only to the lodging part of the bill, not the food; if the hotel does not separate them, the law taxes at least 60% of the total. Airbnb says guests in Quintana Roo pay the 6% on the listing price, cleaning fee included, and that stays in Mexico may also be subject to 16% VAT. The same state law says a rental's extra charges must be shown on the platform and honored as published.

Then add what sits outside each rate: for a rental, groceries, meals out, taxis or a car and any resort day pass; for an all-inclusive, meals and outings away from the resort. Our 2027 family vacation cost guide prices a real all-inclusive week for families of four and six.

Renting a Condo or Villa: Space and State Rules

Space is the main reason to rent. Separate bedrooms let a toddler nap in the dark while older kids swim, a living room holds three generations, and a kitchen keeps breakfast and bottles simple. Plan the first night's food before you land: ask the host where the nearest supermarket is, or order groceries.

Check "beachfront" on the map, not in the listing title. In Cancun's Hotel Zone, a building on the Caribbean side of Kukulcan Boulevard can open onto the sand, while one on the Nichupte Lagoon side means crossing the road to reach the sea.

Quintana Roo regulates rentals as lodging. Its tourism law counts hosts who rent homes through digital platforms as tourism service providers, makes registration in the state tourism registry, the Registro Estatal de Turismo, annual, free and mandatory, and says platforms may not offer the services of providers without a valid registration certificate. The lodging tax law adds a tax registration for each property, so it is fair to ask a host whether the home is registered.

Then ask the safety questions that matter with kids: is the pool fenced, are the balcony rails high enough for a climber, and who answers at night? Airbnb's rules, in effect since April 30, 2024, do not allow security cameras that monitor indoor spaces and require hosts to disclose exterior cameras in the listing. Either way, read the US and Canadian travel advice for Mexico before you book.

All-Inclusives: Kids Clubs, Pools and Food

An all-inclusive trades space for ease. Breakfast is downstairs, lunch is by the pool and nobody washes a dish, which for many parents is the whole point of the trip.

Before you book, check four things for your family: the kids club's minimum age, hours, whether a parent must stay and whether it is in the rate; a shallow pool or splash area for toddlers; how dinner reservations work; and the room's maximum occupancy, every child counted. With five or more, compare a family suite or connecting rooms against a condo. Our guide to kids clubs at Cancun all-inclusives compares ages and hours.

Renting? A Resort Day Pass

If you rent but want one resort day for the pools or the kids club, ask the resort whether it sells day passes to non-guests, what the pass includes, its hours and its age rules. For photos that day, also ask whether a photographer needs a pass or the hotel's outside-photographer fee; we confirm that rule with the hotel before the session. Our guide to resort day passes for family photos walks through the questions.

Family Photos: The Condo Beach or the Resort Rules

At a rental on the beach, we usually photograph on the sand right in front. Under Mexico's General Law of National Property, beaches and the federal zone, a strip of land 20 meters wide next to them, are federal property for common use, and access to them may not be blocked, restricted or conditioned except as the regulations allow. With no hotel in between, there is no outside-photographer fee. The building's pool, gardens and terraces are private, so ask the management before using them. More in our guide to public beaches and the federal zone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a vacation rental cheaper than an all-inclusive in Cancun?

It depends on how many people share the home and what you would spend on food. Compare final totals, taxes included: a rental's rate plus cleaning and service fees, 6% lodging tax, any VAT, groceries, meals out and transport, against an all-inclusive's total, which covers meals and drinks and applies the 5% lodging tax only to the lodging part.

Do vacation rentals in Cancun have to be registered?

Yes. Quintana Roo's tourism law counts hosts who rent homes through digital platforms as tourism service providers, makes registration in the state tourism registry annual, free and mandatory, and says platforms may not offer the services of providers without a valid certificate. The state's lodging tax law also requires a tax registration for each property.

Is the beach in front of a Cancun condo public?

Mexican law makes beaches and the 20-meter federal zone next to them federal property for common use, and says access may not be blocked, restricted or conditioned except as the regulations allow. The building's pool, gardens and terraces are private.

Can we have family photos at our vacation rental?

Yes. At a beachfront rental we usually photograph on the beach in front, where there is no hotel and so no outside-photographer fee; for the building's pool or gardens, ask the management first. There is no limit on people, no per-person charge and no deposit: you pay at the end of the session.

Do Cancun resorts charge a fee for an outside photographer?

Some hotels do. Where yours does, you pay it to the hotel, and we confirm the hotel's rule with you before the session. If you rent and buy a resort day pass, ask the resort whether your photographer needs a pass or the fee.
