In short

Pick the start that follows a good sleep (online: 6:30, 7:00 or 7:30 am, or 5:00 or 5:30 pm), choose 45 minutes with The Keepsake (375 USD) or 60 with The Chapter (475 USD), photograph the whole family first and bring out the cake last. Grandparents add nothing to the price, and a session the day before the birthday or earlier means the 24-hour preview arrives in time to share.

Your baby will not remember their first birthday. Everyone who flew in for it will. A first birthday beach photoshoot on your Cancun trip gives you a short, calm session with the people who came to celebrate, and photos your child will one day ask to see. Like any 1st birthday photos on vacation, it works best when it is planned around the baby, not the party. Below is how we approach them: when to start, how long to stay, where the cake fits and how to bring in the relatives.

Pick a Start Time After the Nap

A one-year-old gives you their best minutes after a good sleep and a recent meal, so we plan the start time backward from the nap, not forward from the sunset.

The booking page offers three morning starts, at 6:30, 7:00 and 7:30, and two in the late afternoon, at 5:00 and 5:30. If your one-year-old's day runs on a different clock, write to us on WhatsApp to ask about other times.

  • Morning, 6:30 to 7:30 am. For an early riser who is wide awake by six. A 45 or 60 minute session can be over before the morning nap.
  • Afternoon, 5:00 or 5:30 pm. For a one-year-old whose long nap comes after lunch. Keep the hours before it calm: a long, hot day at the pool shows up in the photos.

Write the naps down in Cancun time: if you crossed time zones to get here, your baby may still be on home time for the first days. And keep the photos and the party apart: if there is a birthday dinner, put the session at the other end of the day or on another day. Torn between a morning and an evening start? Tell us about the baby

With your booking, send your baby's age, the usual nap and meal times, whether they crawl, cruise or walk, and who is coming. Those answers decide the start time and the running order.

45 or 60 Minutes Is Plenty

A birthday session needs room for the family frames, a pause and the cake, and not much more. For a first birthday, we usually suggest one of these two collections:

  • The Keepsake, 375 USD. 45 minutes, one look and 25 edited photos. Enough for parents and baby, or for a one-year-old whose good mood runs short.
  • The Chapter, 475 USD. 60 minutes, one look and 35 edited photos. Our usual recommendation for families, and the better fit when grandparents come along: the extra 15 minutes cover a feed and an unhurried cake.

Want a separate outfit for the cake? Two looks begin with The Story (75 minutes, 50 edited photos, 545 USD), and you can move up to a bigger collection by paying only the difference. First Birthday Photo Ideas for the Beach

We plan the gallery around a one-year-old's patience: the frames that need everyone happy first, the messy one last. These baby birthday photoshoot ideas work well on the sand:

  1. Everyone together. The big family portrait right after hello, while your baby is fed and curious.
  2. Three generations. Grandparent, parent and baby in one frame.
  3. Up in the air. One of you lifts your baby high over the foam while the other leans in for the laugh.
  4. First steps, or almost. If your baby is cruising or just walking, wet sand and two fingers to hold make a lovely first-steps frame. If not, a crawl toward the water works just as well.
  5. Toes in the foam. A parent holds the baby as a small wave reaches their feet.
  6. The number one. A wooden number 1 or a sign with your baby's name, if you bring one from home.
  7. The quiet one. Your baby asleep on a shoulder after a wobble.
  8. The cake. Last of all.

We never ask a one-year-old to pose. The family plays, we photograph how your baby responds, and if tears come, we stop for a cuddle and wait. If your baby already runs, our family photos with toddlers playbook has more games.

The Cake Comes Last

A cake smash on the beach makes a wonderful closing scene, as long as it is the closing scene: saved for the final minutes, it cannot spoil the outfits or the family portraits.

  • Keep it small. A smash cake just for the baby, on a plate set on a towel rather than on the sand.
  • Keep it cool. Frosting softens in the heat, so leave the cake in the shade or a cooler bag until the last minutes.
  • Let your baby decide. Some one-year-olds dive in with both hands; others poke it once and look at you. Both are real birthday photos.
  • Have the change of clothes ready. When the cake is done, so is the session.

Check with your hotel before the trip. Rules on outside food, and on cakes, candles or decorations on the beach, vary from resort to resort, so ask whether you may bring a cake down to the sand or whether the hotel kitchen can prepare one. Separately, some hotels charge an outside-photographer fee, which you pay directly to the hotel; we check your hotel's policy before the session.

What to bring

The cake, a plate and a candle if your hotel allows one; a towel, wipes, water for sandy hands and a full change of clothes; whatever soothes your baby, from a pacifier to a favorite toy; a sun hat for between frames; and a number or name sign if you want one. Dress your baby in something soft they have worn before, and feed them shortly before we start.

Grandparents Who Flew In

If grandparents, aunts, uncles or cousins came for the birthday, bring them all. Our collections are priced per session, never per person, and there is no cap on the group, so the price is the same for three of you or fifteen. With a big group, the order matters more than the poses:

  • Everyone first. The whole group at the start, while your baby is at their best.
  • Then the generations. Each set of grandparents with the baby, then grandparent, parent and baby together.
  • Helpers behind the camera. Whoever is not in the frame stands behind us, waving and calling your baby's name.
  • A few minutes for the two of you. While a grandparent holds the baby, we photograph the parents who made it through the first year.

For a group of up to 16 that would rather meet somewhere away from the hotel, we can arrange transportation in full vans; ask us for the price.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time for first birthday photos on a Cancun beach?

Whichever start follows a good sleep, overnight or a nap. Online, you can choose 6:30, 7:00 or 7:30 am or 5:00 or 5:30 pm, and ask on WhatsApp about other times. Early risers do well in the morning; a baby with a long midday nap often suits 5:00 or 5:30 pm.

Can we do a cake smash on the beach?

Yes, as the very last scene, so frosting and sand cannot spoil the family portraits. Bring a small cake, a towel, wipes and spare clothes, and ask your hotel first, since rules on outside food vary from resort to resort.

How long should a first birthday photo session be?

Usually 45 or 60 minutes. The Keepsake gives you 45 minutes and 25 edited photos for 375 USD; The Chapter, our recommendation for families, gives you 60 minutes and 35 edited photos for 475 USD. For a second outfit for the cake, The Story has two looks: 75 minutes, 50 edited photos, 545 USD.

Grandparents are flying in for the birthday. Can everyone be in the photos?

Yes, bring everyone. Collections are priced per session, with no per-person charge and no cap on the group. We start with the whole family, move on to the generations and the parents with the baby, and finish with the cake.

Will we have photos to share on the birthday itself?

Yes, if you photograph at least a day before the birthday: a preview reaches you within 24 hours. The complete private gallery follows 1 to 3 business days after the session, in 4K, with unlimited downloads and print rights.

What if our baby is teething or gets sick before the session?

Nothing is paid in advance: there is no deposit, and payment comes after the session. You can cancel free of charge up to one day before, so let us know as soon as you can, and schedule the session early in the trip so spare days remain.
