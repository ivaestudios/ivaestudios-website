Does It Rain Every Day in Cancun? Rain by Month

In short

No. Mexico's National Meteorological Service (SMN) puts Cancun at 116 days with rain in an average year. October is the rainiest month, with 277.1 mm (10.9 in) on 16.4 days, about half its days; April has the fewest, 3.8. Rain usually comes as a shower: in hourly reports from Cancun's airport, just over half of the rainy days showed rain in only one or two reports. A rain icon on your phone means a chance of rain, not rain all day.

A weather app can show a storm icon on nearly every day of a summer or fall trip to Cancun. It does not rain every day. Here is the rain by month from the official averages, how much of a wet day is actually wet, why the app looks so gloomy, and how to plan a family day and a photo session around a shower.

So even in October, rain falls on about half the days, not all of them. In the station's daily record, which goes back to 1988, no complete month has rain on every day; the most is 24 days with rain, in October 2024.

A day with rain is also a generous count. We checked the averages against that daily record, and they include every day with any rain in the gauge, so a day with half a millimeter counts the same as a day with 100 mm. Count only days with at least 1 mm (0.04 in), and the average year drops from 116 days with rain to about 92.

Rain builds in June, dips in July, climbs again through August and September and peaks in October. That July dip matches what the SMN glossary calls the canícula: a marked, uneven drop in rain between July and August that varies from year to year. After October, rain eases, though January, at 103.8 mm, still gets a little more rain than December. February to April are the driest months, with 3.8 to 5.9 days with rain each.

Tropical storms are a separate question. The US National Hurricane Center dates the Atlantic hurricane season from June 1 to November 30, with most activity between mid-August and mid-October. Does It Rain All Day?

Rarely. The normals count days, not hours, so for timing we used an official hourly source: NOAA's Integrated Surface Database, which archives the weather reports from Cancun International Airport. We took the routine reports, about one an hour, from January 2016 to July 2025, more than 83,000 of them, and counted those that recorded rain falling at the airport.

Of the roughly 3,360 days with at least 22 hourly reports, about 820 had rain in at least one. On those days the median was two rainy reports, and on 53% of them rain showed up in only one or two. Days with rain in half or more of their reports were rare: fewer than 40 of them, roughly one day in 90. Each report records the weather at that moment, so a brief shower between two reports can go unrecorded; that is one reason these counts run lower than the SMN's days with rain.

That fits the SMN glossary's description of a shower, or chubasco: intense and short, with an abrupt start and end. No official source we found times a typical shower in minutes, but a wet day here is usually a day with a shower in it.

Is the morning drier? Not at the airport. From June to October, rain was falling in 4.7% of all reports, and no hour was reliably dry: by hour, the share ran from 3.6% to 6.1%. Between 6 and 9 am it was just under 5%, and between 5 and 7 pm, about 4%. Showers are local, so read these numbers as the shape of the weather, not a forecast for your beach.

Why Your Weather App Shows Rain Every Day

A rain percentage is a probability, not a duration. The US National Weather Service defines the probability of precipitation as the chance that precipitation will be reported at a certain location during a specified period, and one of its offices spells out the threshold: at least 0.01 inch. A daily figure covers many hours, so one short shower at any time in that window is enough to count.

In September and October about half the days get some rain, so a chance of rain on most days is an honest forecast, even if the rain turns out to be one shower. Check the hourly view and the percentage, not just the icon.

On the day, two SMN tools help more: its forecast by municipality gives a daily chance of rain (Cancun is in the municipality of Benito Juárez, Quintana Roo), and its radar viewer, which includes a Cancun radar, shows where rain is falling now.

A Rainy-Day Plan With Kids

Most wet days hold a shower, not a washout, so keep the plan loose: beach and pool while the sky is clear, and something under a roof for the wet hour or two.

  • Your resort first. Start with what your hotel has under a roof: a kids club, the lobby, a covered terrace, or a board game in the room.
  • The Maya Museum. Mexico's INAH lists the Museo Maya de Cancun at km 16.5 of Kukulcan Boulevard, open Tuesday to Sunday from 9 am to 5 pm, with last entry at 4:30 pm. The visit includes the San Miguelito archaeological site; food is not allowed inside.
  • The aquarium at La Isla. Delphinus places its Interactive Aquarium Cancun inside La Isla Shopping Village, at km 12.5, open daily from 10 am to 5 pm, with admission at 15.99 USD per person.
  • Thunder means indoors. The US National Weather Service puts it simply: when thunder roars, go indoors. There is no safe place outside when thunderstorms are in the area, beach and pool included.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it rain every day in Cancun?
No. In the SMN's 1991 to 2020 normals, Cancun averages 116 days with rain a year. Even October, the rainiest month, averages 16.4 days with rain out of 31, and April averages 3.8.
What is the rainiest month in Cancun?
October, with 277.1 mm (10.9 in) on 16.4 days on average, ahead of June (184.4 mm) and September (181.5 mm). March is the driest month, at 38.7 mm, and April has the fewest days with rain, 3.8.
When does the rainy season end in Cancun?
Rain peaks in October, then eases: November averages 131.4 mm on 12 days and December 96.6 mm on 10.8. January still gets 103.8 mm on 9.8 days; February to April are the driest months. The Atlantic hurricane season ends November 30.
Does it rain all day in Cancun?
Rarely. In hourly reports from Cancun International Airport, January 2016 to July 2025, rain showed up in only one or two reports on 53% of rainy days, and in half or more of a day's reports on fewer than 40 of some 3,360 days, roughly one day in 90.
Why does my weather app show rain every day in Cancun?
A rain percentage is the chance of some rain at some point in the period, not a forecast of all-day rain. In September and October about half the days get some rain, so a chance of rain shows up on most days. Check the hourly view and SMN's radar.
What happens to our family photo session if it rains?
There is no deposit and cancellation is free up to one day before, so if the forecast looks wet you can cancel at no cost and pick another day. If a shower starts mid-session, a covered spot at your resort can keep it going, where the hotel allows it.
