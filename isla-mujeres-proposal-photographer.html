Light & Timing

Sunrise or sunset on the island

Quintana Roo keeps one clock all year, so the light moves only with the seasons. Be in position 10 to 15 minutes before sunrise; for sunset, we start about an hour before it.

Sunrise on Playa Norte

About 6:05 am in mid-June, 7:08 am on 1 December and, at the latest, about 7:27 am in mid-January. The north end of the beach is nearly empty until about 8 am, which makes sunrise the most private time to propose.

Sunset over the channel

Earliest about 6:04 pm in late November, about 6:08 pm in mid-December and, at the latest, about 7:33 pm in early July. Playa Norte faces roughly west, so the sun sets over the water in front of you, with more people on the sand.

The ferry, the wind and the backup plan

Confirm the first Puerto Juárez departure the day before. Ferries are occasionally suspended with strong north winds, mostly November to February. We agree a mainland backup in advance and decide the evening before; rescheduling for weather is free.

Punta Sur or Playa Norte

Playa Norte is ten minutes on foot from the ferry. The Punta Sur park opens after first light, so at sunrise we use the cliffs and viewpoints outside the gate; inside the park only from mid-morning.

How It Works

How we stay hidden, and what happens after the yes

01

We arrive first

We are in position before you, with a 70 to 200 mm lens from about 25 to 40 metres, dressed like any other visitor. Phones stay on silent, and we never wave or approach before the question.

02

Three signals

The day before, we agree the exact spot, the direction you will face and one small cue, so we know the moment is coming without a word or a glance in our direction.

03

After the yes

We keep shooting hidden for several minutes, then introduce ourselves. Most couples continue into portraits on the empty beach. If family is waiting to celebrate, there is no per-person charge.

04

Your hotel or a restaurant

If the plan involves a hotel on the island, a beach club or a restaurant, we talk to them in Spanish and check their rule for outside photographers beforehand. If a hotel charges a fee for outside photographers, it is paid to the hotel.
