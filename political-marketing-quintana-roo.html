FAQ

Questions from political teams.

Is IVAE Marketing a political marketing agency?
Yes. IVAE Marketing is a marketing and social media agency based in Cancún. Alongside hospitality, health and service brands, we run the digital communication of politicians, candidates and public officials in Quintana Roo.
Do you work with every party?
Yes. We work with candidates from any party and with independent candidacies. Once signed, we offer exclusivity per race: we do not work with another candidate for the same office in that election.
What does the political marketing service include?
Strategy and messaging, in-house photo and video, reels, graphic design, daily publishing on Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, comment and message management, Meta ads and reports. Everything is reviewed and approved in our app before it goes live.
Can you run paid ads on Facebook and Instagram?
Yes. Meta requires a special authorization for ads about elections or politics, and every ad carries a disclaimer with the name of who paid for it. We guide you through that process and run the ads once authorized.
Which municipalities do you cover?
All eleven municipalities of Quintana Roo, from Cancún to Chetumal. Our production crew travels wherever your agenda goes.
Do you work with a contract and invoice?
Yes. Every engagement is formalized with a written contract and an invoice, and we follow the periods and rules of the electoral process.
How much does it cost?
Each proposal is built around the office, the territory and the stage. Write to us and we will send a tailored proposal, with no commitment.
