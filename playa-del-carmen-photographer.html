The Place

The one place on this coast with a street

Cancún is a hotel strip on a barrier island. Most of the Riviera Maya is resort, jungle, resort. Playa del Carmen is a town of well over a hundred thousand people, and that changes what a session can be.

One block back from the sand runs Quinta Avenida, a pedestrian street several kilometres long. At seven in the morning it is empty, the shutters are down and the light comes sideways between low buildings. That hour gives you photographs with texture: doorways, painted walls, a town waking up. By ten it belongs to everybody.

And the beach is still there, two minutes away, facing east like the whole coast. So a session here can be two places instead of one, without anyone getting into a car.

Where We Photograph

The settings that hold up

01

The beach at sunrise

Wide, east facing, and empty before the loungers go out. The same rule as the rest of the coast: the sun comes out of the water in front of you.

02

Quinta Avenida, early

Before eight. Shutters down, no crowd, and light raking down a street that runs the length of the town.

03

Punta Esmeralda

A cenote spills into the sea at the northern end, so cold fresh water meets warm salt. Shallow, glassy and full of local families at weekends.

04

Parque Fundadores

The Portal Maya sculpture and the old ferry pier. Recognisably Playa, and best at first light before the boats start.

05

Playacar

South of the centre: gated, quiet, palm lined, with a longer and emptier beach than the town's own.

06

The Cozumel pier

Boats, water and the island on the horizon. Good in the twenty minutes after sunset, when the town lights come on.

Light & Timing

Two hours, and not the ones you think

This coast faces east, so the good light is not where a photographer from home would put it. Quintana Roo stays on one clock all year, so these hold in every season.

Sunrise, the first hour

The sun comes out of the Caribbean in front of the beach and the town is asleep. This is the only hour that gives you both the empty sand and the empty street.

The last ninety minutes of afternoon

The sun has dropped behind the town and the beach sits in open shade with bright water in front. Comfortable and easy to get out for.

Twenty minutes after sunset

The sky over the town keeps colour, and Quinta Avenida turns on. A different kind of frame entirely.

Not midday

Overhead sun, hard shadow, and a beach at its busiest. We will offer you a different hour rather than take the booking.

Straight Answers

What people ask about Playa

What makes a session in Playa del Carmen different from Cancún?

The town. Cancún is a hotel strip, so a session there is beach or resort. Playa del Carmen has Quinta Avenida one block from the sand, which means you can photograph a street and a beach in the same hour without getting into a car.

What time should we shoot on Quinta Avenida?

Before eight in the morning. The shutters are still down, there is no crowd, and the light rakes down the street. By mid morning it belongs to everyone and it stops working as a backdrop.

Is Playa del Carmen in the Riviera Maya?

Yes. The Riviera Maya is the coast running south from Cancún to roughly Tulum, and Playa del Carmen is the largest town on it, about halfway along. They are not alternatives to each other.

What is Punta Esmeralda?

A small beach at the northern end of town where a cenote spills fresh water into the sea. Cold, clear and shallow, and completely different from the rest of the coast. Busy with local families at weekends, quiet on a weekday morning.

Do you travel to Playa del Carmen from Cancún?

Yes, weekly, and there is no travel charge. Playa del Carmen, Puerto Morelos, Isla Mujeres and the northern Riviera Maya are all inside our normal coverage.

Can you photograph a wedding here?

Yes. The town suits smaller ceremonies and elopements particularly well, on the beach or at a boutique property, with the streets available for everything around the day.

How fast do we get the photographs?

One to three days, in full resolution, with no watermark and no expiry on the gallery.
