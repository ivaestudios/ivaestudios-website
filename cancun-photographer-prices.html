What moves the price

  • Session length. Most packages are sold by time, from 30 minutes to two hours.
  • Edited photos. Packages include from 20 to more than 100 edited photos. Some studios sell extra photos (80 USD for a pack of 10, or 25 USD each), and one charges 15 USD for each photo you choose after the session, with a minimum of 10.
  • Direction and posing. Being guided through every pose and grouping, especially with children or a big group, takes the photographer's full attention for the whole session, and that time is part of the price.
  • Editing time. Retouching color, skin and stray people in the background is done photo by photo, so more edited photos means more hours of work after the session.
  • Delivery speed. Published delivery for sessions runs from 48 hours to three weeks; one studio lists four weeks, longer in high season, and one sells 24-hour delivery for 60 USD more.
  • Deposit and cancellation. Terms run from no deposit at all to 50% paid upfront, and some retainers are non-refundable. Read the cancellation and rain terms before you pay.
  • Hotel fees and travel. Many resorts charge outside photographers a vendor fee or ask for a day pass. The resort sets it and you pay it to the resort, on top of the photographer's price. Some studios also add 20 to 150 USD to travel to Costa Mujeres, Isla Mujeres, Playa del Carmen or Tulum.
  • Group size. Some packages cap the group at 4, 6, 8 or 13 people and move bigger families to a larger package.

What Is Not In The Number

The costs that belong to someone else

01

Resort vendor fee

Many hotels in the Hotel Zone charge an outside vendor fee to photographers who are not on their in-house list. It is paid by you directly to the hotel, it is not ours, and it ranges widely by property. We confirm the current rule at your resort before you sign anything.

02

Travel outside the coast

Cancun, the Hotel Zone, Costa Mujeres, Isla Mujeres, Puerto Morelos, Playa del Carmen and Tulum carry no travel charge. For Mérida, Los Cabos, San Miguel de Allende or anywhere further, we quote flights and one night, at cost, before you decide.

03

Rules at special locations

Thinking of an archaeological site, a nature park or another protected place? Ask us first: we check that place's current rules for professional cameras and tell you before the date whether a permit or entry fee applies, so it never becomes a surprise on the day.

04

Printing your photos

The gallery comes with unlimited downloads and print rights, so you can print your favorite photos at any lab you like. The cost of printing is yours, which is why it is not in the number.

How Payment Works

When money changes hands, and why

There are no hidden stages. A session is paid once, at the end of it. A wedding holds its date with a retainer. All of it is written down before anything is charged.

Weddings: the retainer holds your date

Weddings hold the date with a 10% retainer. Until it clears, the date stays open to anyone. It is credited against the total, never in addition to it.

Sessions are paid at the end

Family, couples and proposal sessions need no deposit: you pay when the session ends, by card or in cash, and cancellation is free up to one day before. For weddings, the balance is due 30 days before the date, so nobody handles payments on the morning of their own wedding.

How you pay

Sessions are paid at the end, by card or in cash, in dollars or pesos; wedding payments can also go by bank transfer. Ask for the invoice if you need one for tax purposes in Mexico.

Delivery does not depend on payment

The gallery arrives in 1 to 3 business days regardless. We do not hold photographs as leverage.
