Booking & payment

A 30% retainer reserves your date, with the balance due 30 days before the wedding. Payments by card, bank transfer or cash in USD or MXN.

Travel

Travel within Cancún and the Riviera Maya is included in every collection. Venues beyond this area are quoted separately, with no hidden fees added later.

Venue fees

Some resorts charge an outside vendor fee or require day passes. These are set by the venue and billed by them. We confirm the policy with your resort before you book.

Good to know

Questions we are often asked

Do you work at resorts?

Yes. We shoot regularly across Cancún and the Riviera Maya and know how resort timelines and light behave.

What if it rains?

We plan covered alternatives with your venue in advance. Weather never cancels your coverage.

How many images do we choose?

None. Every image that makes the final cut is edited and delivered to you in full resolution.

Can we see full galleries?

Of course. We are happy to send complete weddings and full films so you can judge the whole day, not only the highlights.

Do you speak English?

Yes. We work in English and Spanish throughout planning and on the wedding day.

How far ahead should we book?

Peak dates are reserved first. Once your date is held with the retainer, your price is locked in.
