Cancun Family Photoshoot Packages and Prices

By Vianey Díaz · Published November 18, 2026
Home› Prices› Cancun Family Photoshoot Packages

IVAE Studios has six family photoshoot packages in Cancun, priced in US dollars by session length. The Postcard: 35 minutes and 15 edited photos, $295. The Keepsake: 45 minutes, 25 photos, $375. The Chapter, our pick for most families: 60 minutes, 35 photos, $475. The Story: 75 minutes, 50 photos and two outfit looks, $545. The Editorial: 90 minutes, 70 photos and two looks, $645. The Heirloom: 90 minutes, the complete edited gallery and two looks, $895. There is no deposit: you pay at the end of the session, by card or in cash, in US dollars or Mexican pesos. There is no per-person charge and no limit on group size. You get a preview within 24 hours and the full 4K gallery in 1 to 3 business days. We are rated 5.0 from 75 Google reviews, 29 of which mention the children.

The six packages, side by side

Every package is priced for the whole family. The price depends on how long the session runs and how many edited photos you receive, never on how many people are in the photos.

Paying in Mexican pesos? We charge at the day's exchange rate. With the dollar at about 18.6 pesos in mid-November 2026, that came to about 5,500 MXN for The Postcard, 7,000 MXN for The Keepsake, 8,800 MXN for The Chapter, 10,100 MXN for The Story, 12,000 MXN for The Editorial and 16,600 MXN for The Heirloom. The same six packages and prices are on our Cancun photographer prices page.

What every package includes

  • Edited photos: from 15 in The Postcard to the complete edited gallery in The Heirloom.
  • A first preview within 24 hours of your session.
  • The full 4K gallery in 1 to 3 business days.
  • Unlimited downloads and print rights.
  • No per-person charge and no group-size limit: grandparents, aunts, uncles and cousins are included at the same price.
  • English or Spanish: we are a bilingual studio.
  • Upgrades anytime: move up to a larger package and pay only the difference.

Which package fits your family

  • A short session or a small family: The Postcard, 35 minutes and 15 edited photos for $295, or The Keepsake, 45 minutes and 25 photos for $375.
  • Most families: The Chapter, 60 minutes and 35 edited photos for $475. It is the package we recommend for families.
  • A large family or a group with grandparents: The Story (75 minutes, 50 photos, $545) or The Editorial (90 minutes, 70 photos, $645), with time for photos of each group and two outfit looks. The Editorial is our best value.
  • Every edited photo: The Heirloom, 90 minutes, the complete edited gallery and two looks for $895.

Photos from our family sessions, and more about how they work, are on our Cancun family photographer page.

How payment works

There is no deposit and nothing to pay before your session. You pay at the end, by card or in cash, in US dollars or in Mexican pesos at the day's exchange rate. Cancellation is free up to one day before, and if the weather gets in the way, rescheduling is free.

The price does not change with the size of your group, because there is no per-person charge. If you want more photos than your package includes, you can move up to a larger package at any time and pay only the difference.

Where sessions happen

IVAE Studios was founded in 2023 by Vianey Díaz and is based in Cancun. We photograph families on Cancun's public beaches or at your hotel. Sessions at a resort are subject to that resort's rules for outside photographers, so tell us where you are staying when you write.

Questions families ask

How much does a family photoshoot in Cancun cost?
With IVAE Studios, from $295 to $895. The Postcard is $295 for 35 minutes and 15 edited photos, and The Heirloom is $895 for 90 minutes and the complete edited gallery. For most families we recommend The Chapter: 60 minutes and 35 edited photos for $475. There is no per-person charge.
Which package is best for a family of four, or for a large family?
For a family of four we recommend The Chapter: 60 minutes and 35 edited photos for $475. For a large family or a group with grandparents, The Story (75 minutes, 50 photos, two looks, $545) or The Editorial (90 minutes, 70 photos, two looks, $645) leave time for photos of each group. The price is the same however many people join.
Do you charge per person?
No. There is no per-person charge and no limit on group size, so grandparents, aunts, uncles and cousins can all be in the photos at the same price.
Do we pay a deposit, and how do we pay?
There is no deposit. You pay at the end of the session, by card or in cash, in US dollars or in Mexican pesos at the day's exchange rate. Cancellation is free up to one day before, and rescheduling for weather is free.
How soon do we get our photos?
You get a first preview within 24 hours and the full 4K gallery in 1 to 3 business days, with unlimited downloads and print rights.
Can we upgrade to a bigger package?
Yes, at any time. You move up to the larger package and pay only the difference between the two prices.

How to book

Message us on WhatsApp at +52 990 204 6514, in English or Spanish, with your dates, your hotel and who is coming, and tell us which package you would like. There is nothing to pay until the end of your session. Before you book, you can read our 75 Google reviews on our Google listing.
