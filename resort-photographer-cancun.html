The Rule Nobody Explains

The in-house studio is not your only option

Every large resort on this coast has a photography concession inside it, and that studio would prefer you never learned there was a choice. There is. Bringing an outside photographer is normal, legal and common, and the hotel's own contract usually says so in a paragraph nobody reads.

What some properties do charge is an outside vendor fee, paid by you directly to the hotel, for a photographer who is not on their list. It varies enormously by property and it changes without notice. Several hotels waive it entirely if the photographer stays one night as a guest. Others do not charge at all.

We confirm the current rule at your specific resort before you sign anything, in writing, so the number never appears as a surprise on your bill. And the beach in Mexico is federal property up to the tide line, so no hotel can charge you for photographs made on the sand itself.

The properties we work in most

The Hotel Zone runs north to south, and that decides everything: the northern properties look west across the bay, the long eastern stretch takes the sunrise.

Riviera Maya & Playa Mujeres

South of the airport, and north of it

01

Mayakoba: Rosewood, Banyan Tree, Fairmont, Andaz

The canal complex south of Playa del Carmen. Mangrove waterways, boardwalks and a light that behaves like nowhere else. See Rosewood, Banyan Tree and Mayakoba weddings.

Big properties with big beaches and an internal photography team that is used to outside photographers arriving. Announce, check the fee, and the day runs smoothly.

Architecture that photographs as well as the beach does, which is rare. Long walks between areas, so the timeline needs building carefully.

Quiet, mature properties north of Playa del Carmen with wide empty sand at sunrise.

Closer to the airport than to Playa del Carmen, calmer water, and the reef right offshore.

06

Excellence Playa Mujeres and Costa Mujeres

North of Cancún, the newest corridor on the coast. Wide beaches, few people, and the best sunrise light of anywhere we work.

Before You Book

Four questions to ask your hotel

Ask these before you sign the wedding or the room package, not after. The answers change what the session costs and where it happens.

Do you charge an outside vendor fee?

And how much, in writing. Some properties waive it if the photographer books a night. Some do not charge at all. Very few will volunteer the information.

Where can we photograph on the property?

Beach, terraces, lobby, gardens. Some hotels restrict the restaurant areas or ask that other guests never appear in frame.

What time does the beach clear?

Sunrise is empty almost everywhere. Sunset is not. If your hotel runs beach activities until dark, we plan around them.

Is there construction right now?

This coast builds constantly, and a crane in the frame is not fixable later. We check the current state of your property before choosing the spot.

Straight Answers

What guests ask about resorts

Can I bring my own photographer to an all-inclusive resort?

Yes. Bringing an outside photographer is normal and allowed at essentially every property on this coast. What some hotels charge is an outside vendor fee, paid by you to the hotel, for a photographer who is not on their in-house list.

How much is the outside vendor fee in Cancún?

It varies enormously by property and changes without notice, which is why we confirm your specific hotel's current rate in writing rather than quoting an average. Some properties charge nothing. Several waive the fee if the photographer stays one night as a guest.

Can the hotel stop us photographing on the beach?

Not on the sand itself. Mexican beaches are federal property up to the tide line and open to everyone. What a hotel controls is the access through its own property and its pool, terrace and garden areas.

Which Cancún resort photographs best?

It depends on the hour you want. For sunrise, anything on the long eastern beach or in Costa Mujeres. For sunset with the sun in frame, the properties on the north shore near Punta Cancún. For architecture as much as beach, Mayakoba and Grand Velas.

Do you work at resorts in the Riviera Maya too?

Yes, and there is no travel charge for Playa del Carmen, Puerto Morelos, Akumal, Tulum or the Mayakoba complex. It is all inside our normal working area.

What if our resort has its own photographer?

Almost all of them do. You are not obliged to use them, and their package is usually structured around selling you photographs afterwards, one at a time. Ours is not: the full gallery is included and downloadable.

How far in advance should we book?

For a wedding, as early as you have a date, because we take a maximum of 12 a year. For a portrait session, two to three weeks is usually enough outside the December and Easter peaks.
