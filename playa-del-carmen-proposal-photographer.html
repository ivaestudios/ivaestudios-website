How It Works

How we keep it a surprise

01

The written plan

The day before, you receive the exact spot, the minute and a backup point about 100 meters away, with a photo of each so you recognize them. If a hotel, rooftop or beach club is involved, we handle that conversation in Spanish.

02

We arrive first

We are in position before you, 20 to 40 meters away with a long lens, dressed like any other visitor. Phones stay on silent, and we never wave or approach before the question.

03

One small cue

We agree the direction you will face and one small signal, a hand on the shoulder or a turn toward the sea, so the camera is already up when you kneel.

04

After the yes

We keep shooting hidden for several minutes, then introduce ourselves. Most couples continue into portraits on the beach or along Quinta Avenida, and if friends or family are waiting, there is no per-person charge.

Light & Timing

Sunrise over the water, blue hour on the avenue

Playa del Carmen faces the sea with Cozumel offshore: the sun rises over the water and sets behind the town. When you book at ivaestudios.com/book, you can pick 6:30, 7:00 or 7:30 am, or 5:00 or 5:30 pm; for any other hour, write to us on WhatsApp.

Sunrise on the town beach

About 7:04 am in late November, 7:17 am in mid-December and 7:27 am in mid-January; about 6:07 am in mid-June. The sand by the Portal Maya is nearly empty then, which makes it the most private hour to propose.

Blue hour on Quinta Avenida

Right after sunset, about 6:06 pm in late November, 6:10 pm in mid-December and 7:33 pm in early July, the avenue lights come on while the sky is still blue. Lively, public, and the yes gets applause.

Your resort in Playacar

The beach in front of your hotel at first light, or a terrace at golden hour, with your room steps away. We check the hotel's rule for outside photographers before your date.

Rain

If sustained rain is forecast for your hour, we move the proposal within your travel dates at no cost, and decide together the evening before. A brief shower usually passes within the hour.
