What an elopement in Los Cabos actually looks like

An elopement here is usually two people, sometimes two plus a handful, and almost never more than twenty. It runs two to four hours rather than a full day, and the whole point is that nothing about it is a production.

That changes how a photographer should work. There is no getting-ready suite to document for three hours and no reception to cover. What there is, is a short window of very good light, a ceremony that may last eight minutes, and two people who are not used to being photographed. The job is to be close without being in the way, and to come back with a record that feels like the day rather than like a shoot.

IVAE Studios is based in Cancún and travels to Baja, with more than 80 sessions already photographed on the peninsula. We work in English and Spanish, which on an elopement matters because the officiant and the venue coordinator are usually working in Spanish while you are not.

The Packages Question

What a Los Cabos elopement package actually includes

Almost everyone searching for a Cabo elopement is really asking about packages, so here is the straight version. There are two different things being sold under that word, and confusing them is the most common planning mistake we see.

01

The resort elopement package

Sold by the hotel. Typically the venue, the officiant or symbolic celebrant, basic flowers, a cake, sometimes a short toast, and very often a small number of photographs from an in-house photographer. It is convenient and it is the reason all-inclusive elopements exist.

02

What those packages usually leave out

The photography is the part most couples end up replacing. In-house coverage in a package is commonly thirty to sixty minutes and a limited set of images, with no golden-hour portraits after the ceremony and no say in who shoots it. Read the inclusion list for the number of final images, not the number of hours.

03

Where an outside photographer fits

Most Los Cabos properties allow an outside photographer, and some charge a vendor fee for it. That fee is usually far less than what the coverage difference is worth. We handle the arrangement with the property directly.

04

What our coverage is

Two to four hours built around sunset, the ceremony itself in full, portraits in the last light, and the full edited gallery in 4K within 1 to 3 days rather than weeks.

Where To Elope

The corners of Los Cabos that suit two people

01

The Pedregal cliffs

Portraits above Land's End with the Pacific behind you. The most dramatic option on the peninsula and the one that photographs least like a resort.

02

Esperanza and Punta Ballena

A private cove on the Sea of Cortez side. Quiet, contained, and well suited to a ceremony with no guests at all.

03

Lover's Beach and El Arco

Reachable only by boat, at the exact tip of the peninsula. The single frame that cannot be mistaken for anywhere else, and best in the first hour of light.

04

San José del Cabo

The mission church, the art district and the cobbled lanes. Where an elopement stops looking like a hotel and starts looking like Mexico.

05

A beach on the Corridor

The properties between the two towns, including The Cape, Chileno Bay and the Waldorf Astoria at Pedregal. Each has its own vendor policy, which we confirm before the day.

06

The desert behind the coast

Cardón cactus and granite within minutes of the sand, for couples who want something that does not look like a beach wedding at all.

The Practical Part

Cost, legality and when to do it

The four things couples ask before anything else, answered without a brochure.

What it costs

The resort package and the photography are separate numbers. Packages vary widely by property and season, and photography depends on the hours and the travel. Send the date and the venue and you get a real figure the same business day.

Legal or symbolic

A symbolic ceremony can happen anywhere with no paperwork, which is why most destination elopements are symbolic and the couple signs legally at home. A legal Mexican civil ceremony needs documents, translations and a civil judge. Neither is better, but you should choose on purpose.

Time of day

On the Pacific side, finish the ceremony about ninety minutes before sunset. On the Sea of Cortez side the beach cools earlier, so an hour before sunset is usually right. Sunrise at El Arco is the quietest option of all.

Time of year

October through May is the long dry stretch with the most reliable light. Summer works if you move the whole thing to the edges of the day.

Before You Book

What couples ask about eloping in Cabo

Can you get married in Cabo if you are not Mexican?

Yes. A symbolic ceremony has no requirements at all. A legal civil marriage in Baja California Sur requires documents, official translations and blood tests in some municipalities, so most couples who travel choose a symbolic ceremony in Cabo and handle the legal signing at home.

Is an all-inclusive elopement package worth it?

For the venue, the officiant and the logistics, usually yes. For the photography, read the inclusion list carefully: a package that says photography often means a short window and a small number of images, with no portraits after the ceremony.

Can we bring our own photographer to the resort?

Almost always. Most Los Cabos properties allow an outside photographer and some charge a vendor fee. We deal with the property directly so it is settled before the week of the wedding.

How many people can come and still call it an elopement?

We treat anything up to about twenty guests as an elopement or micro wedding. Past that, the coverage starts to need a second set of hands and it becomes a small wedding.

How long does the coverage run?

Two to four hours for most elopements. That covers the ceremony, the portraits in the good light, and enough of the surrounding hour that the day has a beginning and an end.

How far in advance should we book?

Elopements move faster than weddings because there is no guest list to manage. Six weeks is often enough outside peak season, though the best light months from October to May fill earlier.

Can we do sunrise instead?

Yes, and at El Arco it is the better choice. The peninsula is empty before eight and the water is at its clearest.
