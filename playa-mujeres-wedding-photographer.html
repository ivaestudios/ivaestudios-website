The Corridor

Where Cancún gets quiet

Playa Mujeres and the Costa Mujeres stretch sit just north of Puerto Juárez, past the ferry terminals to Isla Mujeres, on a coast that was almost empty until the resort wave of the last decade. Development here is newer and lower density than the Hotel Zone: the properties are enormous, set back behind mangrove and a golf course, with beachfront that runs for hundreds of metres per resort. From Cancún airport it is roughly half an hour to forty five minutes, depending on traffic and which property.

For photography the geography changes three things. The shoreline bends, so the beach faces roughly north and northeast rather than dead east: the water sits calmer and shallower, the sand is wide and pale, and the horizon holds Isla Mujeres in the distance instead of a wall of towers. The beaches are genuinely quiet, so a portrait walk does not have strangers in every frame the way it does at Playa Delfines. And the seaweed that reaches the coast further south generally arrives here in far smaller quantities.

IVAE Studios is based in Cancún, twenty minutes away. We work at Excellence Playa Mujeres, Secrets Playa Mujeres, Dreams Playa Mujeres and the other properties along Costa Mujeres as an outside vendor, and we handle that arrangement with each resort directly.

What We Photograph

Coverage built around the light on this beach

The properties here are large, so the plan starts with where the ceremony is, where the portraits actually live, and how long it takes to walk between them.

Weddings at the resort

Full day or half day, one or two photographers, the ceremony on the beach or gazebo and the reception on the terrace, with the couple's session timed to the last hour of light on the north-facing sand.

Two to four hours built around the ceremony and golden hour for couples marrying with a handful of witnesses. The quiet beach here is made for it.

Where We Shoot

The properties and corners that earn the frame

01

Excellence Playa Mujeres

Adults-only and open since 2015, it anchors the corridor: a curved, calmer shoreline, wide pale sand and almost no high-rises in the background. Plan the day backwards from sunset.

02

Secrets and Dreams Playa Mujeres

Same Hyatt Inclusive Collection, two personalities. Secrets is adults-only and calm at golden hour; Dreams welcomes everyone, with children in the aisle and on the guest list.

03

The north-facing beach

Because the coast turns here, the beach is calmer and wider than in the Hotel Zone and the frames have less clutter. At sunset the light comes across the sand rather than from behind the towers.

04

Gardens, pools and the lagoon side

The properties are big: palm walkways, infinity pools at blue hour and lagoon-facing terraces give a second and third look within the same session, and they do not care about seaweed.

05

Isla Mujeres on the horizon

The island sits in the frame from every beach here. For couples who want it closer, the ferry from Puerto Juárez is minutes away and a sunrise session on Playa Norte is a natural extra.

06

Along Costa Mujeres

The Light

When your ceremony should start

The single decision that changes your photographs more than any other is the ceremony start time. At Playa Mujeres it is dictated by a beach that faces north and northeast.

Ceremony against sunset

Sunset here runs from about 6:06 PM in November to 7:32 PM in July. Finish the ceremony roughly ninety minutes before it, so the golden hour is free for portraits and the vows are in warm, not flat, light.

Sunrise sessions

First light on an empty beach before the resort wakes. The corridor is silent before eight and the water is at its calmest.

The midday rule

Schedule nothing important between 11 AM and 3 PM. The light is overhead and hard, and the resorts are at their busiest.

Season

October to May is the long dry stretch with the most reliable light. Summer brings heat and humidity, so coverage moves to the edges of the day.

Before You Book

What couples ask about a Playa Mujeres wedding

Do Playa Mujeres resorts allow an outside photographer?

Yes, at every property we have worked. Most charge an outside vendor fee, which in this region runs from 150 to 1,500 USD per supplier depending on the resort, season and package. Ask for your resort's current figure in writing before you sign, or let us do it.

How far is Playa Mujeres from Cancún airport?

Roughly half an hour to forty five minutes, depending on traffic and which property. It sits north of Puerto Juárez, past the ferry terminals to Isla Mujeres.

Is there less seaweed at Playa Mujeres?

Generally, yes. The coast turns here and the beach faces roughly north and northeast, so the sargassum that reaches the open coast further south usually arrives in far smaller quantities. We still check the shoreline the morning of the session and keep gardens and pools as backups.

Secrets or Dreams Playa Mujeres for a wedding?

One rule decides it: Secrets is adults-only, Dreams welcomes everyone. Both belong to the same Hyatt Inclusive Collection with the same coordinators and packages, so the difference is atmosphere and who can be in your ceremony, not quality.

How much does wedding photography cost here?

Our published ranges apply to Playa Mujeres as to Cancún: a half day built around the ceremony and golden hour runs 1,800 to 2,800 USD, a full day with a second shooter and venue scouting 3,500 to 5,500, and the full day with a cinematic highlight film 5,500 to 8,000. Every collection includes the complete edited gallery in one to three business days.

When should the ceremony start?

About ninety minutes before sunset, which runs from roughly 6:06 PM in November to 7:32 PM in July. That leaves the golden hour free for portraits on the north-facing beach.

Do you speak English?

Yes, the whole studio works in English and Spanish. On a resort wedding day that matters, because the couple and the guests are usually working in English while the coordinator and the vendors are working in Spanish.
