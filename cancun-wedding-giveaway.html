Straight answers

Questions couples are asking

Do we need to have a date or a venue already?

No. You only need to be planning a wedding. If you win, you choose any available date between February 1, 2027 and March 31, 2028 and we confirm it with you. If you already have a date, even better: tell us in the form.

Is travel included?

Your flights, your stay and your venue are on you, exactly as they would be for any destination wedding. Our travel within Cancún and the Riviera Maya, from Playa Mujeres to Tulum, is included in the prize. If your wedding is in Los Cabos or elsewhere in Mexico, we can apply the prize there and you cover our travel.

Does the resort charge for an outside photographer?

Some resorts charge a vendor fee or require a day pass for outside vendors. That fee, when it exists, is paid by the couple. We know how most resorts in the area handle it and will tell you what to expect before you confirm.

Can we enter if we already booked a photographer?

Yes. The prize is yours to use however it fits your plans, as long as the wedding takes place in the area and within the dates in the rules.

Can we enter more than once?

One entry per person and per email address. You earn extra entries by sharing your personal link and by following us on Instagram. Duplicate or automated entries are removed.

How is the winner chosen?

At random, among all eligible entries, on December 4, 2026. We email the winner the same day. They have seven days to confirm, and once they do we announce them on Instagram. Odds depend on the number of entries received.

What happens with my information?

We use it to run the giveaway and to send you a handful of emails about it and about our wedding work. We never sell or share your details, and every email has a way to unsubscribe. Privacy Policy.

We are not in the United States. Can we enter?

This giveaway is only for legal residents of the 50 United States and the District of Columbia. If you are planning a wedding in Mexico from somewhere else, write to us at info@ivaestudios.com and we will gladly help.
