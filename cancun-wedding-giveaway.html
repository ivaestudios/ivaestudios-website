Straight answers

Questions couples are asking

Is it really free? What's the catch?

Yes. Your full wedding day photographed by our team, all your edited photos, the slideshow and your private gallery with print rights, free. You cover our travel at cost when the wedding is outside Cancún and the Riviera Maya, and any fee your venue charges outside vendors. If you want the film, a second photographer or an album, those are at our regular prices. There is no obligation to add anything.

How much is the travel?

It depends on the city and the dates. Round trip flights from Cancún for two people, two nights near your venue and local transport usually come to between $900 and $1,800. We send you the estimate with real fares before you confirm, and the receipts after. For weddings in Cancún or the Riviera Maya there is no travel cost.

Where can the wedding be?

Anywhere in the United States, or in Cancún and the Riviera Maya. Cancún is a direct flight from most big cities, which is why Dallas, Houston, Chicago, New York, Philadelphia, Atlanta, Miami, Charlotte, Denver and Los Angeles are the easiest for us. Other cities work too; the travel estimate tells you exactly what it costs.

Do we need to have a date or a venue already?

No. Tell us the month you are thinking of and we will tell you what we have open. Dates are first come, first served, and we confirm them in writing.

How many weddings are you doing this way?

A limited number in 2027, because we want to give each one the same care as any other wedding we photograph. When a month fills up, we say so.

Does the venue charge for an outside photographer?

Some venues and resorts charge a vendor fee or require a day pass for outside vendors. That fee, when it exists, is paid by the couple. We will tell you what to expect before you confirm.

Can we apply if we already booked a photographer?

Yes. Many couples add us for the full day or for part of it. Tell us your plans and we will tell you how we can fit.

What happens with our information?

We use it to reply to your application and to send you a handful of emails about this offer and our wedding work. We never sell or share your details, and every email has a way to unsubscribe. Privacy Policy.

We are not in the United States. Can we apply?

This offer is for legal residents of the 50 United States and the District of Columbia. If you are planning a wedding in Mexico from somewhere else, write to info@ivaestudios.com and we will gladly help.
